# PolicyOS Agent Instructions

Scope: entire repo. Product root is `policy-engine/`; follow `policy-engine/CONTRIBUTING.md`. For current E02 organization and status, use [E02-WORK-PLAN.md](E02-WORK-PLAN.md); dated handovers are historical references, not additional live workflows.

## North Star (read first — what we are building)

We are building a **causal operating system for policy**, and its ratified identity is the **epistemic custodian of policy justification**: the system proves *why* a policy recommendation, limitation, abstention, or publication is admissible — and keeps that justification honest for the whole life of the policy. Everything reduces to **data**; abstractions ("worker", "firm") are not objects but **communities over a causal variable graph** (an SCM) — but the causal *structure* (which variable affects which) is a distinct, harder, partly-irreducible knowledge, not just data. An **intervention** is a `do()` operation (direct state edit) carrying a predicted distribution over downstream change `P(Y|do(X))` (indirect); its atom is *(one operator, one target-slot, one bundle of direct effects, one declared intended effect)*, relative to the world-model resolution — goals are a separate selection layer. A **design** composes many atomic interventions, checked individual → pairwise → **joint** by **simulation in the world model**. Data is **required vs available**; the gap is filled by **VOI-driven acquisition** (demand paging). The real product is the **growing causal world model**; policies are programs against it; a deployed policy is monitored in two contours — **confirmatory** (high-authority Bayesian effect update) and **exploratory** (low-authority anomaly discovery under false-discovery control) that grows the model. The **safety kernel** is the honest-grounding firewall: every effect/abstraction/value is a **candidate until grounded + validated**; the worst failure is a **confident-wrong effect** shipped on a fake/weak coupling. Full frame: `policy-engine/docs/system-design-decisions/policy-design-causal-operating-system-north-star.md`; the law: `.../universal-policy-design-system-vision-and-organizing-rules.md`.

## Identity And Boundary (ratified 2026-07-20 — applies to every task, however local)

- **The signature rule:** PolicyOS owns everything it signs, for exactly as long as the signature publicly stands; it consumes everything others sign as **typed evidence**; it makes **no claims it cannot custody**. A claim honest at t0 silently becomes false when law, data, calibration, or the world changes — so epochs, staleness, revalidation, and the perturbation cascade are the *completion* of honesty, not optional operations.
- **Three roles:** design authority (grounded design or costed refusal-with-a-path) · justification custodian (every published signature stays honest over time) · post-deployment learning loop. **Binding anti-roles:** not an administrator, executor, case-management system, court, notification channel, payment system, or CRM — those are other institutions' signatures.
- **Scope questions are adjudicated, not debated:** apply the four-way test — absence makes OUR published claim silently false → **own**; output changes our claims' validity → **integrate** (we own the typed fail-closed evidence contract, not the function); changes only who answers for claims → **observe**; else → **out_of_scope**. Rulings and procedure: `policy-engine/docs/system-design-decisions/policyos-identity-and-custody-boundary.md`.
- **Classify one plane at a time** (`S0-K03`) — the test breaks on a mixed row. The chain is: external institutional act → evidence emission → our receipt/verification/admission → our scoped claim reaction → public projection. Decompose a row that spans several *before* letting it constrain work. Related: fail-closed binds the **authority** band — protected actions, published claims, custody facts (`S0-K06`); the candidate band may work under unknown scope with a **declared** unknown carried forward as a typed limitation. Silently substituting a concrete scope for an unknown one is the violation; a declared assumption is not. The sixteen ratified custody invariants: `policy-engine/docs/system-design-decisions/stage0-custody-kernel-ratification.md`.
- **Extensions:** take those that are *projections of already-built core* (cascade → appeal ingestion; epochs → staleness surfaces; DDM → KPI contract); decline those requiring a new sovereign subsystem. When your local task seems to need an out-of-boundary function, the deliverable is the integrate-contract, never ownership.

## Working Model

- Prefer `wire-existing` → `extend-existing` → `consolidate-existing` → `build-new`. Keep changes typed and boundary-aware; preserve unrelated/user work.
- Named owners edit real source and mirrored tests directly. Assign one writer per overlapping mechanism and mandatory companions; independent authors may work concurrently. RAW/postimages/chat must not be the sole home of new useful code.
- One coordinator owns the canonical Git index, commits, shared generated families and refs. Stage only a released owner's explicit paths, inspect the staged diff and hook changes, and keep the affected dependency set stable through verification and commit binding. Use visible worktree isolation when that cannot be guaranteed; do not copy environments/data per worker.
- Maintain one current plan/status for an existing workstream; update it in place. Git supplies history. Do not add a dated plan, receipt schema, copied source inventory or another approval round for an already authorized routine step.
- Reuse the actual checkout/environment. Check branch/path before assignment; use the [workspace admission check](policy-engine/tools/devx/workspace/README.md#worktree-admission) before creating/resuming a worktree. A receipt is not a reservation.

## Skill Selection and Existing Authorization

- For authorized implementation/maintenance, prefer `codex-engineering-guardrails:code-work`; for independent review or verification, prefer `codex-engineering-guardrails:code-verification`, when available. Use other skills only for a concrete relevant benefit or an explicit user request.
- Use interactive brainstorming for genuinely open design or requested exploration, and planning skills for requested plans or material new scope. Existing requirements, decisions and execution authorization do not need another design/plan approval chain.
- Do not automatically chain generic process skills. Their broad triggers do not add a serial-author rule, a fresh agent/review per micro-step, mandatory new worktrees/setup, a finishing menu, or blanket full-suite reruns to the chosen workflow.
- Preserve useful existing work rather than deleting it to imitate test-first history. Retain causal debugging and meaningful regression evidence. Review one coherent change independently, then review material deltas; clarify only work dependent on an unresolved question.
- Skill guidance cannot expand authorization or override higher-priority platform/user instructions. Honor explicitly requested skills within those boundaries. These project rules govern selection/use; they do not disable host discovery or modify installed plugin caches/global settings. Do not create a wrapper skill to repeat these rules.

## Product Correctness and Pattern Pass

- A capability needs a typed contract/artifact, real producer, persisted artifact/event, orchestration bridge, consumer, verification, visible/API/audit surface or explicit out-of-scope decision, and a meaningful negative/e2e semantic test. Constructor/marker/mock-only checks do not establish that chain.
- Use the [failure/repair register](policy-engine/docs/reference/policy-design-case-failure-patterns.md) for relevant risks before design and closeout. A short pattern pass belongs in the existing plan/change description, not a new document. Name the missing capability precisely using its registered label; a label does not complete it.
- Fix classes and canonical owners (P27/P31), not parallel per-site implementations. Keep status, authority purpose, provenance, rule/schema version and relevant time roles load-bearing. New gates/enums need actual mixed-outcome composition tests.
- Authority/promotion gates require resolved, content-bound evidence and verifier provenance (P29/P32). Test the real property and a distinguishing adversarial/removal case (P33/P38), including affected sibling consumers. Apply these controls to the semantic surface at risk, not mechanically to every docs or fixture edit.
- For load-bearing gate predicates, preserve P37's `recomputed`, `independently_reconciled`, `consumer_asserted`, `institutionally_supplied`, `not_established` classifications and fail closed/degrade on the last three. Candidate work may continue with a declared limitation; it cannot silently become authority.
- Set-level counts require a complete named path/file-type denominator, not a sample/search index (P35). Reuse a pinned complete census when unchanged. Cite the finding ID where one exists, otherwise the primary section; repair dependent bindings, not only prose (P36).
- On the second same-class escape, widen the mechanism or establish the actual missing capability and residual falsifier (P40); keep affected authority fail-closed and the criterion unresolved. A generic verifier derived from the actual source of truth with genuine constraints is a stopping point, not a reason to recursively verify the verifier (P29).
- Call a red inherited only after exact replay from the pre-change slice base and proof of disjoint complete inputs (P41); otherwise attribution is unknown. Isolate that replay without resetting/stashing or overwriting others' work. Do not discard a failure for a convenient green result.
- Keep mechanism budgets separate from mandatory companions (P39). [Measurement instruments](policy-engine/docs/how-to/author-measurement-instruments.md) disclose actual inputs and unresolved boundaries. Apply proportional governance (P13), not more paperwork by default.

## Checks, Evidence and Delivery

- Select checks by changed behavior and affected importers/consumers. Use Ruff and applicable architecture/generated/runtime checks; a changed validator needs a real corrupt-input/removal control. Small docs/fixture/import edits do not automatically require a full backend suite.
- Run broad backend/CI-parity, native/installed and cross-consumer checks when required by the real blast radius, configured gate or product closeout. Review/fix source, freeze relevant inputs/backend, then run the expensive wave once. Reuse source/input/backend-qualified results when unaffected; a new documentation HEAD alone does not invalidate them. Never present unrun work or changed inputs as verified.
- A compact change record gives requirement, actual change, command/result, material profile/input and needed output access. Retain complete deciding-gate/removal output once; keep it concise or losslessly compressed. Deliver actual bytes required by the receiver; a missing file's hash is not evidence access. No duplicate readsets, source/environment/dependency/CAS copies or routine separate receipt commits.
- Oversized raw outputs may use ignored `raw/` storage with a locator/hash; it is temporary/output storage, not hidden authorship or the current status. Committing an exceptional large artifact requires a stated reason; do not silently force-add it. Preserve needed access or name the exact rerun before expiry.
- Use ordinary hooks and required CI. Investigate failures; do not weaken assertions, bypass gates, fabricate authority or relabel errors for green. One fresh independent review per coherent product change; independent G acceptance remains separate where applicable.
- Read back delivered files from their commit/branch and published remote identity. Report checks and limitations accurately. Do not create upload workflows, encoded fragments or credential/network bypasses to work around a blocked channel.

## Git and Resources

- Verify attached branch/path at start and before commits. Commit coherent useful work before stopping; a stash is not storage. Unexpected HEAD/tree/branch state is investigated/reported, not reset or switched away.
- History is append-only: no rebase, destructive reset, force-update of published history, filter-branch or stash deletion. Only an immediately preceding self-authored, unreviewed commit may be amended. An ordinary reviewed merge/cherry-pick preserves originals; reconcile its affected consumers. Recover a regression by checked forward fix or safe revert of the introducing own change, preserving unrelated work.
- Serialize only actual write/dependency/resource conflicts: DB/cache/scratch, ports, generators, source under a deciding check. Measure numerical-job RAM, CPU threads, disk growth and time separately; assistant count is not compute capacity. Reuse measured profiles; an unknown expensive profile starts alone. Do not reinstall a working environment or run cleanup to manufacture capacity.
- Keep edits and tests visible, report meaningful state changes, and review deltas after the first coherent review. Do not poll noisily or produce heartbeat documents.
