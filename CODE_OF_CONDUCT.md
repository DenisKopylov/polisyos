# Code of Conduct

PolicyOS maintainers and contributors are expected to keep the repository
professional, respectful, and safe for collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in code review, issues, design discussions, and docs edits.
- Focus feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Assume good intent, but be explicit about risks, blockers, and tradeoffs.
- Share context generously when reviewing or asking for help.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and confidential material.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or repeated bad-faith engagement.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Disruptive behavior that makes collaboration unsafe or materially harder.
- Knowingly sharing secrets, exploit details, or other sensitive material in
  public channels when private reporting is required.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews,
documentation, and other project-maintained collaboration spaces.

## Reporting

- Conduct concerns should be reported to `skvidvard167m@gmail.com`.
- Security reports and operational incidents should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
  and [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) instead of this channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this policy.
Depending on severity, they may also pause participation or block access to the
project collaboration surfaces.

Enforcement decisions prioritize contributor safety, repository continuity, and
clear explanation of what behavior needs to change.
