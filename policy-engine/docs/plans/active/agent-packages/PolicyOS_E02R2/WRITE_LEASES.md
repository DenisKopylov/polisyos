## Compute admission after the interruption

Run one process for an unprofiled or heavy job. Admit up to three same-blob groups of measured-light jobs only while every group stays at or below 1 GiB resident memory and 100% CPU, aggregate use stays at or below 6 GiB and 300% CPU, free RAM reserve remains at least 45%, and swap stays flat. A native or exclusive-resource job runs alone. When any guard turns red, stop admission and record affected work as typed `UNRUN` or checkpoint it; do not fall back to singleton `Popen` execution. Keep the workspace's 10 GiB free-disk floor.
