# DES-10 Productive Search and Document Quality Acceptance Plan

**Status:** Proposed R14 planning supplement. This document defines future evaluation work allocated as DES-10 in [tasks.json](../tasks.json); it does not accept E02, amend an SDD or report runtime results. Execute only after actual E02 acceptance, scoped source rebind and the specified final review stage.

**Goal:** Show, on bounded and frozen cases, that the existing design-search path can produce decision-relevant, structurally distinct candidate alternatives under equal resource ceilings, and that a selected candidate can be rendered and edited as a role-faithful private policy document.

**Approach:** Extend the existing GYD benchmark, replay, and health-readout owners and connect their artifacts to DES-06/07 and CNT-09. Keep productive quality diagnostic and candidate-scoped; existing authority, evidence, status, rights, and publication owners remain decisive.

**Scope:** The claim is limited to the frozen cases, source snapshots, domains, audience, provider/model exposure, resource profile, rules, and candidate semantics actually evaluated. No result establishes a universally best policy, design, search method, or reader experience.

This is a bounded extension of existing owners, not a second benchmark framework or an independent search/evaluation service.

| Existing owner | Reuse in DES-10 | Boundary |

| GYD-18 / V3 | Existing multi-domain, multi-case benchmark and source-derived denominator | Add pre-registered positive controls and quality annotations to this case set; do not fork a second corpus. |

| GYD-25 / D3 | Retrieval precision, recall, and freshness evidence where search uses retrieved sources | Retrieval correctness does not establish intervention diversity or policy quality. |
| GYD-26 / C2 | Bind any LLM-judge output to its exact input and use | Binding does not establish judge validity; the judge remains secondary and diagnostic. |
| DES-06/07 | Render a selected packet privately; classify human edits and re-evaluate changed semantics | Drafting/editing does not enact, authorize, publish, or prove a proposal. |

At task rebind, reuse existing case, run, trace, report, and consumer artifacts where they can bind the exact candidate set and its provenance. If a join is missing, the owning task may extend its own output minimally. Do not create a parallel benchmark corpus, judge registry, generator, report service, ledger, or public document surface. The complete capability proof names contract/artifact, actual producer, persisted output, bridge, consumer, verification, permitted surface, and semantic positive and negative evidence. The current bounded plan omission is evaluation allocation, not `producer_missing` in the product.

## Frozen comparison protocol

Before any final holdout run, the GYD-18 owner and the relevant case authority freeze and bind:

- case IDs and source/index snapshots, including source versions and freshness; domains and any unseen-domain designation;
- each case's policy question, declared audience, objective/value frame (or explicit absence of authorized weights), decision needs, constraints, and status/authority boundary;
- eligible candidate slots, candidate grammar/schema and rule versions, positive-control witnesses, and cases where no feasible alternative is known;
- comparison arms, run count, seeds/settings where supported, provider/model/prompt/tool profile, candidate limit, budget ceilings, endpoint definitions, analysis, exclusions, and a minimally decision-relevant difference;
- development/validation/holdout membership, label custody, known benchmark/model exposure, every prior query or selection event, and the permitted audience for artifacts.

Use the current no-new-intervention option as the explicit no-op comparator. Compare the existing hybrid production path with a deterministic/grammar baseline; include LLM-proposal-only only where the existing route safely permits it. Memory-on/off or other ablations are secondary, pre-registered arms when they are part of the R14 scope. The no-op arm receives the same maximum resource ceilings and output allowance, but is not forced to spend them. Do not tune an arm or choose a winner from sealed holdout feedback.

### Live-provider evaluation and recorded replay are separate results

**Live-provider evaluation** measures a real execution through the authorized production route. It requires a genuine provider invocation and persisted producer output for each counted run. Bind, using existing ledgers and permitted references, the provider and model/version (or an explicit unknown), endpoint/profile, prompt and tool manifest, input/source/index and rule hashes, settings and seed where exposed, actual trace, timestamps, retries/failures, and actual cost. Provider availability, alias drift, hidden versioning, or unsupported seeds limit reproducibility; they do not justify labeling a run deterministic. `llm_assisted` requires trace evidence that the model actually contributed to the selected candidate. Report the live output distribution across the pre-registered runs. Do not claim exact text replay for stochastic or provider-controlled generation.

**Recorded replay** consumes frozen persisted inputs, candidate artifacts, decisions, and traces without making a new provider call. It proves only the applicable GYD-20/V5 level: deterministic bytes for deterministic operations; trace reconstruction for agentic work; and revalidation of persisted artifacts and their consumers. It must exercise the real downstream comparison/readout and expose altered, missing, stale, or misbound records. A replay can verify that a recorded live result is interpreted consistently; it cannot count as fresh generation, provider availability, new search coverage, or a second quality observation. Any new model/provider call is classified and costed as a live run, not replay.

Emit separate live-evaluation and recorded-replay findings linked to the same frozen case/run identities. If exact model identity or data exposure cannot be established, retain the result with a bounded limitation; the corresponding reproducibility or generalization claim is `not_established`.

### Equal ceilings and actual cost

For every arm, hold the same per-case maxima for model/tool calls, input/output tokens or equivalent provider usage, compute, retrieval/acquisition, simulation/trials, candidate slots, expert/reviewer minutes, and elapsed time. Record both each ceiling and actual use, including retries, failed/aborted calls, cached work, evaluator effort, and unused allowance. Use the same source snapshot and case inputs. No arm receives an unreported top-up, extra expert time, or larger candidate set. Equal ceilings do not mean equal realized spend; do not burn resources to equalize the no-op. Report quality and semantic coverage against actual costs as a vector/front, not only at a selected operating point.

Use existing CAP/LIF/OPS cost, rights, and execution controls. Persist only allowed inputs/outputs and provenance; this plan does not require raw hidden reasoning or unauthorized prompt/document text in an audit surface.

## Case classes and endpoints

Pre-classify cases using an owner-reconciled witness or independent case adjudication, before revealing generated candidates:

- **Known-solvable positive control:** at least one non-no-op candidate is independently known to satisfy the declared structural and case constraints. This is the positive capability check that prevents an all-abstain search path from passing.
- **Open or uncertain search:** no complete solution set is asserted. Unknown effects, unresolved evidence, or unavailable sources remain visible limits, not grounds to suppress candidate hypotheses.
- **Constrained/no-known-alternative case:** evidence or constraints may support an honest no-op, refusal, or no candidate. Do not require a minimum candidate count or diversity score on such cases.

The suite must contain pre-registered positive controls from the existing GYD-18 case strata before DES-10 can claim productive search. For the candidate-producing system under test, all-abstain/all-no-op on its designated positive controls is a failure. The no-op baseline and deliberate removal arms are expected countercontrols; they are not required to produce an intervention. The owner sets the run-level success target and sample size before freeze; an absent target, too-small sample, or inconclusive confidence bound yields `not_established`, not a post-hoc threshold. Do not impose an arbitrary number of distinct options on every case. Measure semantic diversity only where multiple independent decision-relevant branches are possible; report no-feasible/one-feasible cases separately.

| Endpoint | Measurement and acceptance interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| **Positive productivity (hard capability check)** | On designated pre-registered known-solvable controls, the real producer returns a typed non-no-op candidate meeting the known witness AND the predeclared decision need (or a concrete evidence/acquisition path resolving that need), checked by independent labels. Feasible but irrelevant diversity and all-abstain fail. Freeze a bounded run/case criterion before outcomes; all-null support cannot close productive capability. This proves only candidate generation on the scoped controls; it does not prove the proposed effect is true, the candidate is admissible, or the policy is optimal. |
| **Incremental decision support vs no-op** | For each case, independent reviewers decide whether the generated set adds a semantically distinct response to a pre-registered decision need, or a specific evidence/acquisition path that would resolve it, compared with no new intervention. Record paired case outcomes and reasons. A fluent paraphrase, candidate count, unsupported factual assertion, or refusal alone does not count as incremental support. A correctly labelled hypothesis can count as a candidate response; it need not first be proven as fact. |
| **Semantic diversity and redundancy** | Label candidate meaning from the intervention/operator, target, direct effects, intended effect, causal mechanism/coupling, affected populations, implementation conditions, constraints, and relevant assumptions. Compare actual emitted candidates to independently labelled decision-relevant branches; report unique branch coverage, duplicate/equivalence rate, and redundancy. Use embeddings only to triage possible pairs, not decide equivalence. A paraphrase with the same mechanism is one candidate; distinct mechanisms with similar wording remain distinct. Preserve an open “other” category. No fixed diversity minimum applies to impossible, constrained, or single-feasible cases. |
| **Task-qualified quality** | Report separately the dimensions relevant to the case (e.g. feasibility under declared constraints, evidence path, uncertainty, affected groups, cost, robustness, or an authorized value dimension). Use the existing set-valued/Pareto comparison where no principal supplied weights. Do not collapse incomparable designs to a universal scalar winner. Simulated or hypothetical scores remain model/simulation outputs, not observed-world effects. |
| **Claim-role fidelity (safety endpoint)** | Audit material claims in each sampled candidate/document against their declared role, status, scope, time, population, uncertainty, provenance, and contrary evidence. Distinguish observation, externally sourced fact, computed/simulated result, unsupported hypothesis, value assumption, legal premise, and proposal. An unsupported hypothetical effect may remain as an explicit hypothesis with its assumptions and limits; it fails only if presented as established fact, observed effect, current law, selected value, permission, or institutional decision without the required basis. A material role/status/authority contradiction fails that artifact/surface. |
| **Resource and search-boundary reporting** | Show ceilings beside actual costs, candidate coverage, omitted/selected/rejected/unexecuted branches, retrieval state and incompleteness. A limit reached is not evidence that the space is exhausted. Preserve the existing V7 descriptive posture. |
| **`useful_design_rate`** | If reported, it is a frozen-case descriptive diagnostic with its denominator and interval. It must not be an optimization target, model/reward/ranking objective, prompt-selection criterion, stopping rule, memory-training target, promotion evidence, or by itself establish a public performance claim. Any permitted public diagnostic follows existing UI-16/source-qualified performance and audience rules; R14 grants no publication permission. |

## Independent labels, uncertainty, and selection controls

Reviewer panels below evaluate the bounded benchmark claims; they are not a universal runtime/manual approval gate. Missing reviewers leaves the affected evaluation claim not_established, while native machine-checkable relations and other engineering work continue under their own rules.

- At least two qualified reviewers independently label semantic equivalence, decision relevance, and candidate/document claim roles while blind to arm, model/provider, candidate order, and one another's labels. Bind the label to the exact artifact version. Preserve original labels, disagreement, rationales, and missing labels. Use an independently assigned adjudicator for unresolved disagreements; do not erase the disagreement or treat majority vote as authority. Report raw agreement and a suitable agreement statistic with uncertainty. If independent labeling or adjudication is unavailable, the affected endpoint is `not_established`.
- Factual/effect claims are checked by a source/effect reviewer against the native producer and exact basis. LLM judging is secondary triage only. If used, randomize and swap pair order, anonymize provider/style, control verbosity, calibrate only on separately labelled data, and measure disagreement and bias against human labels. GYD-26 binds the judge output but does not validate the judge.
- Treat cases as independent units; repeated seeds, candidates, and reviewers are nested observations. Pre-register sample size and a minimally decision-relevant difference with the case owner. Use paired case-level estimates, a case-clustered bootstrap or paired randomization, and 95% intervals; use exact intervals for binary outcomes where suitable. Correct the pre-registered co-primary family (for example, Holm or simultaneous intervals). Publish all arms, cases, seeds, exclusions, failures, missing results, and disagreement. If the available GYD-18 cases cannot resolve the stated difference, report descriptive results and `not_established`; do not count candidates as independent samples.
- Keep development cases separate from final evaluation cases and label packets. Log every holdout exposure/query, candidate/model selection decision, prompt/rubric change, and exclusion. Do not reuse GYD-08/AS2 or INT-R9's sealed promotion set for this different claim without explicit owner reconciliation. If a holdout was queried during tuning, it becomes development data and a fresh untouched evaluation set is required. Report public/likely model-training exposure, prior API use, provider retention uncertainty, and case-source overlap. Unknown exposure limits superiority/generalization claims to `not_established`; a hash or sealed local file alone is not a contamination guarantee.
- Pre-register co-primary metrics and exclusions; report every tested arm. A winning arm chosen after looking at the holdout requires a new holdout for confirmation. Multiplicity correction does not repair adaptive holdout reuse or undisclosed exclusions.

## Document endpoint: private reviewer/expert and CNT-09

DES-10 document evaluation follows a selected packet through the planned DES-06/07 authoring loop and CNT-09's real consumer. The first endpoint is an authorized private reviewer/expert surface, with exact candidate/source/version/status references. Assess the document as a readable projection of typed records: action/decision question, no-op and alternatives, affected groups, evidence and counterevidence, assumptions, uncertainty, cost/feasibility, missing information, and next decision. Values and legal proposals retain their roles; numbers derive from permitted producer artifacts. A DES-07 edit that changes a number, claim, affected group, duty, or mechanism must create a classified candidate delta and recompute the affected basis before a refreshed document is treated as aligned. Unbound edits remain visibly unbound.

The reader task for this engineering endpoint asks an authorized reviewer/expert to locate the proposal's status, basis, limits, unresolved choice, and next step. Record item-level task outcomes, time, and errors as diagnostics. Readability formulas, fluent prose, and an LLM score do not substitute for comprehension or truthfulness.

This document endpoint does **not** use UI-02 as text authorization. UI-02's applicable public practice is data-only, and the G6 PUBLIC boundary excludes raw recommendation/prompt text. CNT-09 and the DES-06/07 private reviewer/expert route are the selected consumer/surface for this evaluation. Public recommendation text, raw candidate documents, enactment, or publication remain outside DES-10 (`surface_out_of_scope`) pending their own exact authority and projection decision.

If representative target-reader recruitment or approval is unavailable, mark the audience-comprehension study `not_established`; do not substitute internal experts, readability scores, or an LLM judge as a representative study. This does not block an engineering pass for a correctly wired private document flow, exact claim-role fidelity, and successful CNT-09 readback. It does block any claim that the document is comprehensible to the broader target audience. Record engineering status and the future reader-study status separately using existing status semantics; do not add a new product enum.

## Distinguishing acceptance probes

The DES-10 owner incorporates these property tests in the existing evaluation/review wave; this plan does not implement tests or add a test framework.

1. Replace every candidate-producing positive-control result with abstention/no-op, or feasible/structurally distinct but decision-irrelevant proposals: productive-capability acceptance fails even if refusal and authority checks remain green.
2. Give the same intervention multiple IDs and paraphrases: semantic coverage does not increase. Give two mechanisms similar prose but different causal/target/effect structure: independent reviewers can retain the distinction.
3. Remove evidence/source bindings while retaining candidate IDs, scores, report markers, or declared family counts: claim-role audit downgrades/fails; actual emitted-family counts remain source-derived.
4. Remove factual support for a hypothetical effect while retaining its explicit hypothesis role, assumptions, and candidate status: the candidate remains eligible for candidate-band diversity/relevance evaluation. Change the same claim to assert an observed/established effect without a source: claim-role fidelity fails.
5. Stale the source/index or remove a known seed while retaining an “adequate search” declaration: the report shows the retrieval/search limit; it cannot claim a domain ceiling or exhaustive result.
6. Swap reviewer order, reveal provider identity, or pad only one answer: label instability/bias is visible and cannot create a winning arm. Retain independent disagreement rather than replacing it with a majority label.
7. Remove the actual live provider invocation but retain an `llm_assisted` label: live-provider evaluation fails. Re-run recorded replay with no provider call and prove it is not counted as a fresh generation or quality observation.
8. Omit a case, failed arm, seed, cost item, holdout query, or disagreement from the readout: source-derived denominators and exposure/cost reconciliation fail.
9. Remove a material document's source/role/status qualifier, alter a policy-relevant number, or edit a proposal as enacted: the document audit or DES-07 recomputation fails. A public/raw-document request remains denied; the authorized private reviewer/expert and CNT-09 path remains the only document endpoint in this scope.

## Sequencing and closure

Preparation of rubric, budget matrix, positive-control witnesses, reviewer instructions, label custody, and holdout exposure log may proceed against agreed interfaces before the expensive run. Final evidence waits for actual E02 acceptance and scoped source rebind, GYD-17 producer output, GYD-18 frozen cases, GYD-20 replay profile, GYD-25 retrieval inputs when applicable, GYD-26 judge binding when an LLM judge is used, and the actual DES-06/07 + CNT-09 private consumer path for the document endpoint. GYD-22 reads only after those bound measurements exist. Include all evidence in the already planned final verification wave; do not schedule a second global replay.

Composed productive-capability acceptance additionally requires the predeclared meaningful decision-support positive above; an all-null result leaves that obligation not_established despite a plumbing PASS. It is a frozen diagnostic discriminator, not a reward/optimization/promotion target.

Engineering acceptance requires: (1) real producer output on the frozen positive controls; (2) no all-abstain pass; (3) equal ceilings and fully reconciled actual costs; (4) semantic/relevance labels with raw disagreement and case-level bounds; (5) claim-role fidelity including the unsupported-hypothesis case; (6) replay at the applicable V5 level, separately reported from live evaluation; (7) complete denominator, failure, and exposure readback; and (8) a real private DES-06/07 document path consumed by CNT-09 with the public text boundary intact. Comparative superiority, broad generalization, and target-audience comprehension are separate claims; each may remain `not_established` without invalidating the scoped engineering pass.

**No universal optimum:** absent an authorized value weighting, retain a set/front of incomparable candidate options and the no-op. A comparative win supports only the frozen scope; it proves neither causal policy benefit nor admissibility, publication authority, or universal search quality.

**Pattern pass:** P10 semantic adequacy; P13 proportional governance; P14 effective independence; P15 candidate-to-authority firewall; P25 search-boundary honesty; P29 recomputation from live evidence; P32 trust-by-form; P33 adversarial/removal coverage; P35 complete denominator; P37 evidence grade for each gate predicate; P38 property/proxy divergence. The target pattern is an owner-reused, source-bound comparative evaluation with a positive productivity control and authority-independent candidate diagnostics. The distinguishing divergent case is a safe, correctly labelled abstention on an impossible case that cannot substitute for a non-abstaining positive control. ## Primary sources and transfer limits

These sources support evaluation methods, not PolicyOS acceptance, authority, or any particular threshold.

1. Clarke et al., [“Novelty and Diversity in Information Retrieval Evaluation” (SIGIR 2008)](https://doi.org/10.1145/1390334.1390446): separates non-redundancy from intent coverage and motivates diminishing credit for duplicate coverage. Retrieval intent labels do not establish policy relevance, causal validity, or normative value.
2. Zhang et al., [“Trading Off Diversity and Quality in Natural Language Generation” (2021)](https://aclanthology.org/2021.humeval-1.3/): supports measuring a quality/diversity frontier rather than assuming a universal decoder. Dialogue/story results do not establish policy-search quality.
3. Zheng et al., [“Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena” (NeurIPS 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685): documents position, verbosity, and self-enhancement biases that motivate blind, order-swapped human comparison. Chat preference agreement does not validate legal, causal, or policy judgments.
4. Dwork et al., [“Generalization in Adaptive Data Analysis and Holdout Reuse” (NeurIPS 2015)](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/hash/bad5f33780c42f2588878a9d07405083-Abstract.html): motivates exposure logging and fresh/sealed evaluation under adaptive reuse. Formal reusable-holdout guarantees require their assumptions and do not follow from a checksum or local seal.
5. Australian Government Style Manual, [“Quick guide: plain language”](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/style-manual-resources/quick-guides/quick-guide-plain-language): supports audience-first organization, familiar words, and comprehension-oriented review. Style advice establishes neither truthfulness nor actual reader comprehension.
