`ds8-public-case-publication` (DEBT-REGISTER.md:L480) and `ds8-signed-public-decision-surface` (L600) have latest source notes saying their original signal is owner/scope acquisition and the later publication language conflicts with it; both require architect scope/signal reconciliation. Yet UI-01 and UI-02 list both as original debt refs (tasks.json:L3801, L3905; refs at L3893/L3898 and L4007/L4012), and UI-02's deliverable is the full publication capability. The separate Atlas workstream summary still says DS12's signed-public-decision row “remains bridge_missing” (workstreams/04-atlas.md:L35), repeating the superseded capability framing.

**Divergent case:** UI-02 can ship a signed public record while the original scope-acquisition closure signal remains unresolved; the linked debt rows would then look addressed by the wrong property.
**Smallest fix:** in P0-REBIND, add these IDs to its explicit decision-only reconciliation refs and remove them from UI-01/UI-02 closure refs. If that decision creates a distinct publication obligation, add a successor ID with its own closure signal. Refresh the workstream summary. Do not change the source statuses until the owner records the ruling.
**Pattern lens:** P36/P38 — the crosswalk and stale summary substitute a nearby capability for the source criterion.
