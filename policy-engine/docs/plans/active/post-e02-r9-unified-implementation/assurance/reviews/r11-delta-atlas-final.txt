## Disposition

**A-R11-10 is addressed in the plan; no further acceptance or precedence finding remains in this narrow delta.** UI-04 now sets explicit positive, adversarial, and removal criteria for actual large-text, screen-reader, and plain-language behavior across consultation, response, and history surfaces. The plain-language bar requires audience-qualified human semantic assessment, not a keyword/presence check. Its negative cases include clipping/lost controls, screen-reader ordering/labels, and meaning-changing or inaccessible copy; the removal probe strips behavior or substitutes meaning-changing copy while keeping markers (`policy-engine/docs/plans/active/post-e02-r9-unified-implementation/tasks.json:4295-4298`). The acceptance is therefore testing the stated user-facing properties rather than locale/accessibility markers.

The earlier marketing requirement for formal Ukrainian/Russian register is preserved with its audience and jurisdiction conditional (`policy-engine/docs/plans/active/post-e02-r9-unified-implementation/tasks.json:4374-4379`). The task names D4-A1 as the controlling locale contract: English is the authored primary, Ukrainian its translation, and the Russian UI catalog remains `legacy_continuity_frozen`; it explicitly bars automatic Russian UI reactivation. The locale owner must bind each commissioned use to its audience, jurisdiction, and admitted locale. If a use truly needs active Russian presentation, the plan requires recording that conflict and obtaining a competent locale-contract amendment or an admitted, source-qualified translated-evidence integration; it prohibits silent waiver, activating `ru`, or blocking unrelated admitted locales. The workstream repeats this boundary (`policy-engine/docs/plans/active/post-e02-r9-unified-implementation/workstreams/04-atlas.md:132`).

This precedence matches the ratified source-of-truth decision: D4-A1 sets `en` as authored primary, `uk` as translation, and `ru` as frozen and excluded from active locale exposure/public support claims; Russian source-content rendering is a separate read-only capability (`policy-engine/docs/brand/ATLAS_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md:263-274, 306-320`). Thus the task does not silently waive the older criterion or allow it to override the later owner decision. Any external translated-evidence path remains scoped evidence integration; it is not permission to activate the frozen `ru` UI without an amendment.

The task remains planned after E02 acceptance and its `product_verification` field is `UNRUN` (`policy-engine/docs/plans/active/post-e02-r9-unified-implementation/tasks.json:4313-4314`); this review confirms only the documentary acceptance/precedence language.
