### UI-06 capstone value is composition, not a freeze prerequisite

`UI-06` has only `P0-REBIND` as a hard source dependency and declares `dependency_stage: owner_readiness` (`tasks.json:4451+`). Its conditional capstone requirement names `GYD-17` at `composed_acceptance`; consumer/surface preparation can use a pinned interface before final replay but cannot claim real-data acceptance. `GYD-17` itself has only `P0-REBIND` as a hard dependency and places `FINAL-REVIEW` plus the exact frozen source/input/backend profile in its acceptance dependencies (`tasks.json:2845+`). Therefore freeze may accept the reviewed grammar/consumer preparation while withholding capstone acceptance; after review, the actual GYD-17 output can feed UI-06 composition in the selected replay. This splits the apparent `FINAL-FREEZE → FINAL-REVIEW → GYD-17 composition → UI-06 composition` path from UI-06's earlier owner-readiness prerequisite and introduces no build cycle.

### OPS-02 separates local rehearsal from real first use

`OPS-02` hard-depends on `P0-REBIND` and `OPS-01` at owner readiness (`tasks.json:7028+`). A local candidate rehearsal has a separate execution requirement for `LIF-06` and `OPS-01`; shared-host execution adds `OPS-08`. A first real practical use has its own execution requirement with `FINAL-USE` as supplier (`tasks.json:7119+`). The contract further states that controlled rehearsals may verify internally under relevant resource/rights permits, while no actual first practical use or public launch occurs before `FINAL-USE` and required external authorization (`execution/05-stage-and-release-contracts.md:34–38`; `operations/01-deployment.md:80`). This is an execution-permission edge, not a build edge; it does not make FINAL-USE wait on a use that it must authorize.

`FINAL-USE` now carries a concrete whole-set predicate over the rebound GY, Atlas, debt, and R9 inventories. Each mandatory original needs accepted evidence or competent-owner formal closure; owner readiness, refusal, test PASS, unselected-by-preference, and planned external requests do not close it. Its negative explicitly leaves `GY-O0-NC-01` unresolved and requires FINAL-USE to remain blocked. Earlier use with deferred mandatory work requires explicit human scope change (`tasks.json:9177–9224`; `execution/05-stage-and-release-contracts.md:34–38`). This preserves the full programme gate.

The updated task contract also gives phase-specific owner-readiness/composition/original-disposition rules to GYD-01, DEB-09, CNT-09, and UI-16 (`execution/stage-requirements.json:19–54`). An authentic GYD near-miss remains an attempted request blocked before execution; it is unresolved until real canonical evidence or a competent ruling, not a freeze-time positive and not a reason to conduct an unsafe trial (`execution/05-stage-and-release-contracts.md:24–31`).
