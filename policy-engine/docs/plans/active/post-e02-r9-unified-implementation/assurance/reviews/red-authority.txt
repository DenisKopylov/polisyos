## Blocking residual

### R1. First-use gate still allows original obligations to remain open by disposition

`operations/02-practical-use.md:5` says the default is “all mandatory internal work” accepted and every original GY/Atlas/debt/R9 remainder “dispositioned.” Lines 9 and 35 permit external facts and optional tiers to remain, with the human/G release disposition deciding if their limits are compatible with first use. Line 7 properly says the narrower earlier-launch alternative is not selected, but the exception language can recreate it: an unfinished original row may be categorized as optional, deferred, or not needed for the selected profile and still coexist with first-use admission.

The controlling user requirement is all unfinished GY/Atlas/debt and R9 work before the first limited practical application. The current test therefore still has a possible P38 divergence: stated property = every original unfinished obligation is completed/closed before first use; operative property = every mandatory item is accepted and remaining items have a disposition compatible with this use. A concrete divergent case is an open original Atlas surface or R9 tier marked “not needed for this profile,” while a limited real advisory launches.

**Fix:** define the pre-use denominator as every non-closed obligation in the complete canonical GY, Atlas, debt, and R9 source set. Admit first use only when every item is completed and accepted, or its owning canonical source has formally closed/cancelled it before the gate. “Dispositioned,” “optional,” “unselected,” “deferred,” “not needed for this case,” and “external fact unavailable” do not close an unfinished obligation. Keep genuinely future external facts outside that denominator when they are not themselves an outstanding source obligation; they may still limit the claims that require them. Report zero unresolved original obligations as the acceptance signal. This preserves bounded first-use claims without making the selected profile a scope shortcut. Relevant patterns: P35 (complete denominator) and P38 (gate exercises the stated property).
