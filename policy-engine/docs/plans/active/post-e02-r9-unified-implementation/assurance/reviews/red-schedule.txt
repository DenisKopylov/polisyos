## Schedule and source checks

- The manifest has **106 unique nodes**: 100 mechanism tasks, P0-ACCEPT/P0-REBIND, and four final gates. All 311 hard edges resolve; the hard DAG has no cycle. Recomputed levels exactly match `execution/dependency-levels.json`: **14 levels**, widths `[1, 1, 55, 9, 12, 6, 9, 6, 2, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1]` (maximum 55).
- `acceptance_dependencies` are correctly distinguished from hard `depends_on` edges in both the manifest and Wave 0. A naive union of hard and acceptance edges does form expected witness cycles: for example CAP-03 consumes LIF-05, while LIF-05’s eventual composition witness is CAP-03; GY V tasks need final review for composition while their pre-freeze preparation feeds FINAL-FREEZE. These are not dispatch cycles under the written semantics. The manifest also gives the GY V rows a pre-freeze preparation deliverable and a separate `execute_during` stage, so the full capability is not presented as complete before replay.
- The final chain is FINAL-FREEZE → FINAL-REVIEW → FINAL-REPLAY → FINAL-USE. FINAL-USE requires owner acceptance or formal closure for every original nonclosed obligation and keeps missing external facts gating the affected claim. The 22 GY source-not-executed rows and the 14 measured-incomplete GY predicates are separately represented in the task graph.
- Complete inventory walks, not samples: `inventory/debt-inventory.json` has 277 rows (191 closed, 86 nonclosed); `inventory/gy-inventory.json` has 77 rows (55 source-executed, 22 not executed); `inventory/atlas-inventory.json` has 68 records, 28 with direct `program_tasks` routes, all resolving to valid task IDs and carrying the DS row ID in `source_ids`; `inventory/r9-obligations.json` has 115 obligation rows, all routed to valid task IDs. The R9 source map distinguishes its 26 families, 103 grounds and 197 source records instead of summing unlike denominators. CNT workstream references all EF-01…EF-26.
- **100-direct-agent arithmetic checks:** `execution/02-parallel-execution.md` gives 4 leads + 48 concurrent independent writer leases + 20 read-only mappers + 20 reviewers + 8 resource/run stewards = 100; root is additional. `prompts/orchestrator.md` and the worker prompts prohibit children/delegation. Agent capacity is correctly presented as a ceiling, not 100 concurrent code writers.
- **Lease/alias semantics:** all tasks have a canonical lease rule and lease-key field. The task manifest and execution docs say `resource_locks` are descriptions, aliases/wildcards must be expanded, and exact paths, generated families, semantic owners and resource tickets must be leased before a write/run. Eighty-five of 106 rows still have no concrete `canonical_write_candidates`; they are future post-E02 bindings, not ready-to-write paths. The known exact overlaps are CAP-01/CAP-03 on `acquisition_action_service.py` (no hard order, so the path lease must serialize them) and CAP-02/CAP-03 on `acquisition_executor.py` (CAP-03 also hard-depends on CAP-02).
