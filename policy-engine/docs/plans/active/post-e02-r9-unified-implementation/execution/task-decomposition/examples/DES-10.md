## Почему такая граница

The current card joins independent properties with different producers and evaluators: productive search, learning transfer, numerical/reexecution correctness, document fidelity, and target-reader comprehension. Reuse the existing GYD/R19 slices as five bounded campaigns. The DES-10 parent retains frozen scope, equal ceilings, source-derived denominators, exposure/cost/disagreement accounting, authority-independent reporting, and one FINAL-REPLAY/FINAL-USE integration; this is not five corpora, services, or global waves.

## Общие условия родителя

Названные ниже свойства сохраняют все применимые original criteria, extensions, status/authority/time/rights и mandatory companions. Root/lead перед назначением раскладывает **каждый** source clause в существующий coverage journal; этот пример не заменяет такую раскладку. Один actual semantic owner и write lease сохраняются. Независимая подготовка возможна по согласованному интерфейсу; composition и protected use требуют настоящих supplier outputs.

План не даёт права выбирать только удобные profiles или считать unknown applicability исключением. Условный join включается по фактическому контракту/claim. Родитель требует полный selected denominator и fresh end-to-end witness; локальные PASS не суммируются в closure.

## DES10-productivity — Test decision-relevant alternatives on known-solvable controls

**Свойство.** The real candidate producer returns a non-no-op, semantically distinct response to a predeclared decision need on designated known-solvable cases.

**Граница и owner.** GYD-17/18 producer and case owners plus DES-02; candidate productivity only, not factual effect truth, admissibility, or universal superiority.

**Входы.** Frozen case/source/control denominator, independent witness, actual candidate set and blinded semantic/relevance labels.

**Результат.** Per-case productive/limited result with semantic branch coverage, duplicates, disagreement, and exact run refs.

**Подготовка.** Freeze positive controls, alternatives rubric, comparator, arms, labels, and holdout exposure before outputs.

**Composition join.** Join actual GYD-17 output and GYD-18 case identity in the single FINAL-REPLAY; GYD-22 reads the bound report.

**Полезный positive.** A candidate meets the independent witness and declared decision need, or a concrete evidence path resolves it.

**Различающий negative.** All-abstain, paraphrases, or feasible but decision-irrelevant alternatives cannot pass the positive controls.

**Matched removal.** Remove actual producer invocation or independent semantic labels while keeping IDs/counts; the productivity claim fails.

## DES10-learning — Measure actual transfer of retrieved experience into search

**Свойство.** Eligible memory changes a later search choice usefully or preserves a justified no-transfer result without laundering evidence.

**Граница и owner.** DES-08/DES-04 and GYD-25 retrieval evidence; no causal/effect update, authority promotion, or retrieval-service rebuild.

**Входы.** Actual evaluated runs/lessons, rights and influence lineage, matched memory-on/off cases, and measured resource use.

**Результат.** Scoped transfer/false-transfer report with selected/rejected influence and fresh retrieval refs.

**Подготовка.** Freeze relevant context matches, negative-transfer cases, outcome labels, and contamination/exposure plan.

**Composition join.** Use actual persisted lesson and subsequent candidate outputs in FINAL-REPLAY; preserve GYD-25 and LIF-07 owners.

**Полезный positive.** A compatible lesson changes a later candidate/search choice, or a mismatch correctly prevents transfer.

**Различающий negative.** Failure-only recall, stale/wrong-scope lessons, descendants, or held-out exposure appears as independent empirical support.

**Matched removal.** Disable retrieval or persistence while retaining lesson refs; measured influence disappears.

## DES10-numerical — Check selected numerical, trajectory, and reexecution properties

**Свойство.** A selected native method returns the correct result/uncertainty and reexecutes or limits correctly on the bound basis/profile.

**Граница и owner.** Use GYD-20 and selected GYD-30/31, DES-03/11/12 owners; correctness and useful design stay separate; no universal solver comparison.

**Входы.** Actual trials/results, exact method/profile and versions, small independent oracle, changed-basis control, and reconciled costs.

**Результат.** Method-scoped numerical/reexecution result with unsupported dimensions, uncertainty, and exact consumer refs.

**Подготовка.** Select exact claims/profiles and independent small oracles; freeze clean-versus-selective and changed-basis controls.

**Composition join.** Consume actual selected owner outputs in FINAL-REPLAY; don't add a second replay or infer live result from a seed/marker.

**Полезный positive.** Independent oracle agrees within the declared method guarantee and a material bound/revision changes only affected results.

**Различающий negative.** Wrong units, omitted rival/model, hidden weights, stale basis, or a deterministic label without actual invocation passes.

**Matched removal.** Remove the actual trial or changed-basis input while retaining score/certificate markers; fresh numerical reader detects the loss.

## DES10-document-fidelity — Verify private document semantic fidelity and edit readback

**Свойство.** Material document bytes/edits faithfully project exact semantic roles and changed meanings trigger native recomputation before CNT-09 reuse.

**Граница и owner.** DES-06/07 private reviewer route and CNT-09; no public recommendation text, enactment, or publication authority.

**Входы.** Selected packet, bound source/role/status refs, exact DOCX carrier bytes, revision/sidecar, and real edit/readback.

**Результат.** Carrier fidelity and fresh-reader result, with changed/unbound cells and affected claim recomputations identified.

**Подготовка.** Select material paragraphs/cells and edit mutations; bind expected semantic roles and authorized private audience.

**Composition join.** Read the actual DES-06/07 output through CNT-09 in the shared FINAL-REPLAY endpoint.

**Полезный positive.** A fresh consumer resolves the exact carrier; a material edit creates a candidate delta and recomputes affected basis.

**Различающий negative.** Changed text/cell, counterfeit DOCX, altered number, or proposal-as-enacted keeps old IDs and appears aligned.

**Matched removal.** Remove content/role binding or edit-to-recompute bridge but keep watermark/sidecar; fidelity/readback fails.

## DES10-reader-comprehension — Study comprehension with the selected target audience

**Свойство.** The intended readers can identify status, authority, basis, limits, unresolved choice, and next action in the exact selected edition.

**Граница и owner.** Evaluation claim only; no reader certification, training authority, universal audience generalization, or replacement of the engineering fidelity campaign.

**Входы.** Approved target-reader recruitment, exact edition/support, task/oracle, and item-level answers, time, and errors.

**Результат.** Audience- and edition-scoped comprehension result; absent representative evidence stays not_established.

**Подготовка.** Prepare reader protocol and blind task against agreed interface; internal experts/readability scores are not substitutes.

**Composition join.** Join real target-reader outcomes only for the selected audience claim; its absence does not block DES-06/07/CNT-09 engineering proof.

**Полезный positive.** Selected readers correctly identify status, authority, uncertainty, limits, and next step for that edition.

**Различающий negative.** Fluency, readability scores, internal-expert review, or reader findings on a different edition stand in for comprehension.

**Matched removal.** Remove real reader outcomes, task oracle, or exact-edition binding; comprehension becomes not_established.
