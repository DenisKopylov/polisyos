## Почему такая граница

The exact-record publication and four-audience projections are one cross-surface invariant and stay together. Pre-persistence telemetry privacy and LIF-05 placement lifecycle are different properties with different owner evidence and removal probes; separating them shrinks implementation context without cutting transports apart. Keep accessibility, locale, security-header, mobile, performance, and human semantic review as required publication companions.

## Общие условия родителя

Названные ниже свойства сохраняют все применимые original criteria, extensions, status/authority/time/rights и mandatory companions. Root/lead перед назначением раскладывает **каждый** source clause в существующий coverage journal; этот пример не заменяет такую раскладку. Один actual semantic owner и write lease сохраняются. Независимая подготовка возможна по согласованному интерфейсу; composition и protected use требуют настоящих supplier outputs.

План не даёт права выбирать только удобные profiles или считать unknown applicability исключением. Условный join включается по фактическому контракту/claim. Родитель требует полный selected denominator и fresh end-to-end witness; локальные PASS не суммируются в closure.

## UI02-PUB — Issue and project one exact public record

**Свойство.** A permitted data-only record or typed refusal is issued from one exact CAS view; authority stays typed-empty unless the separate signer predicate is met, and every selected audience reads the same edition with its own permission.

**Граница и owner.** public_export → exact ArtifactRef/CAS view → issue/verify → PUBLIC/REVIEWER/EXPERT/MACHINE projections across actual browser/API/CLI readers → authorized-read audit. Do not rebuild the closed verifier or create another store.

**Входы.** GY-PR1a evidence for data-only issue; GY-PR1b and actual signer/holder policy only for a signature claim.; Exact CAS ArtifactRef/profile, owner-issued public-use/tenant/audience decisions, INT-K02/K06/K08 semantics.

**Результат.** Typed refusal/chronology or persisted exact-view record with source, authority, audit, rule/schema, audience, issue/status times and limits.

**Подготовка.** Bind the actual readers and exact owner response; prepare refusal/projection and source-bound route checks independently of a signer appointment.

**Composition join.** Issue real data-only content only after the applicable PR1a predicate and exact rights/view gates; a protected signature remains separate. Jointly accept against UI-01 consumers and UI-03 index references where selected.

**Полезный positive.** Before PR1a, only refusal chronology; after it, all selected audience readers consume the exact admitted edition and preserve their permission and limits.

**Различающий negative.** Candidate, wrong view/tenant, pending intent, revoked rights, bad signature, hidden private sibling, or reviewer/expert/MACHINE permission inferred from PUBLIC fails closed.

**Matched removal.** Remove exact-view or audience gate while retaining labels; a fresh selected reader must expose the wrong/unauthorized edition and fail.

## UI02-EGRESS — Gate private data before persistence and egress

**Свойство.** No private field escapes through public request, telemetry, beacon, fetch-failure, or error paths; classification/redaction occurs before signed persistence.

**Граница и owner.** Actual public route and every selected egress builder, including route/query/tags/breadcrumbs/extras/beacon/fetch/error paths; reuse the approved telemetry policy and existing audit boundary.

**Входы.** Selected self-hosted/scrubbed/absent telemetry policy.; Actual nested request/proof/identifier fields and complete selected egress-builder denominator.

**Результат.** Allowed scrubbed/absent telemetry behavior and a source-bound egress decision before persistence.

**Подготовка.** Enumerate actual builders and bind the selected telemetry policy; develop the canary witness against the exact route.

**Composition join.** Exercise the built route and real egress after publication wiring; a destination allowlist alone does not qualify content. Parent jointly accepts the emitted result with UI02-PUB.

**Полезный positive.** No protected canary reaches any selected destination or persistence path; permitted telemetry still follows the selected policy.

**Различающий negative.** Nested or encoded private values never become public bytes or telemetry, even when the destination is allowed.

**Matched removal.** Remove classification/projection while leaving signature and status markers; the actual egress canary must reach a forbidden path and make the semantic check fail.

## UI02-RIGHTS — Apply owner retention and disclosure decisions to selected readers

**Свойство.** Selected publication/history readers apply LIF-05 placement-specific display, retention, withdrawal, erasure, cache, and restore decisions without implying recall of unknown downloaded copies.

**Граница и owner.** UI-02 selected history/index/feed/API/browser/MACHINE/audit/export/cache/restore readers; compose with UI-04 history and LIF-05 owner outputs. No second retention engine.

**Входы.** Resolved LIF-05 per-placement disposition and current generation.; Exact record, identifiers, lineage and permitted existence-disclosure limits.

**Результат.** Reader-specific permitted history, corrected status/link, withheld projection, or erased managed payload/identifiers; explicit residual limits.

**Подготовка.** Bind actual reader placements and join points; retain prior edition semantics and define canary checks without claiming an unissued LIF-05 result.

**Composition join.** Require the actual owner result before protected serving/deletion/restore; parent jointly accepts selected history with UI-04 and currentness with LIF-02.

**Полезный positive.** Authorized reader sees an allowed exact historical edition; an owner-qualified erasure makes selected managed readers unable to recover prohibited canaries.

**Различающий negative.** Hidden parent, count, link, cache, export, or restore must not disclose denied existence/content; caller request alone cannot erase.

**Matched removal.** Remove the LIF-05 join from one selected history/export/restore reader while preserving labels; a fresh canary reader must recover prohibited content and fail.
