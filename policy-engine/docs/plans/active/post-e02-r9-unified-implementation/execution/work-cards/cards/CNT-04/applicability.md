# CNT-04.applicability — Квалифицировать applicability и provision→lever

Группа: [G02 — Смысл оснований: право, метрики, ценности и участники](../../groups/G02.md). Родитель: [CNT-04](../../parents/CNT-04.md). Роль: `verification`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** Reviewer-attributed result binds exact provision/version, jurisdiction/time/notice/transition/exception and intervention lever; legal interpretation and uncertainty are carried with the result.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Reuse canonical applicability route and competent reviewer. Consume law-version; keep legal effect, PolicyOS knowledge/publication time, actual act and model intervention separate. Disputed interpretation remains contested/blocked.

**Граница владельца.** Existing legal-applicability/claim-lifecycle owner persists relation under canonical legal rule/claim lease; qualified reviewer supplies judgment. No parser/model becomes legal authority. Resolve exact writer/reader paths and overlapping leases after E02.

**Входы.** Law-version output; actual selected legal reviewer; qualified issuer-act output only when act/recipient/competence affects selected conclusion.

**Выход и consumer.** Versioned reviewer-qualified applicability/provision→lever result with rule/source version, provenance, time roles and affected claims; reader is current legal applicability/status route.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** Reviewer protocol can be planned independently; protected interpretation waits for accepted E02 source, qualified source/ref, exact leases and parent permit.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Join exact law-version and applicability result; issuer-act is consumed only for the authority claim that requires it. Bind/deliver the existing gated public applicability projection under its native surface owner, including same-generation version, contested/effective limitation, audience and rights. CNT-09 tests that exact selected reader; absent bridge is surface_missing assigned here, not a new legal authority.

**Positive.** Applicable, partially effective, exception/transition or disputed case produces exact scoped result/status.

**Distinguishing negative.** Wrong jurisdiction/issuer, absent notice, expired delegation or latest-arrival rule establishes law/effect.

**Matched removal.** Remove applicability/event/competence relation with labels intact: fresh legal consumer stays unknown/limited/blocked.

**Граница контекста.** Accountable joined legal interpretation; conditional DES-05 and succession consumers consume typed outputs, not a new legal subsystem.
