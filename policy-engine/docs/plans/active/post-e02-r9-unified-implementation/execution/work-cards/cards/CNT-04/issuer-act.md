# CNT-04.issuer-act — Связать competence/delegation с внешним актом

Группа: [G06 — Допустимость, полномочия и квалифицированные решения](../../groups/G06.md). Родитель: [CNT-04](../../parents/CNT-04.md). Роль: `input`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** Evidence binds competent issuer/delegation to authentic act, recipient, exact scope, conditions, effectivity and revocation; signature alone is not authority.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Reuse existing issuer/authority evidence owner and verifier. Capture act/delegation separately from law text and PolicyOS intervention, then provide qualified refs to applicability and selected commission consumer.

**Граница владельца.** Institution supplies the external act; existing authority-evidence owner verifies/binds it under its canonical issuer/evidence lease. CNT-04 does not issue acts. Exact paths unresolved after E02; do not presume independent writers.

**Входы.** Competent institution/issuer supplies original act/delegation, recipient, conditions, scope, time, correction/revocation. Kind=input describes evidence flow and does not appoint supplier.

**Выход и consumer.** Qualified issuer/delegation/actual-act evidence ref consumed by selected applicability or successor-scope reader.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** Read-only route census first; evidence admission waits for actual issuer evidence, accepted E02 rebind, verifier, exact lease and permit.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Fresh applicability/commission reader uses act only for authenticated recipient/scope/time; absence/dispute limits only affected scope.

**Positive.** Verified act changes only its authenticated target/time.

**Distinguishing negative.** Draft/signature, wrong recipient, expired delegation or conflicting act by newest-arrival does not establish competence/effect.

**Matched removal.** Remove recipient/competence/act binding but retain bytes: fresh protected reader cannot assert authority.

**Граница контекста.** Institutional evidence is not generated by this system. Separately qualify issuer, governing law, applicability and act.
