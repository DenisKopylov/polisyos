# CNT-07.mandate-evidence — Проверить remit, selection и evidence for mandate

Группа: [G02 — Смысл оснований: право, метрики, ценности и участники](../../groups/G02.md). Родитель: [CNT-07](../../parents/CNT-07.md). Роль: `input`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** Process evidence binds commissioning body/remit, participant selection/representation, supplied information, missing groups, promised influence/response and authorized principal; it does not choose CNT-05's objective/metric.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Reuse current participation/claim authority owner and named community protocol. Distinguish response/preference from mandate; preserve competing principals without averaging.

**Граница владельца.** Commissioning/collective authority supplies remit, selection method and response obligation; actual principal supplies mandate. Existing participation/evidence owner records it under its native canonical participation/evidence lease family. CNT-05 remains normative selector; exact paths/shared leases rebound after E02, with possible overlap retained until resolved.

**Входы.** Real authorized body/principal and process record; testimony input is contextual only. Signature, count, resolution vote or model consensus does not appoint an authority.

**Выход и consumer.** Versioned scope/remit/representation/authority evidence with verifier provenance and affected choices; consumed by value/admissibility reader and CNT-05 when relevant.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** Map process read-only; operative authority waits for the named body/principal and E02 native verifier/lease. No authority by a sample count.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Fresh value/admissibility reader and conditional CNT-05 selector check remit/authority; absent authority limits operative choice.

**Positive.** Real mandate within remit is distinguished from participation level and preference; missing groups/responses remain visible.

**Distinguishing negative.** Consultation count or self-appointed speaker becomes mandate/custom/binding law, or disclosure proves authority.

**Matched removal.** Remove remit/selection/principal verification but keep signers/labels: protected choice/gate cannot rely on mandate; testimony remains separately available.

**Граница контекста.** Supplies mandate evidence only; does not make legal determination (CNT-04) or select metric tradeoffs (CNT-05).
