# DES-02.hypothesis-set — Сформировать и сохранить разные гипотезы

Группа: [G04 — Содержательный поиск политик и перенос опыта](../../groups/G04.md). Родитель: [DES-02](../../parents/DES-02.md). Роль: `mechanism`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** The actual N4 route persists semantically distinct, decision-relevant intervention candidates with truthful channel/ancestry and routes only grounded, source-bound candidates to N5; other ideas remain research/refinement inputs.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Wire the existing N4 drafter/formalizer/critic and grammar fallback into the persisted alternative set. Treat LLM output as candidate; compare structure and decision meaning rather than paraphrase/count markers. Preserve independent deterministic/grammar availability and the existing candidate firewall.

**Граница владельца.** DES-02 owns the N4 output and hypothesis-ledger delta under write:n4-design-generation and write:hypothesis-ledger. DES-03 owns numerical trials, DES-04 owns N6 feedback/acquisition bridging, and DES-09 owns the shared invocation contract. The shared formulator lease with DES-01/DES-08 is single-writer; exact paths/overlap are resolved only at E02 rebind.

**Входы.** DES-01 original request and selected frame refs; DES-09 actual traced-call record and allowed context when a live LLM arm is selected.; Existing grammar/resource registry and the bounded known-groundable control; model absence leaves truthful non-LLM channels available.

**Выход и consumer.** A persisted candidate set with channel, input, ancestry, rejection/incompleteness, material semantic distinction and source refs; fresh N6 reads it, and N5 receives only executable source-bound candidates.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** After E02 acceptance and scoped source rebind, bind actual N4 channels and the ledger sink. Grammar and deterministic preparation can proceed without model credentials; live-call outcomes stay UNRUN until an allowed real invocation is observed.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Join actual N4 outputs to the fresh N6 reader and existing N5/Research routes. Channel attribution comes from observed invocation, not configured client presence or a marker.

**Positive.** On a predeclared known-groundable case, the real producer yields the required alternatives including an independently checked material mechanism distinction; a new registered conforming mechanism appears without a central template edit.

**Distinguishing negative.** Paraphrases do not inflate diversity, causally distinct proposals do not collapse, unknown facts cannot become executable effects, and a missing provider never becomes a fabricated LLM result.

**Matched removal.** Replace substantive alternatives with paraphrases while retaining count/diversity markers, or remove source binding/firewall; the semantic discriminator and protected downstream consumer refuse.

**Граница контекста.** One persisted N4 candidate-set property includes generation, semantic comparison and routing; no separate writer is appointed for drafter, critic, grammar, or ledger. R14 decision relevance is part of the real positive control, not a structural-only pass.
