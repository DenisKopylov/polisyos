# DES-05.legal-branches — Составить проверяемые ветви правового толкования

Группа: [G02 — Смысл оснований: право, метрики, ценности и участники](../../groups/G02.md). Родитель: [DES-05](../../parents/DES-05.md). Роль: `mechanism`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** Selected multi-provision legal readings preserve source/counterevidence, exception/transition, version and distinct legal-valid, observed/verified, and analysis times; current-law admissibility stays distinct from a not-enacted amendment candidate.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Extend existing Lex/NormPack and the selected Scientist consumer. Reuse CAP-05 relation/applicability proof, provision extraction and existing verification; represent competing readings and unknowns as candidate branches. Do not create another legal store or universal precedence table.

**Граница владельца.** DES-05 owns generic native Lex interpretation composition and its commissioned design adapter under write:lex-interpretation-composition, write:legal-consistency-if-changed and write:legal-design-adapter. CAP-05 alone owns source/relation proof; CNT-04 supplies actual case corpus/competence inputs; CNT-08 compares bases; CNT-09 composes selected real cases. DES-06 consumes branch artifacts and does not reinterpret law. Shared paths remain one writer after exact E02 rebind.

**Входы.** Actual CAP-05 content-bound relation/applicability evidence and its native verifier result; citation presence or agreed interfaces are insufficient.; CNT-04 selected case corpus and competence/review evidence; official-source interpretation only for claims that need it.; Legal-valid time plus received/verified time and analysis-as-of; missing authority permits declared hypothetical analysis, not enacted-law claims.

**Выход и consumer.** Versioned interpretation branches with premises, support/contrary spans, language/source version, unresolved links, temporal roles, derivation provenance and affected design refs; fresh selected Scientist/CNT-09 readers distinguish current-law and proposed-law branches.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** After E02 source rebind, census actual Lex/NormPack/conflict/map owners. Review profiles may resolve computable facts independently; actual current-law permission waits for CAP-05 relation evidence and competent authority where applicable.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Route each materially supported reading to existing candidate generation/legal checks; join actual source/translation/review changes through native LIF invalidation and fresh design/document readers. No branch itself grants authority.

**Positive.** A selected exception/transition case changes the design or limits it across legal dates/readings; a lawful amendment candidate remains available while present-tense authority claim is refused.

**Distinguishing negative.** Retrieved prose, fake citation, omitted exception, conflicting translation, invalid date, wrong jurisdiction or asserted precedence cannot establish current permission; genuine disagreement is not collapsed to a single winner.

**Matched removal.** Keep citation/status counts but remove source/exception binding, branch-to-design consumer or change subscription; the affected consumer changes/refuses and the stale-projection probe fails.

**Граница контекста.** Retain one legal interpretation/design branch property; source proof, case facts, base comparison and document projection remain named native suppliers/consumers. The committed source owner and exact lease are rebound after E02.
