# DES-10.learning-transfer — Измерить перенос опыта в следующий поиск

Группа: [G04 — Содержательный поиск политик и перенос опыта](../../groups/G04.md). Родитель: [DES-10](../../parents/DES-10.md). Роль: `verification`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** Eligible evaluated experience changes a later search choice usefully, or a justified no-transfer is preserved, without converting memory into causal/effect evidence.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Use actual DES-08/DES-04 retrieval/influence outputs and GYD-25 retrieval evidence. Compare matched memory-on/off cases with source-disjoint/chronological controls; keep successes, failures and opportunities, rights/currentness, contamination, false transfer and cost visible.

**Граница владельца.** DES-10 owns the comparison recipe only. DES-08 owns memory retrieval/persistence; DES-04 owns feedback; GYD-25 owns retrieval adequacy; LIF-07 owns influence lineage. No causal/effect update, authority promotion, or retrieval-service rebuild.

**Входы.** Actual evaluated lesson/run, rights and influence lineage; matched memory-on/off later cases and selected/rejected retrieval refs.; GYD-25 retrieval output as a distinct adequacy endpoint; it is not a transfer-utility proxy.

**Выход и consumer.** Scoped transfer/false-transfer result with actual later choice, eligible/ineligible lesson, cost and fresh retrieval refs; non-transfer and false exclusion remain visible.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** Freeze applicability matches, negative-transfer cases, source/cohort-disjointness and exposure plan before the single final wave.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Consume actual persisted lesson and subsequent candidate outputs in FINAL-REPLAY under the actual FINAL-REVIEW immutable reviewed identity; keep GYD-25 and LIF-07 owner results separately identifiable; this composition does not block independent preparation or owner readiness.

**Positive.** A compatible evaluated lesson changes a later candidate/search choice, or a mismatch prevents transfer for a reasoned scope; a useful opportunity survives failure-heavy memory.

**Distinguishing negative.** Failure-only recall, stale/wrong-scope lesson, descendants, same-cohort dependence, restricted rights or held-out leakage appears as independent support or useful transfer.

**Matched removal.** Disable retrieval/persistence while retaining lesson refs or labels; measured actual influence disappears in the matched later task.

**Граница контекста.** R25 campaign 2 measures actual later-task influence, not retrieval quality, causal learning or evidence independence. Preserve a distinct output even when no transfer is justified.
