**Distinguishing negative.** Paraphrase inflation, semantic irrelevance, post-hoc arm/control, omitted positive control, hidden failed branch or self-scored novelty cannot count as useful design.

**Matched removal.** Remove actual producer invocation or independent semantic/relevance labels while retaining IDs/counts; the productivity claim fails.
