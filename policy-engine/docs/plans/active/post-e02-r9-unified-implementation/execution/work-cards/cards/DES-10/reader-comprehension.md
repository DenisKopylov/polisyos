# DES-10.reader-comprehension — Проверить понимание выбранной аудиторией

Группа: [G10 — Независимые проверки и качество свидетельств](../../groups/G10.md). Родитель: [DES-10](../../parents/DES-10.md). Роль: `verification`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** The actual selected target readers identify status, authority, basis, limits, unresolved choice and next action in the exact selected edition/support; missing representative evidence remains not_established.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Prepare a separate audience-qualified reader task/oracle on exact rendition bytes and fresh current projection. Measure reader behavior, not staff competence/training, fluency, readability score or engineering fidelity. Preserve any selected absent/denied consumer probe as a separately reported endpoint, never a comprehension proxy.

**Граница владельца.** DES-10 owns comparison protocol/result only. Actual reader recruitment, approved audience and evidence are legitimate external inputs; DES-06/07/CNT-09 own document/fresh projection. This card grants no reader certification, training authority, universal audience generalization or publication.

**Входы.** Approved target-reader recruitment, exact selected edition/support, blind task/oracle and item-level answers/time/errors.; Fresh current projection and selected consumer closure for any paired absent/denied probe; no direct-event-log-only substitute.

**Выход и consumer.** Audience- and edition-scoped comprehension result with outcome/oracle, item errors and limits. Any absent/denied consumer-closure probe is recorded as a distinct access/projection endpoint, not folded into comprehension.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** Prepare the reader protocol against an agreed interface; internal experts/readability scores cannot stand in. If target readers are unavailable, preserve that specific result as not_established without blocking engineering/fidelity proof.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Execute and compose the selected reader protocol only in FINAL-REPLAY under FINAL-REVIEW’s actual immutable reviewed identity; actual target-reader outcomes flow to the selected audience claim in FINAL-USE. Selected absent/denied probe results remain separately labeled, and their absence does not block DES-06/07/CNT-09 engineering evidence; independent protocol preparation and owner readiness are not blocked.

**Positive.** Selected readers correctly identify status, basis, authority, limits, dissent and next step in the exact edition; a fresh projection supports interpretation of correction/currentness where selected.

**Distinguishing negative.** Fluency, expert-only review, reader findings on another edition, WCAG/translation presence or access-probe PASS cannot establish comprehension; a denial/absence probe is not a reader outcome.

**Matched removal.** Remove real target-reader outcomes, oracle or exact-edition/support binding; comprehension becomes not_established. Removing a selected-consumer probe from its complete denominator separately invalidates that probe.

**Граница контекста.** R25 campaign 5 studies exact-audience comprehension. The bundled selected consumer closure probe is kept as its own reported sub-result; it never substitutes for cognition, and absent audiences do not block internal engineering.
