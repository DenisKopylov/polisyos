# DES-12.study-return — Связать фактический результат с выводом

Группа: [G03 — Каузальное обоснование и обучаемая модель мира](../../groups/G03.md). Родитель: [DES-12](../../parents/DES-12.md). Роль: `mechanism`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** Actual owner-produced returned evidence is bound to exact target, exposure/receipt, protocol/looks, outcome, cohort ancestry and per-outcome denominator; fresh claim consumer changes only justified scope and does not activate an effect posterior by default.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Reuse native acquisition/causal workflow and existing CNT-06 claim consumer. Record actual returned/absent/null/deviated outcome and cohort lineage; analyze by the selected method/estimand and observation regime. Keep protocol-as-planned, actual conduct and as-known result distinct.

**Граница владельца.** DES-12 owns study design/return bridge within write:native-causal-study-design; the external institution/source owner conducts the study and supplies actual results, while CNT-06 owns claim binding and GYD-12 alone owns any qualified structural/effect update. No autonomous executor, posterior writer or new authority.

**Входы.** study-design protocol and study-authorization exact operation decision where conduct is selected.; Actual CAP-01/native acquisition or external study result with source/use rights, cohort, protocol/looks/deviations, outcome presence/return and assignment/receipt/exposure.; CNT-06 actual claim/target and current-use evidence rights; no result or permission marker substitutes for source-bound evidence.

**Выход и consumer.** Persisted actual study-return record with per-outcome zero/absent/unreturned distinctions, denominator, protocol deviation/looks, cohort ancestry and method-qualified result; fresh CNT-06 consumer reports supported scope/limitation only.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** Return adapter, source/result contracts and denominator cases may be prepared against agreed interfaces without external study execution or permission. Actual conduct is dispatched only after the native owner admits the exact operation; an actual return/use claim requires an owner-produced result and applicable current-use rights. Late/absent/null remains visible.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Only when the study is selected and actually conducted, fresh CNT-06 consumes the owner-produced return. The exact authorization result joins only for protected conduct; a qualified structural result reaches the sole GYD-12 writer/next-cycle reader only under an actual applicable structural-write rule. O1 effect-posterior activation remains separate and is not implied. Candidate design can stand without authorization or a return.

**Positive.** A controlled owner-produced informative return changes only supported scope; duplicated observationally equivalent cohort information does not create a second independent claim.

**Distinguishing negative.** Off-target receipt, unlogged look, rewritten preregistration, published date as receipt, same cohort counted multiple times, no-return coerced to zero, or fabricated permission/result cannot resolve the rival.

**Matched removal.** Keep protocol/completed/status fields but remove actual returned-evidence, exposure, cohort or claim bridge; independent effect/claim oracle detects misattribution or limits the result.

**Граница контекста.** R25 slice 3 binds actual return/denominator/inference separately from design and permission. Preparation may proceed against agreed interfaces; actual conduct remains owner-authorized and conditional. The bundled source clause joins native authorization, CAP/source and CNT-06 outputs only when the affected operation/result is selected. No new posterior or world writer is granted.
