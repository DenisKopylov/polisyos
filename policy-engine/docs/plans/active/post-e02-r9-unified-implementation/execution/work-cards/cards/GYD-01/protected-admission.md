# GYD-01.protected-admission — Защищённый допуск по S6/S8/safety/S7

Группа: [G06 — Допустимость, полномочия и квалифицированные решения](../../groups/G06.md). Родитель: [GYD-01](../../parents/GYD-01.md). Роль: `consumer`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** Protected eligibility is admitted only when the same candidate has recomputed, source-bound S6 evidence for all five axes, S8 normative admission, promotion safety, and a competent S7 HumanDecision bound to the full scope.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Extend the existing CanonicalN9PromotionPort and consume existing S6, S8, safety and S7 owners. Preserve the original ADR-0178/0179 criteria; S6 is necessary, not sufficient; LLM outputs remain candidates.

**Граница владельца.** The existing N9 port is the sole promotion writer. Axis, frontier, safety and mandate producers retain their native owners; the S7 decision is an external institutional act. Do not create an S6 authority/admin owner, synthesize a pilot event, or authorize/execute the pilot.

**Входы.** All five original S6 axes with verifier-reachable, refutation-capable evidence, including actual capacity feasibility and strategic response/take-up inputs from their existing producers.; Same-candidate S8 frontier/generation and promotion-safety results.; Competent S7 decision with candidate/mode digests, mandate record/class/role, stop/rollback, validity and reviewer separation.; Separate authentic O0 attempted-field-pilot near-miss or explicit unavailable/owner-decision state.

**Выход и consumer.** Persisted protected-mode result bound to the full S6/S8/safety/S7 conjunction; keep the O0 near-miss as a separate audit event or explicit unavailable state.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** Resolve S6 capacity/response suppliers to actual native producer paths, outputs and canonical leases after E02; G02/G03 are navigation groups, not owners. An absent internal producer becomes assigned native extension work or explicit producer_missing, not a request for external authority. S7 institutional authority remains independently supplied where the protected action requires it.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** The protected result joins n4-data-only only for full GY-PR1 parent reconciliation; PR1a never satisfies this gate. S7 remains institutional evidence. O0 near-miss is an independent original criterion, not a fixture or unsafe production trial.

**Positive.** At original scope, all five S6 axes plus same-candidate S8, safety and S7 digests resolve at the fresh N9 consumer; authentic O0 evidence is recorded separately.

**Distinguishing negative.** Missing batch, caller-declared axis, selection-driven registration, wrong scope/epoch, absent S7, or unbound digest cannot promote.

**Matched removal.** Change the actual S6 capacity/response basis while leaving declarations intact, or remove a required binding; the protected consumer loses support.

**Граница контекста.** This is the G06 admissibility join. Capacity and strategic-response evidence remain with existing G02/G03 producers, not this consumer. P21/P24 and P37/P38 apply; unresolved O0 criterion stays open without simulation or waiver.
