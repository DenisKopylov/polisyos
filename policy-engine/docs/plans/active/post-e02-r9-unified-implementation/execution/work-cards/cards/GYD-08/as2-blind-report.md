# GYD-08.as2-blind-report — AS2 sealed holdout и отчёт risk-coverage

Группа: [G10 — Независимые проверки и качество свидетельств](../../groups/G10.md). Родитель: [GYD-08](../../parents/GYD-08.md). Роль: `verification`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** A single sealed AS2 corpus/oracle evaluates a predeclared diagnosis rule and risk-coverage operating point against the all-unresolved baseline without leaking holdout outcomes into selection.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Reuse the existing Runtime/quality diagnosis evidence owner after exact module appointment; persist a content-addressed corpus, custody/access history, source-qualified labels, rule and scoped audit receipt.

**Граница владельца.** The assigned GYD-08 task lead owns engineering discovery/rebind of the existing diagnosis-evidence owner, oracle-custody path, blind boundary and exact reader before dispatch; freeze the native path/lease at accepted-source rebind. This is internal wiring work, not an external authority hold or permission to create an adjacent corpus owner. Actual E02 acceptance remains the pre-implementation gate. The formal phase ruling and competent criterion-owner clarification gate comparator interpretation and any quantitative conclusion, not the permitted sealed-corpus, access-history or refusal engineering after normal entry conditions. Oracle labels stay sealed and source-qualified. This scoped instrument report is not a product threshold, universal guarantee or authority to mutate WMR.

**Входы.** Accepted source and formal phase ruling.; Task-lead engineering rebind identifying the existing diagnosis producer, oracle custodian, blind path, exact reader and lease; this does not supply the external evidence.; Competent criterion-owner clarification of the original comparator interpretation; remain open until received.; Source-qualified cause adjudications including disagreement/competing completions and provenance.; Exact intervention/deployment version, observation regime, interference/context and actuals.; Predeclared estimator, risk-coverage measure and operating point; access/exposure history.

**Выход и consumer.** Immutable sealed AS2 version and scoped audit report, plus a concrete comparator clarification for the competent criterion owner; O1 receives only the resolvable report/provenance, not protected oracle labels.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** The assigned GYD-08 lead traces/rebinds the existing diagnosis-evidence owner, oracle custodian, blind path, O1 reader and exact lease. If no native path is found, report that state rather than creating a parallel owner.; Keep the source-owner clarification of comparator meaning and formal phase ruling separate from engineering discovery. Actual E02 acceptance and parent dispatch conditions remain required; after normal entry, permitted sealed-corpus/access-history/refusal preparation may proceed without a resolved comparator.; Pre-register threshold/estimand from training/calibration inputs; include unresolved baseline and original OPS-R7 cases only as allowed by the clarified criterion.; Run the sealed comparison and emit a quantitative audit receipt only if the formal ruling and competent criterion-owner clarification permit that exact comparator; otherwise emit only a scoped limitation/refusal and keep the comparator open.; Prepare exact comparator clarification while preserving the original criterion as open.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Join the sealed report at O1 intake, which must resolve corpus membership, custody, label provenance and run refs before considering an update. The original holdout-tuned comparator stays open pending source-owner clarification; WP-09/WP-10 numbers remain withheld.

**Positive.** Under the approved scoped measure, the predeclared design beats the all-unresolved baseline; the fresh O1 intake resolves the exact sealed receipt without exposing oracle contents.

**Distinguishing negative.** Leakage, post-hoc threshold, repeated look, forced coverage, wrong version, missing oracle or unresolved source label refuses O1 admission; removal of the sealed oracle makes that same reader refuse.

**Matched removal.** Remove exposure separation while retaining sealed IDs or reseal after changing a threshold; the blindness consumer still detects the changed selection/access history.

**Граница контекста.** One blindness property includes corpus, oracle, baseline, exposure and measure. A local seal proves internal consistency, not blindness (P37/P38); keep source-owner ambiguity and uncertainty explicit.
