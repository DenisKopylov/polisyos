# GYD-09.o1-confirmatory-update — O1 confirmatory update с activation gate

Группа: [G03 — Каузальное обоснование и обучаемая модель мира](../../groups/G03.md). Родитель: [GYD-09](../../parents/GYD-09.md). Роль: `mechanism`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** The live comparison and typed refusal keep the operative posterior unchanged until a competent, scope-specific WP-01 activation is admitted; only then may the selected qualified Bayesian profile append an immutable posterior read by O3.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Extend the existing Scientist DecisionFeedbackService, selected Foundry Bayesian estimator and Runtime WMR reader after canonical-owner review; no separate updater unless that same owner approves it.

**Граница владельца.** Scientist owns comparison/diagnosis/provenance; the existing WMR owner is the only world writer. WP-01 activation is an external competent decision, and optional WP-02/R4-Q01 is a distinct routine-schedule ruling. AS2, passing tests, or vocabulary alone never activates mutation.

**Входы.** GYD-08 sealed AS2 report and source-qualified diagnosis.; Real post-deployment actuals and named measurement process.; W4-K02 provenance; intervention/version ancestry; W5-K03 identification, context, interference, observation validity and mechanism.; Owner-approved Bayesian profile/uncertainty mapping.; Actual scoped WP-01 activation record; WP-02/R4-Q01 only if routine assimilation is proposed.

**Выход и consumer.** Before activation: persisted typed refusal/no-mutation result and concrete activation packet with live witnesses/failures. After actual activation only: immutable profile-qualified posterior with prior/model/estimator/uncertainty refs, audit record and exact O3 readback.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** Build the live comparison, diagnosis and provenance path plus a fresh refusal consumer; no posterior mutation is the current positive.; Present the concrete activation packet to the competent principal.; Only after actual scoped WP-01 activation, add the selected estimator and O3 reader. Keep WP-02 separate.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Consume the sealed AS2 output from GYD-08. The interim refusal is independently demonstrable; the operative posterior positive remains conditional on actual WP-01 activation. Routine WP-02 assimilation is not inferred.

**Positive.** Current: real comparison/diagnosis traverses the producer and fresh reader and produces no posterior mutation. Conditional future: after admitted WP-01 activation, exact eligible evidence yields the selected immutable update read by O3.

**Distinguishing negative.** Unresolved cause, implementation/measurement/behavior change, insufficient support, W5-K03 failure, wrong-version or absent/forged activation never defaults to prediction_error and never mutates the posterior.

**Matched removal.** Remove the AS2/causal binding or activation consumer while retaining refs/flags; the real writer refuses. Until activation, positive operative mutation remains unavailable by policy.

**Граница контекста.** One confirmatory lifecycle state machine, not a forced positive. Keep typed uncertainty and competing completions, profile plurality, activation scope and optional routine schedule distinct (P04/P05/P07/P08/P14).
