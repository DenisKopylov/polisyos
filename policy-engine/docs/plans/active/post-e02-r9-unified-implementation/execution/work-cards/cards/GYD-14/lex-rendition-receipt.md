# GYD-14.lex-rendition-receipt — Полная квитанция реальных Lex renditions

Группа: [G08 — Документы, интерфейсы и понятные человеку результаты](../../groups/G08.md). Родитель: [GYD-14](../../parents/GYD-14.md). Роль: `consumer`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** Every source-inventoried required rendition/context member is compared from real CAS-held bytes and bound to one complete receipt; candidate comparison alone never establishes legal authority or human comprehension.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Extend Lex knowledge/multilingual_assurance.py through one appointed existing production rendition consumer; version changes to its synthetic-candidate input contract.

**Граница владельца.** Lex owns comparison; CAS owns byte custody. The assigned GYD-14 task lead traces/rebinds the existing production consumer and source-inventory reader, resolving exact paths and native leases before dispatch and freezing them at accepted-source rebind; actual E02 acceptance remains the implementation gate. Finding an inventory owner does not supply or certify the independent denominator. Missing trust roots, institutional signer/holder/legal evidence or accessibility authority stay typed-empty. Do not add an assurance engine or local signer.

**Входы.** Real source and target rendition bytes.; Independently reconciled inventory of required locales, contexts and versions from the existing accountable source; task-lead discovery does not create or certify this evidence.; Language/jurisdiction, rule, source version and validity time.; Institutional trust-root/signer, holder, glossary/legal and accessibility evidence only when supplied by the competent source and independently verified.

**Выход и consumer.** Persisted complete content-bound candidate assurance receipt with exact denominator and per-member digests; fresh production consumer/audit readback.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** The assigned GYD-14 lead traces/rebinds the existing Lex production consumer and source-inventory reader/owner; record their native lease overlap. This engineering task does not appoint an evidentiary source.; Retain actual E02 acceptance and parent dispatch conditions before implementation. Feed real bytes through the canonical comparator/CAS and persist each member, context and time binding.; Independently reconcile the receipt denominator against the accountable source inventory and replay it through the actual consumer.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Join the actual source inventory to the Lex receipt and fresh consumer. Keep finite candidate semantics and may_not_use_for; legal equivalence, signer, holder, WP-11/WP-12 and RTL accessibility claims remain blocked without their own evidence.

**Positive.** The independently inventoried real rendition set resolves to one complete, content-bound receipt at fresh readback.

**Distinguishing negative.** Partial/self-declared denominator, wrong bytes, stale rule, unresolved CAS item or absent required trust root refuses the relevant admission.

**Matched removal.** Remove a source/content binding while keeping the packet’s contexts/denominator markers; complete-set reconciliation fails.

**Граница контекста.** One real-rendition assurance capability mapped to G08 because its output serves human/machine surfaces. P38 divergent case is packet.contexts omitting a real source context; reconcile to independent inventory.
