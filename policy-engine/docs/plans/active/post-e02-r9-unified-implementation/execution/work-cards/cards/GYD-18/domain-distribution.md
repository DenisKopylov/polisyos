# GYD-18.domain-distribution — Пересчитать V3 per-domain распределения

Группа: [G10 — Независимые проверки и качество свидетельств](../../groups/G10.md). Родитель: [GYD-18](../../parents/GYD-18.md). Роль: `consumer`.

Плановая рабочая карточка. Реализация только после E02 acceptance и exact source/lease rebind; эта запись не является execution grant или closure. [Общие правила](../../README.md).

**Проверяемый результат.** Complete frozen N10 corpus and required acquisition/domain/depth strata produce terminal/evidence/decision-grade/rung distributions, including non-panel, unseen-third and exact post-N13b typed-deeper-terminal case.

**Предпочтительное решение.** Reuse the frozen N10 corpus and canonical N13b/PDC certificate owners; reuse V2 results only for identical case/input/rule/source identity and execute only missing required cases under permit.

**Граница владельца.** Within GYD-18's native canonical lease family write:exact-path/{canonical_repo_path}. Exact paths and overlap remain unresolved until accepted E02 lease resolution; do not presume disjointness. Owns the denominator-bound V3 distribution/readout only; N10/N13b corpus and PDC certificate remain canonical owners.

**Входы.** Frozen case/source hashes and acquisition labels; Identity-matched V2 outputs; N13b re-entry and exact JOINT witness only when tested

**Выход и consumer.** Versioned per-domain distribution receipt with empty/mixed strata explicit.

**Что можно готовить независимо.** Preparation may proceed before freeze; final execution follows the parent’s canonical stage and conditional acceptance. No new gate is introduced; resolve accepted E02 source and exact lease before writes.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Fresh consumer: V3 distribution reader resolves the full corpus and per-domain outputs; a removed corpus member changes denominator/readout or becomes not_established.

**Positive.** All required strata and named re-entry terminal appear at correct local/recursive depth.

**Distinguishing negative.** Failed arm, unseen third, seed-vs-case inflation, wrong terminal or world-growth epoch cannot be pooled away.

**Matched removal.** Remove one bound corpus member; denominator/readout changes or becomes not_established.

**Граница контекста.** One property over a fixed governed case set; no forced usefulness, invented sample-size threshold or historical overwrite.
