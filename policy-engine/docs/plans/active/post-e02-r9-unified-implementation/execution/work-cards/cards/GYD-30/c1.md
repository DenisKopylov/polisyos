Exact paths and overlap remain unresolved until accepted E02 lease resolution; do not presume disjointness. One reconciled PDC/IR CompositionCertificate owner plus shared GYD-30 compiler/graph/method-chain executor.

**Входы.** ProgramGraph/ExecPlan and owner-derived typed facets; participant outputs/full source-derived method-family inventory; exact coupled oracle; DES-03/GYD-30 native intent/trial/attempt/accepted-result identity; LIF-04 same-intent recovery if selected; selected trajectory/heterogeneous-model outputs, shared resources/exogenous dependence and final actual-materialization residual only where required by the resolved owner contract

**Выход и consumer.** Persisted typed joint result/certificate and full family-to-workflow consumer receipt

**Что можно готовить независимо.** Preparation may proceed before freeze; final execution follows the parent’s canonical stage and conditional acceptance. No new gate is introduced; resolve accepted E02 source and exact lease before writes.

**Где результат соединяется с другими.** Fresh consumer: actual workflow caller’s fresh joint-result consumer. Reuse R16 C1 and preserve B01/C01/C02; C2/C3 outputs join only when the real case selects those profiles. One joint-result writer; no family branches, parallel lineage/scheduler or second stochastic ledger, and no blanket simulator mandate.

**Positive.** Actual multi-participant output matches exact coupled oracle and every source family reaches consumer or exact limitation.

**Distinguishing negative.** Pairwise/marginal substitution, fake registry capability, missing/duplicate active node, raw-order fallback, hidden mutable participant, same ABI with changed law, stale final residual, wrong support/units, unsupported feedback/cycle, or an extra stochastic attempt treated as independent evidence fails; unsupported feedback is refused even when C2 is unselected.

**Matched removal.** Remove non-first consumer, required facet or joint property but keep certificate marker; full coverage/oracle fails.

**Граница контекста.** R25 C1 retained: one joint-result writer and no second stochastic intent/attempt ledger; no family branches, parallel lineage/scheduler or blanket simulator mandate.
