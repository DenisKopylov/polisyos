## Реальный join

Join facet-map's common 26-family grammar/routes/limits with commissioned-case's actual sponsor-selected scope, gap owner/output/consumer and blocker state. Existing acquisition planner/request queue and Expert/Machine audit are the fresh consumers; exact reader must be rebound after E02 or record consumer_missing. Selected successor authority may consume CNT-04-qualified facts conditionally; this is a consumer contract, not a new unconditional task edge.

**Outputs:** [CNT-01.facet-map](../cards/CNT-01/facet-map.md), [CNT-01.commissioned-case](../cards/CNT-01/commissioned-case.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Existing acquisition planner/request-queue selected-case reader and Expert/Machine audit projection, exact consumer/path confirmed on E02 rebind.

**Review размера:** Sequence separates common owner/facet mapping from real sponsor commissioning. The first is not a source acquisition; the second must not infer case/supplier/authority from the first.
