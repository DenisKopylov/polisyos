## Реальный join

Join capture-reuse's durable receipt/artifact through existing CAP-03 acquisition and fresh epoch admission for the exact purpose/audience; connect permitted status to Expert/Machine reader. Query-only raw data stays custodian-side. Revocation/deletion and copy reconciliation remain with LIF-01/LIF-05/security owners.

**Outputs:** [CNT-03.capture-reuse](../cards/CNT-03/capture-reuse.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Existing fresh epoch/use-admission reader on selected acquisition path plus Expert/Machine receipt/status projection.

**Review размера:** Retained because capture receipt and fresh, permission-bounded reuse are one bridge. Actual custodian and grant are external suppliers; acquisition/CAS/rights mechanisms remain native.
