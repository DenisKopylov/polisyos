## Реальный join

Compose only selected outputs: testimony card gives permitted occurrence/context; mandate-evidence gives verified remit/representation/principal; capacity-feasibility consumes GYD-30 owner-qualified state. Fresh readers are existing claim evidence, authorized value/admissibility and G4/G5 feasibility consumers. No PASS-sum creates authority; each output keeps scope/time/rights/provenance.

**Outputs:** [CNT-07.testimony](../cards/CNT-07/testimony.md), [CNT-07.mandate-evidence](../cards/CNT-07/mandate-evidence.md), [CNT-07.capacity-feasibility](../cards/CNT-07/capacity-feasibility.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Existing claim evidence and authorized value/admissibility readers, plus the selected G4/G5 feasibility consumer of GYD-30 joint capacity/feasibility output; bind exact readers after E02. Operators/technical authorities remain the source of operational facts/commitments.

**Review размера:** Split by external suppliers, authorities and properties. Joint capacity/value clause is a bounded feasibility join; testimony and response counts never become mandate or capacity.
