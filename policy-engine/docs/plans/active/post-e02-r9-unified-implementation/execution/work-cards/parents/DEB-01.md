## Реальный join

measurement-disclosure emits Versioned source-qualified measurement receipts, migrated instrument census, disclosed roots/predicates/read errors and unknown/limited outcomes.; runner-residual emits Bounded falsifier receipt and explicit not_established integrity residual.. Root reconciles these exact outputs at the original parent disposition and sends the composed result to Fresh tools/quality/validation/check_debt_ledger.py and check_trust_claim_posture.py consumers over the disclosure receipt and separate runner falsifier.. This join does not create implementation, a second owner, or an extra start gate.

**Outputs:** [DEB-01.measurement-disclosure](../cards/DEB-01/measurement-disclosure.md), [DEB-01.runner-residual](../cards/DEB-01/runner-residual.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Fresh tools/quality/validation/check_debt_ledger.py and check_trust_claim_posture.py consumers over the disclosure receipt and separate runner falsifier.

**Review размера:** Instrument disclosure and transitive-runner integrity are separate trust boundaries.
