## Реальный join

corr-ceiling-allocation emits Source-bound owner/path/meaning/use/producer/consumer disposition for every current destination.; estimand-relations emits Versioned target-bound relation record consumed by selection/strength comparison.; wdi-cost-reconciliation emits DEB-07 source-row cost basis/allocation disposition referencing CAP-03 receipt.; ds15-route-composition emits Real-route bundle receipt plus fresh consumer admission result; fixture-only status remains explicit.; gap-case-union emits Owner-bound union/interface disposition and evidence of actual consuming path.. Root reconciles these exact outputs at the original parent disposition and sends the composed result to Fresh destination/Foundry selection, selected WDI, DS15/UI-17 and INT-R2 case consumers; CAP-03 remains the only cost writer.. This join does not create implementation, a second owner, or an extra start gate.

**Outputs:** [DEB-07.corr-ceiling-allocation](../cards/DEB-07/corr-ceiling-allocation.md), [DEB-07.estimand-relations](../cards/DEB-07/estimand-relations.md), [DEB-07.wdi-cost-reconciliation](../cards/DEB-07/wdi-cost-reconciliation.md), [DEB-07.ds15-route-composition](../cards/DEB-07/ds15-route-composition.md), [DEB-07.gap-case-union](../cards/DEB-07/gap-case-union.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Fresh destination/Foundry selection, selected WDI, DS15/UI-17 and INT-R2 case consumers; CAP-03 remains the only cost writer.

**Review размера:** CORR allocation, estimand relations, CAP-03 cost reconciliation, DS15 route handshake and INT-R2 case union have different owners and predicates.
