## Реальный join

Reconcile carrier bytes/base/rights, semantic delta/dependency route, each actual native-owner recomputation and fresh rendition. Parent join cannot claim a patch was applied from its route marker; no external submission or adoption is implied.

**Outputs:** [DES-07.carrier-identity](../cards/DES-07/carrier-identity.md), [DES-07.semantic-patch-routing](../cards/DES-07/semantic-patch-routing.md), [DES-07.fresh-owner-readback](../cards/DES-07/fresh-owner-readback.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Fresh authorized document reader/CNT-09 selected-case reader, consuming exact carrier identity, routed patches, actual native recomputation and DES-06 rendition.

**Review размера:** Sequence is carrier identity/rights, candidate semantic patch routing, then real owner recompute/fresh readback. The three results are distinct resumable checkpoints; DES-07 does not write native legal/numerical/design results or acquire storage rights.
