## Реальный join

Join the persisted eligible lesson and selected/rejected influence record at a fresh subsequent Scientist search consumer; prove the resulting candidate/search choice and fresh-process retrieval, then use DES-10 matched-task transfer comparison for transfer quality. GYD-25 reads retrieval adequacy only. LIF-07 owns revocation/invalidation; memory does not update O1/O3.

**Outputs:** [DES-08.memory-transfer](../cards/DES-08/memory-transfer.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Fresh subsequent Scientist search consumer, plus DES-10’s matched later-task transfer comparison; GYD-25 remains the retrieval-adequacy reader.

**Review размера:** Retain one later-search memory path, but make its staged proof explicit: evaluated-vs-candidate intake; persisted provenance; rights/currentness/invalidation eligibility; recorded influence on a real later search; fresh-process and memory-on/off transfer readback. DES-10 owns transfer-quality measurement, GYD-25 retrieval adequacy, LIF-07 influence invalidation and DES-03 numerical evaluation.
