## Реальный join

DES-10 retains the frozen case/source denominator, matched arms, equal ceilings/actual cost, holdout exposure, failures/exclusions and judge disagreement/bias; it joins each of the five named campaign outputs to its named existing owner endpoints and FINAL-REVIEW’s actual immutable reviewed identity in one FINAL-REPLAY, then FINAL-USE decides disposition. The FINAL-REVIEW acceptance relation is composition-stage only: independent preparation and owner readiness are not blocked. Report productivity, learning, numerical correctness, document fidelity and reader comprehension separately; do not sum leaf PASS, collapse to one scalar, create five corpora/services, add a global wave, or treat an unavailable audience/live/provider result as authentic PASS.

**Outputs:** [DES-10.productivity](../cards/DES-10/productivity.md), [DES-10.learning-transfer](../cards/DES-10/learning-transfer.md), [DES-10.numerical-methods](../cards/DES-10/numerical-methods.md), [DES-10.document-fidelity](../cards/DES-10/document-fidelity.md), [DES-10.reader-comprehension](../cards/DES-10/reader-comprehension.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Single FINAL-REPLAY identity consuming all five campaign reports under FINAL-REVIEW’s actual immutable reviewed identity, then FINAL-USE disposition; actual selected GYD-17/18/20/22/25/26 and CNT-09 endpoint outputs and any live/reader result that the selected claim requires.

**Review размера:** Split into exactly the five R25 campaigns, retaining one shared frozen denominator/equal-ceiling/cost/failure/exposure/disagreement protocol and a single FINAL-REPLAY/FINAL-USE composition. Productive hypotheses/search, learning transfer, numerical methods/studies, document fidelity and target-reader comprehension remain distinct; no aggregate score.
