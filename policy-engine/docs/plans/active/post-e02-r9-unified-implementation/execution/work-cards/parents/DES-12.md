## Реальный join

Reconcile the candidate design independently. Only selected protected conduct joins the native exact-operation authorization result; only owner-conducted selected studies join actual returned evidence/denominator at CNT-06. Optional prospective EVSI uses qualified pre-study predictive outcomes/current evidence before the acquisition decision; an actual return may update/validate it afterward. Net value and feasibility require their own cost/capacity inputs. No evidence posterior, policy authority, scheduler or executor is introduced; absent EVSI does not block a justified bounded route.

**Outputs:** [DES-12.study-design](../cards/DES-12/study-design.md), [DES-12.study-authorization](../cards/DES-12/study-authorization.md), [DES-12.study-return](../cards/DES-12/study-return.md), [DES-12.optional-economics](../cards/DES-12/optional-economics.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Fresh CNT-06 claim consumer for actual owner-produced returned evidence; existing GYD-12 structural writer/next-cycle reader only under its actual structural-write rule; DEB-13/DES-04 next-choice consumer for optional value outputs.

**Review размера:** Keep four distinct outputs: internal study design; exact-operation permission consumption; actual result/denominator/inference when a study is selected; optional economics with prospective EVSI and later update/validation kept distinct. Interface-based preparation proceeds independently; external conduct and its result are conditional, and neither EVSI nor permission gates candidate design. No executor, grant writer, posterior activation, or universal DTO.
