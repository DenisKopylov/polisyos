## Реальный join

independent-review emits Bound review receipts, classified finding ledger, delta-review result and final immutable review identity.. Root reconciles these exact outputs at the original parent disposition and sends the composed result to FINAL-REPLAY dispatcher consumes the accepted review identity and complete matrix.. This join does not create implementation, a second owner, or an extra start gate.

**Outputs:** [FINAL-REVIEW.independent-review](../cards/FINAL-REVIEW/independent-review.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** FINAL-REPLAY dispatcher consumes the accepted review identity and complete matrix.

**Review размера:** Independent review/delta review binds to immutable candidate and classifies findings under P40; owners repair their own scopes.
