## Реальный join

Join real rendition bytes, an independently reconciled full source denominator and the exact content-bound receipt at the existing Lex production consumer. The assigned GYD-14 lead owns engineering discovery/rebind of that consumer and the existing source-inventory reader; this does not create, supply or independently certify the denominator. Missing or partial members remain unresolved. Preserve candidate status; trust roots, signer/holder authority, legal evidence and accessibility labels remain separate required inputs and cannot be inferred from the path assignment or local signer. Actual E02 acceptance and parent dispatch/lease conditions remain authoritative.

**Outputs:** [GYD-14.lex-rendition-receipt](../cards/GYD-14/lex-rendition-receipt.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** The existing Lex production rendition consumer over multilingual_assurance.py and CAS, identified by the GYD-14 lead’s engineering rebind; fresh exact-denominator receipt/audit readback. Freeze the existing consumer path/lease at accepted-source rebind; actual E02 acceptance remains required for implementation.

**Review размера:** R25 retain: one full-denominator production-consumer capability. The consumer path and inventory owner remain unallocated at source rebind; legal, trust-root and human-comprehension authorities are separate inputs, not hidden scope.
