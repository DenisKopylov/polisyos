## Реальный join

Root reconciles source denominator, row-chain evidence and affected generated/public readback; closure is the recomputed semantic chain, not row count.

**Outputs:** [GYD-16.moved-denominator](../cards/GYD-16/moved-denominator.md), [GYD-16.chain-matrix](../cards/GYD-16/chain-matrix.md).

**Review размера:** Two checkpoints separate inventory from chain join. Native owners, migration/journal/review companions and parent status remain intact.
