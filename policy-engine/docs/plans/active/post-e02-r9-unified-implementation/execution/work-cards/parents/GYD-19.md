## Реальный join

Root joins the source-indexed a–v mapping with actual post-freeze consumer decisions, retained traces and matched removal probes.

**Outputs:** [GYD-19.negative-matrix](../cards/GYD-19/negative-matrix.md), [GYD-19.negative-battery](../cards/GYD-19/negative-battery.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Fresh V4 reader of the persisted case matrix and actual owner decision traces.

**Review размера:** Two stages separate mapping from execution without creating test/product capability layers. P34 and P41 never default green.

Ниже сохранённые parent-wide clauses, а не разрешение спрятать реализацию. Они должны быть доказаны outputs названных карточек, собственным coordination decision или настоящим external input. Shared constraint сам не является выполненной работой.

- **binding** — Original source/oracle/implementation/probe/review and permit conditions remain parent acceptance.
  `task.acceptance_steps[0]`; `task.acceptance_steps[1]`; `task.acceptance_steps[2]`; `task.acceptance_steps[3]`; `task.acceptance_steps[4]`
- **join** — Preferred solution is the combination of a–v mapping and actual-consumer execution with one persisted witness per case.
  `solution.preferred_solution`
