## Реальный join

Root reconciles the complete applicability map with one real final owner wave and fresh public/generated readback.

**Outputs:** [GYD-21.artifact-validator-map](../cards/GYD-21/artifact-validator-map.md), [GYD-21.final-wave](../cards/GYD-21/final-wave.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Fresh consumer of actual owner validator results and affected public/generated artifacts.

**Review размера:** Applicability is derived before the single execution wave. No substitute registry, duplicate replay, global gate or rewritten Phase 5/6 status.
