## Реальный join

Root reconciles persisted role event/no-client result, Ring-2 construction decision and VOI normalized record without treating one as another.

**Outputs:** [GYD-27.role-events](../cards/GYD-27/role-events.md), [GYD-27.ring2-refusal](../cards/GYD-27/ring2-refusal.md), [GYD-27.voi-normalization](../cards/GYD-27/voi-normalization.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Actual candidate proposal reader plus the Ring-2 constructor/admission consumer, each resolving its source-bound result.

**Review размера:** Three unrelated properties stay separate; no LLM authority, new verifier roster, transcript-based role or invented VOI formula.
