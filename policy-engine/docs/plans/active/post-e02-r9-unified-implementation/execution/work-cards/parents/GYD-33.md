## Реальный join

Root reconciles Scholar L2 and Lex L3 outputs at one N4/N8/N1/N2 bridge, plus separate measured S2 emitted-family coverage.

**Outputs:** [GYD-33.scholar-l2](../cards/GYD-33/scholar-l2.md), [GYD-33.lex-l3](../cards/GYD-33/lex-l3.md), [GYD-33.binding-bridge](../cards/GYD-33/binding-bridge.md), [GYD-33.emission-coverage](../cards/GYD-33/emission-coverage.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Fresh N4/N8/N1/N2 consumers resolve the bridge; the actual selected S2/N12 reader separately resolves emitted-vs-available coverage.

**Review размера:** R25 L2/L3 source-owner split retained; bridge made explicit as real consumer. Distinct R14 output denominator separately measured. No duplicate source/bridge owner, GY-K appointment, GY-G composition or menu-based proof.
