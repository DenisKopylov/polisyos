## Реальный join

Join enforced aggregate-cap and actual allocation lifetime, full measured class envelope, G05 nested-work and matched-reexecution evidence, and the bounded service-profile input. LIF-04 consumes measured eligibility/age targets and persists the admitted duty/next trigger; LIF-02 consumes overdue coverage. Exact duty output and claim reaction are mandatory parent composition, not OPS-08 ownership.

**Outputs:** [OPS-08.aggregate-cap](../cards/OPS-08/aggregate-cap.md), [OPS-08.class-envelope](../cards/OPS-08/class-envelope.md), [OPS-08.research-meter](../cards/OPS-08/research-meter.md), [OPS-08.reexecution-evidence](../cards/OPS-08/reexecution-evidence.md), [OPS-08.service-profile](../cards/OPS-08/service-profile.md).

**Fresh consumer / ответственный контур:** Existing LIF-06 admission consumer, G05 method-result consumers, LIF-04 due-intent/durable duty owner and LIF-02 selected claim reader.

**Review размера:** Five cards isolate physical enforcement, empirical class qualification, two G05 workload/effectiveness contexts, and ongoing custody input. No card creates a scheduler. R15/R16 fragments move to G05 navigation; durable service promise stays with LIF-04/LIF-02. Provider spend remains OPS-07. The parent resource lease does not prove child write paths disjoint.
