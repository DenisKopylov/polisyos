# Completion and admission to limited practical use

## Default programme target

The user's requested target is comprehensive completion before the first limited practical application. Therefore the default release gate is **all mandatory internal work in the reconciled programme accepted**, with every original nonclosed GY/Atlas/debt/R9 obligation completed and accepted or formally closed by its competent owner. An original open obligation cannot be deferred as profile-irrelevant before first use without an explicit human change of scope. A small successful demonstration does not silently replace that target.

There is a useful alternative: permit a narrower private/as-of advisory profile before all unrelated optional tiers and surfaces are complete. This document describes how such a profile would be evaluated, but **does not select that earlier-launch alternative**. It needs an explicit product decision and a list of excluded capabilities. The programme's unfinished scope remains visible in either case.

External authority and optional deployment tiers require a different treatment from missing code. No universal system can pre-acquire every future law, dataset, institutional mandate or backend. Complete their generic intake/refusal/reconciliation mechanisms and selected conformance cases; record genuinely unavailable facts and unselected tiers with precise limits and re-entry triggers. The human/G release disposition determines whether those limits are compatible with the selected first use. Do not disguise internal missing producers or researchable contracts as unavailable external inputs.

## Four claim bands

| Use | What it may establish | What it cannot inherit automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Synthetic rehearsal | A mechanism, negative path and fresh-reader behavior on visibly synthetic inputs | Real recommendation, causal identification, permission, institutional act or actual publication authority |
| Limited real advisory | Grounded analysis or a costed, actionable refusal for a named real decision and recipient | Automatic institutional action, general validity beyond population/period/method, publication or field-pilot safety |
| Signed/public claim | The exact admitted claim, audience and historical/current validity promised by the issuer | An authenticated record alone is not an admissible policy; public-current use needs an active custody duty |
| Institution adopts/executes policy | PolicyOS may receive typed evidence of the external act and reassess its own claims | PolicyOS does not become a government executor, administrator, court, CRM, notification or payment system |

An evaluation or field intervention has a separate EvalSafety boundary. GY-PR1a data-only promotion does not imply PR1b pilot-grade authority or the protected-pilot near-miss evidence in GY-O0-NC-01.

## Commission a real use before observing its preferred outcome

Name the policy question, institution/cell, population, geography, period, options/interventions, target estimands and metric alternatives, recipient/audience, authority band, methods/rules, source versions, actual rights and deployment profile. Select claim-specific evidence and independent oracle rules before inspecting a candidate. If using the first-promotion protocol, apply INT-R9 separately: preserve prospective sequence, failed attempts, sealed/independent evaluation and its prohibition on a sequence-level numeric risk claim.

No particular country, sector, first policy or 14-day/two-run cap is chosen by this plan. WDI's single-response route is the first infrastructure witness, not a universally sufficient policy case. Heating affordability is an illustrative substantive case, not a commissioned dataset or mandate. An inflation series may support a descriptive claim; it does not identify an intervention's causal effect.

The selected real path must preserve input → producer → durable artifact/event → native admission → decision/limitation → fresh consumer → permitted surface. A positive governed recommendation requires its complete promotion predicate. A substantiated refusal is a useful real result, but it is not proof that the positive capability or the entire programme is complete.

## Release dossier

The existing release owner records:

1. Actual E02 acceptance and the program's accepted source/input/backend identity.
2. Reconciled original obligations: completed mechanisms, remaining explicit limitations, external facts and optional tiers. Every exception names affected claims and revisit conditions.
3. Independent source reviews, complete deciding outputs, semantic/removal negatives and the final composed replay on frozen source.
4. Actual operational profile: running workers, effective auth/rights, storage/copy locations, secret/provider principals, measured memory/CPU/disk/queue/cost, backup/restore and migration.
5. Owner-selected limits and SLO/RPO/RTO based on the real decision deadline and custody obligations. Repository seed values and vendor examples are not measured commitments.
6. A usable authorized surface: limitations, competing bases, alternatives, sources, denied uses and current/as-of meaning. Clerk use requires its DS14 consumer chain; public use requires DS12 and relevant DS13 surfaces. No UI-derived authority badge.
7. Accountable operator and currentness-duty handoff/cutoff. A human signature or approval cannot upgrade weak evidence.

## Stop and recovery

Stop the affected action on rights/scope mismatch, wrong tenant/recipient, secret disclosure, unestablished source/verifier binding, uncontrolled resource or provider spend, inconsistent restore, unexpected promotion/publication or expired relevant duty. Preserve the failed source/configuration and complete evidence. Quarantine restored state until newer rights, corrections and dependencies reconcile. Reconcile ambiguous remote effects before retry. Independent operations with valid independent boundaries may continue.

Planned retirement stops new admissions, drains or reconciles jobs, preserves required history and either transfers the currentness duty with authenticated acceptance or publishes the claim owner's explicit cutoff. An offline reader must not keep a perpetual current badge. Revoking a local grant cannot recall bytes already lawfully disclosed or establish physical erasure outside controlled placements.

Full programme acceptance, first-use admission and subsequent institutional adoption are three separate decisions. None is reported as achieved by this planning packet.


## Конечный объём программы и открытый мир оснований

Полное завершение программы до first use означает закрытие конкретных исходных engineering/verification/operational obligations. Это пользовательский порядок релиза, а не универсальный runtime gate. Для R9 обязательны общая грамматика всех26семейств, их маршруты/пределы и доказательства заявленных классов; конкретному case нужны фактические допустимые основания его вопроса. Это не требование собрать все197источников, сделать каждый ground authority или завершить бесконечное будущее расширение каталога. Невыбранная семья/новый будущий источник может оставаться unadmitted без незавершённого пункта текущей программы. Однако уже назначенную обязательную реализацию или проверку нельзя превратить в будущий контент ради раннего запуска. Условность определяется исходным критерием, а не удобством исполнителя.
