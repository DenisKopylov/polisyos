## What the writer must resolve and check

- The structural proposition, parent WMR/edition, variable resolution, target
  population/regime and time; the exact proposed graph delta and retained rivals.
- Exact protocol/analysis/result and evidence versions, deviations, sample/cohort
  lineage and dependence, measurement bridge or explicit regime separation.
- Native method/profile, its assumptions and identification support, actual
  output semantics and uncertainty. A set, bound or qualitative support statement
  stays that type; it is not relabeled posterior probability.
- Evidence distinguishing the claim-relevant rivals from measurement/schema
  changes, selection, implementation failure, feedback/interference and parameter
  drift. This is claim-scoped support, not a claim to know every possible graph.
- Contrary/missing evidence, permitted use, limitations, verifier provenance and
  the applicable current admission/write rule. CNT-06 qualifies support; it does
  not become a second world writer or grant mutation authority.

At admission freeze the actual P37 grade of each required predicate: evidence
binding, method result, target/rival/identification support, rule identity and
rule applicability. Use the existing `recomputed`, `independently_reconciled`,
`consumer_asserted`, `institutionally_supplied`, `not_established` classifications;
do not predeclare a grade from the field name. The last three cannot turn on a
protected write. Externally supplied evidence needs independent reconciliation;
a verifier stamp without the discriminating basis does not qualify it. This is
an acceptance criterion of the existing qualifier/writer, not another gate owner.

Retain W5-K03 diagnosis/admission for post-deployment evidence at its stated
scope. A report, protective response, fit, activation flag, near-zero average or
effect-posterior update alone cannot add/delete an edge. Structural exclusion
requires structural and identification support at the declared resolution;
otherwise retain the supported edge/rivals or explicit unknown.

After qualification, GYD-12 appends the new WMR/Fabric generation, predecessor,
evidence/rule/schema refs and scoped dependencies through its existing atomic or
reconciled multi-store publication path. A later cycle consumes the exact new
version; an old operative model and its historical meaning remain inspectable.

## Admission rule and the genuine decision remainder

At accepted-source rebind, identify whether a current O3/world-write rule admits
this type of structural assessment. If yes, cite and use it. If only the producer,
verifier or bridge is missing, implement the missing mechanism in CNT-06/GYD-12;
do not classify internal engineering as an external input.

If no accepted rule covers **operative structural promotion**, preserve that
specific authority boundary as `not_established`; candidate/versioned research
branches and qualified conditional analyses remain available. Record the existing
rule and exact unsupported transition rather than inventing an O1 flag or a new
institutional appointment. Completing a candidate path does not count as the
operative positive or close the whole structural-learning obligation.

The recommended proposal, **only if rebind establishes this normative gap**, is
to permit the already-appointed world writer to consume a method-qualified
structural assessment under one versioned, claim-scoped admission rule, with
resolved provenance, identification/measurement assumptions, retained rivals,
write fencing and downstream invalidation. It must preserve applicable W5-K03
and allow profile-specific evidence semantics. Reject both a second structural
writer and a rule that treats posterior mutation as structural confirmation.
The human principal decides a change to ratified meaning; this plan does not
apply it to SDD. No per-edge human gate or global programme hold is proposed.

## Acceptance and scheduling

**Positive:** a feasible discriminating result, or an already-qualified scientific
input, reaches the real CNT-06 qualifier with native partial-ID/robust/frequentist
semantics intact. Under an actual applicable structural write rule, GYD-12
publishes one new generation retaining unresolved rivals; the fresh design reader
loads that exact generation and affected claim scope changes. Separately exercise
the original O1 effect-posterior route under its actual active policy.

**Adversarial:** preserve method name, refs and activation markers while changing
the result to a schema/selection explanation or removing identification support.
An O1 near-zero interval without a structural warrant must not delete the edge.
A scientifically supported assessment with no applicable write rule must remain
conditional/candidate rather than mutating the operative world.

**Removal:** remove the content-bound warrant or its rival/identification binding
while retaining all flags and ref shapes; the real writer refuses the structural
change, the old model remains addressable, and an independently supported effect
bound remains separately usable. Removing required post-deployment diagnosis
invalidates every claimed use that depended on that diagnosis.

CNT-06's R18-D06-Q02 slice is a question-scoped supplier; it adds no whole-task
dependency. Prepare both sides after exact interface agreement. Composed acceptance
requires the actual result and applicable rule; freeze/replay uses the existing
stage protocol. Numerical/profile admission and model publication are distinct
predicates; neither a field check nor a successful commit proves them.
