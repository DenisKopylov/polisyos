# S01. Поручение и custody-subject связи между делами

Статус: выбранные направления для реализации после фактической приёмки E02. Это документация, не product PASS, не acceptance/closure и не ратификация SDD.

[Общие правила композиции](../../architecture/10-full-lifecycle-composition.md) · [Матрица 84 вопросов](README.md) · [Независимые проверки](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/review.md)

Имена полей/артефактов ниже задают семантические обязанности и минимальные связи. Окончательные DTO, owner и версии связываются с принятым E02 source; существующие owners/registries расширяются без параллельных систем. Статические наблюдения не доказывают runtime absence или PASS.

## Повторно используемые механизмы

### S01-M1. Candidate signal → bounded framing → later external commission

**native_owner.**

DES-04 feedback/acquisition; DES-01 request-frame/formulator; CNT-01 case-scoped commission. CNT-07/08 own participation/source context and competing-basis lineage; UI-05 is the existing private intake surface.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/scientist/policy_design/formulator.py
- **symbol**: FormulatorCandidate.to_ledger_entry; CandidateProvenance; CandidateAuthorityEnvelope
- **observation**: Static: candidate input refs/provenance flow to hypothesis-ledger shape under a candidate-only envelope; no case commission or source-right admission.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/fabric/provenance/lineage.py
- **symbol**: FabricLineageTracker; trace_claim_origin; impact_analysis
- **observation**: Static: existing origin/impact graph; it does not decide case identity or mandate.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/case_lifecycle.py
- **symbol**: build_case_lifecycle_record; validate_case_lifecycle_record
- **observation**: Static: append-only events under required case_id with evidence/runtime refs and stale-publication validation; per-case, not cross-case identity.

**decision.**

Treat outside-case anomaly, study, counterexample or permitted group account as a source-bound candidate/refinement request. Preserve origin, time, known/unknown scope, rights and affected-claim refs through existing candidate/source/claim/lineage owners. DES-04 may select acquisition or open a candidate; DES-01 retains competing frames and binds only an explicitly selected in-scope frame to N4. A future case is created only when the actual authorized sponsor supplies decision, purpose, boundary, audience and accountable principal through CNT-01. Link its commission to the candidate as provenance if no native ref slot exists; the link never grants mandate. No new inbox, registry or status enum.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Auto-open/expand from anomaly, search, model or message.
- **tradeoff**: Turns low-authority discovery into mandate/target and invents sponsor power; drifts into case management.

- **option**: Discard outside-case signals until a sponsor appears.
- **tradeoff**: Loses useful provenance/dissent; a private candidate-intake path preserves it without authority.

- **option**: Second signal registry or LLM-assigned sponsor/scope.
- **tradeoff**: Bypasses native owners; discovery establishes neither permission nor mandate.

**contract.**

Bind exact signal content or permitted receipt; producer/issuer; observed, received, verified and analysis-as-of times; scope as known/unknown; purpose/audience/rights; candidate and affected-claim refs; and later exact commission selection. Candidate origin is provenance only. Authority/scope changes require actual external commission evidence.

**flow.**

- Producer: DES-04 or CNT-04/07 source-specific input emits a content-bound signal; monitoring remains exploratory.

- Persist: existing candidate, source/evidence, claim and Fabric lineage owners retain allowed signal and origin.

- Bridge: DES-01 binds only selected in-scope frame to real N4; out-of-scope frame routes to UI-05/CNT-01 intake, not a case.

- Fresh consumer: CNT-01 resolves actual sponsor/commission; claim consumers resolve refs and rights before protected use.

- Surface: existing private reviewer/intake and audit show origin, uncertainty and missing commission; no public claim from signal alone.

**migration.**

Do not backfill alarms, search cards or LLM suggestions as commissions. Keep candidate history/limitations; a later sponsor adds an append-only commission link without rewriting prior scope.

**free_grow.**

New signals/languages add data/adapters under existing contracts. A genuinely new authority role needs its owner/verifier; no universal catalog is prerequisite.

**resource_behavior.**

Bound search/detection to case and justified acquisition value; cutoff preserves incomplete frontier, never verified absence/commission. Avoid per-signal case creation.

### S01-M2. Distinct case identity, adapter-local custody ref, optional aliases and append-only succession

**native_owner.**

Reuse the existing PDC case/artifact path to persist a PolicyOS-local technical ref in the selected adapter namespace; exact writer/consumer is rebound after P0. CNT-01 owns each commission; DES-06/PolicyArtifactBuilder keeps editions; Fabric provenance/claim support keeps ancestry. S0 K01 ratifies the need/reference boundary, while §7 leaves a canonical owner, issuer, schema, relation vocabulary and cardinality unratified.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/case_lifecycle.py
- **symbol**: build_case_lifecycle_record; validate_case_lifecycle_record
- **observation**: Required case_id and append-only events exist; inspected contract has no subject identity/relationship semantics.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/fabric/provenance/lineage.py
- **symbol**: FabricLineageTracker; trace_claim_origin; impact_analysis
- **observation**: General provenance/impact is reusable; it does not establish case sameness or child admissibility.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/evidence/claims/claim_registry.py
- **symbol**: normalize_runtime_claim_registry; apply_runtime_claim_registry_to_claim
- **observation**: Read-only claim-ref normalization exists; it does not own external identity or parent/child admissibility.

- **path**: policy-engine/docs/plans/active/post-e02-r9-unified-implementation/solutions/17-design-custody-and-succession.md
- **symbol**: D1; common contract/migration
- **observation**: Plan only: reuse case lifecycle/claim refs and edition manifest; migration does not inherit protected authority. Not runtime proof.

**decision.**

Keep each separately commissioned decision slice as its own immutable case_id. For PolicyOS custody across cases, the existing case/artifact path may persist a stable local technical subject ref qualified by the selected adapter namespace (opaque value; include tenant/authority-domain qualification whenever uniqueness or cross-tenant safety is unproved). This is a PolicyOS-local reference, not a global owner/issuer/schema/cardinality decision and not a claim of real-world or legal continuity. External institutional identifiers are optional, zero-or-more time-qualified aliases with their own source/issuer and verification provenance; their absence does not block the local ref or useful case-scoped work. Reuse the exact local ref or an explicit verified profile-local mapping; absent or ambiguous linkage remains unresolved. Similar titles, targets, sponsors or datasets never infer continuity or merge cases. A mandate/decision-scope split or merge creates successor case_ids; an in-remit frame revision remains a DES-01 branch in the same case.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Create global subject registry/relation/cardinality vocabulary now.
- **tradeoff**: Stage0 deliberately leaves these unratified; this risks sovereign case-management semantics and universal assumptions.

- **option**: Use case_id or matching title as subject identity.
- **tradeoff**: Conflates commissioning slice and subject, losing scope/history on false merge or split.

- **option**: Accept automatic linkage top-match as identity.
- **tradeoff**: Feature/error-dependent statistical match is not continuity or commissioning authority.

**contract.**

Persist a local ref as adapter namespace + opaque value, with tenant/authority qualification where required, and bind it to the native case_id and provenance. Optional external aliases bind the exact source/issuer, opaque value, valid-time and known-at/verification-time, plus any profile-local relation and permission/audience limits. No alias or link grants evidence, claim, mandate, currentness or permission. No universal mapping cardinality or relation semantics are chosen. Parent/successor links append; equality establishes only equality in the qualified namespace, never external continuity or inherited admissibility.

**flow.**

- Producer: the existing case/artifact path records its stable adapter-local technical ref even when the sponsor supplies no external subject ID; external aliases enter only when an actual source provides them.

- Persist: lifecycle/edition owners retain case history and the local ref; artifact/claim lineage records parentage and source versions; a verified alias is appended with provenance and time roles.

- Bridge: explicit local refs or source-resolved profile mappings connect custody records; changed mandate creates successor cases, while DES-01 keeps in-remit frame branches.

- Fresh consumer: claim-support/source-use owners resolve the exact ref/alias and independently recheck child target, scope, time, method, rights and authority; a local ref alone is not proof of external continuity.

- Surface: existing audit/reviewer shows case IDs, namespace-qualified local ref, optional alias qualification, uncertainty and claim limitations.

**migration.**

Do not rewrite case IDs, auto-merge, backfill external aliases or introduce a global subject. For new commissioned work, persist a local ref in the selected adapter namespace without requiring an external identifier. Legacy records without a local ref remain unlinked until an explicit source/profile-qualified mapping is supplied; never infer it from titles or current schema.

**free_grow.**

Local adapter-scoped refs and optional alias rows are data growth under existing owners. Choosing a canonical global owner/issuer/schema/cardinality or cross-profile authority/inheritance semantics remains unratified; a new semantic claim requires a scoped SDD decision and verifier/consumer.

**resource_behavior.**

Use qualified-key lookup in chosen owner. Any fuzzy review is bounded, records method/comparison/error limits and may remain unresolved; timeout/count never selects a merge.

## R18-D01-Q01. От сигнала к поручению

Когда мониторинговая аномалия, новое исследование или сообщение затронутой группы обнаруживает проблему вне порученного дела, как сигнал связан с candidate hypothesis, будущим matter и внешним commissioning, чтобы сам сигнал не считался mandate?

**Выбранное решение.**

Связь — provenance candidate, не автоматическое дело. Вне-case сигнал связывается с exact source, affected claim/participant refs and time; DES-04 may open candidate/acquisition, and DES-01 preserves a separate frame. Only an actual authorized sponsor later commissions the question/scope/audience in CNT-01. The new commission may cite the candidate as motivation but does not inherit its authority. Without sponsor, it remains a useful private candidate; no future PolicyOS matter is created.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S01-M1

**specific_steps.**

- Bind exact content/permitted receipt, issuer, time roles, rights and known/unknown scope.

- Persist candidate and lineage through DES-04/native source owners; DES-01 binds only selected in-scope frame to N4.

- Route outside-case hypothesis through existing UI-05/CNT-01 intake; commission only on actual external selection.

**authority_time_status.**

Candidate-only under native status. Keep observed/received/verified/analysis-as-of distinct. Future authority is externally supplied and resolved; no silent expansion. Unknown scope limits only dependent protected use.

**external_inputs.**

- Exact permitted signal/report/participant receipt and its source/use rights; testimony also needs actual context, selection/translation and audience.

- Actual sponsor/institution commission naming decision, scope, purpose, accountable principal, audience and answerers.

**unresolved_research.**

Standing/commission rules vary by jurisdiction. The pilot institution supplies issuer/delegation/review route; no universal rule is invented.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: DES-04
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Outside-case anomaly may change acquisition/frontier or open a candidate, never commission/scope; retain source/claim lineage.
- **positive**: Permitted counterexample persists and changes next acquisition/candidate choice.
- **adversarial**: Unresolvable, revoked or foreign-scope signal remains candidate.
- **removal**: Remove origin lineage with candidate IDs intact: fresh reviewer loses source; remove commission link: case stays uncommissioned.

- **task_id**: DES-01
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Consume origin refs as candidate-only; bind authorized in-scope frame to real N4.
- **positive**: Fresh N4 sees original intent/unknowns and selected frame; alternative remains visible.
- **adversarial**: Population/goal/jurisdiction substitution stays a separate hypothesis.
- **removal**: Keep frame IDs but remove intent/scope binding: fresh input reveals semantic loss.

- **task_id**: CNT-01
- **role**: commissioning consumer
- **delta**: Bind candidate ref to actual sponsor, decision, scope, purpose, audience and accountable principal; preserve typed blocker when absent.
- **positive**: Real commission is linked to origin and owner/consumer route.
- **adversarial**: Search no-hit, LLM summary, alarm or participant signature alone cannot commission.
- **removal**: Remove authority relation: candidate remains useful but protected case use cannot pass.

**Основания выбора.** [S01-R01](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s01-r01), [S01-R02](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s01-r02)

## R18-D01-Q02. Оспаривание выбранной области

Если допустимые правовые, эмпирические или participatory основания оспаривают выбранные population, jurisdiction, period или goal, кто может инициировать re-commissioning и покрывает ли прежний mandate новую scope? Как сохраняется оспаривание до внешнего решения?

**Выбранное решение.**

A person/source may submit a contest only through a permitted selected intake/evidence route; right to contest is not authority to change the commission. CNT-04/07 preserve legal/participatory context and dissent; CNT-08 retains competing bases; DES-01 records exact frame delta across population, jurisdiction, period, goal and decision boundary. A frame change or successor scope is authorized only where the resolved, currently effective commission plus authenticated delegation covers the named sponsor, act and exact scope; the authorized sponsor can select that successor without a fresh institutional act. Seek new authority evidence only for uncovered scope. Candidate analysis continues under unknowns, but expanded-scope protected claims stay limited/blocked under S0-K06.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S01-M1

- S01-M2

**specific_steps.**

- Compare proposed frame to the actual commission fields; no arbitrary universal materiality threshold.

- Persist source, challenger context/permissions, disputed premise and counterevidence in CNT-04/07/08.

- DES-01 forks the proposed frame; CNT-01 resolves current commission plus delegation against the exact scope and authorized successor-selection act. A covered selection is recorded append-only; only an uncovered scope needs additional external authority evidence; out-of-remit remains candidate.

**authority_time_status.**

Contest is input, not authorization. A source-resolved current commission/delegation may authorize a specific successor selection; otherwise authority is externally supplied for the uncovered scope. Proposed change is candidate, not implicit extension or retroactive knowledge. K06 limits only claims needing unresolved scope.

**external_inputs.**

- Actual commission and, where relied upon, authenticated delegation, issuer competence, scope, effective/revocation dates and sponsor successor-selection act. A new institution act is needed only when existing resolved authority does not cover the changed scope.

- Relevant legal/empirical/participatory sources and permissions; external principal authorized to accept any uncovered boundary change.

**unresolved_research.**

Standing to file/decide is institution- and jurisdiction-specific; retain candidate dissent while the selected institution resolves it.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-01
- **role**: commission/successor authority consumer
- **delta**: Resolve the exact contest scope and sponsor selection against the current commission and authenticated effective delegation; consume CNT-04-qualified issuer/effectivity facts and seek new authority only for uncovered scope.
- **positive**: CNT-04 verifies the instrument; its delegation covers the exact successor scope, which the sponsor selects append-only without a new institution act.
- **adversarial**: Keep the declaration but resolve delegation expiry or scope so the child is uncovered; limit only that child.
- **removal**: Remove commission/delegation scope resolution while keeping frame and labels; protected successor authority is not established.

- **task_id**: DES-01
- **role**: branch producer
- **delta**: Keep scope/goal/decision changes distinct and bind only selected frame within permitted remit.
- **positive**: Real scope edit creates branch and changes actual generation input.
- **adversarial**: Change period/jurisdiction but preserve label: fresh scope comparison detects it.
- **removal**: Remove scope fields with IDs intact: meaningful distinction is lost and output bounded.

- **task_id**: CNT-04
- **role**: legal/applicability owner
- **delta**: Resolve applicable law/version, effectivity, issuer competence and actual-act facts; supply qualified refs to CNT-01/CNT-08 without deciding whether a contest is commissioned.
- **positive**: An effective applicable provision from a competent issuer changes only the matching legal premise; CNT-01 consumes its qualified ref.
- **adversarial**: Draft, wrong-jurisdiction, expired or signature-only instrument stays unestablished.
- **removal**: Keep citation/signature but remove applicability/effectivity/competence resolution; the protected legal premise cannot pass.

- **task_id**: CNT-07
- **role**: participation evidence owner
- **delta**: Preserve testimony/contest context, selection/representation, translation, permission, audience, dissent and correction; do not infer mandate or scope authority.
- **positive**: Permitted, contextualized testimony informs only the affected claim with representation limits visible.
- **adversarial**: Unpermitted input or self-appointed speaker cannot authorize a new frame or broaden scope.
- **removal**: Keep report label but remove context/permission resolution; fresh protected use cannot rely on testimony.

- **task_id**: CNT-08
- **role**: basis comparison owner
- **delta**: Consume qualified CNT-04/CNT-07 refs; compare changed bases, ancestry and dependencies, preserving dissent and refreshing only affected claims.
- **positive**: Verified legal or participation premise change updates linked support; common study/cohort is not independent evidence.
- **adversarial**: Citation/hash alone, false target/time edge or mirrored testimony cannot count as new or independent support.
- **removal**: Remove dependency/ancestry binding but keep labels; fresh reader cannot claim affected refresh or independence.

**Основания выбора.** [S01-R01](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s01-r01), [S01-R02](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s01-r02)

## R18-D01-Q03. Идентичность и кратность дел

Когда один продолжающийся PolicyOS custody subject представлен несколькими design cases или похожие поручения могут дублировать друг друга, какие отношения subject-to-case различают непрерывность и самостоятельные дела, не схлопывая их истории?

**Выбранное решение.**

Separate case_id, a stable PolicyOS-local custody ref qualified by its adapter namespace (plus tenant/authority qualification where needed), and optional time-qualified external aliases. The local ref works without an external subject ID but identifies only PolicyOS custody records in that namespace, not real-world continuity. Across cases, use the exact local ref or an explicit verified profile mapping; otherwise continuity stays unknown and case-scoped work continues. Alias equality or similarity never merges cases or proves applicability/permission.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S01-M2

**specific_steps.**

- Persist the qualified local ref through the existing case/artifact path; no external ID or global registry is required.

- Bind cases explicitly to that ref. Append supplied external aliases with source, time roles and verification provenance.

- Use exact refs or verified profile mappings only; leave ambiguous/rotated aliases unresolved.

**authority_time_status.**

Local ref equality is referential within the qualified PolicyOS adapter namespace. Optional external aliases require source qualification and distinct valid-time, observed, verified and known-at times. Neither local ref nor alias grants authority/currentness/permission; external continuity remains institutionally sourced when asserted.

**external_inputs.**

- No external subject identifier is required to create/use the qualified local technical ref for PolicyOS custody and case-scoped work.

- Issuer/assignment-system facts and actual referent are inputs only when an external alias or continuity claim is used; profile-local relation/cardinality and linkage error costs remain selected-context inputs, not global defaults.

**unresolved_research.**

No universal owner, issuer, schema, cardinality or method for cross-profile subject relations is ratified. Use the local technical ref now; keep external aliases and any real-world continuity claim source-qualified and unresolved until the relevant institution supplies grounds.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-01
- **role**: case owner
- **delta**: Distinguish case_id from a stable adapter-namespace-qualified PolicyOS-local custody ref; accept optional external time-qualified aliases, with no global owner/issuer/schema/cardinality decision.
- **positive**: No external ID: a local ref is explicitly reused across two distinct case histories; a later verified alias appends without rewriting either.
- **adversarial**: A reused/rotated token or same value in an unqualified namespace does not merge cases.
- **removal**: Removing namespace or alias verification limits that cross-reference; local ref and separate histories remain.

- **task_id**: CNT-08
- **role**: ancestry/duplicate consumer
- **delta**: Use exact local refs or verified profile mappings to find related custody records; ancestry is not external continuity or independent support.
- **positive**: With no external ID, a fresh reader retrieves records explicitly attached to one local ref across distinct case_ids, without claiming real-world continuity.
- **adversarial**: Similar questions or unqualified/reused aliases do not collapse cases or make evidence independent.
- **removal**: Remove ref/mapping resolution but retain hashes: aggregation and independent-support claims become unknown; cases stay separate.

**Основания выбора.** [S01-R01](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s01-r01), [S01-R02](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s01-r02), [S01-R03](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s01-r03)

## R18-D01-Q04. Наследование оснований между делами

После split или merge какого из связанных cases могут касаться parent claims и evidence, и какие scope, time или use-rights должны оставаться привязаны к исходному делу?

**Выбранное решение.**

A frame/edition change within the remit is a DES-01 branch in the same case. A split/merge changing decision scope creates successor case_ids and append-only parent links. Existing authenticated, effective delegation may authorize the exact successor selection when it covers the sponsor, act and child scope; a new institutional act is needed only for uncovered scope. Case links and parent evidence do not transfer authority, applicability or rights. Reuse requires fresh CNT-03/04/07 and claim-use checks for each child’s content, scope, time, method, audience and rights. CNT-08 retains conflicts/common ancestry; shared data never merges mandates.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S01-M2

**specific_steps.**

- Freeze parent scope; compare exact proposed child scopes.

- Resolve commission/delegation, issuer, effectivity and coverage for each child. Record a covered sponsor selection; seek new authority only for uncovered scope.

- Create distinct child case_ids and append parents; IDs themselves grant no authority. Recheck child artifact rights and claims; refresh only affected uses.

**authority_time_status.**

Parentage/derivation is provenance only. A child’s commission may be supported by the current parent commission plus authenticated effective delegation that covers the exact act and scope; otherwise obtain authority evidence for the uncovered scope. A new case ID itself adds no authority. Each child claim needs fresh content-bound evidence/source-use checks; distinguish source effective time from received/verified/knowledge-as-of. Missing grounds limit only dependent use; candidate work continues.

**external_inputs.**

- Parent commission and any relied-upon delegation: issuer, authenticity, scope, effectivity/revocation and sponsor selection. New act only if coverage is insufficient.

- Child-use rights and selected source-specific method/target facts.

**unresolved_research.**

Delegation/standing is institution-specific. Resolve supplied evidence against exact scope; only uncovered protected uses wait. SDD concerns whether a link itself transfers admissibility, not the local-ref engineering.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-01
- **role**: commission/successor authority consumer
- **delta**: Resolve current commission and successor selection against sponsor, act and exact child scope, consuming CNT-04-qualified issuer/effectivity facts; accept covered effective delegation and seek new authority only for uncovered scope.
- **positive**: CNT-04 verifies effective delegation; CNT-01 confirms it covers the split, then records sponsor selection, distinct child IDs and parent links without a new instrument.
- **adversarial**: Keep delegation bytes but resolve expiry/coverage outside one child; only that child authority stays limited.
- **removal**: Remove commission/delegation scope resolution while retaining child IDs; fresh protected reader cannot establish authority and candidate work remains.

- **task_id**: DES-01
- **role**: scope branch producer
- **delta**: Emit child frames/scope diffs and carry resolved sponsor-selection ref; never infer authority from a frame/link.
- **positive**: A split under verified existing delegation yields selectable child frames and new IDs, preserving intent/unknowns.
- **adversarial**: A child beyond delegated territory/period remains a candidate, not authorized.
- **removal**: Remove selection ref but keep frame IDs: candidate continues; child is not shown as commissioned.

- **task_id**: CNT-03
- **role**: artifact/right admission
- **delta**: Resolve exact representation/query receipt and recheck purpose, audience, processor, transformation, retention/revocation for child use.
- **positive**: Same artifact is re-admitted where verified grant covers child scope/purpose.
- **adversarial**: Changed body, expired/wrong-purpose grant or foreign audience is denied/incomplete.
- **removal**: Keep citation but remove content-bound grant resolution: fresh protected child use fails.

- **task_id**: CNT-04
- **role**: legal/issuer evidence owner
- **delta**: Resolve legal instrument version/effectivity and issuer/competence evidence for a relied-upon delegation; provide qualified facts to CNT-01, not commission selection or child coverage.
- **positive**: The source-resolved delegation is authentic and effective at the selected cut; CNT-01 can decide exact successor coverage.
- **adversarial**: Same declared bytes but wrong issuer, expired term or inapplicable jurisdiction fails qualification.
- **removal**: Remove source, effectivity or competence verification but keep signature; delegation evidence is not established.

- **task_id**: CNT-07
- **role**: participation evidence owner
- **delta**: Resolve whether testimony/participation context, selection, translation and permission cover each child use; preserve dissent and limits, not authority to commission.
- **positive**: Existing permission covers testimony for child A but not B; only A may reuse it as a basis.
- **adversarial**: Unpermitted, misrepresented or out-of-context testimony cannot support the changed child scope.
- **removal**: Keep participant/citation labels but remove child-specific context/permission; the child cannot rely on that testimony.

- **task_id**: CNT-08
- **role**: basis comparison owner
- **delta**: Consume qualified CNT-03/04/07 refs; compare parent/child bases, target/method/time applicability and common ancestry, dedupe overlap, and refresh dependent uses. Do not re-decide law, delegation or permission.
- **positive**: Child-specific qualified bases are reused only where applicable; merge retains conflict and counts common cohort once.
- **adversarial**: False target/time edge or shared cohort cannot pass as applicable or independent support.
- **removal**: Remove child dependency/ancestry binding but retain parent IDs; fresh support reader limits the unsupported child basis.

- **task_id**: DES-07
- **role**: revision consumer
- **delta**: Route material imported edits to native owners; hash/unbound prose cannot inherit parent verification.
- **positive**: Population/period edit changes child scope and triggers affected evaluation.
- **adversarial**: Copied citation with changed clause does not transfer support.
- **removal**: Keep revision IDs, remove edit dispatch: fresh consumer cannot reproduce changed scope.

**Основания выбора.** [S01-R01](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s01-r01), [S01-R02](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s01-r02)

## composition_edges

- **producer_task**: DES-04
- **consumer_task**: DES-01 → CNT-01
- **artifact_or_property**: Origin-bound candidate ref followed by explicit sponsor commission/scope
- **preparation_stage**: Agree existing candidate and request refs; rights/claim ancestry remain CNT-03/04/07/08 owners.
- **composed_stage**: Signal stays candidate; only external commission authorizes protected use.
- **condition**: Interface prep is parallel; composed positive requires actual N4 output and sponsor evidence, not fixture.

- **producer_task**: CNT-03/04/07
- **consumer_task**: CNT-08 → child claim-use reader
- **artifact_or_property**: Exact source/legal/testimony artifact, permission, scope/time applicability, shared ancestry
