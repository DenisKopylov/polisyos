Status follows method support: branch/bound/limited/contested or unresolved. Static/UNRUN.

## R18-D02-Q01. Что сохраняет смысл после split/merge онтологии?

Когда новая версия исходной таксономии разделяет прежнюю категорию на несколько или объединяет несколько в одну, какой предметный basis позволяет считать старые членства, знаменатели, subgroup-выводы и эффекты сопоставимыми с новыми категориями? Какие выводы остаются только о прежней категории, а какие могут ограниченно переноситься через частичное или необратимое crosswalk?

**Выбранное решение.**

Split/merge does not transfer old effect to each new category. Keep the claim at its old scope unless source evidence shows exhaustive/disjoint mapping, compatible construct/instrument/denominator and selected-method support. Child estimates need actual finer data or variable-specific validated crosswalk. Recompute all supported mapping variants; if support changes the result, branch or derive method-grounded bounds. Labels/URIs and SKOS equivalence do not establish causal comparability.

- Pin old/new definitions, versions, unit, period, denominator and measurement basis.

- Validate source split/merge coverage and variable-specific weights; geometry alone is insufficient.

**authority_time_status.**

См. global_semantics; transfer authority remains at source-supported resolution.

**external_inputs.**

- Versioned source definitions and official split/merge relationships.

- Variable-specific child data/margins/weights.

## R18-D15-Q01. Преемственность единицы при split/merge

Когда реестр или статистический источник делит, объединяет либо повторно использует идентификатор единицы, какое source-qualified основание позволяет считать временной ряд той же целевой единицей, преемником или новой популяцией, и как альтернативы соответствия отражаются в знаменателе и целевом estimand downstream-анализа?

**Выбранное решение.**

ID reuse is not continuity by default. First type the unit (legal actor, programme episode, statistical unit, place or analytic cohort); use the issuer’s lifecycle rules and effective intervals. Split/merge means predecessor/successor, not each child inheriting the whole history. Reused ID after closure/reassignment is a distinct episode unless the defining source establishes continuity. Parent roll-up requires exhaustive/disjoint mapping plus compatible construct, denominator and method; child history needs finer data or valid crosswalk. Legal continuity is external evidence and does not establish causal-population identity.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S02-M2

- S02-M1

**specific_steps.**

- Declare identity role and source scope; preserve ID separately from episode.

- Bind lifecycle/act evidence to valid and known-at dates.

- Recheck denominator, membership, interference and method; merge only if compatible.

**authority_time_status.**

См. global_semantics; issuer defines its keys, competent institution defines legal continuity.

**external_inputs.**

- Source data dictionary and lifecycle events with content/version.

- Legal act only if legal continuity is in scope.

- Selected method’s unit, cohort and windows.

**unresolved_research.**

No universal entity continuity rule; preserve episodes/candidate relations, branch on source evidence, then re-evaluate selected method.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: LIF-01
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Bind identity role, source, scope, time and purpose; separate assertion, assessment and act.
- **positive**: Registry shows closure/successor and distinct episodes.
- **adversarial**: Reused ID after gap without act is not continuity.
- **removal**: Remove source act but keep key; identity remains unresolved.

- **task_id**: LIF-07
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Track lifecycle artifact and downstream membership influence.
- **positive**: Correction triggers affected claims, historical mapping retained.
- **adversarial**: Same key in two incarnations conflicts.
- **removal**: Remove lifecycle parent; claim stale/limited.

- **task_id**: GYD-05
- **role**: input collection
- **delta**: Legal membership only, with competent source/ruling.
- **positive**: Dated official source supports scoped legal succession.
- **adversarial**: Name/ID or LLM conclusion alone.
- **removal**: Remove act; authority returns not_established.

- **task_id**: CNT-06
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Bind identity episode to target denominator and method.
- **positive**: Predecessor observations excluded after closure; successor included with evidence.
- **adversarial**: Merge programme/person IDs and duplicate exposure.
- **removal**: Remove relation from target constructor; no population proof.

**Основания выбора.** [S02-R02](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s02-r02), [S02-R04](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s02-r04)

## R18-D15-Q02. Согласованность срезов независимых источников

Как для конкретного estimand определяется достаточная временная согласованность вектора срезов независимых источников, если у них нет общего атомарного snapshot: какие отношения observation time, valid time, known-at time и source cut допускают совместный вывод, а какие оставляют его ограниченным или неразрешённым?

**Выбранное решение.**

Independent-source cut is a vector, not the maximum timestamp. Record each source’s incarnation/version/native position/coverage plus valid, observation, effect, receipt, publication and known-at coordinates. Sufficiency is defined by selected estimand: ordering, window overlap, target stability, lag or observation completeness. Native transaction/cursor proves only its own boundary; no global atomicity follows. With no atomic cut, report conditional on exact vector and test plausible source-supported neighboring cuts; derive useful bound only if method permits. If exposure order/support/decision changes, limit/contest or leave unresolved. LIF02 owns currentness and late CAP04 events trigger replay while old as-known state is preserved.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S02-M3

- S02-M1

**specific_steps.**

- Capture native position, incarnation and coverage per source.

- Bind corrections and valid/known-at semantics; state estimator-required relations.

- Recompute supported neighboring vectors; preserve old as-known result and reissue affected claims.

**authority_time_status.**

См. global_semantics; each owner attests native cut only; cross-source consistency is method property.

**external_inputs.**

- Native snapshot/cursor semantics, coverage and continuity evidence per source.

- Change/correction event effective and known-at times.

- Selected estimand’s ordering, lag and completeness requirements.

**unresolved_research.**

No universal tolerance; source-supported sensitivity gives only conditional evidence and cannot prove unobserved revisions absent. If continuity contract is missing, preserve as-of bound or currentness not_established; candidate analysis can continue.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **adversarial**: Event predates enrollment or has late effect date; replay retains ordering.
