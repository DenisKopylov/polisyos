## Повторно используемые механизмы

### S03-M1. Claim-scoped search coverage witness

**native_owner.**

CAP-01 produces and persists the exact candidate/search receipt; CNT-08 consumes search frontier and scope when comparing a basis. No second catalog, crawler, or global completeness service.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/fabric/catalog/discovery.py
- **symbol**: SemanticDatasetCatalog.refresh/search/resolve
- **observation**: Static: contract-backed candidate search, ambiguity and stale-entry handling; not an external census or claim-coverage evaluator.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/fabric/connectors/contracts/source_contract.py
- **symbol**: SourceContract / SourceContractQuality / SourceContractLineage
- **observation**: Static: declared source/schema, quality-check reference and lineage seed; not empirical search or sample-quality measurements.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/scientist/validation/claim_support.py
- **symbol**: evaluate_claim_support
- **observation**: Static: claim predicates/counterevidence affect support/readiness; no search-recall estimator.

**decision.**

Extend CAP-01's exact receipt with a claim-scoped assessment only where search breadth/absence matters: target population, geography, period, languages, evidence classes and inclusion rule; each source/query/filter/index cut and update; records screened; access, truncation and omitted routes; benchmark provenance, per-source indexability and relative recall; and a case-approved stop rule. Assemble seeds through multiple routes independent of the target query where feasible and peer-review queries (PRESS when relevant). Relative recall is a source-local diagnostic, not absolute recall. Report ‘none retrieved by these permitted routes/cut’; an inaccessible, unindexed or unsearched source stays a coverage limitation, not a zero.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Single provider hit count, top-k or LLM assertion.
- **tradeoff**: Hides source/query/language selection and conflates retrieval with support.

- **option**: Exhaustive global index or all-routes-before-any-work.
- **tradeoff**: Unachievable and needlessly blocks bounded candidate research.

- **option**: Known-item relative recall plus selected complementary routes and expert review where warranted.
- **tradeoff**: Preferred bounded check; benchmark representativeness and absolute recall remain unknown.

**contract.**

Subject: claim/search generation and declared scope. Inputs: permitted source/index cuts, exact route/query or opaque allowed binding, filters, benchmark refs/provenance/indexability, screening counts and failures. Output: persisted route coverage, relative-recall denominators, exclusions/gaps, budget and bounded wording. Bind source/profile, query-disclosure decision, search-rule version, authority purpose/audience and recorded/as-of time. It is selection history, never source admission or claim support.

**flow.**

- CAP-01 reuses selected catalog/provider and existing query-disclosure/source-use checks.

- Persist exact permitted search receipt plus scoped benchmark/route assessment and omissions.

- CNT-08 consumes it as bounded selection history; claim-type owner evaluates retrieved evidence.

- Fresh consumer exposes exact no-hit/cut and limits, never global absence.

**migration.**

Legacy URLs, catalog rows or model answers are candidate hints. Backfill no adequacy without recoverable scope, route/cut, denominator and benchmark provenance; otherwise reassess for the next claim use.

**free_grow.**

Add sources/languages/seeds as existing data/config. A new protocol requires a typed adapter, not a second registry. Bound candidates, query bytes, time, routes and citation hops; truncation/access failure remains partial.

### S03-M2. Claim-specific evidence dependence and effective independence

**native_owner.**

CNT-08 owns ancestry/dependence comparison; CNT-06 owns claim-specific method/synthesis. CAP-03 admits source roots and their lineage; CAP-06 binds restricted remote-compute receipts when needed. Neither CAP task decides independence. LIF-05 disclosure overlap remains a distinct rights property.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/fabric/provenance/lineage.py
- **symbol**: FabricLineageTracker.attach_evidence_bundle/trace_claim_origin/impact_analysis
- **observation**: Static: graph connects evidence bundles and ancestry; a graph edge/hash does not establish sampling covariance or independence.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/scientist/validation/claim_support.py
- **symbol**: ClaimSupportAssessment/evaluate_claim_support
- **observation**: Static: support/counterevidence lifecycle exists; no study resolver or overlap estimator.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/fabric/connectors/contracts/source_contract.py
- **symbol**: SourceContractLineage
- **observation**: Static: source lineage seed is not participant/cohort ancestry or independence evidence.

**decision.**

Use CNT-08's basis/lineage graph for report→study/cohort/site/admin source/participant/control→estimate ancestry; bind dependence to claim, estimand, target, outcome and sample. Resolve overlap only from source/custodian evidence. Different IDs, hashes, authors or domains never imply disjoint samples. Use an appropriate multilevel model for effects nested in known clusters; multivariate/overlap-adjusted covariance when overlap is measured; robust variance only for identified clusters with adequate assumptions; and sensitivity over evidenced bounds for partial overlap. If cluster or bound is unknown, do not assert independent replication or tighten precision by report count. Preserve useful separate/descriptive results; unknown is not a blanket block. Keep LIF-05 protected-unit disclosure overlap distinct.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Count publications, datasets, hashes or domains as independent.
- **tradeoff**: Inflates support/precision when records share participants, cohorts or controls.

- **option**: One result per likely cluster.
- **tradeoff**: Conservative if covariance is unavailable, but loses information and needs a declared grouping rule.

- **option**: Dependence-aware models for known structure; robust variance for known adequate clusters; sensitivity for evidenced partial overlap.
- **tradeoff**: Preferred when assumptions fit. Unknown cluster membership is not solved by any model; otherwise withhold only independent-confirmation/precision claim.

**contract.**

Subject: claim-specific estimate/basis generation. Inputs: source-supported study/sample ancestry, permitted overlap/covariance evidence, effect/target/estimand and method assumptions. Output: dependency relation plus separate method/uncertainty result and unresolved bound. Bind source roots, verifier, rule/method/schema version, purpose/audience and observation/recorded times. Unknown overlap remains explicit; identity alone cannot mint independence.

**flow.**

- CAP-03 admits exact roots and attached source lineage.

- CAP-06 binds permitted custodian-side overlap output to the exact remote analysis when restricted data cannot leave.

- CNT-08 links bases; CNT-06 selects/runs the dependence method and records uncertainty.

- Fresh reader distinguishes independent, dependent and unresolved support.

**migration.**

Citation counts, hashes and prior lineage do not backfill independence. Link only from owner evidence; preserve unknown and historical generations.

**free_grow.**

Add cohorts/outcomes to existing lineage. New relation/method is conditional on accepted-schema gap. Bound estimate clusters/sensitivity scenarios; budget exhaustion is inconclusive, not precise.

### S03-M3. Target/estimand-specific support diagnostics across quality drift

**native_owner.**

CNT-06 owns target-support diagnostics; CNT-08 compares bases. CAP-03 owns source-root/use admission; CAP-06 restricted remote receipts. CAP-04 owns external change events; LIF-02 owns dependent-claim currentness for qualified own assessments and enrolled events. No second watcher/cascade.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/fabric/connectors/contracts/source_contract.py
- **symbol**: SourceContractQuality / SourceContractSLA / SourceContract.version
- **observation**: Static: quality/SLA are declared contract fields; fixed version does not measure frame, missingness, mode or transport.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/scientist/validation/claim_support.py
- **symbol**: evaluate_claim_support
- **observation**: Static: support lifecycle seam, not a survey-quality/transport recomputation.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/fabric/provenance/lineage.py
- **symbol**: FabricLineageTracker.record_transform_stage/trace_claim_origin
- **observation**: Static: transform lineage can be recorded, not semantic OCR/translation validity or drift by itself.

**decision.**

Bind each diagnostic to source root, wave/frame, target/estimand, instrument/mode/language and processing; separate version, observation, effective and recorded times. Assess frame/subgroup coverage, missingness/attrition, weights/ESS, target overlap and measurement bridges as the method requires. Recompute from admitted data or label supplied values; response rate alone is not bias. Narrow only affected support; missing measurement stays not_established. A qualified CNT-06 own assessment changes dependent claim currentness through LIF-02 without a webhook or source event. CAP-04 remains the producer of real external source events; LIF-02 consumes those separately.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Hash/version/ETag is the sole drift signal.
- **tradeoff**: Misses sampling, missingness and measurement change under identical bytes; byte change does not identify claim impact.

- **option**: Universal feature threshold, response cutoff or quality score.
- **tradeoff**: Mixes error types; false passes/fails. Census cutoffs are not universal.

- **option**: Source/wave quality evidence plus claim-specific coverage, missingness, transport and measurement tests.
- **tradeoff**: Preferred and interpretable; needs actual measurements and cannot detect undisclosed/unmeasured drift.

**contract.**

Bind support metrics/denominators, assumptions, qualified own assessment and unknown dimensions to root/wave, claim target/period, method/rule, verifier, purpose/audience and effective/recorded time. LIF-02 may update the dependent owned claim from a qualified assessment without a webhook; this is not a source event. CAP-04 supplies only actual source-qualified events for separate LIF-02 replay. No global score/threshold.

**flow.**

- CAP-03 admits roots/rights; CAP-06 binds restricted remote results only when needed.

- CNT-06 qualifies target-support diagnostics; CNT-08 compares immutable bases.

- LIF-02 applies qualified own assessments to dependent-claim currentness even without source event.

- CAP-04 emits actual external events; LIF-02 replays them separately. Never derive an event from a diagnostic.

**migration.**

Unchanged hash, release ID, QC contract or old result does not backfill stable support. Reuse old metrics only if frame/wave/denominator/method resolve; otherwise unknown until fresh assessment.

**free_grow.**

Add waves, frames, groups, instruments and measures to existing CAP/CNT owners; new estimand method only by reviewed adapter. Bound work to permitted strata/aggregates; unavailable private data means narrow/not-established, not substitute.

## R18-D03-Q01. Когда поиск достаточен для заявленного охвата?

Для утверждения о заданных популяции, географии, периоде и языке какие свидетельства и границы поиска позволяют судить о достаточности discovery, если часть каталогов, закрытых источников, языков или контрпоиска недоступна? Как эта недоступность должна ограничивать вывод, чтобы no-hit или отсутствие найденной альтернативы не звучали как отсутствие основания во внешнем мире?

**Выбранное решение.**

Adequacy is claim- and route-bounded. Reuse CAP-01's exact receipt and expose target scope, sources/index cuts, queries/filters/languages, update and citation routes, screened denominator, access failures, and known-item provenance/indexability/relative recall under a case-approved stop rule. Assemble seeds from multiple routes independent of the target query where possible and peer-review strings (PRESS when relevant). A seed missing from an index is a coverage gap, not a query miss. Optional registries, citation chaining, grey literature or institutional queries are chosen to match the claim, never universally required. Say only ‘not retrieved in these permitted routes as of this cut’; unresolved sources/languages limit absence or breadth claims but do not stop candidate work or support a narrower claim.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S03-M1

**specific_steps.**

- Declare claim, target population/time/language, evidence rule and the inference that depends on absence.

- Record exact permitted routes, query/filter/index cut, date, results/screening, failures and stop rule; keep restricted query content protected.

- Use independently sourced known items; check provider indexability before recall; review misses/gaps and narrow wording.

**authority_time_status.**

Candidate/search history only; exact query/source/index/cut and verifier remain visible. No-hit means not retrieved there, not absent externally. Missing access/language is not_searched/not_established; only claim owner determines support/status.

**external_inputs.**

- Case owner/method reviewer supplies target, eligibility rule and materiality.

- Provider/custodian supplies index coverage/cut/access and permitted query/result terms; existing CAP-01 verifies disclosure/use.

- Known items need scoped references and independent-route provenance; their representativeness remains unknown.

**unresolved_research.**

No method proves a seed set represents every policy domain. For high-consequence absence claims, a reviewer must judge which unsearched source/language could change the result; record and route that gap now, then acquire only where material.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CAP-01
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Extend the existing exact-source candidate/no-hit receipt with a claim-scoped coverage assessment and known-item/source-local relative recall evidence. Keep source/use/query-disclosure admission and the catalog/connector owner unchanged; no global index or new no-hit authority.
- **positive**: Diverse known items are indexed and retrieved per route; receipt reproduces within declared scope.
- **adversarial**: Withhold an in-scope seed or route/language while leaving “finished”; broad absence claim becomes limited.
- **removal**: Remove benchmark/indexability/cut binding but keep green marker; fresh CNT-08 consumer refuses broad absence.

- **task_id**: CNT-08
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Consume search/exposure trace only as bounded selection history and keep frontier, cutoff and blind spots separate from the admitted empirical basis; apply the claim's stated scope when comparing bases.
- **positive**: Completed bounded search supports only “not located in routes X as of cut Y.”
- **adversarial**: Truncated budget, stale index or unsearched source cannot become full no-hit.
- **removal**: Drop selection-history dependency but keep count; broad conclusion loses its warrant.

## R18-D03-Q02. Когда похожие источники дают независимую поддержку?

Если несколько найденных работ, наборов или публикаций могут разделять исходную когорту, административный источник, синдицированный текст или наблюдения, а их lineage нельзя надёжно установить, какое основание позволяет считать их независимым подтверждением конкретного утверждения и что можно утверждать при частично известном overlap?

**Выбранное решение.**

Distinct reports, IDs, hashes, authors or disclosure units are not independent evidence. Link reports to study/cohort/sample/admin source/control ancestry; bind dependence to the claim's target, outcome, sample and estimand. Use multilevel models for effects nested in known clusters, overlap-adjusted covariance when actual overlap is evidenced, robust variance only for known adequate clusters, and sensitivity over defensible partial-overlap bounds. If cluster or covariance remains unknown, do not claim independent replication or tighten precision from report count; retain useful results separately/descriptively or use conservative cluster-level synthesis. Unknown overlap limits the independence claim, not all research.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S03-M2

**specific_steps.**

- Link reports through registrations, protocol, site/cohort/frame/wave/participants/controls/outcomes; keep uncertain links unresolved.

- Resolve exact overlap with source/custodian evidence; keep statistical dependence separate from LIF-05 protected-unit/disclosure accounting.

- Select covariance-aware/bounded method for the estimand; surface sensitivity and do not pool incompatible targets.

**authority_time_status.**

Lineage is source evidence; independence is claim/method-specific. Preserve source-supplied, verified, recomputed and unknown states plus sample/wave, report, model, effective and recorded times. Unknown dependence removes only unsupported independent confirmation/precision.

**external_inputs.**

- Custodian supplies cohort/site/sample/control lineage, overlap/covariance and corrections; restricted data may stay local behind a verified aggregate.

- Method owner supplies estimand, dependence assumptions, cluster adequacy and falsifier.

- Sharing permission for any overlap witness is resolved externally through current rights.

**unresolved_research.**

No general algorithm proves disjointness when custodians hide lineage. Try registration and custodian-side overlap routes for a consequential replication claim; otherwise keep unknown dependence and retain scoped, non-independent findings.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-08
- **role**: ancestry/basis-comparison owner
- **delta**: Link immutable study/cohort/control ancestry; retain partial and unknown overlap.
- **positive**: Shared cohort does not add independent confirmation.
- **adversarial**: New DOI/hash over shared controls cannot tighten precision.
- **removal**: Drop the dependence edge but keep count; fresh synthesis loses independence.

- **task_id**: CAP-03
- **role**: source-root/use-admission producer
- **delta**: Bind exact admitted roots and any source-supported protocol/cohort lineage to them; do not decide independence.
- **positive**: CNT-08 receives content-bound root and qualified lineage.
- **adversarial**: A citation/hash without a resolved root is not a disjoint sample.
- **removal**: Keep count labels but remove root binding; ancestry consumer loses its basis.

- **task_id**: CAP-06
- **role**: restricted remote-compute receipt producer (conditional)
- **delta**: For data that cannot leave the custodian, bind permitted overlap output to roots, executable, purpose and recipient; do not classify independence.
- **positive**: Allowed aggregate overlap result is tied to the exact analysis.
- **adversarial**: Receipt/hash or rights-overlap ledger alone is not statistical independence.
- **removal**: Keep cohort labels but remove remote result binding; method consumer refuses it.

- **task_id**: CNT-06
- **role**: claim-specific dependence-method owner
- **delta**: Select and run covariance-aware/bounded synthesis with cluster assumptions, adequacy, target/estimand and uncertainty bound.
- **positive**: Known shared sample changes method-specific uncertainty.
- **adversarial**: Unknown links or too few clusters cannot pass as independent/robust.
- **removal**: Keep method label but remove ancestry input; oracle changes uncertainty or refuses.

## R18-D03-Q03. Когда качество основания ухудшилось без смены его версии?

Если байты или release ID источника не изменились, но меняются sampling frame, доля пропусков, охват групп, измерительный инструмент либо состав участников, какое evidence позволяет решить, что основание всё ещё поддерживает текущую популяцию и estimand, а не только более узкий прежний срез?

**Выбранное решение.**

Stable bytes/release/contract do not prove current support for a target/estimand. Bind quality evidence to source root, wave/frame, target, instrument/mode/language and processing; assess coverage, subgroup/unit/item missingness, attrition, weights/ESS, target overlap and measurement bridges. Recompute from admitted data or label supplied indicators; use method-specific sensitivity. Response rate or a generic score alone is not bias/transport. Narrow or bound only affected claims; absent diagnostics mean not_established beyond the observed wave/target, not “unchanged.” A computed diagnostic is not a source event, but a qualified CNT-06 target-support assessment can change our dependent claim through LIF-02 assessment/currentness without a webhook or source-release event.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S03-M3

**specific_steps.**

- Bind old/current target, outcome, estimand and frame/wave/instrument.

- Acquire permitted coverage, missingness/attrition, weights, target covariates and relevant bridge evidence.

- Qualify the CNT-06 assessment and compare bases. LIF-02 consumes own assessments without a webhook; CAP-04 emits only real, qualified external source events.

**authority_time_status.**

Keep source version, quality interval, observation/assessment, method/rule, effective and recorded time distinct. LIF-02 can react to a qualified own assessment without implying an external event; CAP-04 supplies only qualified source events. Historical signatures stay historical.

**external_inputs.**

- Custodian supplies frame, eligibility, subgroup, missingness, attrition, weight and instrument/mode facts; source access remains permitted.

- Method reviewer supplies target/estimand, effect modifiers, assumptions and falsifier; bridge data need lawful access.

- Permitted custodian-side aggregates may substitute for raw rows. A webhook is not prerequisite for a qualified own assessment to affect dependent currentness.

**unresolved_research.**

Unmeasured/unreported drift cannot be detected without suitable observations. Check source-side audits, administrative benchmarks, follow-up or bridge studies for the case; absent these, retain last observed target/frame boundary with explicit uncertainty.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-06
- **role**: target-support assessment producer
- **delta**: Bind claim target/estimand to wave-quality evidence and method-specific transport/measurement results; emit a qualified own support assessment.
- **positive**: Measured subgroup/mode shift changes only the affected broad claim despite fixed source version/no webhook.
- **adversarial**: Metric-only markers or response rate alone cannot stale/fail a claim.
- **removal**: Remove target/diagnostic binding; LIF-02 fresh currentness no longer sees the measured support loss.

- **task_id**: CNT-08
- **role**: immutable basis comparison consumer
- **delta**: Compare basis generations with target, quality premise and method changes explicit; retain prior generations and affected dependencies.
- **positive**: Only claims depending on changed target support are affected.
- **adversarial**: Same bytes do not erase measured drift or downgrade unrelated claims.
- **removal**: Remove the quality edge but keep snapshots; current comparison loses its basis.

- **task_id**: CAP-04
- **role**: external source-event producer
- **delta**: Emit source change/retraction/rights events only when actually supplied or supported by source/custodian evidence; do not turn CNT-06 metrics into events.
- **positive**: A genuine qualified correction/retraction reaches enrolled dependents.
- **adversarial**: No webhook plus a measured CNT-06 shift updates own claim via LIF-02 but creates no source event.
- **removal**: Remove CAP-04 event bridge; external corrections are missed, while own-assessment path remains.

- **task_id**: LIF-02
- **role**: dependent-claim currentness consumer
- **delta**: Use existing assessment/currentness for qualified CNT-06 own assessments without a webhook; separately replay genuine CAP-04 events. Do not add a watcher or synthesize events.
- **positive**: Fixed source version/no webhook still yields changed currentness for the dependent claim only.
- **adversarial**: No webhook cannot hold a qualified own limitation; unqualified metrics cannot stale a claim.
- **removal**: Remove assessment input but keep version marker; fresh read remains falsely green after measured drift.

## self_attack

- **case**: Seeds come only from the evaluated English query; reported recall is 100% while local-language evidence is missing.
- **repair_or_bounded_limit**: Record seed origin and per-index presence, use independent routes where possible, and limit the absence claim; representativeness remains unknown.

- **case**: Three distinct DOIs/hashes reuse one cohort/control group and are counted as replications.
- **repair_or_bounded_limit**: Link evidenced ancestry, pass dependence to CNT-06, and lower only unsupported independent confirmation/precision.

- **case**: Bytes/version stay fixed while subgroup coverage or mode changes; no webhook arrives; or response rate alone triggers global invalidation.
- **repair_or_bounded_limit**: A qualified CNT-06 assessment changes only the dependent claim through LIF-02 assessment/currentness without a source event; CAP-04 remains for actual external events.
