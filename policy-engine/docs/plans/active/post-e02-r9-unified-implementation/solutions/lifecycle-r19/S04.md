## Повторно используемые механизмы

### S04-M1. Юрисдикционно-квалифицированный source→applicability branch

**native_owner.**

CAP-05 captures source/act; CNT-04 qualifies a content-bound rule/envelope once. Native consumers reuse it only after fresh predicate resolution. Competent review is for novel/disputed meaning or effect, conflict/competence, out-of-envelope use, or explicit own-contract requirement. External issuers retain acts; LIF-01 admits each use.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/data_forge/domains/legal/corpus/ingest.py
- **symbol**: ingest_legal_doc_bytes
- **observation**: Static: persists exact bytes/provenance/events; ingestion alone proves neither legal force nor applicability.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/data_forge/domains/legal/contracts.py; .../corpus/index.py; .../corpus/versioning.py
- **symbol**: LegalDocSource; VersionEntryV1; resolve_active_version
- **observation**: Static: source IDs, jurisdiction/language, publication/effect dates and source-local version selection exist; no cross-source conflict resolution or legal interpretation.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/ir/analytics/applicability.py; .../lex/normpack/applicability.py; .../lex/legal_evaluation/context_builder.py
- **symbol**: NormApplicability; applies_to_context; _build_rule_observation
- **observation**: Static: schema has jurisdiction/time/subject/object/conditions/exceptions, while builder/helper check only jurisdiction/date and the legal context consumes that result; see P38.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/data_forge/domains/legal/source_relation.py
- **symbol**: require_legal_source_relation_verification (historical task anchor)
- **observation**: File absent in this checkout. Historical plan reference is not evidence of a live verifier; rebind owner after E02.

**decision.**

Keep co-applicable rules and competing readings as scoped branches. Auto-apply a qualified rule only when current version and every required scope/time/subject/object/condition/exception/case predicate is recomputed or independently reconciled at use. Review genuine interpretation/effect, conflict, competence or out-of-envelope use; meanwhile limit only affected protected claims and permit declared candidates. No universal hierarchy, latest-arrival winner or LLM authority. Comparison remains jurisdiction-specific (VCLT relations, ICJ Art.38, ILC fragmentation, TFEU 267, Aarhus). Pattern pass P04/P05/P09/P10/P15/P22/P26/P31/P32/P37/P38/P41.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Universal legal ranking/resolver
- **tradeoff**: Rejected: conflates different domestic, treaty, precedent and procedural regimes.

- **option**: Latest date/arrival or source-version ID wins
- **tradeoff**: Rejected: does not prove repeal, conflict, scope or precedence.

- **option**: Source-bound parallel branches plus qualified review and real consumer checks
- **tradeoff**: Chosen: preserves partial application and explicit uncertainty.

**contract.**

Reuse legal source/version/provision and applicability types; version a native contract only for a proven missing predicate. Bind exact artifact/version/span, authentic language/translation, issuer/competence evidence, selected rule+version, parties/jurisdiction/territory, subject/object/group/action, exceptions/transition, legal interval, received/known/verified/admitted cuts, reviewer scope/provenance, contrary sources, status, claim-use, purpose and audience. Bind qualification to exact content/version/envelope; LIF-01/native evaluator resolves each required predicate afresh. Unsupported dimensions stay limited. Actual orders bind text, addressee, scope, effect, service and revocation. Freeze P37 predicate basis.

**flow.**

- CAP-05 captures exact official source/act and persists source events.

- CNT-04 qualifies the source-specific rule/envelope once or emits an unresolved branch; no per-use approval.

- At use, native consumer resolves current version and all required predicates; novel/disputed or out-of-envelope grounds return to CNT-04.

- LIF-01 content-binds that relation to one Claim/use; DES-11/CNT-08 consume scoped incumbent/competing branches.

- Fresh Claim/legal consumer and permitted audit/API projection show basis, time, scope and limitation.

**migration.**

Rebind legacy roots to exact content, version, reviewer, scope and use; incomplete records stay candidate/not_established. Corrections append new branches and affect only dependent uses; preserve as-known history.

**free_grow.**

Jurisdictions/instruments/profiles grow as records under CAP-05/CNT-04. New legal semantics need a reviewed versioned adapter/conformance in the existing owner, not a central closed legal taxonomy.

**resource_behavior.**

Index only selected instruments/provisions, parties, target groups, action and time cuts. Keep incomplete searches distinct from legal absence; no whole-law crawl is required for candidate work.

## R18-D04-Q01. Пересечение правопорядков и частичная применимость

Для межтерриториальной политики какое компетентное основание связывает каждую норму с рычагом, группой и временем, когда территории, органы и потоки данных пересекают несколько правопорядков? Как представить частичное применение и неразрешённый конфликт охватов?

**Выбранное решение.**

Build a potentially applicable source set per actual territory, group, action, exception and time. Compose compatible duties; for a true conflict, use the selected jurisdiction's rule. Reuse a qualified rule after fresh in-envelope checks; review actual conflict/competence. Until resolved, keep partial/contested branches: candidate work proceeds under declared assumptions, but affected protected claims cannot get a global pass.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S04-M1

- S04-M2

**specific_steps.**

- Capture exact sources and source-local versions; resolve target/scope intersections.

- Ask the named reviewer to resolve only the actual conflict; keep out-of-scope subsets separate.

- DES-11 compares mixed incumbents; recommendation never enacts replacement.

**authority_time_status.**

Source, applicability and actual operation are distinct. Preserve legal effective, received, known, verified/admitted and publication cuts; unknown scope stays candidate/limited. Freeze P37 predicate basis.

**external_inputs.**

- Official source/status records and factual target/territory/action.

- Named legal reviewer and selected conflict rule where sources contradict.

**unresolved_research.**

No actual jurisdiction/program is supplied. A concrete review must decide co-application vs conflict; no global resolver is justified.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CAP-05
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Persist exact source, provision, party/jurisdiction and act provenance; relation-writer owner is unresolved until rebind.
- **positive**: Party A evidence supports A only.
- **adversarial**: Saving clause excludes legacy cohort; global date must not override it.
- **removal**: Drop party/scope relation, keep URL/hash: fresh consumer limits.

- **task_id**: CNT-04
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Qualify exact rule/envelope once; review actual conflict or competence uncertainty.
- **positive**: Compatible norms both apply to covered subgroup.
- **adversarial**: Same-action instruments conflict; no latest-version winner.
- **removal**: Drop legal rule/reviewer, keep labels: protected result is limited.

- **task_id**: LIF-01
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Bind applicability to one claim, action, population, purpose and audience.
- **positive**: Data-transfer relation supports only that use.
- **adversarial**: Reuse citation for a different group/action without review.
- **removal**: Remove content-bound use verifier; fresh Claim reader refuses.

- **task_id**: DES-11
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Consume a verified mixed incumbent and declared uncertainty.
- **positive**: V1 remains for S/legacy while V2 covers new entrants in R.
- **adversarial**: Candidate ID or REPLACE layer turns off V1 globally.
- **removal**: Remove scope/time consumption; trajectory reader finds false incumbent.

- **task_id**: CNT-09
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Conditional selected legal premise feeds status and permitted surface.
- **positive**: Selected status/audit/projection reflects exact legal scope and limits.
- **adversarial**: Selected premise limits claim but decision/status stays green.
- **removal**: Remove relation→status/surface edge, keep markers: selected case fails; unselected proceeds.

## R18-D04-Q02. Конкурирующие толкования и их правовой вес

Каков вес и охват LLM/парсинга, юррецензии, ведомственного руководства, прецедента и обязательного решения, когда языки или прочтения расходятся? Какая позиция поддерживает claim, а какая оставляет его условным?

**Выбранное решение.**

LLM parsing/translation is candidate only. Preserve original/authentic-language text and scoped reviewer readings qualified once per exact rule/envelope, reusable after fresh predicates; admit new/disputed meaning only when exact source, method, reviewer scope and contrary authority are bound to the claim. A supplied court/agency decision gets only the effect its forum and applicable law give it. Material unresolved disagreement remains conditional/contested; no model majority or invented precedent.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S04-M1

- S04-M2

**specific_steps.**

- Bind language versions/translations to source spans.

- Persist materially different readings, scope, method, reviewer and counterevidence.

- If an actual decision is supplied, verify its text, jurisdiction, parties/issue, status and effect.

**authority_time_status.**

Interpretation is source-, issue-, rule- and time-bound; court/agency authority is not a free-floating rank. Unknown material reading caps protected claim, not candidate analysis.

**external_inputs.**

- Authentic source/language and named reviewer mandate.

- Actual decision plus evidence of jurisdiction/scope/status if claimed.

**unresolved_research.**

No legal system or actual competing ruling is supplied; effect of precedent must be resolved under the selected forum's rules. No actual holding is asserted.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-08
- **role**: consumer

- **adversarial**: Several paraphrases of one source count as independent authority.

## R18-D04-Q03. Оспаривание, приостановление и преюдициальный вопрос

Какие события (оспаривание, preliminary reference, обеспечительная мера, stay, апелляция, отмена) меняют применимость конкретного акта, с каким охватом и времени? Имеет ли незавершённое производство приостанавливающий эффект для этого акта или дела?

**Выбранное решение.**

Separate filed challenge/referral/appeal from interim measure, stay, finality, annulment and effect. Pending status alone does not suspend a rule; only the applicable procedural rule or an actual competent order establishes that effect and its case/recipient/time scope. A qualified rule is reusable after fresh case predicates; review unresolved effect/competence. CAP-05/CNT-04 supply evidence; LIF-01 binds use, LIF-02 reacts to affected uses; PolicyOS never runs the docket.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S04-M1

- S04-M2

- S04-M3

**specific_steps.**

- Persist each actual event/order with issuer, parties, operative scope and legal/received times.

- Review automatic-effect rule vs order; do not infer from labels.

- LIF-02 downgrades only dependent claims; DEB-06 reissues only when needed.

**authority_time_status.**

Event report is institutionally supplied until content/source are reconciled; legal effect needs governing rule/order. Keep filed, issued, effective, served, received and known times distinct.

**external_inputs.**

- Actual official order/docket/appeal/referral with scope/status.

- Applicable procedural rule and service evidence where effect depends on notice.

**unresolved_research.**

Need the real proceeding, rule and order to resolve stay/finality. EU Article 267 is a bounded example, not evidence this hypothetical is stayed.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CAP-05

- **delta**: Capture actual official order/status, text, addressees, proceeding and source cut.

- **task_id**: CNT-04
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Qualify automatic-effect rule once; assess actual order; review unresolved stay/effect/competence.
- **positive**: Review limits suspension to the order's case/parties.
- **adversarial**: Assume all appeals suspend—or none do—without rule.
- **removal**: Drop effect/rule review; claim becomes contested/limited.

- **task_id**: LIF-02
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Enroll/replay event and invalidate only exact dependent uses.
- **positive**: Linked claim stales immediately; unrelated claim stays current.
- **adversarial**: Pre-enrollment event or missing replay floor leaves cached green.
- **removal**: Remove event edge/fence, retain marker: fresh read detects stale use.

- **task_id**: DEB-06

- **delta**: Use existing linked epoch only for material PolicyOS claim reissue.
- **positive**: New release binds source cut and prior head.

## R18-D04-Q05. Договор, обычай и мягкие нормы

Что определяет, действует ли источник как обязательное правило, incorporated condition, официальное толкование, обещание или ориентир? Как учитывать участие/оговорки договора, практику и opinio juris обычая, принятие либо включение soft law? Когда источник блокирует admissibility, а когда информирует candidate?

**Выбранное решение.**

Do not assign one global binding/soft rank. Treat treaty status by instrument, parties, reservations, scope and relevant domestic implementation; custom by practice plus opinio juris for the supported states/group/time; soft law by issuer, mandate and any actual incorporation into law, contract, funding or policy objective. Reuse qualified rules after fresh predicates; disputed custom/effect remains for review. A source blocks admissibility only where a claim-specific applicable legal/contractual basis supports that consequence. Otherwise it can inform a candidate without being mislabeled law.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S04-M1

- S04-M2

**specific_steps.**

- Resolve authentic source/version and source family.

- Use depositary and domestic records for treaty status/effect; retain custom practice and opinio juris separately.

- Trace soft-law force to an actual incorporation/mandate; absent it, keep informative/candidate status.

**authority_time_status.**

Treaty consent, custom evidence, soft-law issuance and incorporation are separate predicates with version/scope/time/provenance. Institutionally_supplied or not_established force alone cannot carry protected authority; an informative claim may still be supported at that narrower scope.

**external_inputs.**

- Official depositary/issuer and domestic implementation record.

- Practice/opinio-juris evidence with contrary examples for custom.

- Actual contract/regulation/funding/mandate if soft law is said to bind.

**unresolved_research.**

No instrument, rule or incorporation is selected. A concrete legal reviewer must decide scope; architecture can represent the evidence and limitation without a universal force engine.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CAP-05
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Store official text/version/source family; never infer force from `source_type`.
- **positive**: Party/reservation records limit treaty claim to applicable relations.
- **adversarial**: Non-party signature or aggregator called proof of consent.
- **removal**: Drop status/implementation evidence, keep `binding`: consumer limits.

- **task_id**: CNT-04
- **role**: primary writer

## R18-D04-Q06. Воздействие правового изменения на собственную подпись

После компетентного толкования, отмены или иной правовой перемены когда подписанная рекомендация PolicyOS остаётся исторически точной, но перестаёт быть текущей? Как адресат, временной эффект и saving provisions влияют на revalidation связанных claims?

**Выбранное решение.**

A signature is historical under its admitted source/rule/use basis and known-at cut. A later qualified interpretation, repeal, correction or act changes current truth only for claims, groups, actions, territory and time it legally reaches, including saving/transition clauses. Reuse qualified changes after fresh predicates; unresolved retroactivity/saving/effect stays limited for review. LIF-02 first marks affected current uses stale; it then supports scoped reassessment or DEB-06 reissue. Preserve old release/as-known history, maintain separate current view, and never infer retroactivity from arrival time or auto-sign a new claim.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S04-M1

- S04-M2

- S04-M3

**specific_steps.**

- Bind change to exact prior source, provision, time, scope and saving terms.

- Separate legal effect, receipt, verification/admission and original publication times.

- Replay enrolled dependencies; limit affected current view before recompute, then reissue only under existing publication rule.

**authority_time_status.**

Store legal valid/effect interval; issuer publication; PolicyOS receipt/visibility; verification/admission; claim-signature/publication separately. Historical view uses old basis; current view latest admitted scope. Retroactivity dispute remains limited.

**external_inputs.**

- Official change act and saving/transition scope.

- Qualified reviewer determination and exact claim/dependency/source cut.

**unresolved_research.**

No actual later law or signed claim is supplied. Resolve its effect against the exact source dependency and affected audience; preserve both views until then.
