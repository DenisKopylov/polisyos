**native_owner.**

- **observation**: static only: CAS record has actor/mandate/scope-time/groups/dissent/disclosure; validator rejects LLM/rejected but does not resolve issuer/content.

- **path**: src/polisyos/core/contracts/value_outer_set.py
- **symbol**: ValueOuterSet
- **observation**: static only: carries trust/identification/model/epoch and incomparable/unknown.

- **path**: src/polisyos/pdc/_impl/layer2_design_search.py
- **symbol**: Layer2S8ValuePostureInput; _s8_refinement_decision; _s8_firewall_status
- **observation**: static only: contested posture routes to human decision/limit; injected refs remain unverified.

- **path**: src/polisyos/scientist/nodes/builtins/governance/run_normative_arbitration.py
- **symbol**: _synthesize_legacy_normative_frame; _requires_explicit_normative_frame; _evaluate_policies
- **observation**: static only: priority default 5 becomes weight; weighted_delta drives outcome. Governed/production guard requires explicit frame; other reachability UNRUN.

**decision.**

Keep ungrounded frames as candidates, but do not make a missing collective mandate a computation gate. When assumptions/schedule provenance, inputs, method, rights and currentness meet their own contracts, emit and sign an admissible scoped conditional comparison (for example, outcomes under schedules A and B) with uncertainty and scope. This is not a collective-welfare conclusion or selection. A named principal's choice requires that principal's in-scope mandate; collective selection/endorsement requires an act covering groups, audience, objective, aggregation and time. If a schedule is only hypothetical or attributed, say so and do not present it as an authorized preference. No default vote/weights/LLM; incomparable coordinates stay side by side.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Default scalar/majority/mean
- **tradeoff**: Creates unassigned authority over whose values and which aggregation rule decide.

- **option**: Stop every comparison until collective agreement
- **tradeoff**: Hides useful conditional results and a principal's narrower authorized decision.

**contract.**

Bind the conditional calculation to versioned measure/schedule assumptions, source/content, model/method, inputs, uncertainty, rights, currentness, population/groups, audience/use and effective/known-at time. Keep the existing result-support/admissibility disposition separate from authority to select or endorse: an unresolved collective mandate blocks only collective-choice claims; an unresolved premise required by the calculation limits that result. Bind a principal's authority only when claiming its authorized choice, and collective authority only when claiming collective welfare/selection.

**flow.**

- CNT-07 records preference/testimony separately from actual principal or collective mandate; unknown mandate is not a missing premise for an otherwise supported conditional calculation.

- CNT-05 binds schedule, model/input/method assumptions, rights and currentness; persist result support separately from choice authority (CAS is carrier, not proof).

- GYD-31/PDC S8/DES-03 compare sets; fresh consumer verifies the conditional result and separately checks authority for any named choice or collective endorsement.

- Audit shows conditional result, assumptions, alternatives, reversals and uncovered groups, plus the separate fact that no collective choice was authorized.

**migration.**

New act/schedule versions stale dependent claims only; retain as-of history. Rebind legacy path after E02 acceptance.

**free_grow.**

Add local schedules in existing grammar; no universal aggregation default.

**resource_behavior.**

Bound case-specific schedule grids; report incomplete runs; retain uncertainty.

## R18-D05-Q01. Что утверждает рекомендация при несовместимых мандатах?

Если несколько принципалов имеют действительные, но несовместимые порядки ценностей для пересекающихся затронутых групп, а ни один подтверждённый мандат не покрывает все эти группы, какое содержание PolicyOS вправе подписать: условное сравнение, выбор названного принципала в пределах его полномочий или вывод о коллективном благосостоянии? Что в конкретном случае устанавливает границу между этими утверждениями?

**Выбранное решение.**

Если schedule/assumptions, source provenance, model/method, inputs, rights и currentness проверены для конкретного use, PolicyOS может подписать и допустить scoped conditional scientific/computational comparison (например, результаты при schedule A и schedule B) даже без collective mandate. Отдельно маркировать, что это не collective-welfare endorsement и не выбор победителя. Названный principal's choice требует его реального mandate scope; collective selection/endorsement требует акта, покрывающего groups, audience, objective, aggregation и время. Если schedule только hypothetical/attributed, сравнивать условно под этой явной предпосылкой, не утверждая principal authorization. Неизвестный mandate не превращает все per-principal results в candidate; неподтвержденная load-bearing calculation premise ограничивает только зависимый result. Без average/default vote/weights/LLM winner.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S05-M1

**specific_steps.**

- Зафиксировать schedule и статус его источника (authorized, attributed или hypothetical), общие outcomes/units/population/time, модель, методы, входы, неопределённость, права и currentness.

- Если common basis подходит, вычислить и показать signed admissible conditional results по каждой schedule с обозначенной областью и sensitivity; при incomparable coordinates оставить отдельные колонки.

- Проверить authority отдельным потребителем только для названного principal choice или collective selection/endorsement. Отсутствие collective act сохраняет conditional result, но не разрешает collective winner.

- Negative: назначенная/LLM weight или результат условного сравнения не превращается в collective endorsement. Removal: удалить model/input/method/rights/currentness binding при сохранённых labels — calculation fails; удалить только collective mandate — conditional comparison remains admissible, collective selection is refused.

**authority_time_status.**

Conditional result status follows its own verified assumptions, provenance, method, rights and currentness; it can be signed/admissible without a collective mandate. The mandate gives authority only to choices/endorsements within its scope/effective time; no collective act means no collective winner. Incomparable bases remain side by side/contested as appropriate.

**external_inputs.**

- For a conditional calculation: actual selected schedule/assumption provenance, data/model/method, rights, currentness and comparison scope. A principal mandate is needed only to claim that principal's authorized choice; a collective act covering groups/audience/objective/aggregation/time is needed only for a collective-welfare selection or endorsement.

**unresolved_research.**

If a local procedure (including vote) is invoked to select or endorse collectively, resolve its legal authority/scope with the competent institution. Until then, do not state a collective winner; implementation can still publish the independently grounded conditional comparison. Product/runtime reachability remains UNRUN.

- **adversarial**: Wrong-scope ref/default weight cannot promote; a missing collective mandate must not demote an otherwise grounded conditional result, and no result can become a collective winner without that mandate.
- **removal**: Remove model/input/method/rights/currentness binding but keep schedule/status labels: the fresh result consumer fails. Remove only the collective mandate: the calculation remains admissible but collective choice/endorsement is denied.

- **task_id**: CNT-07
- **role**: input collection
- **delta**: Separate mandate from preference/testimony.
- **positive**: Preference and mandate stay distinct; an unknown collective act leaves a fully grounded conditional comparison admissible while collective authority is visibly absent.
- **adversarial**: Response count is not mandate.
- **removal**: Remove issuer/mandate evidence: do not attribute authorization or permit a choice; retain a separately grounded calculation under explicit assumptions.

## R18-D05-Q02. На чём основана граница затронутых групп?

Какие основания позволяют считать карту затронутых групп достаточной для конкретного утверждения, если причинные эффекты могут быть косвенными, кумулятивными или затрагивать группы, не названные заказчиком при постановке задачи? Как обнаружение такой группы после начала наблюдения меняет область допустимого утверждения, не превращая исходную карту в доказательство полноты?

Достаточность карты claim-relative: causal pathways, outcome, population, geography и horizon. Она означает evidence-backed известные/правдоподобные группы as-of epoch, не полноту. Учитывать indirect/cumulative paths, exclusions и unknowns. Новая группа создаёт новую epoch и пересчитывает только зависимые claims; прежняя подпись остаётся исторической as-of, а широкий текущий claim ограничивается до проверки.

**mechanism_ids.**

**specific_steps.**

**authority_time_status.**

Подписывается claim-relative map as-of epoch; unknown не равен отсутствию impact.

**external_inputs.**

- Source-backed population/geography/time and direct/indirect/cumulative mechanisms.

**unresolved_research.**

No advance proof excludes every future group. Use domain evidence/monitoring and retain residual unknowns.

## R18-D05-Q04. Как интерес участника влияет на пригодность его суждения?

**Выбранное решение.**

Оценивать интерес к конкретной роли/суждению: actor, исход, source-selection, applicable institutional rule и mitigation. Обновлять при изменении интереса; ограничить зависимые claims об independent framing/mandate/metric/expertise, сохраняя отдельный подтверждённый факт. Disclosure само по себе не доказывает independence; универсальную COI шкалу не вводить.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S05-M3

**specific_steps.**

- Связать actor-role-interest-source-selection with exact claims and time.

**authority_time_status.**

Suitability зависит от роли и applicable rule; changed interest reopens linked claims.

**external_inputs.**

- Interest declarations and evidence of role/source-selection activity, with updates.

**unresolved_research.**

## root_resolution

- **review_id**: RT-S05-F01
- **disposition**: addressed
- **resolution**: Separate scoped conditional-result admissibility from authority to choose or endorse. A fully grounded, rights/currentness-valid comparison can be signed and admitted without a collective mandate. A principal's actual selection needs only that principal's in-scope authority; collective-welfare selection/endorsement needs the covering collective act. Hypothetical or merely attributed schedules remain explicit assumptions, not principal/collective authorization. No social-choice default or new top-level status is introduced.
- **behavioral_discriminators**:
  - Positive: under the same verified model/common basis, schedules A and B reverse ranking; with no collective act, a fresh reader still admits and displays both signed conditional results and states that neither is a collective winner.

  - Negative: no collective act, a default/LLM weight or conditional comparison cannot become collective selection/endorsement; invalid required model/value premise cannot be promoted merely by a valid mandate.

  - Removal: remove model/input/method/rights/currentness binding while keeping labels and the calculation loses admissibility; remove only collective mandate and the conditional result remains admissible while collective selection is denied.
