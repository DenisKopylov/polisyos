# S06. Причинные основания, конкурирующие миры и пределы знания

Статус: выбранные направления для реализации после фактической приёмки E02. Это документация, не product PASS, не acceptance/closure и не ратификация SDD.

[Общие правила композиции](../../architecture/10-full-lifecycle-composition.md) · [Матрица 84 вопросов](README.md) · [Независимые проверки](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/review.md)

Имена полей/артефактов ниже задают семантические обязанности и минимальные связи. Окончательные DTO, owner и версии связываются с принятым E02 source; существующие owners/registries расширяются без параллельных систем. Статические наблюдения не доказывают runtime absence или PASS.

## shared_semantics

- **contract**: Claim basis связывает утверждение/estimand, intervention/version, outcome/construct, target population/cases, time/horizon, method, assumptions, observation regime, content-bound evidence refs/lineage, contradictions, limits и rule/schema version. Existing lifecycle сохраняет и передаёт basis; fresh consumer заново разрешает refs, проверяет binding и применяет claim-specific support; API/audit показывает тот же статус и причины. Citation, подпись, LLM output или наличие поля не доказывают warrant.
- **authority_time_status**: Раздельно хранить event/measurement time, source/protocol version, target period, receipt time и model/rule version. История неизменна; новая активная модель — новая WMR version с re-evaluation зависимых claims. Authority требует существующих resolve + content-bind + verifier provenance для нужной области; внешний мандат остаётся у института. Unknown scope допустим в candidate research, но protected action и public signature требуют actual grounds. Применять текущий status calculus claim-by-claim без нового универсального causal gate.
- **uncertainty_and_conflict**: Не сводить uncertainty к одному score: sampling interval условен на graph/measure/selection/transport assumptions; structural ambiguity — явный rival set/PAG; partial ID — bounds; qualitative warrant — method и scope. Probability forecast калибровать лишь для заданных outcome/horizon/population/regime по proper score/coverage; это не доказывает причинную идентификацию. Lineage сохраняет общие источники/данные как зависимые; не усреднять несовместимые или противоречащие основания.
- **verification**: UNRUN: проверены документы, статические source anchors и первичные методические источники; runtime, end-to-end producer/bridge/consumer, public surface и semantic tests не запускались.

## Повторно используемые механизмы

### S06-M1. Claim-scoped grounds и противоречия

**native_owner.**

CNT-06: method-specific support; CNT-08: source lineage/intake.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: scientist/validation/claim_support.py
- **symbol**: evaluate_claim_support; ClaimSupportRule; ClaimSupportAssessment
- **observation**: Static only: evaluates rules; does not resolve/content-bind evidence or prove causal adequacy.

- **path**: scientist/orchestration/workflows/causal_full.py
- **symbol**: causal_full_workflow_spec
- **observation**: Static only: names graph/readiness/transport/uncertainty stages; spec is not runtime evidence.

**decision.**

Process tracing and qualitative comparison can support a scoped mechanism/sequence claim when diagnostic observations distinguish rival predictions. They can inform conditional design, safeguards or refusal despite unidentified average P(Y|do(X)); they do not supply effect size, mean effect or transport. Keep conflicting sources visible and group shared-data derivatives by lineage.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Numeric effects only
- **tradeoff**: Throws away useful local mechanism evidence or misstates it as an average effect.

- **option**: Narrative/LLM synthesis
- **tradeoff**: No rival test or binding; candidate only.

- **option**: Count citations as independent
- **tradeoff**: Duplicates lineage and inflates support.

**contract.**

Bind claim type/scope to method, diagnostic observations, predicted rivals, contradictions, missing evidence and explicit limits; no common evidence score across incompatible methods.

**flow.**

- CNT-08 resolves source, content, time, sample and lineage.

- CNT-06 compares claim/rival predictions and records diagnostic warrant.

- Persist claim basis and conflict map.

- Fresh consumer re-resolves evidence and recomputes scoped support.

- Audit/API shows basis, scope, uncertainty and contradiction.

**migration.**

Old citations remain candidate evidence; do not invent missing sample, independence or diagnosticity retroactively.

**free_grow.**

New claims and evidence types grow through evidence, not a closed domain template or universal qualitative score.

**resource_behavior.**

Resolve only claim-linked records; update lineage/conflict indexes incrementally.

### S06-M2. Competing causal structures

**native_owner.**

GYD-12 activates versioned WMR; GYD-10 supplies candidates; DES-12 consumes rivals for design.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: runtime/quality/hypothesis_ledger.py
- **symbol**: HypothesisLedgerEntry; persist/append
- **observation**: Static only: candidate persistence exists, not warrant/promotion.

- **path**: ir/analytics/causal_graph.py
- **symbol**: CausalGraphModel; DAG/CPDAG/PAG/MGRAPH/ADMG
- **observation**: Static only: partial/latent graph types exist; they do not form a complete rival set.

- **path**: scientist/nodes/builtins/causal/reconcile_causal_graph.py
- **symbol**: _SPEC
- **observation**: Consumes data, literature and LLM hints; reconciliation is not admitted causal proof.

- **path**: pdc/_impl/world_model_record.py
- **symbol**: WorldModelRecord; WorldModelLimitations; BranchMode
- **observation**: Versioned model concepts exist; completeness is not inherent.

**decision.**

Separate graph structure from effect parameters/bounds. Anomaly, registration shift or GYD-09 posterior alone cannot change an edge. Nonsignificance or an equivalence/decision-negligible bound applies only to its named estimand; it does not prove structural zero. Keep supported nonzero/heterogeneous edges in WMR. Exclusion requires claim-resolution structural evidence plus identification assumptions at declared variable resolution/regime; otherwise absence is not_established and the edge remains active/contested/unknown under existing semantics. Preserve equivalent graphs/partial structures with distinct do-predictions. GYD-12/WP-01/02 activates a new version, retains its predecessor and re-evaluates dependents. DES-12 uses scoped bounds across retained rivals and calls decisions robust only where admissibility/ranking hold throughout.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Choose best-fit DAG
- **tradeoff**: May orient a false edge and ship a confident-wrong effect.

- **option**: Average incompatible graphs
- **tradeoff**: Can erase different intervention predictions.

- **option**: Require complete social graph
- **tradeoff**: Confuses local claim adequacy with global completeness.

**contract.**

Bind structural edge status/warrant separately from effect bounds. Candidate records resolution, scope, graph type, included/excluded/unknown edges, assumptions/regimes, lineage, rival predictions, evidence, activation and predecessor. Each bound binds estimand/intervention contrast, outcome, target population, regime, horizon, margin, identification assumptions, provenance and time. A small bound never rewrites an edge as absent; exclusion needs structural plus identification support, otherwise absence is not_established.

**flow.**

- GYD-10 persists anomaly as candidate, never active edit.

- DES-12 seeks a feasible study where claim-relevant rivals predict differently.

- Compare scoped effects separately from evidence distinguishing structural from measurement, selection, implementation, feedback/interference or drift explanations.

- GYD-12 activates through the existing handshake; edge exclusion needs structural and identification support, while effect bounds remain scoped parameter evidence.

- Fresh consumers compare affected claims/designs across retained rivals and scoped bounds; keep old versions addressable.

**migration.**

Legacy graph is usable only under recorded assumptions/limitations; do not infer completeness or invent old rivals.

**free_grow.**

Add claim-relevant variables/resolutions/rivals as evidence arrives; no global graph ceiling or auto-promotion.

**resource_behavior.**

Generate local rival set; cache predictions by WMR version, intervention, target and horizon.

### S06-M3. Versioned measurement bridge

**native_owner.**

**reuse_anchors.**

- **symbol**: MeasurementRegistry; SchemaRegimeRegistry; RegimeCalendar; IdentificationModeRouter

**decision.**

Separate latent construct from observed proxy. After survey/language/code/linkage/reporting changes, split series until overlap/paired measures, anchors or validated crosswalk support a bridge in the target population and period. **alternatives.**

**flow.**

- Measurement owner validates pair-specific bridge or records unbridged split.

- CNT-06 propagates bridge uncertainty into construct claim.

- Fresh consumer separates proxy/reporting from construct effects.

- Surface bridge scope, test and residual limitation.

**migration.**

Keep historical joined series; until validated, attach limitation rather than retroactive equivalence.

**free_grow.**

Add evidence-backed bridges per regime/construct; no universal mapping ontology.

**resource_behavior.**

Evaluate only the affected regime pair; reuse requires matching version/population/time.

### S06-M4. Missing-results denominator and conditional synthesis

**native_owner.**

CNT-08 owns search boundary/lineage; DES-12 consumes missingness risk for design/acquisition.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: scientist/validation/claim_support.py
- **symbol**: evaluate_claim_support
- **observation**: Static only: consumer assessment exists; does not retrieve missing results or establish denominator.

- **path**: scientist/orchestration/workflows/causal_full.py
- **symbol**: causal_full_workflow_spec
- **observation**: Static only: lifecycle stages are named; no executed registry reconciliation shown.

**decision.**

Build an inception/result matrix per outcome: eligible study, registry/protocol/SAP version, planned vs measured, available result, source/date, missing reason and retrieval attempt. Never encode missing as zero or no effect. Reconcile records and request results first; assess the specific synthesis with ROB-ME. With sufficient denominator use declared selection scenarios; use bounds only when missing mass and outcome support are constrained. Otherwise show conditional observed-results synthesis plus unresolved missingness. Funnel asymmetry is diagnostic, not proof.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Pool published positives only
- **tradeoff**: Silently conditions on selection.

- **option**: Fill missing with zero
- **tradeoff**: Invents an outcome.

- **option**: One funnel correction
- **tradeoff**: Cannot identify the missing result and asymmetry has other causes.

**contract.**

Bind matrix to claim/outcome, eligibility rule, study and protocol versions, planned/measured status, result availability, known/unknown reason, retrieval and sensitivity assumptions. Protocol proves planned, not measured.

**flow.**

- CNT-08 indexes eligible studies/results and source versions.

- Reconcile protocol, registry, report and retrieval status per outcome.

- Analyze observed results under declared selection scenarios/bounds where justified.

- DES-12 directs acquisition or limits design claims using this gap.

- Fresh consumer exposes denominator, included results and unresolved boundary.

**migration.**

Retain old search cutoff/snapshot; append newly discovered registry records without rewriting history.

**free_grow.**

Add source-specific result records/adapters as needed; no universal publication registry or bias correction.

**resource_behavior.**

Index only eligible studies/outcomes and update on new records, not full corpus replay.

## R18-D06-Q01. Нечисловые основания при неидентифицируемом эффекте

Если анализ последовательности событий, качественное сравнение случаев или свидетельство о механизме поддерживает условный причинный вывод, но P(Y|do(X)) не идентифицирован, какой ограниченный тип утверждения вправе подписать PolicyOS и для какой области? Как не свести такое основание к числу и не сделать его бесполезным для ограниченного дизайна или отказа?

**Выбранное решение.**

Process tracing/qualitative comparison may support a local mechanism or sequence claim when diagnostic evidence distinguishes it from named rivals. It can guide conditional design, safeguards or refusal despite unidentified average P(Y|do(X)); it does not estimate magnitude, mean effect or transport.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S06-M1

**specific_steps.**

- Bind cases, target, time and mechanism.

- Compare diagnostic observations against rival predictions; preserve contrary/missing facts.

- Check selection, source lineage and repeatability conditions.

- Permit only design uses within evidenced scope; keep effect unidentified.

**authority_time_status.**

Local mechanism support does not authorize numerical do-effect or transport.

**external_inputs.**

- Original records, case-selection frame, source/time and author competence.

- Separate estimand/study if an average effect is requested.

**unresolved_research.**

No universal numeric threshold for qualitative causal warrant; CNT-06 can require explicit method, rivals and scope.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-06
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Separate mechanism/sequence claims from average effects and require scope plus rival predictions.
- **positive**: Local diagnostic evidence supports a scoped mechanism claim and cautious design.
- **adversarial**: Keep story/citations but make all observations equally likely under a rival; support falls.
- **removal**: Remove the differentiating observation; fresh claim/design becomes limited.

- **task_id**: DES-12
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Allow qualitative grounds for local design/refusal, not numeric simulation claims.
- **positive**: Design operates only in evidenced cases and timeframe.
- **adversarial**: Move the same design to an unevidenced population; transport limit appears.
- **removal**: Remove applicability condition; claimed mechanism benefit disappears.

## R18-D06-Q02. Переход от кандидата к структурному изменению модели

Когда наблюдение или исследование поддерживает новую причинную связь либо пересмотр старой, какие основания допускают изменение структуры для конкретного утверждения, а не только параметра, диагноза измерения или сценария? Как сохранить конкурирующие структуры, одинаково объясняющие прежние наблюдения, но дающие разные последствия вмешательства?

**Выбранное решение.**

A structural candidate needs content-bound evidence that discriminates it from measurement, selection, implementation, feedback/interference or parameter drift. Claim-use needs a stable/bridged regime; activation follows existing GYD-12/WP-01/02. GYD-09 posterior change alone is not an edge update. Preserve observationally equivalent models when their do-predictions differ.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S06-M2

- S06-M3

**specific_steps.**

- Persist candidate and rivals at fixed variable resolution.

- Plan evidence where claim-relevant predictions diverge.

- Check measurement and selection alternatives.

- Activate new WMR version only through existing handshake; revise dependents.

**authority_time_status.**

Candidate supports conditional research; active structure changes only in a new activated WMR version.

**external_inputs.**

- Protocol/data and provenance; regime bridge; study design that distinguishes structural from measurement/selection causes.

**unresolved_research.**

Best feasible discriminator is setting-specific; DES-12 can optimize over declared rivals without asserting completeness.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: GYD-10
- **role**: input collection
- **delta**: Record anomaly as candidate with rivals and distinguishing evidence.
- **positive**: Verified observation matches candidate-specific prediction.
- **adversarial**: Same signal comes from schema/reporting shift; candidate stays unconfirmed.
- **removal**: Remove differentiating evidence; structural support falls.

- **task_id**: DES-12
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Design across rival structures and seek discriminating studies; current designer is mainly single-graph.
- **positive**: Study result separates rival intervention predictions.
- **adversarial**: Old data fit multiple graphs with different do-effects; no single recommendation from fit alone.
- **removal**: Drop a rival but keep complete-set declaration; consumer detects set change.

- **task_id**: GYD-12
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Write activated structure as new WMR version after evidence/activation handshake.
- **positive**: Bound evidence creates new version and reevaluation of dependents.
- **adversarial**: Keep activation flag, remove evidence binding; remain candidate.
- **removal**: Remove evidence ref; fresh/restored consumer loses support, prior version stays.

## R18-D06-Q03. Неизвестные связи вне сохранённого множества моделей

Если рекомендация устойчива во всех сохранённых вариантах причинной модели, когда отсутствующую связь можно считать исключённой для этого утверждения, а когда она только не найдена или не смоделирована? Что допустимо утверждать, если полнота набора не обоснована, а пропущенная структура может изменить вывод?

**Выбранное решение.**

Robustness is only over a versioned model set, assumptions and claim scope. Nonsignificance or an average effect inside a decision margin supports only that estimand/population/intervention/outcome/regime/horizon under its identification assumptions; it does not prove structural zero. Preserve supported nonzero/heterogeneous edges in WMR. GYD-12 excludes an edge only when claim-resolution structural evidence plus identification assumptions support absence at declared variable resolution; otherwise absence is not_established. DES-12 calls a decision robust only if admissibility and ranking hold across the scoped bound and retained rivals. If an unbounded omitted relation could flip the choice, report conditional/partial results or abstain on that claim; a complete social graph is not required.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S06-M1

- S06-M2

**specific_steps.**

- Bind model-set version, graph resolution, intervention/contrast, outcome, population, regime, horizon and assumptions.

- Store each effect bound separately from edge status, binding its margin, evidence, identification assumptions, lineage and time; retain known subgroup/pathway or horizon heterogeneity.

- Challenge omitted selection, feedback, measurement and cause paths; nonsignificance or average cancellation is not structural absence.

- Use bounds/scenarios only when assumptions make them finite; DES-12 checks decisions across the bound and retained rivals.

- GYD-12 changes edge status only with claim-resolution structural evidence plus identification support; otherwise preserve the edge and mark absence not_established.

- Reopen when evidence changes the bound scope or reveals decision-relevant structure.

**authority_time_status.**

No truth-in-set or complete-graph certification; claim can remain useful with declared outside-set limitation.

**external_inputs.**

- Domain-informed omitted-cause challenge set and empirical detection coverage for any exclusion.

**unresolved_research.**

Completeness is generally not provable globally; implement explicit set, missing-structure limits and justified bounds now.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-06
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Separate effect/equivalence bounds from structure; bind estimand, intervention, outcome, population, regime, horizon, margin, identification and lineage.
- **positive**: Target-average interval lies inside its margin while supported subgroup/path effects remain; report the scoped bound and retain the edge.
- **adversarial**: Promote a nonsignificant/near-zero average to structural zero despite cancellation or heterogeneity; reject.
- **removal**: Remove scope, regime, identification or evidence binding; bound loses qualified use, WMR edge/rivals remain.

- **task_id**: DES-12
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Report robustness against an explicit model-set version and scoped bound; never infer absence from negligible effect.
- **positive**: Admissibility and ranking hold throughout the bound across retained rivals; report only scoped robustness and retain edges.
- **adversarial**: Average effects cancel while subgroup/pathway/horizon ranking flips; withhold global robustness.
- **removal**: Remove bound or change rival/WMR version but keep its old declaration; recomputation loses robustness without deleting an edge.

- **task_id**: GYD-12
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Separate edge exclusion from effect estimation; require structural evidence plus identification for WMR removal, and persist bounds with exact scope.
- **positive**: A near-zero average with supported nonzero/heterogeneous paths keeps the edge; exclude only on structural and identification warrant at declared resolution.
- **adversarial**: Keep a near-zero interval/activation flag but remove structural warrant or identification support; reject edge deletion, absence stays not_established.
- **removal**: Remove structural/ID binding but keep a scoped effect bound; fresh consumer rejects deletion while retaining any independently supported bound.

**Основания выбора.** [S06-R02](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s06-r02), [S06-R03](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s06-r03), [S06-R04](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s06-r04)

## R18-D06-Q04. Один конструкт или смена измерительного режима

После изменения анкеты, языка, кода, связывания записей или стимулов к регистрации какие основания позволяют считать ряд измерением того же конструкта, а когда связь остаётся условной? Если политика меняет и состояние, и способ его регистрации, какие причинные утверждения возможны без проверенного моста между измерениями?

**Выбранное решение.**

A construct comparison across questionnaire, language, code, linkage or incentive regimes needs a bridge validated in the target population and period. Use paired/overlap measures, anchors or validated crosswalk; test invariance/DIF and propagate error. Without it, keep regime-specific outcomes and do not infer latent construct change; effect on reporting is a distinct estimand. A bridge does not prove transport.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S06-M3

**specific_steps.**

- Freeze instrument/schema versions and regime dates; define construct vs proxy.

- Validate pair-specific bridge on overlap, anchors or audited sample.

- Carry bridge error into estimate; split regimes if validation fails.

- Keep reporting and construct effects separate.

**authority_time_status.**

Bridge is version-, population-, construct- and time-specific; no retrospective equivalence from labels.

**external_inputs.**

- Instrument/code history, overlap validation, relevant subgroups/language, policy-linked reporting incentives.

**unresolved_research.**

Bridge adequacy is empirical; store tests and preserve split regime output when it is unknown.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-06
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Separate observed endpoint and latent construct; bridge cross-regime causal claims.
- **positive**: Target overlap validates bridge; effect includes bridge uncertainty.
- **adversarial**: Keep field name but change wording/incentive/code; equivalence fails.
- **removal**: Remove overlap evidence; combined construct claim becomes limited.

- **task_id**: CNT-08
- **role**: input collection
- **delta**: Persist regime, schema/code version, coverage, bias and bridge source.
- **positive**: Bridge applies only to matching versions and validated subgroup/time.
- **adversarial**: Change source version or target population while keeping bridge label; binding rejects.
- **removal**: Remove calibration content; fresh consumer cannot support bridge.

**Основания выбора.** [S06-R05](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s06-r05), [S06-R06](../../research/full-lifecycle-solutions-r19/sources.md#s06-r06)

## R18-D06-Q05. Селективно отсутствующие результаты исследований

Если реестр показывает существование подходящего исследования или результата, но тот не опубликован, недоступен или сообщён выборочно, какие данные об отборе допускают синтез эффекта или его границ? Когда вывод должен остаться условным или неразрешённым, а не трактоваться как нулевой эффект либо отсутствие свидетельств?

**Выбранное решение.**

Missing study/results are neither zero effect nor no evidence. Reconcile inception sources, protocol/SAP versions, planned/measured outcomes, result availability, reason and retrieval attempt; assess specific synthesis with ROB-ME. Use selection scenarios when denominator permits and bounds only under constrained missing mass/outcome range. Otherwise present conditional observed-results synthesis with unresolved selection, not an unqualified causal estimate.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S06-M4

- S06-M1

**specific_steps.**

- Build study-by-outcome matrix before pooling; preserve source versions.

- Request results and separate known not measured, unreported and unknown.

- Show selection scenarios; bound only under explicit supported assumptions.

- Expose denominator/limitations to consumer; direct acquisition where useful.

**authority_time_status.**

Completeness is relative to source boundary and cutoff; unknown reason remains unknown, prior snapshots immutable.

**external_inputs.**

- Registry/protocol/SAP/results, retrieval responses, eligibility/cutoff and outcome range if bounding.

**unresolved_research.**

Selection probabilities and unregistered studies are generally not identified from published outcomes alone; matrix, ROB-ME and scenarios remain useful.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-08
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Extend search boundary to result-level denominator, protocol versions and missing reasons.
- **positive**: Inception cohort reconciles each eligible result to report or explicit state.
- **adversarial**: Keep complete-search declaration but add eligible registry record without results.
- **removal**: Remove a result but leave completeness declaration; reconciliation catches mismatch.

- **task_id**: DES-12
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Use missing-result risk for acquisition/design; do not impute null.
- **positive**: Choose acquisition or show finite bounds when assumptions justify them.
