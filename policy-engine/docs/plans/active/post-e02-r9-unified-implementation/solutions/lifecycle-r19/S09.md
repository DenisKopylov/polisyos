## shared_contract

- **decision**: Развести внешнюю commission (только когда применима к conduct), protocol/site/ethics permits (только применимые), фактический conduct, returned evidence и action-specific current use rights. Read не дает analyze; analyze не дает derive/export/publish. Для secondary analysis не требовать нерелевантную commission/site permission: нужна собственная актуальная scoped authorization на фактические actions и все иные применимые approvals. Связывать exact source/data/artifact, competent issuer, scope, purpose, recipient, action, times, receipt and corrections.
- **authority_time_status**: Общий P37/P08: external status/reason — institutionally_supplied до resolve + content-bind + reconciliation; иначе not_established для protected claim. Разделять effective/observed, access/look, receipt/verification, analysis cutoff и reuse time; статусы source-native. Unknown может сопровождать candidate, но не authority claim.
- **runtime_status**: DES-12 R15/R17 product verification UNRUN. Static anchors подтверждают отдельные owners, не composed external-return → fresh-consumer chain; статус review: not_established_by_this_review. No external study executor, commission/fund, ethics approval, enrollment, conduct, slot reservation, registry or scheduler.

- **tradeoff**: Conflates actors, objects and scope/time.

Bind study/protocol/commission/permit only when that external act applies to the proposed conduct or use; do not require irrelevant commission for secondary analysis. Read is not analyze, and analyze is not derive/export/publish: verify the right for each performed action. Resolve content and verifier provenance; marker/hash alone is insufficient.

**flow.**

**migration.**

**free_grow.**

**resource_behavior.**

### S09-M4. Условный EVSI и зависимый feasible portfolio

**native_owner.**

DES-12 designs; DES-04 next-choice; DEB-13 selected numeric metric; CNT-07 external capacity; CNT-08 dependence; OPS-08 internal compute; DES-08 lawful learning.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/acquisition_planner.py
- **symbol**: plan_evidence_acquisition; run_acquisition_closed_loop
- **observation**: Existing acquisition path; DES-04 allows bounded nonnumeric route when EVSI unsupported.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/foundry/methods/catalog/causal/optimal_design.py
- **symbol**: CausalExperimentDesigner
- **observation**: Single-graph modes/defaults, not joint portfolio EVSI or external commitment.

**decision.**

Choices: permitted data reuse, measurement, audit/validation, observation or external-trial proposal (registry recruitment is a lead only). Gross EVSI=E_X[max_a E[U(a,θ)|X,S]]−max_a E[U(a,θ)|S], conditional on stated alternatives, utility, prior/uncertainty, design-specific observation/missingness law, target/horizon and version. Hypothetical/unqualified inputs permit a labeled conditional result; missing required inputs mean no number. DEB-13 uses exact finite sums plus oracle for small discrete cases; otherwise approximation/error. EVSI ≠ compute VOI. Cost/time/opportunity cost is required for ENBS/net or cost-based rank; sourced capacity/window/deadline for feasibility or resource-constrained rank, not gross EVSI. State whether delay is in utility. Compute joint V(P) and marginal V(P∪{i})−V(P), model dependence via CNT-08 or preserve bounds/partial order. OPS-08 covers compute. Show Pareto options; invent no weights.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Add entropy/standalone scores and sum them.
- **tradeoff**: Misses redundancy, complementarity, shared cohort and decision changes.

- **option**: Require numeric EVSI or add a portfolio scheduler.
- **tradeoff**: Contradicts DES-04 route and crosses external scheduling boundary.

**contract.**

DEB-13 binds gross metric, decision/utility, prior/uncertainty, observation/missingness law, target/horizon, provenance, error and rule version. Bind cost/time basis only for net/ENBS or cost-based rank; bind sourced capacity/window/deadline only for feasibility/resource-constrained rank. Joint gross value binds dependence. External facts require reconciliation; a score authorizes no conduct or slot.

**flow.**

- DES-12 proposes under explicit assumptions.

- CAP resolves rights; CNT-07 supplies feasibility capacity; CNT-08 supplies overlap/dependence.

- DEB-13 computes qualified metric; DES-04 persists chosen/unrun/pruned options.

- OPS-08 meters compute; fresh view separates conditional gross EVSI, net value, feasibility and unknowns.

**migration.**

Do not reinterpret compute-VOI or old isolated scores as scientific EVSI; new returns update a new portfolio basis.

**free_grow.**

Use current method registries; no new scheduler/institution registry. New objective/dependence needs owner-specific rule.

**resource_behavior.**

Bound portfolio set; exact-enumerate small cases, otherwise report controlled approximation/error and pruned frontier. OPS reservations cover compute only.

## R18-D09-Q01. Комиссия, разрешение на conduct и права данных

Какие внешние свидетельства различают commissioning/acceptance исследования, разрешение на конкретную версию протокола и права на use возвращённых данных, если commissioner/sponsor, разрешающий орган, площадка и хранитель данных различаются либо предложение принято частично?

**Выбранное решение.**

Комиссия PolicyOS не есть внешняя study commission. Для commissioned primary conduct связывать принятую область commission, применимые protocol/site/ethics permits, фактического actor и текущие rights на конкретное действие. Для secondary analysis не требовать нерелевантную commission/site permit; нужна собственная актуальная scoped authorization на analyze (и отдельный derive/export/publish, если эти действия выполняются), плюс иные применимые требования. Read не включает analyze; analyze не включает derive/export/publish. Отзыв ограничивает future conduct/use, не стирая историю. Candidate design или analysis неп protected inputs с declared unknown допустим; analysis protected data и соответствующие derived outputs остаются ограничены до проверенного права.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S09-M1

**specific_steps.**

**unresolved_research.**

No single legal form or competent actor applies across selected jurisdictions and research types. Preserve the exact external requirement per case; do not invent it.

## R18-D09-Q03. Нет результата, нулевой эффект и незавершённое исследование

## R18-D09-Q04. Портфель исследований и общие ресурсы

Как сравнивать портфель исследований, если предложения делят площадку, когорту, команду, инструмент или короткое окно сбора данных либо используют общий источник? Как учитывать взаимодополнение/вытеснение, зависимые результаты и разные decision deadlines, не складывая индивидуальные EVSI и не считая общую информацию дважды?

**Выбранное решение.**

Считать conditional gross EVSI joint portfolio, не сумму standalone scores: V(P)=E_X[max_a E[U(a,θ)|X,S]]−max_a E[U(a,θ)|S], marginal i=V(P∪{i})−V(P). It needs stated alternatives, utility, prior/uncertainty and observation law; hypothetical/unqualified inputs yield labeled conditional value, not policy value, and no required model input means no number. Cost/time/opportunity cost are for ENBS/net or cost-based rank; sourced capacity/window/deadline for feasibility/resource-constrained rank. Delay counts only if modeled in utility. ## R18-D09-Q05. Полнота внешнего набора study–report–outcome
