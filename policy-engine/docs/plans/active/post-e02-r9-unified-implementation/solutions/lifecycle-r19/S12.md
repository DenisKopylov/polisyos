**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: docs/adr/0171-review-effectiveness-telemetry-advisory-first.md
- **symbol**: Decision; Tuned Parameter
- **observation**: Thresholds stay advisory absent governed maturity and longitudinal evidence.

**decision.**

Estimate two different effects without calling both “before/after an independent judgment.” For post-judgment revision, record a judgment before any advice, then randomize matched cases/participants to advice revealed versus withheld and record the final judgment. For advice-first behavior, randomize a separate advice-first versus no-advice arm; its first response is post-exposure and is never labeled independent. Use a fixed independent oracle and correct, plausible-wrong, and contradiction cases. Prefer parallel randomized arms for the primary estimate; repeated within-person cases must be counterbalanced, record prior exposures, and treat learning/carryover as a limitation or sensitivity. No individual score or universal cutoff.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Use find time, readability, WCAG, agreement, no-change, or confidence as a comprehension proxy
- **tradeoff**: These can conceal misunderstanding or wrong acceptance.

- **option**: Apply one sample size, threshold, or forced interaction to every use
- **tradeoff**: Audience/risk and intervention effects vary; no global cutoff is established.

**contract.**

Study record binds task/source digest, audience/use/risk, edition, locale/modality/model, advice content and exposure order, randomization unit/arm, matched-case basis, prior-exposure history, carryover plan, frame/preregistration/exclusions, support context, oracle/answer, six INT-R3 metrics, uncertainty, evaluator provenance and rights. Results are descriptive for that slice, not policy authority. Existing human_review.py covers different events. C07 has no generic comprehension evidence kind; do not relabel manual-AT evidence.

**flow.**

- DES-10 freezes the real or controlled cases, independent oracle, audience frame, estimand, randomization arms and carryover handling; CNT-09 supplies selected real participants/permissions for authentic-use claims.

- For the revision estimand, capture a pre-advice judgment before randomizing advice/withheld control. For advice-first, use a distinct randomized arm with no claimed independent first judgment. Record every exposure and response against the exact edition.

- The authorized producer records minimal outcomes; the evidence owner verifies rights/content binding; INT-R3 consumes a versioned profile.

- A fresh consumer reports error, uncertainty, burden and carryover limits by slice; FINAL-USE admits evidence only for a matching first-use audience. Missing oracle, frame or actual participants remains not_established.

**migration.**

Version INT-R3 through its owner; retain six metrics/four conditions and current review telemetry. Without a generic human evidence path, authentic comprehension remains UNRUN.

**free_grow.**

Add selected audience/task/locale strata as profile data. New authority consequences need an authorized rule; no new human role or trust score.

**resource_behavior.**

Use bounded frozen tasks; report denominators, exclusions and uncertainty. Minimize personal data; never collect hidden chain-of-thought.

## R18-D12-Q02. Осознанное согласие или автоматизационное смещение

Какие наблюдения позволяют отличить обоснованное согласие рецензента с кандидатной рекомендацией от rubber-stamping или automation bias, особенно после показа уверенного LLM-резюме; и как вывод ограничивается конкретным reviewer/task/model profile?

**Выбранное решение.**

Define separate randomized estimands. For advice-driven revision, first record the participant’s judgment before any advice; then randomize matched cases/participants to reveal the bound advice or withhold it and record the final judgment. For advice-first behavior, use a separate randomized advice-first versus no-advice arm; its first answer is post-exposure, not an independent baseline. Balance correct, plausible-wrong and contradictory advice against a fixed independent oracle. Measure false acceptance, missed contradiction, false rejection, confidence, correctness and burden. Use parallel arms for the primary estimate; if participants see repeated cases, counterbalance order, bind prior exposure and report learning/carryover sensitivity or limit inference to the first period. Agreement, speed or no-change alone cannot diagnose rubber-stamping; do not score individuals or promote candidate summaries.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S12-M1

**specific_steps.**

- Bind actual advice/model context, oracle, randomization unit, case assignment and exposure timeline before data collection.

- Separate pre-judgment→randomized reveal/withhold from the distinct advice-first arm; log order and prior exposure.

- Compare correctness-conditioned errors, uncertainty and burden; where carryover cannot be separated, limit the claim and keep thresholds advisory.

**authority_time_status.**

Behavior is scoped to shown model/context/task; LLM stays candidate.

**external_inputs.**

- Selected participants and actual model profile where claimed

- Independent correctness labels

**unresolved_research.**

Advice effects and cognitive burden remain empirical for each selected task/profile/audience. Estimate first-decision and revision effects separately; repeated-exposure carryover may be bounded by parallel arms or reported as a limitation. No universal interaction, sample size or cutoff.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: DES-10
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Define the two estimands and randomized arms: pre-advice judgment followed by randomized reveal/withhold for revision; a separate advice-first arm for first-decision effects. Bind matched-case assignment, order, prior exposures and carryover analysis.
- **positive**: In the revision arm, the independent judgment is recorded before advice; randomized reveal versus control changes only the advice exposure, and the fixed oracle supports correctness-conditioned estimates.
- **adversarial**: An advice-first response is labeled independent, or repeated advice exposure contaminates the control while no carryover is recorded; the affected estimate is not established.
- **removal**: Remove timing/arm/exposure records while keeping accuracy labels; DES-10 cannot claim an advice effect.

- **task_id**: DES-09
- **role**: input collection
- **delta**: Supply the actual provider/model/profile, prompt/context selection and exact advice presented for each randomized case; record no-call/withheld conditions distinctly from unobserved advice.
- **positive**: Displayed advice and participant decision join to the actual call/context and edition.
- **adversarial**: A model/profile label is present but the advice bytes/context differ or no call occurred; attribution fails.
- **removal**: Remove actual invocation/context binding while retaining an advice label; the study loses its exposure claim.

- **task_id**: CNT-09
- **role**: input collection
- **delta**: Supply the commissioned audience/frame, participant permissions and work/support context; authentic frontline claims require selected frontline participants, not expert substitution.
- **positive**: The report names who was sampled, task context and applicable limits.
- **adversarial**: Reviewer-only or convenience participants are presented as frontline behavior.
- **removal**: Remove actual audience/permission evidence; authentic-use inference becomes not_established while controlled mechanics remain usable.

- **delta**: Admit only outcomes matching the actual audience, task, model/context, advice-order estimand and support profile; no general staff competence or trust claim.
- **positive**: A matching bounded result informs only its selected first-use claim.
- **adversarial**: A result from another model, task, audience or exposure sequence is reused as a general threshold.
- **removal**: Remove the bound profile/arm result but keep the summary; first-use admission refuses that inference.

## R18-D12-Q05. Понимание реакции на исправление или несогласие

Когда человек оспаривает перевод, отображение evidence, interpretation или value choice, как он может понять, изменила ли PolicyOS собственное затронутое утверждение, сохранила ли несогласие как отдельную позицию или ждёт внешнего институционального решения, не воспринимая отсутствие нового ответа как разрешение спора?

UI-04 appends the attributed correction/dissent against the exact claim edition; receipt, volume or informal comment never adopts it. LIF-02 checks whether the corrected source changes an enrolled material dependency and updates only affected current use before recomputation; DEB-06 issues a successor epoch only when the native claim needs one. UI-01/UI-02/UI-04 fresh selected views show the reaction while preserving the old as-known edition and dissent. External adjudication remains external. DES-10 tests that readers distinguish the event from the current claim state.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S12-M5

**specific_steps.**

- Bind event and source correction to exact prior claim edition, permission, authority and separate time roles.

- Have LIF-02 evaluate the actual enrolled dependency/content effect; do not infer materiality from event kind or automatically adopt dissent.

**authority_time_status.**

Event authority/time and PolicyOS reaction are separate.

**external_inputs.**

- Actual correction/act and permission where claimed

- Native claim owner and valid affected-claim link

**unresolved_research.**

Reader understanding under real response delays remains empirical. External adjudication and issuer decisions remain external; the system can immediately limit only its own affected current claims through LIF-02.

## R18-D12-Q06. Различение неизвестного и скрытого по правам

Evidence state is source/scope/time-bound; access is audience-bound.

**external_inputs.**
