## Повторно используемые механизмы

### S13-M1. Внешнее решение, исполнение и эффект — раздельные evidence bands

**native_owner.**

CAP-05 legal relation; CAP-04 source events; LIF-02 claim currentness; CNT-09 selected consumer; LIF-09 existing frozen composition assurance only. GYD-12 only O1-qualified causal writer; UI-04/UI-19 projection. OPS-11 — только собственная succession PolicyOS.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/scientist/validation/decision_validity.py
- **symbol**: record_dependency_event; read_current_projection
- **observation**: Static: dependency event/current projection/pending overlay; не удостоверяет внешний source.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/fabric/world/events.py
- **symbol**: persist_world_event_with_facts
- **observation**: Static persistence primitive, не source verifier/activation authority.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/world_model_record.py
- **symbol**: persist_world_model_record; load_world_model_record
- **observation**: Static typed WMR CAS lineage; не приравнивает publication, execution и effect.

**decision.**

Связать actual instrument/source с issuer/custodian, verifier basis, authority purpose, permitted use, jurisdiction, cohort/site и episode. Раздельно хранить receipt/known-at, issued, valid/effective, observed, verified, corrected times и proposed/adopted/executed/coverage/feedback status. Получение ≠ adoption; adoption ≠ effectiveness; legal effect ≠ operation; observation ≠ causality. Неизвестное остаётся declared unknown или limitation конкретной claim, без newest-wins/global hold.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Один current/adopted/executed статус по последнему файлу
- **tradeoff**: Скрывает effective date, partial coverage, conflicting sources и knowledge cutoff.

- **option**: Карточка учреждения или подпись как единое доказательство
- **tradeoff**: Не подтверждает competence, applicability или фактическую работу.

**contract.**

Existing typed artifact/event + schema/rule version; content-bound source identity/verifier provenance; exact claim/edition relation; scope, purpose, rights, time roles, status and gaps. External act и наша реакция — разные записи; institution-supplied факт не становится independently reconciled от label. For a selected CAP-04 path, the existing LIF-09 receipt binds the actual source/custodian, emitted event or correction and cut, exact LIF-02 enrolled predicate/claim scope, replay, and fresh-reader closure. Reuse is valid only for the same path identity and predicate (including consumer closure); a different path or predicate needs its own composed proof. This is scoped evidence reuse, not a new owner or global gate.

**flow.**

- CAP-05 admits actual legal act/competence/applicability; CAP-04 records only source-native operation, exposure or change; receipt stays receipt.

- Persist originals/corrections append-only with exact source, cut, parent and time roles.

- LIF-02 replays enrolled dependency and limits only affected claim; heavy recomputation may follow.

- CNT-09 and selected fresh UI-19 reader consume the qualified evidence/currentness cut; check affected and unaffected projections. GYD-12 writes only under current O1 rules.

- Allowed UI/audit surface distinguishes own publication, external act, observed execution, contradiction and own correction; external notices/acknowledgements remain external.

**migration.**

Legacy signature/card/publication is not proof of adoption/effect/execution. Preserve as known-at evidence with unbound relation; append corrections and invalidate affected claims only.

**free_grow.**

New source may add versioned adapter/verifier; instances/scopes are data. New authority relation needs owner, evidence, consumer and witness, not closed country templates.

**resource_behavior.**

Use exact refs/cuts and explicit coverage denominators. Empty inbox ≠ source silence; no bulk record retrieval implied.

### S13-M2. Ручная производная — отдельный artifact без унаследованной authority

**native_owner.**

DES-07 import/revision with CAS/claim lineage; UI-02 corrects own bytes; UI-04 projects linked events. Recipient institution owns downstream purpose/record status.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/http/services/bureaucratic_rendering.py
- **symbol**: render_document; export_document
- **observation**: Static export includes packet/hash, template version and draft mark; does not prove receipt, re-import, fidelity or external use.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/http/routes/artifacts.py
- **symbol**: render_bureaucratic_artifact; export_bureaucratic_artifact
- **observation**: Static access/integrity/audit checks; no external-copy inventory.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/core/artifacts/store.py
- **symbol**: FileSystemCAS.get_bytes; get_manifest
- **observation**: Static content/manifest identity; digest proves byte relation, not user or semantic fidelity.

**decision.**

DES-07 is smallest owner for an actually re-supplied DOCX/slide/table/note derived from a PolicyOS edition. Retain exact bytes/ref, parent, established source, rights and transformation. Align stable spans; route material/unmapped text to native owner or explicit human mapping; unbound paraphrase stays candidate. A changed external derivative alone marks that derivative changed/unbound; it does not automatically downgrade the parent claim. LIF-02 reacts only to an admitted source/own-assessment change that changes an enrolled premise. External adoption remains external. Correct managed PolicyOS surfaces only; unsupplied/offline copies remain unknown, with no recall/inventory claim.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Compare title/hash only
- **tradeoff**: Cannot map paraphrase or detect lost caveat/scope.

- **option**: Assume downloads remain aligned or promise recall
- **tradeoff**: Unmanaged copies cannot be proven current or remotely retracted.

- **option**: Block all manual reuse
- **tradeoff**: Closes useful advisory use; unknown edits can remain bounded candidates.

**contract.**

Input: permitted carrier/ref, parent edition, established provenance/time, purpose/audience/rights and transformation. Output: immutable derivative, exact mapped/changed/unmapped spans and fresh derivative/affected-claim projection. Hash/receipt/copied signature does not infer author, semantic equivalence, adoption or recall.

**flow.**

- DES-07 receives real supplied carrier under existing rights/source checks; imported content is not executed.

- Persist distinct CAS artifact with parent; align unchanged spans and mark semantic deltas.

- Route material changes to native owner/human mapping. LIF-02 changes only the enrolled claim whose premise an admitted source event or qualified own assessment changes; external derivative text alone does not trigger global downgrade.

- Fresh UI-19/API reader resolves current owner projection plus derivative mapping: changed/unbound derivative is visible; an admitted event limits only affected claims. Preserve old signed bytes as history, not present basis.

- UI-02/UI-04 correct linked managed surfaces; dissemination and unsupplied offline copies remain unknown absent recipient evidence.

**migration.**

Old exports keep parent ref but are not retroactively treated as read/adopted/fidelity-checked; no implicit re-verification. External retention law remains with institution.

**free_grow.**

Add carrier adapters to DES-07 under versioned contracts; no per-recipient workflow or new authority class.

**resource_behavior.**

Only permitted bytes/refs and material delta under import/CAS/rights limits; no copy crawl or assumed retention.

### S13-M3. Ранний contested report меняет claim, но не adjudicates harm

**native_owner.**

CNT-07 permissioned context/report; CAP-04 actual source event; LIF-02 scoped reaction; UI-04 history; CNT-09/GYD-09/10 methods. GYD-12 only O1-confirmed world update.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/scientist/evidence/claims/lifecycle.py
- **symbol**: append_lifecycle_event; validate_claim_transition
- **observation**: Static append-only reasoned transitions; no harm adjudication.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/http/routes/governed_projections.py
- **symbol**: get_governed_projection
- **observation**: Static replay-pinned consumer; not report producer.

**decision.**

Capture report as fact that it was made, with source, scope, time and consent; separately qualify event, causality and external outcome. If it contradicts a narrow claim such as no reports, LIF-02 limits that claim immediately; no need to await adjudication. Report alone proves neither occurrence, incidence, causation nor legal finding. Keep duplicates/conflicts linked; no newest-wins or count-as-independent.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Wait for final external case before any reaction
- **tradeoff**: Leaves a no-report guarantee current after receipt changes its basis.

- **option**: Treat every report as established harm
- **tradeoff**: Confuses allegation, event and causality; FDA guidance cautions against it.

- **option**: Suppress unresolved reports
- **tradeoff**: Loses an early signal and can falsify a claim about what was known.

**contract.**

Bind report/content or lawful ref, reporter/source/verifier provenance, asserted vs checked fields, claim/edition/exposure scope, supported reported/occurred/received/known/verified/corrected times, duplicates/conflicts, permission/audience and proceeding refs. Keep own reaction separate; no universal harm enum.

**flow.**

- CNT-07 accepts permitted account or CAP-04 emits actual source event; distinguish self-report from verified event.

- Append corrections/conflicts with source/parent/time; deduplicate only with evidence of relation.

- LIF-02 limits claims whose enrolled premise is contradicted; unaffected claims remain usable.

- CNT-09/GYD-09/10 use selected method/denominator; only qualified O1 result reaches GYD-12.

- UI-04 shows permitted account, external reply/outcome and own reaction separately; investigation/remedy remain external.

**migration.**

Legacy counts without source/time/binding stay limited; no causal backfill. Preserve prior as-known view and append a reaction if current basis changes.

**free_grow.**

New harm source uses its verifier/method; common lifecycle remains provenance, dependency, status/time and scoped reaction.

**resource_behavior.**

Capture enough for timely claim reaction; detailed investigation/study remains with external body/method. Limit only dependent claims.

**source_ids.**

- S13-R04

- S13-R05

## R18-D22-Q01. Что доказывает переход от рекомендации к реальному использованию?

Для выбранного учреждения какие внешние свидетельства различают получение рекомендации, решение о принятии, вступление меры в силу, фактическое исполнение/охват и обратную связь? Как связать их с точной редакцией, областью и ограничениями PolicyOS, отличая событие, меняющее обоснованность наших утверждений, от внешнего факта о решении учреждения?

**Выбранное решение.**

Связать receipt, adoption decision, legal effectiveness, actual operation/coverage и feedback с точной edition/scope, но не смешивать authority bands. Receipt доказывает получение; компетентный акт/applicability — только decision/effect; запуск и охват требуют source-native operational record; outcome/attribution — отдельной меры/design. Путь CAP-05/CAP-04 → LIF-02 → CNT-09; для выбранного реального CAP-04 source path LIF-09 даёт frozen receipt, повторно применимый лишь к той же path identity и predicate; источники не выбирают канал для неуказанной организации.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S13-M1

**external_inputs.**

- Actual matter/body, target scope, audience and permitted use.

- Competent instrument for adoption/effect; source-native operation/coverage for execution; feedback method only if outcome claimed.

- Source custodian, rights, correction route and conflicting/late acts; missing inputs explicit.

**unresolved_research.**

Организация, система, кейс и channel не выбраны; CNT-01 должен сделать это до source adapter. Bounded candidate/limitation возможны сейчас.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-01
- **role**: input collection
- **delta**: Комиссионно выбрать реальный matter/source/answerer и allowed use.
- **positive**: До protected use bound реальная запись и competent answerer.
- **adversarial**: URL или generic card выдается за commissioned act.
- **removal**: Убрать source/scope relation при labels: consumer не подтверждает act.

- **task_id**: CAP-05
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Проверить content, issuer competence, jurisdiction и effective scope actual act.
- **positive**: Fresh consumer меняет только scope/time, поддержанные актом.
- **adversarial**: Дата публикации/подпись или newest arrival равны effect.
- **removal**: Убрать competence/content check при labels: CNT-09 отказывает.

- **task_id**: CAP-04
- **role**: producer
- **delta**: Связать custodian/source cut с operational/exposure measure отдельно от law.
- **positive**: Source record меняет только matching cohort/site/period.
- **adversarial**: Announcement, empty feed или unmatched aggregate равны execution.
- **removal**: Убрать scope/dependency, оставить executed badge: LIF-02 ловит stale green.

- **task_id**: LIF-02
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Реагировать на enrolled event/correction до тяжёлой переоценки.
- **positive**: Меняется affected claim, независимые claims доступны.
- **adversarial**: Late correction/pending batch оставляет unsupported current.
- **removal**: Убрать event-to-claim при сохранённом event: fresh read ошибочно зелёный.

- **task_id**: CNT-09
- **role**: assurance
- **delta**: Witness различие receipt/adoption/effect/execution/coverage/feedback.
- **positive**: Реальный bounded chain даёт useful status или точную limitation.
- **adversarial**: Receipt=adoption, execution=causality или fixture=authentic use.
- **removal**: Убрать source/time edge при labels/counts: witness выявляет unsupported band.

- **task_id**: FINAL-USE
- **role**: assurance
- **delta**: Сверить authority, operations, rights, audience и claim limits на use date.
- **positive**: Dossier даёт exact scope/bands и human authorization.
- **adversarial**: Внешний пробел исчезает через post-hoc scope narrowing.
- **removal**: Удалить source disposition при totals: reconciliation fails.

- **task_id**: LIF-09
- **role**: assurance
- **delta**: Bind LIF-09 receipt to the exact CAP-04 source/custodian, emitted event/cut, Q01 operation/coverage predicate and fresh-reader closure; reuse only for this same path and predicate.
- **positive**: Selected operation record/correction reaches LIF-02; receipt binds the exact tuple and affected/unaffected fresh projections.
- **adversarial**: Reuse Q04 capacity or Q05 report receipt for Q01, or reuse a same-source receipt for a different predicate.
- **removal**: Remove path/predicate identity while keeping the LIF-09 marker: Q01 composed acceptance fails.

## R18-D22-Q03. Как связать ручную передачу с исходной редакцией?

Если результат вручную перенесен или пересказан в записке, таблице, презентации либо системе учреждения, как установить, продолжает ли производное содержание представлять подписанную редакцию PolicyOS, было ли существенно изменено и какие заявления об использовании принадлежат внешнему учреждению? Где заканчивается наша проекция и начинается внешнее утверждение?

**Выбранное решение.**

Ручная записка/таблица/слайд — отдельная derived revision, связанная с parent только через content-bound provenance/mapping. DES-07 принимает разрешённый supplied carrier; hash подтверждает bytes, не fidelity caveat/scope или external use. Удалённая caveat делает сам derivative changed/unbound. Это само по себе не понижает parent claim: LIF-02 реагирует только на admitted source event/qualified own assessment, меняющий enrolled premise. UI-19 читает свежий owner projection; исправляются managed copies, неподанные offline copies неизвестны, recall не обещается.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S13-M2

**external_inputs.**

- Actual permitted carrier/ref, parent edition, established provenance/time, rights and audience.

- If claiming institutional use/adoption, actual source-native response/decision; export log/download is insufficient.

- If the underlying PolicyOS claim is said to change, the admitted source event/qualified assessment and exact enrolled premise; the derivative alone is not that evidence.

**unresolved_research.**

No real carrier/route selected. DES-07 can represent supplied bytes and uncertainty; cannot establish unobserved copies or acknowledgements.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: DES-07
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Import actual carrier as distinct revision with parent, rights and mapped/changed/unmapped spans; dispatch material changes to the native owner.
- **positive**: Dropped caveat makes derivative changed/unbound; parent stays historical and is not downgraded from derivative text alone.
- **adversarial**: Copied title hides lost caveat; derivative-only edit is misread as an admitted source event or global stale trigger.
- **removal**: Remove semantic dispatch/derivative binding but keep hash/version: UI-19 fails to show changed/unbound derivative.

- **task_id**: LIF-02
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Consume only admitted source/qualified own-assessment changes to enrolled premises; derivative-only variance is not a parent-currentness event.
- **positive**: A linked admitted correction limits its dependent claim; a manually altered but unadmitted derivative alone leaves parent claim status unchanged.
- **adversarial**: Treat any imported prose delta as an event or keep a dependent claim green after an admitted correction.
- **removal**: Remove event-to-enrolled-premise binding while retaining the current marker: affected fresh read stays falsely green; remove derivative-only guard and parent is wrongly downgraded.

- **task_id**: UI-19
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Select the fresh current-status API/MACHINE projection; read LIF-02 owner status and derivative mapping, not CAS packet digest alone.
- **positive**: For a caveat-dropping supplied derivative show changed/unbound while old signed bytes remain historical; for an admitted correction show only affected claims limited and unrelated claims current.
- **adversarial**: CAS bytes/digest and positive badge survive pending LIF-02 change or mapped derivative loses a caveat.
- **removal**: Remove read_current_projection/currentness binding but keep digest/status marker: fresh reader must fail to return green.

- **task_id**: UI-02
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Keep exact issued bytes and link a managed correction/current-status projection; bind only evidenced managed dissemination. No recall claim for unknown copies.
- **positive**: Audience distinguishes historical signed original, changed derivative and current correction; no remote recall implied.
- **adversarial**: Correction implies recall or recipient adoption.
- **removal**: Remove exact-view link/audience gate but keep badge: export mismatches.

- **task_id**: UI-04
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Append actual response/correction to exact parent; separate informal reply, formal act and own reaction.
- **positive**: History identifies addressed edition and correction; old bytes remain immutable, receipt does not prove adoption.
- **adversarial**: Local memo appears as formal adoption/adjudication.
- **removal**: Remove event-parent join but keep markers: reader cannot reconstruct target edition.

- **task_id**: CNT-09
- **role**: assurance
- **delta**: Witness one permitted real derivative and mapping/copy limits.
- **positive**: Real supplied derivative changes its own status; fresh UI-19 shows only any independently affected claim change and leaves unrelated claims current.
- **adversarial**: Fixture/byte identity is offered as fidelity or adoption proof, or derivative text alone stales all parent claims.
- **removal**: Drop parent/change and current-projection check but keep hash: reviewer equates versions or serves stale positive status.

- **task_id**: FINAL-USE
- **role**: assurance
- **delta**: Include examined carrier/use and unknown dissemination limits in actual dossier.
- **positive**: Use states version/audience/period examined.
- **adversarial**: Unselected channel disappears despite all-audience claim.
- **removal**: Delete derivative disposition but keep count: complete-set reconciliation catches it.

## R18-D22-Q04. Какая преемственность важна для работы учреждения?

Если меняются люди, отвечающие за применение или наблюдение за выбранной мерой, какие внешние факты подтверждают изменение полномочий или операционной способности, а какие меняют только то, кто отвечает? Как отличать это от уже описанной преемственности ключей и обязанности PolicyOS поддерживать актуальность собственных подписей?

**Выбранное решение.**

Разделить смену лица/мандата от operating capacity, outputs/outcomes. CAP-05 admits appointment/mandate if authority is claimed; CNT-07/CAP-04 evidence resources/operation if feasibility is claimed; LIF-02 limits only dependent claim; if a selected CAP-04 source event path is used, LIF-09 supplies a proof bound to this exact source path and feasibility predicate; reuse requires an identical path/predicate. New name neither proves nor disproves capacity. OPS-11 is PolicyOS own trust succession, not external personnel.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S13-M1

**external_inputs.**

- Competent appointment/delegation source, effective date/remit if authority changes.

- Operator capacity/coverage basis with units, target, period and planned/committed/measured/executed status if feasibility claimed.

- Custodian and usage rights; absent facts leave feasibility conditional.

**unresolved_research.**

No organization, personnel, mandate or resource series selected; handoff impact is open. Claim-specific dependency can be built without institution-specific role model.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-07
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Separate mandate evidence from current inputs/execution; bind period, scope, source.
- **positive**: Authority transfers with stable capacity; lost coverage limits feasibility only.
- **adversarial**: New signature means capacity, or turnover means capacity loss.
- **removal**: Remove resource relation but retain actor labels: feasibility consumer refuses.

- **task_id**: CAP-05
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Verify mandate only when authority is a claim premise.
- **positive**: Admitted act updates only its institution/role/scope/time.
- **adversarial**: Roster/transferred file proves legal power.
- **removal**: Remove issuer/scope check with marker intact: protected reader refuses.

- **task_id**: CAP-04
- **role**: producer
- **delta**: Emit source-cut event for material operational/resource change; do not estimate workforce.
- **positive**: Supported delta reaches enrolled feasibility claim.
- **adversarial**: Projected budget, missing feed or new owner equals capacity.
- **removal**: Remove source binding but keep current: fresh reader exposes unsupported premise.

- **task_id**: LIF-02
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: React only where premise enrolled; answerer-only change may alter attribution, not global currentness.
- **positive**: Affected claim limited before rerun; unrelated claim current.
- **adversarial**: Any contact globally stales or no handoff can stale.
- **removal**: Remove dependency edge but retain event: claim falsely green.

- **task_id**: OPS-11
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Restrict succession/cutoff to PolicyOS trust root and own duty owner.
- **positive**: Own operator transition updates our custody/signature duties.
- **adversarial**: External staff change becomes our trust event or our approval proves their mandate.
- **removal**: Keep OPS marker but remove external typed evidence: external claim still denied.

- **task_id**: FINAL-USE
- **role**: assurance
- **delta**: Reconcile authority/capacity grounds at selected use.
- **positive**: Dossier separates accountability from admissible grounds/capacity.
- **adversarial**: Post-result handoff drops obligation or blocks unrelated claim.
- **removal**: Omit capacity disposition but retain totals: reconciliation fails.

- **task_id**: LIF-09
- **role**: assurance
- **delta**: For the selected CAP-04 resource/operation path, bind exact event/cut and Q04 feasibility predicate to the existing frozen receipt; reuse only for the same path and predicate.
- **positive**: Real supported resource change reaches LIF-02; receipt proves Q04 affected feasibility claim and unaffected fresh reads for this exact path.
- **adversarial**: Q01 operation/coverage or Q05 report receipt is reused for a different feasibility predicate, or a fixture stands in for a selected source.
- **removal**: Remove Q04 path/predicate binding but keep the composition marker: LIF-09 does not prove Q04.

## R18-D22-Q05. Как трактовать вред и несогласие до разрешения спора?

Когда поступает сообщение о вреде, жалоба, апелляция или профессиональное несогласие, а проверка еще идет либо стороны расходятся в причинах, что различает факт сообщения, подтвержденное событие, причинный вывод и итог внешнего разбирательства? Когда это меняет обоснованность наших утверждений, а когда остается внешним результатом?

**Выбранное решение.**

Принимать ранний report как факт сообщения и сразу ограничивать только claim, которой он противоречит (например, no reports by cutoff); не ждать внешнего adjudication для исправления этой гарантии. Report не доказывает occurrence/incidence/causality/legal finding. Сохранять duplicates/conflicts и времена; поздний метод может подтвердить event/effect; selected real CAP-04 report/correction path is composed through LIF-09 with this path’s exact report predicate; reuse requires identical path and predicate, not a local fixture alone. Redress, investigation, notice and ruling внешние.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S13-M3

**external_inputs.**

- Actual report/source, asserted occurrence/received time, affected claim/exposure scope, provenance and consent/rights.
