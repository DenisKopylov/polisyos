### S14-M0. Shared admission, authority and lifecycle contract

**native_owner.**

Reuse source-native CAP/CNT evidence, Claim dependency binding and LIF consumers; no new security subsystem.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/scientist/evidence/claims/head_index.py
- **symbol**: ClaimDependencyDenominatorResolver.resolve
- **observation**: Static: maps declared dependency paths only; not every runtime influence.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/scientist/validation/decision_validity.py
- **symbol**: record_dependency_event/read_current_projection
- **observation**: Static lifecycle owner; this report did not execute it.

**decision.**

Classify each claim by property. Fresh consumers may recompute a deterministic result from resolved content-bound inputs and pinned qualified method/build/evaluator/rule version; that proves only “correctly computed from these inputs.” Assess truth, issuer authority, scope and independence separately. Common ancestry limits corroboration, not recomputation. Freeze predicates as recomputed, independently_reconciled, consumer_asserted, institutionally_supplied or not_established; authority-grade passes require recomputed or independently reconciled support for the property at issue.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Trust a digest, signature, status string or field presence
- **tradeoff**: Tests form/identity and misses false content, wrong authority and incomplete influence.

**contract.**

Require resolve + content bind + verifier provenance + property-matched consumer check. A narrow derivation does not prove source truth/authority; unknown authority, scope or dependency stays limited.

**flow.**

- Source event/artifact → typed intake and provenance verification.

- Claim binding → property-specific recomputation/reconciliation → lifecycle/currentness → fresh consumer.

**migration.**

Missing source/execution/descendant links limit dependent scope; missing input/method binding bars a derivation claim.

**free_grow.**

New producers bind native evidence to the existing lifecycle.

**resource_behavior.**

Use existing dependency index and bounded replay; unknown scope stays limited.

## R18-D14-Q01. Доверие к производителям и внутренним ролям

Какие независимо проверяемые основания позволяют доверять происхождению и смыслу результата подписанного источника, модели или сборки, если один поставщик/администратор влияет на данные, исполняемый код, evaluator и подпись? Где доказательство идентичности артефакта перестаёт доказывать истинность его содержания?

**Выбранное решение.**

Separate provenance, deterministic derivation, source truth/authority and independent support. A fresh consumer may recompute a pinned transform from resolved content-bound inputs, proving only “correctly computed from these inputs.” Common administration limits input-truth and independence claims, not that narrow property. No universal external truth oracle is required; factual claims need their own grounds.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S14-M0

- S14-M1

**specific_steps.**

- Classify property; pin source/provider/build/evaluator and rule version.

- Resolve exact input, attempt and method.

- Recompute at fresh consumption; limit result to those inputs.

- Qualify truth, authority and independent support separately.

**authority_time_status.**

M0: recomputed transform is limited to bound inputs; truth, authority and independence need separate grounds. Keep source-effective, build and verification times distinct.

**external_inputs.**

- Bound source/provider and build record

- Competent source/issuer grounds only for factual or authority claims

- Independent evidence only when independent corroboration is claimed

- Competent trust-root succession evidence

**unresolved_research.**

Trust-root independence is profile-specific; absent proof limits that claim, not a valid local recomputation.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CAP-06
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Bind source/version, exact input, executable/build parameters, evaluator, rule version, receipt and attempt.
- **positive**: Fresh consumer reproduces the claimed transform from resolved bytes.
- **adversarial**: One admin controls data/code/evaluator/signer; hashes match while source may be false.
- **removal**: Drop input/method binding but retain hashes; GYD-28 cannot qualify derivation.

- **task_id**: GYD-28
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Resolve content/root and recompute the claimed property; keep source truth and authority separate.
- **positive**: Exact transform passes under shared admin; unsupported factual claims remain limited.
- **adversarial**: Valid manifest describes poisoned data; arithmetic still matches it.
- **removal**: Keep digest/signer but remove content or method binding; derivation fails.

- **task_id**: CNT-08
- **role**: assurance
- **delta**: Group source/evaluator ancestry for independence claims.
- **positive**: Common outputs count once; narrow recomputation still stands.
- **adversarial**: Two models repeat the same supplied data/evaluator.
- **removal**: Drop ancestry binding; independent-support claim fails although arithmetic may pass.

- **task_id**: OPS-11
- **role**: input collection
- **delta**: Qualify trust-root succession on a competent basis.
- **positive**: Compromised key cannot authorize its successor.
- **adversarial**: Same operator presents its replacement key as external.
- **removal**: Allow self-attested rotation; authority check fails.

## R18-D14-Q02. Дефект или компрометация производителя: какие claims затронуты?

Как независимо квалифицированный факт о correctness defect или compromise source custodian, provider/model, worker, connector, method либо executable связывается с затронутыми попытками, артефактами и собственными claims? Как неизвестный интервал и неодинаковое влияние ограничивают current use, новые вызовы и historical replay, не отзывая независимые выводы и не выдавая software update за институциональную смену политики?

**Выбранное решение.**

Pinned-build/input repro establishes a local defect without a second institution. Resolve enrolled execution/descendant impact and apply scoped currentness. Reissue only for material change to the owning signed PolicyOS claim, through DEB-06 corrected basis, expected prior head and issuer admission. Otherwise preserve the prior as-known issue; missing links limit dependent uses.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S14-M0

- S14-M2

**specific_steps.**

- Bind incident/repro to source, build, method and input; separate local defect from reported cause/interval.

- Resolve enrolled execution/descendant links; recompute impact and apply scoped currentness.

- No material claim change: preserve as-known issue and currentness scope; no epoch.

- Material signed change: DEB-06 appends only from corrected basis, expected head and issuer admission; retain prior.

- Limit uncovered scope; preserve unaffected supports.

**authority_time_status.**

Pinned repro is local evidence; cause/interval and enrolled impact are separate. DEB-06 epochs only a materially changed signed claim.

**external_inputs.**

- Incident report and pinned defect repro

- Invocation/version/descendant coverage

- Changed claim, corrected basis, expected head and issuer admission when reissue applies

**unresolved_research.**

Unknown execution coverage limits dependent impact even when the local defect is established.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Admit versioned incidents; exact pinned repro qualifies local defect.

- **adversarial**: Reported window exceeds repro; defect does not prove interval/cause.
- **removal**: Remove repro support; local defect fails and supplied interval cannot widen impact.

- **task_id**: LIF-02
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Apply event to enrolled claims at bounded replay; currentness only.
- **positive**: Affected claim limited; unaffected claim current.
- **adversarial**: Late or duplicate event meets missing/old enrollment.
- **removal**: Drop currentness read with marker; stale claim publishes.

- **task_id**: LIF-07
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Resolve execution and model descendants; gaps limit dependent impact.
- **positive**: Bad-version model use affected despite same source hash.
- **adversarial**: Dynamic influence omitted from lineage.
- **removal**: Drop execution link; non-impact cannot pass.

- **task_id**: DEB-06
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Append only for material owning-claim change; bind corrected basis, expected prior head and issuer admission.
- **positive**: Material change creates linked successor; prior issue remains as-known.

## R18-D14-Q03. Накопительное раскрытие через запросы и модели

Какие дальнейшие outputs/queries это ограничивает, если каждый отдельный ответ выглядит допустимым?

**Выбранное решение.**

At each real sink LIF-05 consumes current source policy (permitted non-DP operations, unit, purpose, audience/recipients, cumulative rule, version) with shared exposure. A bounded non-DP release passes only if policy permits; CAP-03/06 consume that outcome, never local counters. Hold uncertain possible releases. Budget/counter/attack PASS are not privacy guarantees. With no applicable policy, invent no threshold: retain private candidate only where rights allow and limit protected/public release. DP requires a valid selected mechanism and complete denominator.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S14-M0

- S14-M3

**specific_steps.**

- Resolve source-authorized operations, unit, purpose, recipients, cumulative rule and version.

- Persist actual calls, output/recipient and policy version.

- LIF-05 evaluates at every real sink; CAP-03/06 consume the same result on resume/cache/export.

- Unknown overlap or possible-release timeout stays held/exposed; no retry headroom.

- Bind descendants to source influence/current rights.

- Use probes for counterexamples only; freeze full mechanism/adjacency/output set for DP.

**authority_time_status.**

M0/M3: complete call history and rule evaluation are recomputed; source policy authority/content/version are separately qualified. Unknown policy/state holds the dependent release.

**external_inputs.**

- Current source-authorized rule and issuer, including operations, unit, purpose, recipients, cumulative rule and effective version

- Source-defined unit and alias/overlap relation

- Valid DP mechanism/adjacency only for formal DP claim

- Actual call/output receipts and recipient set

**unresolved_research.**

Auxiliary data and future queries prevent finite probes from proving no leakage. Missing policy or complete state limits dependent protected/public release without inventing a threshold.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **removal**: Remove policy/admission binding but keep green budget marker; actual sink holds.

- **task_id**: CAP-03
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Consume shared LIF-05 admission for source operations; local counts are not permission.
- **positive**: Bounded operation passes for its authorized purpose/audience.
- **adversarial**: Later overlap or changed purpose exceeds the source grant.
- **removal**: Drop admission read but keep counter badge; sink rejects/holds.

- **task_id**: CAP-06
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: On resume/cache/restore/export, resolve current LIF-05 outcome and policy version.
- **positive**: Resume shares the original exposure reservation/outcome.
- **adversarial**: Old cached success is replayed for a new recipient/version.
- **removal**: Keep budget marker but drop admission binding; sink stays closed.

- **task_id**: DES-09
- **role**: input collection

- **task_id**: LIF-07

## R18-D14-Q04. Consent, производное использование и отзыв

Если основание обработки — consent людей или представляемого сообщества, какие сведения компетентного источника подтверждают scope для чтения, обучения/памяти, производных моделей, агрегирования и публикации? Что меняется для каждого use после отзыва, с учётом исторической проверяемости и уже раскрытого внешнего результата?

**Выбранное решение.**

Consent is source-specific and operation-specific evidence, distinct from participation, representation, mandate and a processing basis. Bind competent issuer and notice version to collection, analysis, training, memory, derivative model, aggregation, publication, recipient and retention as applicable. Withdrawal stops covered future uses, rechecks descendants and triggers the source-specific deletion/retraining/correction rule; keep only retention that the competent basis permits. Do not promise universal unlearning or recall of downloaded copies.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S14-M0

- S14-M4

**specific_steps.**

- Resolve the authoritative notice and issuer/representation, subject and data scope.

- Bind allowed purposes/operations/recipients and jurisdiction to each use.

- On withdrawal, freeze new covered use and traverse known descendants.

- Apply the competent source’s retention/deletion or method-specific retraining rule.

- Link any public correction while stating the limits of external-copy control.

**authority_time_status.**

M0: source-specific consent/right authority remains qualified by its competent basis; preserve notice, withdrawal and use times.

**external_inputs.**

- Competent person/community/source issuer and notice version

- Applicable jurisdiction, lawful basis and retention exception

- Provider or recipient evidence of release/deletion/retraining

**unresolved_research.**

A prior release, third-party retention and exact model influence can be partly unobservable; state measured coverage, not universal erasure.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: CNT-07
- **role**: input collection
- **delta**: Keep source-issued consent, participation, representation and mandate as distinct evidence.
- **positive**: Only a competent operation-scoped permission is consumed.
- **adversarial**: A participant checkbox is treated as community representation and model-training authority.
- **removal**: Replace resolved notice/issuer with a consent string; authority admission fails.

- **task_id**: CAP-03
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Resolve current rights for read, analysis, training, reuse and publication purpose.
- **positive**: Granting data access alone does not enable a different operation.
- **adversarial**: Consent is withdrawn after original read but before model reuse.
- **removal**: Remove current rights check at reuse; stale derived artifact remains consumable.

- **task_id**: LIF-05
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Fence actual disclosure/restore at the sink using current rights and applicable source release decision.
- **positive**: Withdrawal or changed source rule holds pending/ambiguous release.
- **adversarial**: A remote call may have released output before timeout.
- **removal**: No shared current-rights/admission hold; retry emits after withdrawal or uncertain release.

- **task_id**: LIF-07

## R18-D14-Q05. Злоупотребление рекомендацией после adoption

Если adopter применяет допустимую policy-level рекомендацию для индивидуального скоринга, enforcement или иной цели вне audience/purpose, какие внешние evidence достаточно, чтобы изменить собственный scope/currentness PolicyOS claim? Где заканчивается evidence integration и начинается внешняя надзорная функция, особенно после выгрузки документа?

**Выбранное решение.**

An adopter’s out-of-scope use is external evidence to integrate only when it can change a PolicyOS claim about audience, purpose, currentness or representation. Keep an unsupported complaint as candidate evidence; reconcile actual use through a competent record, then issue an append-only scoped correction if the PolicyOS statement is affected. Do not infer compliant use from silence or take on a supervisory/enforcement role. A public correction can update PolicyOS’s own surface but cannot promise recall of external copies.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S14-M0

- S14-M5

**specific_steps.**

- Collect the reported use, exact policy edition, recipient, audience and occurrence/effective/receipt times.

- Classify evidence as allegation, institutionally supplied, independently reconciled or not established.

- Resolve which PolicyOS claims and public projections depend on that use.

- Append a scoped response/correction only where own claim validity changes.

- Report monitoring coverage and external-copy limits.

**authority_time_status.**

M0: complaint is candidate/institutionally supplied until competent reconciliation. Preserve occurrence, effective, received and publication times.

**external_inputs.**

- Adopter/recipient/affected person or regulator evidence

- Applicable external authority or rule defining the boundary

**unresolved_research.**

Post-adoption activity may be unobservable and complaints incomplete; no report never proves all uses remained in scope.
