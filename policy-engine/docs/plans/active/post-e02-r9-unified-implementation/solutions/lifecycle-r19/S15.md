## Повторно используемые механизмы

### S15-M1. Рост и оспаривание у существующего владельца смысла

**native_owner.**

GYD-32: supported-resource growth/replay; consumers: requiredness. GYD-33: Scholar/Lex owner binding. CAP-05: legal-source meaning only. No central steward.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/fabric/connectors/contracts/source_contract.py
- **symbol**: SourceContract, SourceContractSemantics, SourceContractTrust
- **observation**: Static model already carries versioned schema/semantic IDs, owner/reviewer, trust declarations and lineage. Those fields do not prove authority, semantic equality or runtime consumption.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/data_forge/domains/catalog/registry.py
- **symbol**: CatalogSourceRegistrySpec, CatalogSourceRegistryEntry, enabled_sources
- **observation**: Existing source/profile discovery seam; discovery does not prove meaning, local requiredness or authority.

- **path**: policy-engine/docs/system-design-decisions/universal-policy-design-target-architecture-and-gap.md
- **symbol**: D2.2, D2.3, D2.9
- **observation**: Target architecture references within a draft whose B-on-A north star is accepted. D2.2/D2.3/D2.9 mechanisms are not thereby ratified; exact applicable Vision/owner clauses govern implementation. No runtime delivery is claimed.

**decision.**

Use the ratified Vision §11 and the exact applicable owner contract. D2.3 supplies useful design hypotheses (unexpressible demand, stable new measurement meaning, overloaded construct, missed obligation), not a ratified exhaustive eligibility gate. One substantive counterexample can justify investigating an extension; repeated demand is not universally required. Same-meaning resources grow through owner registry and reader conformance. Changed meaning, inference, verifier, permission or claim authority needs a typed revision from its native owner. Challenge goes there with exact counterexample/scope; independent review only when its contract requires it. Missing competent owner => that predicate `not_established`; no novelty score/classifier.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Central semantic board or universal threshold
- **tradeoff**: Displaces native authority and serializes ordinary growth (P27/P13).

- **option**: Treat all new fields/profiles as config
- **tradeoff**: Misses unit, population, legal-scope and guarantee changes behind a stable schema (P05/P10).

- **option**: Fail all unknowns globally
- **tradeoff**: Blocks independent work; keep declared candidate analysis available instead.

**contract.**

Bind predecessor and candidate content/version, changed predicate, substantive extension rationale and applicable accepted owner rule, source/schema/rule/method, affected consumer/use, entity/population, unit, time role, jurisdiction, assumptions/rights as relevant. Owner emits versioned accept/reject/limited result with evidence, challenge, valid/known-at time and verifier provenance. Consumer derives requiredness from its own versioned contract and runs conformance. Authority is native-owner; require external appointment only when the owning contract requires institutional authority.

**flow.**

- Producer: owner emits delta, predecessor, ground and affected use.

- Persist: versioned content/lineage; retain predecessor.

- Bridge: GYD-32/33, LIF-08 or domain bridge resolves and runs real consumer conformance.

- Fresh consumer: same-class profiles need no per-resource code; unknown required meaning limits dependent claims.

- Surface: existing audit/API exposes versions, scope, reason, time, review and disposition.

**migration.**

Keep historic basis under its own rules. New revisions re-evaluate affected uses; never rewrite archive or backfill authority. Missing evidence remains candidate.

**free_grow.**

Same-class resources use existing discovery/readers. New primitive, measurement meaning, inference or authority contract stays domain-owned; unknown resources remain discoverable for candidate work.

**resource_behavior.**

Resolve proposal and indexed impacted consumers; reference payloads/manifests. Preserve complete denominators for coverage claims.

### S15-M2. Контекстные связи для конфликтов и чужих значений

**native_owner.**

Source/semantic owner supplies meaning; receiving consumer owns local compatibility/requiredness. Reuse ConstructOntologyDelta, SourceContract, LIF-08 and LIF-01; no master terminology.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/fabric/connectors/contracts/source_contract.py
- **symbol**: SourceContract.version, SourceContractSemantics, compatibility_status

- **observation**: Claim-level outcomes can express a scoped conflict; they do not prove the relation.

- **path**: policy-engine/docs/reference/ir/interoperability.md
- **symbol**: Existing interoperability contract
- **observation**: Use existing directional mappings and consumer binding; avoid a new cross-domain owner.

**decision.**

Compare exact source/target versions and consumer use across entity/population, measure/unit, time/interval, jurisdiction, method/verifier, assumptions and requiredness. Disjoint scope stays separate. Owner-backed equivalence is allowed only after lossless tests for named consumers. Otherwise preserve broader/narrower/inexact/related/unmatched direction and lost distinctions. Unresolved overlapping meanings stay contested only for dependent claims; independent/conditional results continue. Split only when evidence shows overloaded meaning used by affected consumers. No ID/schema inference, averaging, latest-wins or majority.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Canonical-ID/schema equality
- **tradeoff**: False merges survive when units, population, time or legal scope differ.

- **option**: Merge all valid owner assertions
- **tradeoff**: Conflates independently valid scopes and hides consumer-specific loss.

- **option**: Global conflict stop
- **tradeoff**: Suppresses claims independent of the disputed facet.

**contract.**

Bind source/target content versions, purpose/consumer, direction, context/boundaries, evidence/owner, conditions and validity. Output scoped relation, lost distinctions, unresolved predicates and fresh local result. Reverse compatibility is not inferred; foreign `publishable` remains origin-only.

**flow.**

- Producer: owner emits competing profiles or scoped map with evidence.

- Persist: preserve source roots/cuts; version owner mapping.

- Bridge: LIF-08 resolves receipt/source/reader generation and receiver contract.

- Fresh consumer: recompute local requiredness; optional unknown stays inert, required unknown limits use, conflicts affect dependent claims.

- Surface: separate origin assertion from local relation/rule/scope.

**migration.**

Keep foreign artifact and local projection separate; version directional maps and retain supported readers. Preserve cuts absent common-cut protocol.

**free_grow.**

New same-class sources add owner contract/mapping without central dispatch. New relation/guarantee needs adapter and consumer conformance.

**resource_behavior.**

Resolve selected indexed scope/facets; stream where supported. Never sample a completeness denominator.

### S15-M3. Внешняя компетенция связана с точным утверждением

**native_owner.**

External institutions own any institutional appointment they issue; no appointment is presumed for scientific sources/reviewers. Native owner verifies its predicate; LIF-01 binds use; CAP-05 handles legal competence only; OPS-11 root succession only.

**reuse_anchors.**

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/fabric/connectors/contracts/source_contract.py
- **symbol**: SourceContractTrust
- **observation**: Trust/calibration fields are declarations and cannot establish an appointment.

- **path**: policy-engine/src/polisyos/scientist/evidence/claims/models.py
- **symbol**: ClaimSupportStatus, ClaimPublishability
- **observation**: Local claim outcome is separate from issuer identity and verifier competence.

- **path**: policy-engine/docs/plans/active/post-e02-r9-unified-implementation/solutions/17-design-custody-and-succession.md
- **symbol**: OPS-11 trust-root succession
- **observation**: Root authenticity/currentness does not grant subject-matter authority.

**decision.**

Separate attribution/integrity, empirical validity, review-based method support, contract-required institutional authority and local disposition. Either recompute the exact property from bound inputs/method or rely on a review whose expertise and any required independence are evidenced to the native method owner’s scope-specific criteria; reviewer self-attestation is insufficient. Require appointment only when the owner/legal contract requires institutional authority. Missing qualification limits review-only promotion, not attributed candidate evidence or direct proof. Signature/trust tier proves neither truth nor competence; revocation affects dependent authority use.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: Signature or trust-root equals competence/validity
- **tradeoff**: Authenticity proves neither empirical truth nor mandate.

- **option**: Require institutional appointment for every external reviewer/resource
- **tradeoff**: Blocks ordinary attributable science and methodological review; confuses evidence with authority.

- **option**: Universal issuer/verifier roster
- **tradeoff**: Creates unbounded central authority and currentness burden.

- **option**: Reject every non-appointed scientific input
- **tradeoff**: Loses useful evidence; retain it as attributed candidate support without authority promotion.

**contract.**

Bind artifact/version, predicate/test/result, source/provenance, evidence/method, rule/schema, use/audience and valid/known-at times. For review-based support, include owner criteria and resolved expertise/independence evidence; self-assertion alone is insufficient. Add verified appointment/delegation only if contract requires institutional authority. Emit attribution, integrity, direct proof, reviewer qualification, authorization (when applicable) and local disposition separately; no global roster.

**flow.**

- Producer: source supplies attributable data/method; a reviewer supplies scope-relevant qualification evidence when review is relied on. Appointment is conditional.

- Persist: retain evidence/provenance and time/version in existing lineage.

- Bridge: native method owner recomputes the property or resolves reviewer expertise/required independence from evidence; LIF-01 binds result/use.

- Fresh consumer: admit only direct proof or contract-qualified review. Check mandate only when required; revocation affects dependent authority.

- Surface: show source, proof/review basis, qualification, any required mandate, time and limitation.

**migration.**

Preserve the basis and time of historical review/qualification; do not infer current expertise or authority. Missing qualification limits review-only support; revocation limits uses that rely on the mandate.

**free_grow.**

Existing owner contracts admit sources and reviewers. Scientific evidence can be attributed or directly tested without institution appointment; review qualification is owner-scoped, not a global credential registry.

**resource_behavior.**

Cache proof only for same content/method/scope and current qualification/evidence interval; refresh required revocation. No broad crawl.

## R18-D18-Q01. Разбор пограничного расширения по существующим критериям

Если конкретное расширение спорно классифицируется как conforming ресурс или изменение смысла, какие основания позволяют применить уже заданные критерии роста фасетов, новой inference guarantee, meaning verification, permission mechanism или claim authority? Кто рассматривает оспаривание и что допустимо в затронутой области до решения, не блокируя обычный free-grow и не требуя универсального семантического порога?

**Выбранное решение.**

Use the ratified Vision §11 and the exact applicable owner contract. D2.3 supplies useful design hypotheses (unexpressible demand, stable new measurement meaning, overloaded construct, missed obligation), not a ratified exhaustive eligibility gate. One substantive counterexample can justify investigating an extension; repeated demand is not universally required. Do not invent a universal classifier. Same-meaning field/URL/dataset/profile is resource growth. Typed extension is for evidenced new primitive/measurement, repeated unexpressible demand, overloaded construct or missed obligation; new inference, verifier, permission or claim authority changes its native contract. Challenge goes to that owner with a scoped counterexample. Until resolved, retain old protected meaning and allow declared candidate/conditional work; limit only dependent claims.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S15-M1

**specific_steps.**

- Name the predecessor, exact changed predicate and substantive need/counterexample; identify the applicable accepted owner rule. Draft examples are not a closed admission list. Without adequate grounds retain candidate scope; internal missing owner/bridge is assigned work.

- Same meaning: existing registry + real reader/consumer conformance. Changed predicate/authority: typed owner revision.

- Challenge binds version, criterion, scope and time; unresolved competent conflict stays contested, never majority/latest/LLM-selected.

- Positive path: new supported resource reaches fresh consumer. Removal probe: retain declaration, remove conformance, protected use fails.

**authority_time_status.**

Ратифицированное правило берётся из точного Vision/owner clause; D2.3 examples остаются проектными ориентирами, не blanket authority. Решение и authority принадлежат owner. Версии, valid-time и known-at остаются раздельны; нет назначенного владельца — только этот predicate `not_established`.

**external_inputs.**

- Для expert-identified obligation — конкретный экспертный counterexample и reviewer только в объёме существующего owner contract.

**unresolved_research.**

Различающий кейс должен показать конкретное существенное свойство/потребность, которое существующие facets/compositions не выражают корректно. Повторяемость спроса может обосновывать приоритет, но единичный material counterexample не отбрасывается; универсальная novelty-метрика и закрытый список оснований не нужны.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: GYD-32
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Apply the exact ratified Vision/native-owner rule to a real facet; use draft D2.3 examples as non-exhaustive design guidance, separating conforming resource growth from semantic delta without requiring repeated demand.
- **positive**: Fresh consumer discovers a new supported profile without central edit.
- **adversarial**: Same schema hides changed unit while semantic ID stays fixed.
- **removal**: Keep `conforming` marker, remove real conformance: protected use is denied.

- **task_id**: LIF-08
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Use SourceContract for same-meaning growth; adapter for new meaning.
- **positive**: Fresh reader finds supported new profile without per-resource branch.
- **adversarial**: Schema-valid source changes meaning or required facet becomes optional.
- **removal**: Keep status/schema, remove reader generation/requiredness: use fails.

- **task_id**: GYD-33
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Bind Scholar/Lex owner, consumer and time/source evidence.
- **positive**: Fresh downstream receives amendment as-of.
- **adversarial**: Incomplete denominator/effective date stays green on graph-row presence.
- **removal**: Keep graph row, remove source/bridge binding: support disappears.

- **task_id**: CAP-05
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Use only for legal source meaning/applicability.
- **positive**: Exact official version/scope supports matching claim.
- **adversarial**: Signature/URL with unknown competence/time claims legal force.
- **removal**: Keep official labels, remove applicability check: legal read limits.

- **task_id**: DES-02
- **role**: input collection
- **delta**: Keep unrepresented proposal as LLM/grammar candidate for native owner.
- **positive**: Research can inspect candidate; grounded proposal reaches evaluator.
- **adversarial**: LLM declares novelty/authority without evidence.
- **removal**: Keep diversity label, remove source firewall: protected consumer rejects.

## R18-D18-Q02. Сочетание конфликтующих расширений

Если два по отдельности conforming расширения перекрывают один фасет, mapping, verifier predicate, методическую гарантию или юрисдикционный scope, но задают несовместимые значения либо requiredness, какие содержательные основания позволяют считать их совместимыми, сохранить раздельными, объявить contested, объединить, разделить или ограничить затронутый вывод?

**Выбранное решение.**

Resolve only the overlap and selected consumer. Disjoint scope/time remains separate; lossless owner-backed equivalence can alias for tested consumers; otherwise preserve broader/narrower/inexact/related direction and lost distinctions. Unresolved overlapping meanings stay contested only for dependent claims. Split when evidence shows mixed units, populations, periods, jurisdictions or guarantees. Never merge by ID/schema, average, latest-win or globally block independent results.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S15-M2

**specific_steps.**

- Compare exact versions, predicate, consumer, context, entity/population, unit, time/jurisdiction and requiredness.

- Test relation for one use and direction; equivalence needs owner evidence.

- Change only the disputed boundary in a counterexample; output/limitation must change. Otherwise preserve branches and limit dependent claims.

- Version split/merge and reader matrix; retain hashes and separate source cuts.

**authority_time_status.**

Source owner утверждает исходное значение; receiving consumer решает local use. Effective interval и assessment/known-at не взаимозаменяемы; conflict остаётся source-attributed.

**external_inputs.**

- Нужны определения источника/consumer и, при споре, evidence владельца о units/population/effective scope; внешний legal act нужен только для правового вопроса.

**unresolved_research.**

На конкретном pair могут остаться эмпирические relation/consumer tests; до них сохранять направленные альтернативы, а не вводить общий resolver.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: GYD-32
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Use typed facet delta; consumer contract owns requiredness.
- **positive**: Overlap yields conditional contested branch; disjoint scope still works.
- **adversarial**: Same ID/schema, different unit/population/time is not merged.
- **removal**: Keep alias marker, remove owner/consumer binding: oracle catches false merge.

- **task_id**: GYD-33
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Propagate contested evidence/thresholds to existing readers.
- **positive**: Only disputed output limits; independent grounded result passes.
- **adversarial**: Latest arrival with different effective date wins.
- **removal**: Keep edge/status, remove propagation: fresh reader loses limitation.

- **task_id**: LIF-08
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Check directional compatibility, completeness and reader generation.
- **positive**: Unknown optional stays inert; mapped required facet appears.
- **adversarial**: Stable schema hides changed unit or required-to-optional downgrade.
- **removal**: Keep source ID, remove receiver check: selected read no longer admits.

- **task_id**: DES-03
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Keep partial order and non-dominated model/scenario alternatives.
- **positive**: Fresh frontier exposes alternatives and distinguishing study.
- **adversarial**: Surrogate score prunes a feasible model without dominance evidence.
- **removal**: Keep score, remove joint composition: oracle detects false dominance.

- **task_id**: CAP-05
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Apply source-specific legal authority to overlapping legal scope.
- **positive**: Partial effective scope preserves verified subset.
- **adversarial**: Draft/arrival order chooses law without competence.
- **removal**: Keep institution/version, remove competence/time check: claim limits.

## R18-D18-Q03. Доверие к внешним авторам и верификаторам

Какая связь между внешним учреждением, автором расширения, назначенным проверяющим/ratifier (если он требуется owning contract), предметом проверки, юрисдикцией и действующей делегацией позволяет PolicyOS опереться на семантическое заявление этого расширения в собственном выводе? Как меняется допустимость, если полномочия проверяющего оспорены, отозваны или конфликтуют с другим компетентным источником?

**Выбранное решение.**

Separate attribution/integrity, empirical validity, review-based method support, institutional authority and local admissibility. A consumer can directly recompute a method property from bound evidence without appointing an institution. If a review is relied on, qualify the reviewer’s relevant expertise and any contract-required independence using evidence resolved to the native method owner’s scope-specific criteria; self-attestation is insufficient. Require official appointment only where the owner/legal contract requires it. Missing review qualification limits review-only promotion, not attributed candidate evidence or direct proof.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S15-M3

**specific_steps.**

- Resolve exact evidence, method, artifact/version and time; directly recompute the claimed property where feasible.

- If relying on a reviewer, obtain owner-defined scope criteria and independently resolved evidence of relevant expertise and required independence/conflict controls. Do not accept reviewer self-assertion alone.

- Check institution appointment/delegation only when the owning contract requires authority. If review qualification is missing, retain attributed candidate evidence; do not use it as the sole promoted premise.

- Fresh reader reruns the method or re-resolves qualification, evidence version and applicable expiry/revocation.

**authority_time_status.**

Attribution, method result, review qualification, contract-required institutional authority and local disposition stay distinct. Direct evidence needs no appointment; review cannot self-qualify.

**external_inputs.**

- Provide method/data evidence. If review is relied on, resolve scope-relevant expertise and any required independence evidence under the native owner contract. Official appointment is needed only when that contract/legal rule requires it.

**unresolved_research.**

The native method owner must state what evidence qualifies a reviewer for this predicate and whether independence is required. Without it, review is attributed/candidate; direct recomputation remains available.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: LIF-01
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Bind result to exact predicate/rule/time/use; consume native-owner qualification evidence when review is relied on. Appointment is conditional.
- **positive**: Exact method property recomputes from bound inputs with no institution appointment.
- **adversarial**: Valid signature/scope and `qualified` label, but self-attested expertise or unresolved required conflict.
- **removal**: Keep signature/label; remove qualification evidence and review-only support fails. Direct proof remains usable if independently reproduced.

- **task_id**: GYD-32
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Native method/facet owner defines scope-specific reviewer qualification and required independence evidence; no global roster.
- **positive**: Fresh consumer resolves expertise/independence evidence for exact scope, or admits direct recomputation.
- **adversarial**: Reviewer self-validates credentials; no owner criterion/evidence, or conflict defeats required independence.
- **removal**: Retain marker/signature, remove resolved qualification evidence: review-only support downgrades; direct proof stands only on its own evidence.

- **task_id**: CAP-05
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Require legal source competence/applicability only for law; do not generalize to science.
- **positive**: Official competent act covers exact jurisdiction/effective scope.
- **adversarial**: Valid digest from noncompetent publisher has no legal force.
- **removal**: Keep official metadata, remove source relation: legal reader refuses.

- **task_id**: OPS-11
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Check root succession/currentness separately from subject-matter evidence or reviewer mandate.
- **positive**: Independent successor updates trust generation; history stays.
- **adversarial**: Root signs itself, rollback, or absent watch owner stays green.
- **removal**: Keep signature chain, remove succession/revocation: fresh reader refuses.

## R18-D18-Q04. Смысловая совместимость между экземплярами и юрисдикциями

Когда API или экспорт приносит в другую институцию метод, фасет, verifier, source relation или результат, чьи версии правил, статусы, таксономии и юрисдикционные границы отличаются от локальных, какие основания позволяют принимающей системе признать смысл эквивалентным либо частично переносимым для собственного вывода? Что остаётся допустимым, если такой связи пока нет?

**Выбранное решение.**

Foreign import carries bytes and origin assertions, not sender rules, authority or `publishable` status. Preserve exact source/contract/schema/rule, jurisdiction, scope, cut and times. Receiver binds a directional context-specific map and reruns local requiredness/admissibility. No reverse equality from ID/schema/key. Without a map, allowed transport, attributed reporting and independent local facets continue; dependent protected claim is candidate/limited. Keep independent source cuts separate absent a common-cut protocol.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S15-M2

- S15-M3

**specific_steps.**

- Resolve foreign artifact/profile/rule, status meaning, source authority and original times; keep origin and local claims separate.

- For selected use, bind directional map, context, conditions, evidence, validity and reviewer. Unknown optional fields stay inert; required fields are limited.

- Receiver recomputes local rule, applicability, audience and status. Foreign `publishable` is origin-only.

- Surface origin, mapping and local result separately. With no map, keep attributed/candidate path; do not relabel meaning.

**authority_time_status.**

Receiver emits local authority/admissibility only; foreign status remains attributed history. Preserve source rule, mapping, cut, valid/effective, assessment and known-at times.

**external_inputs.**

- Источник поставляет authentic artifact/profile/rule and its own authority/time facts; receiver institution — local rule and official appointment/jurisdiction facts where required.

**unresolved_research.**

Для конкретной foreign/local pair определить mapping strength по definitions, boundary probes и selected use. До этого preserve provenance and independent facets; dependent claims remain limited.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: LIF-08
- **role**: primary writer
- **delta**: Bind source receipt/cut, reader generation, requiredness and directional map.
- **positive**: Mapped facet reads with scope; unmapped optional stays inert.
- **adversarial**: Foreign publishable/schema-valid conflicts on local unit/rule.
- **removal**: Keep foreign status, remove map/generation: dependent result denied.

- **task_id**: LIF-01
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: Bind exact foreign content to one local use/audience.
- **positive**: Mapped assertion supports that use; purpose change triggers review.
- **adversarial**: Hash/verifier ref exists but no relation to local use.
- **removal**: Keep refs, remove content/purpose bind: protected use refuses.

- **task_id**: CAP-05
- **role**: consumer
- **delta**: For foreign law, verify source, act/version, local scope and effective time.
- **positive**: Partial verified jurisdiction supports matching claim only.
- **adversarial**: Origin publication implies local commencement.
- **removal**: Keep source labels, remove local applicability: claim limits.

- **task_id**: CAP-06
- **role**: input collection
- **delta**: Only selected remote capture: bind rights, recipient, receipt, output.
- **positive**: Permitted persisted result reaches qualified receiver.
- **adversarial**: Remote completion/encryption stands in for meaning.
- **removal**: Keep remote marker, remove output admission: receiver blocks.
