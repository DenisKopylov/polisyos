Имена полей/артефактов ниже задают семантические обязанности и минимальные связи. Окончательные DTO, owner и версии связываются с принятым E02 source; существующие owners/registries расширяются без параллельных систем. Статические наблюдения не доказывают runtime absence или PASS.

## shared_status_authority_contract

- **standing**: R19 research only; no E02 acceptance/ratification. Only a dated accepted human-principal record changes PolicyOS SDD meaning; signatures/LLM/repetition do not create authority. All proposed SDD edits remain pending.
- **binding**: This process decides PolicyOS SDD standing/meaning only. It cannot reorder or override active system/developer/user instructions, change tool permissions, waive legal/safety limits, or authorize forbidden acts. Bind any SDD act to purpose, scope/audience, version, provenance and valid/known time. Unresolved standing limits only the affected protected PolicyOS use; other authorized engineering and candidate work continues.
- **delivery_state**: R19 research; E02/product checks UNRUN. Q01/Q02 absent/unallocated; Q03 cross-record bridge missing. No implementation or capability claimed.
- **pattern_pass**: P04/P05/P07-P10/P13/P29/P31-P38/P40: no invented status/default authority; verify instruction-boundary non-effect and generic source-derived set/consumer. Route P/A/R refs point to shared discriminators.

## R18-D23-Q01. Приоритет при конфликте действующих правил

Если две ратифицированные нормы либо текущая repo-инструкция из предложенной конституции и ратифицированная норма несовместимы для одного защищённого use, кто устанавливает, что это конфликт, и какое standing действует до датированного решения уполномоченного человека?

**Выбранное решение.**

Any consumer may raise a source-bound candidate finding; this resolver decides PolicyOS SDD standing/meaning only. It cannot rank or override active system/developer/user instructions, tool permissions, law or safety limits, waive a prohibition, or authorize a forbidden act; a project finding/disposition is not permission. Within SDD scope, only a competent current human principal’s dated accepted record changes standing after a bound same-use incompatibility. Until then, only the dependent protected PolicyOS use is not admitted; independent authorized candidate/common engineering continues. No automatic newest/strictest rule.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S18-M1

**specific_steps.**

- Separate the SDD-standing question from active instructions/permissions; this process cannot alter the latter or permit forbidden acts.

- Bind both rules to the same act, purpose/audience and effective time.

- Establish incompatible obligations; separate applicability and mixed planes.

- Append the open finding; choose no priority. Rebind only the affected consumer after accepted principal disposition.

**authority_time_status.**

Shared contract: SDD standing only; active instructions/permissions remain controlling under their actual hierarchy. Bind source/version/scope/time; accepted principal act alone changes standing.

**external_inputs.**

- Если разрешение требует сведений о внешнем акте/мандате, принять только исходный authority evidence с issuer, subject, act, scope и актуальностью; сейчас такие факты не установлены.

## self_attack

- **case**: A repo SDD workflow appears to require an action that active instruction/tool/safety constraints forbid.
- **repair_or_bounded_limit**: The workflow cannot alter the actual hierarchy or grant permission; follow active constraints, and scope any unresolved SDD question to the affected PolicyOS use.

- **case**: Root successor or architecture-team consensus treated as SDD authority.
- **repair_or_bounded_limit**: Verify exact accepted amendment delegation; otherwise proposal only.

- **case**: Provisional G8/WP trigger interpreted as ratification or universal blocker.
- **repair_or_bounded_limit**: Signal opens review only; preserve remainder and scope effect to dependent claim.

- **case**: Canonical §8.4 adds a sixth ID, but ledger replaces an old row so count remains five.
- **repair_or_bounded_limit**: Derive full expected set from the versioned canonical source; a fresh consumer detects missing/orphan IDs and opens scoped review. No empirical threshold/global stop.

- No new task: deltas fit DEB-14, DEB-09, OPS-11, GYD-22 and existing FINAL stages; rebind exact ownership after E02 acceptance.
