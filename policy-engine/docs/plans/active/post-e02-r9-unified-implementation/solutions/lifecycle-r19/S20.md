- **symbol**: CompositionCertificate
- **observation**: Static: same-named owners coexist; GYD-30 already calls for reconciliation. Do not create a third certificate.

**decision.**

Distinguish member-preserving display, a scoped descriptive/arithmetic derivation from aligned observed quantities, and authority-bearing joint policy/system claims. A verified sum may be signed only as that exact derivation; it asserts no independence, joint intervention effect, feasibility or program admissibility. Joint uncertainty still needs its covariance/dependence basis or valid bounds. For combined interventions, causal effects, feasibility or program admissibility, consume existing D2.6/CouplingGate decomposition evidence and relevant emergent/system-level grounding. Unknown coupling is not modularity; standalone causal estimates cannot be added into a joint-policy claim. Define the aggregate estimand first. Observed descriptive quantities may support a scoped arithmetic derivation without asserting causal independence; joint-policy/system claims require the coupling and emergent-grounding basis. Members remain separately answerable. Explicit source-grounded hypothetical weights can produce a labeled conditional scalar comparison; a normative selected winner requires CNT-05 authorization. Unknown weights are never defaulted. Membership never proves adoption; external act and applicability do. CNT-05 measure/aggregation-rule qualification remains applicable to the selected aggregate even when normative authorization is unnecessary.

**alternatives.**

- **option**: One universal portfolio
- **tradeoff**: Invents a common mandate and over-invalidates unrelated claims.

- **option**: Narrative-only co-review
- **tradeoff**: Cannot bind aggregate support or freshness.

**contract.**

Bind target, measure, denominator/unit, population/time coverage, exclusions and exact member editions. Any conditional scalar binds explicit source-grounded assumed weights; normative selection binds the authorized CNT-05 rule/principal/version. Unknown weights never default. Reuse the reconciled GYD-30 composition owner, no new receipt/certificate. LIF-02 alone enrolls dependencies and fences publication. Missing support returns vectors/limited results, not invented coupling or blanket refusal. Bind the claim purpose/derivation class to the chosen native operation. Its scoped arithmetic validity cannot be promoted to system authority without the existing coupling and emergent-grounding route.

**flow.**

- DES-03 pins editions and the requested aggregate/interaction; member owners supply supported outputs.

- CNT-05 supplies the applicable measure/aggregation-rule profile; GYD-30 composes aligned outputs and adds interactions only from supported edges. Explicit versioned hypothetical weights yield conditional analysis; normative selection additionally binds CNT-05 authority. Keep observed descriptive derivation separate from policy-program authority; the latter consumes CouplingGate and emergent grounding. Unknown dependence limits uncertainty; unknown coupling limits joint-policy claims.

- LIF-02 fences current use; UI-19/API/MACHINE show fresh currentness, while prior issuance remains history.

**migration.**

Reconcile GYD-30 owners and use DES-03 exact editions. Reuse CNT-05 measure/rule qualification for the selected aggregate; require value authorization only for normative selection. A hypothetical weight is a bound declared assumption, not proof of endorsement. DES-11 remains the selected feasibility/trajectory method.

**free_grow.**

More members/edges are data growth; a new edge class needs grounded producer, consumer and validation.

**resource_behavior.**

## R18-D20-Q01. Когда несколько PolicyMatter становятся одним портфелем?

Что связывает независимые PolicyMatter в одну PolicyPortfolio с собственным системным утверждением, а не просто в один совместно рассмотренный набор? Если PolicyOS подписывает aggregate claim, что задаёт его охват и актуальность при изменении одного дела; как отличить условный сценарий совместного принятия от подтверждённого институционального принятия?

**Выбранное решение.**

Distinguish member-preserving display, a scoped descriptive/arithmetic derivation from aligned observed quantities, and authority-bearing joint policy/system claims. A verified sum may be signed only as that exact derivation; it asserts no independence, joint intervention effect, feasibility or program admissibility. Joint uncertainty still needs its covariance/dependence basis or valid bounds. For combined interventions, causal effects, feasibility or program admissibility, consume existing D2.6/CouplingGate decomposition evidence and relevant emergent/system-level grounding. Unknown coupling is not modularity; standalone causal estimates cannot be added into a joint-policy claim. The unit is exact case/design editions and a scoped cross-root claim, not a PolicyMatter entity. Define any requested aggregate estimand separately: descriptive quantities may support only the scoped arithmetic derivation; joint-policy/system claims need their coupling/decomposition and emergent-grounding proof. Keep members separately answerable. An aggregate or scenario never proves adoption; that requires an admitted external act and applicable scope.

**mechanism_ids.**

- S20-M1

**specific_steps.**

- Bind target/measure/denominator/unit/population/time, exclusions, exact editions and the selected aggregation rule. Explicit version-bound assumed weights qualify only a conditional comparison; normative selection additionally needs current authority.

- Separate scoped descriptive arithmetic from joint-policy/system authority. The latter requires grounded coupling/decomposition and emergent support; absent evidence is not modularity. Preserve supported individual results and conditional scenarios.

- Enroll/fence current claims through LIF-02; UI-19/API/MACHINE expose currentness. Adoption remains external.

**authority_time_status.**

Aggregate support is scoped to bound member cuts; external adoption remains distinct.

**external_inputs.**

**unresolved_research.**

Универсального порога нет: проверить, decision-relevant ли edge для estimand; пока — отдельные результаты/conditional scenario.

**Изменения существующих задач и различающие проверки.**

- **task_id**: DES-03
- **role**: input
- **delta**: Pin exact editions and the aggregate/interaction question.
- **positive**: Aligned supported outcomes aggregate without a causal edge.
- **adversarial**: Mismatched denominator/period or omitted member invalidates aggregate.
- **removal**: Remove member/denominator binding; aggregate loses support.

- **task_id**: GYD-30
- **role**: composer
- **delta**: Reuse the reconciled native composer. Distinguish a supported descriptive/arithmetic derivation from joint causal/system/policy-program authority; the latter requires D2.6/CouplingGate decomposition and emergent-claim grounding. No third certificate.

- **adversarial**: Equal-looking numbers with incompatible measures/denominators cannot aggregate; a hypothetical ranking cannot become a normative winner and unknown weights cannot default.
