# External Foundations — Post-E02 R9 + GY + Atlas Workstream

**Goal:** Convert task-selected external foundations into source-bound, purpose-bounded, versioned evidence that native owners validate, workflow bridges consume, and audit/public surfaces explain without making the source, an LLM, or PolicyOS a substitute authority.

**Architecture:** Reconcile and reuse existing owners first; extend locally only where a consumer cannot express a required relation. Share acquisition, identity, rights, provenance, change and projection mechanisms while keeping data, law, measurement, causality, values, testimony and institutional acts semantically distinct. Family labels route discovery; they never grant admissibility.

**Standing:** The complete editorial arrays are `families.json:.families` = 26 (EF-01–EF-26), `grounds.json:.grounds` = 103 and `sources.json:.sources` = 197; the same denominators are pinned in `research-receipt-r9.json`. This pass read source-card metadata only. Cards range from selected primary text to search-extract/abstract access, so a card or URL establishes neither task applicability nor corpus completeness. R9 is `final_documentary_revision_runtime_unrun`; its receipt states `G_code_acceptance=false` and product probes UNRUN. This outline is not capability closure.

## Boundary and pattern pass

PolicyOS owns claims it signs and consumes other institutions’ acts as typed evidence. It can request, capture, preserve, resolve, compare, validate against an owner rule, constrain use and expose a limited/stale/contested result. It cannot issue a law, grant, consent, mandate, testimony or external operational act. Signature validity does not prove issuer competence, effect, truth or scope.

Relevant patterns: P01/P02/P03 (unwired capability and hidden status); P04/P05/P09/P15 (authority/status/warning/LLM boundary); P07/P08/P10/P14 (replay, time, semantics, dependence); P25/P26 (search coverage); P27/P31 (owner duplication/instance patch); P29/P32/P36 (marker/form/authority-by-adjacency); P35 (complete denominators); P37/P38 (gate predicate vs proxy); P40/P41 (repair scope and red provenance). The research context exposes the specific risks: editorial presence → admission, ID/URL/hash → meaning or law, no-hit → absence, metric name → same estimand, paper count/mirror → independence, signature/consultation count → authority, adaptive exposure → confirmation. Close them structurally at the owner and consumer.

Treat the research map itself as editorial. A new task-specific chain starts `absent/unallocated` only until A0 reconciles existing owners; then label each hop precisely using the repo’s capability vocabulary. Never call an unrun probe a missing implementation, or a prose plan a contract. Do not give one status to the whole foundation corpus.

## Minimal input queue and owner/delivery schemas

One task-scoped queue, owned by the first-vertical Runtime integrator; not a world registry. Reuse existing Source/Artifact/Claim/Permission types, adding an owner-local type only when an actual consumer needs a missing relation.

**Need/request:** `request_ref`; task/claim/atom/estimand; missing predicate and why it could change the decision; open family/facets; population/geography/institution/intervention/period/language/audience; competing bases and falsifier; eligible route/source/custodian; rights/purpose/processor/retention ceiling; internal owner/external actor; expected delivery and consumer; VOI/cost only when grounded; state/blocker/next action; refresh/deadline where justified. Keep no-hit, unavailable, denied, unknown and verified absence distinct.

**Delivery envelope (logistics only; preserve native meaning):** request and producer/event refs; source/issuer/custodian identity resolver; exact original/version/representation binding; capture/receipt vs publish/observed/valid/effective/recorded times; scope/coverage; native dataset/schema/provision/span/study/testimony/value/act/operations refs; transformation and input/output refs; exact-use permission; verifier/method/version/result; conflicts/dependencies; correction/revocation trigger; status and consumer. No generic `trusted`, `independent` or `current` boolean. A no-hit/set result also binds namespace, predicate version, complete bounded denominator, search rights/cutoff and blind spots. An external act has a separate original, issuer/delegation, scope, effect and revocation receipt; PolicyOS receipt ≠ external act.

**System work:** scoped discovery, capture, identity, permitted parsing/calculation, owner-rule validation, lineage, comparison, refresh detection/cascade, request generation, refusal and projection.

**External people/custodians:** deliver datasets and definitions/corrections; issue rights/consent/grants; resolve legal meaning/applicability; emit mandates/delegations/actual acts; select values/tradeoffs; give testimony and permission to reuse it; produce expert judgement under protocol; attest capacity and actual execution. System checks signatures only against an independently admitted trust path; it cannot create competence, authorization, representation, consent or independence.

## First pilot: one selectable coherent input packet

Use the R9 heating-affordability example as a candidate design case: **one actual service area, winter period, household population, existing support/retrofit instrument and accountable institution**. The commissioning owner must name the jurisdiction/program and real suppliers/custodians; they are not inferable from the editorial map. Existing metadata names actual methodological issuers (OECD, World Bank poverty-line methodology, HM Treasury evaluation guidance) and official legal publishers (Verkhovna Rada/Cabinet of Ministers), but these are research references, not local production inputs or task admissions.

The packet requests: (1) permitted household energy/income/service data with frame, definitions and period; (2) two legitimate alternative burden/poverty/inequality definitions with method/mandate owner; (3) official provision/amendments plus legal review of exact instrument and competent actor; (4) causal studies/local observations with comparator, take-up, lineage and transport limits; (5) permission-bounded testimony/participation, translation, missing voices and dissent; (6) staffing/capacity/cost/schedule from delivery owner; (7) purpose/audience/retention rights and update route.

Result is a candidate intervention or costed acquisition/refusal path consumed by G2/G4/G5, with Expert/Machine audit and safe Atlas projection. It is not production-complete because all 26 families were gathered. Missing genuine input blocks only dependent claims. `fixture_only` inputs can exercise a mechanism but cannot fill a production slot. Retain future case-specific source population in the living atlas; this does not defer any concrete obligation of the current programme.

## Конечный объём программы и открытый мир оснований

Полное завершение программы до first use означает закрытие конкретных исходных engineering/verification/operational obligations. Это пользовательский порядок релиза, а не универсальный runtime gate. Для R9 обязательны общая грамматика всех26семейств, их маршруты/пределы и доказательства заявленных классов; конкретному case нужны фактические допустимые основания его вопроса. Это не требование собрать все197источников, сделать каждый ground authority или завершить бесконечное будущее расширение каталога. Невыбранная семья/новый будущий источник может оставаться unadmitted без незавершённого пункта текущей программы. Однако уже назначенную обязательную реализацию или проверку нельзя превратить в будущий контент ради раннего запуска. Условность определяется исходным критерием, а не удобством исполнителя.

## Selected facets become concrete input edges

The absence of a sponsor case does not stop independent mechanisms. It prevents an authentic-case positive and remains visible in [the staged release contract](../execution/05-stage-and-release-contracts.md). A successful costed refusal does not close unrelated original obligations.
