# Research

Research documents carry source material, interpretations, proposals, and handoffs into PolicyOS
design. They are not runtime authority by themselves. Preserve each source's date, owner, evidence
cut, and unresolved premises; an implementation claim still needs its typed producer-to-consumer
chain and the owner decision that admits it.

## Read by subject

- [Policy-operations research](policy-operations/README.md) follows the research pipeline and keeps
  finding-level research separate from independent verification and ratification.
- [Universal policy design](universal-policy-design/README.md) collects design rationale; use the
  owning architecture records and semantic tests for accepted behavior.
- [Universal Policy Design Case research-results consolidation](../backlog/universal-policy-design-case-research-results-consolidation.md)
  is a `normalized-final-synthesis-draft`, not an accepted requirement or
  execution authority; its synthesis status does not ratify its findings.
- [Post-E02 development programs](development-programs/2026-09-30-post-e02/README.md) is a proposed
  portfolio and sequence, not authorization to start work, obtain data, or publish a claim.
- [Remaining deep-research backlog](remaining-deep-research-backlog.md) contains research prompts,
  not accepted product requirements.
- [Documentation organization research](documentation-practices-20261014.md)
  records advisory practices; the style guide and verification owners define
  the repository's applied documentation rules.
- Topic-specific notes and ledgers remain beside their subject. Read their local README and cited
  owner source before reusing a claim.

## E02 evidence path

Start with the [E02 documentation snapshot](../reference/e02-documentation-snapshot.md). It
separates the transferred cloud-test results, historical G integration checkpoints, and the later
Source41 author candidate, then routes a finding to its criterion, canonical owner, receipts, and
integration decision.

The original E02 package is `policy-engine/docs/research/e02-cloud-test-plan/README.md@sha256:7032d97b39f15d45c8f4d7b8f514f143ce1e6bc41e9038719d89495bdbb16793`. Its sentence that the A–G
prompts have not launched predates the transferred results and integration receipts. Use the
snapshot for the dated evidence cuts; the original publication and owner records remain the sources
for their own historical scope.

Keep these roles distinct while following the route:

- Assignments, criteria, execution prompts, and the bundle/owner maps are documentation inputs.
- Transferred reports, owner handoffs, deciding receipts, and G checkpoints are documentary
  evidence for their pinned source, command, and scope. A source-reported result is not independent
  verification or finding closure.
- Source41 is an owner-authored candidate. Its bounded checks do not establish the shared freeze or
  G acceptance. The separate `PolicyOS_E02R2` package records a separate assignment and stop; it
  does not update Source41 status.

The E02 tree contains both current navigation and a large historical evidence archive. A file's
presence under `docs/research/`, `implementation-handoffs/`, or a folder named `active` does not
establish that its evidence is current, that a candidate is verified, or that a finding is closed.
