## Seven decisions with the highest cost of premature commitment

### D01: Question, action, and execution model

Fix the exact quantity, population, units, horizon, comparator, and actual semantics of the intervention. Keep one versioned target with its existing owner; a projection must not become a second editable source of truth. BOUND, IDENTIFIED, ESTIMABLE, and ESTIMATED are distinct properties.

Numerical N4→N5 requires owner-produced materialization: set-to/offset, parameter, domain, and target slot. For WMR, establish the world-model graph, exact ExecPlan/order, and horizon alignment separately. Policy CompileResult is not automatically a world graph. New typed limitation fields already exist and should not be ordered again.

**Preference:** one native operator/profile, direct reference, and a check for loss/preservation of meaning at the actual consumer. **Alternative:** an honest symbolic/candidate result before a numeric basis exists. **Defer:** a universal action DSL and shared public schema until several real needs arise. **Discriminating test:** change only the materialized action while markers remain unchanged; preserve no-op and equivalent-entry behavior.

### D02: What uncertainty represents

Distinguish an identification set, statistical interval, point functional, model uncertainty, and ordered joint law. A mean need not lie inside an equal-tail interval. Two identical marginal distributions do not define the distribution of a difference or individual gains.

**Preference:** native versioned channels and a loss-explicit projection to the consumer that needs them. **Alternative:** a useful point or bounded result with the missing uncertainty type stated explicitly. **Defer:** a general confidence score, symmetric proxy interval, and mandatory samples from every method. **Discriminating test:** skewed distribution, units scaled by ×100, tied/untied covariance, and identical marginals with different joint laws.

B31, B201, and B202 are different owner decisions; their direct links to DS16 were removed by the source crosswalk and must not be reconstructed. Programmatic applicability is not a new official edge. B194/B197 repairs do not prove empirical validity of the sampler or calibration.

### D03: Historical meaning and present use

Keep immutable bytes, the selected typed view, and the rule in force when the original decision was made separate. Currentness is a new owner observation. A withdrawal may change admissibility at the same count; an unrelated tenant write should not automatically invalidate everything.

**Preference:** a version-dispatched historical evaluator where it adequately expresses the original rule, plus a separate currentness projection. **Alternative:** an admitted, content-bound rule artifact if history cannot otherwise be reproduced. **Defer:** backfilling unknown historical meaning, restamping, and a new global event store. **Discriminating test:** with the same prior bytes and a changed current rule, return the prior historical interpretation and a separate new current result.

### D04: Lifetime and scope of the risk budget

A stable policy subject, exact basis, and candidate occurrence are distinct. If the guarantee applies to a continuing subject, recreating the basis must not silently create a new budget. A different scientific family may require a different account. Choose according to the guarantee, not the current key name.

A separate question is global deployment risk versus tenant/cell budgets with a required aggregate cap. CG2 admission-only run budget is a third, separate owner decision. Store injection, a local checker session, and an identical CAS root do not resolve all of these questions at once.

**Preference:** preserve the selected guarantee's non-resettable meaning and explicit approved transitions; agree tenant isolation together with aggregate risk. **Defer:** rekey/delete/reissue of durable roots until a decision and inventory exist. **Discriminating test:** the same subject across B0→B1, retry, and relabel does not receive an unearned budget; two tenants do not gain visibility into each other.

### D05: Shared representation or only a native profile

A semantic contract, shared library, and shared authoritative store are three different decisions. Non-equivalent economic/time/normative profiles remain distinct even when their code looks similar.

**Preference:** use the existing owner, native payload, and typed refs; separate a shared kernel only after two actual consumers with identical meaning exist. **Alternative:** separate versioned profiles with explicit incompatibility. **Defer:** universal migration of selected views, a large public ABI, and a new schema registry. **Discriminating test:** a second profile changes only its permitted local scope; if a central fork is necessary, determine whether it represents new semantics or a poor boundary.

### D06: When the world actually needs an update

O1 diagnosis, O2 exploratory hypothesis, state estimation with a fixed model, policy-parameter training, and procedural research learning are distinct. O3 requires specific new knowledge, a scientific/update policy, and actual next-cycle consumption.

**Preference:** useful O1 without mutation; O2 only behind a candidate firewall; O3 only with its own basis. **Defer:** routine posterior update, broad learned diagnosis, and DS21 until O1 AND O3 are both present. **Discriminating test:** the same residual with different evidence yields different allowed conclusions; a self-confirmed observation stays quarantined after shared reprocessing.

### D07: Which operational or protected claim is actually needed

Local candidate computation, repeatable audit, a served production route, and writer-independent authority are different promises. Not every useful question requires FA1, V3 publication, or all-backend signing.

**Preference:** the narrowest useful supported envelope and a specific reader. For a stronger claim, name the deployment/trust owner and the corresponding check. **Defer:** global rollout, a new distributed framework, and a public lifetime obligation until an actual inventory exists. **Discriminating test:** a writer tries to change the actual admitted set while local receipts are preserved; the absence of an appointment must not prevent an honest lower-band demonstration.
