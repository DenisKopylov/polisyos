## Selected default for B31 / B201 / B202

Keep identification and statistical uncertainty as separate, linked typed artifacts. `ValueOuterSet` remains the canonical identification-set carrier; its bounds do not stand in for sampling/model uncertainty. The `ValueEvidence` composition points to the existing typed `UncertaintyEnvelopeRef` for its uncertainty summary. The summary carries two independently named functionals: a point functional (for example, posterior mean) and an interval functional (for example, equal-tail quantiles with mass and method). A point need not lie inside an equal-tail interval. Preserve finite values, ordered interval bounds, explicit functional identity, estimand/coordinate/unit binding, rule/version, provenance, and time roles.

Use one persistence boundary for new uncertainty output: existing `persist_uncertainty_envelope` / `load_uncertainty_envelope` in `ir/analytics/uncertainty.py`, returning/resolving the existing `UncertaintyEnvelopeRef`. `ValueEvidence` composes the identification carrier with this typed summary ref; when a new law-dependent computation or authority-bearing result needs the posterior law, the summary carries an `ArtifactRefModel` to the immutable typed law stored in the existing CAS. The proposed v2 implementation of these same helpers is the only envelope/law write-and-read path: the writer externalizes the input carrier through the existing artifact-store CAS, and the loader resolves and content-binds the typed ref. Preserve named axes, shared ordered draw identity across coordinates, weights, and source/calibration lineage; marginals with equal values but different pairings must remain distinguishable. **Proposed B202 default, pending owner ratification:** new v2 joint-law outputs are CAS-only rather than embedded draws. Existing inline `PosteriorSamplesCarrier` remains supported as a compatibility/v1.1 representation and producer input. A display-only summary may omit the law ref, but must remain explicitly non-compositional; before law-dependent computation, resolve, content-bind, and verify the CAS law ref or refuse that computation—never fall back silently to summary moments.

Make the changed semantics a **new 2.0 wire schema with a distinct `$id`**, proposed `urn:policyos:ir:uncertainty-envelope:2`. Keep the v1.1 reader and replay meaning unchanged; do not issue new semantics under its version or `$id`, and do not rewrite historical artifacts. New writers emit v2 only after the semantic owner ratifies it; legacy reads remain v1.1. Until then, unsupported mixtures stay candidate/limited and cannot pass a law-dependent or authority gate.

**Ownership route.** A owns the EMP writer work: keep `ValueOuterSet` identification-only and compose its evidence output with the typed uncertainty ref. E owns the UQS summary/law writer and calibration adapter: v2 functionals, proposed CAS-only joint-law output, compatibility-only inline input, and exact producer lineage. C and F update their consumer contracts to resolve and verify these refs while preserving status, provenance, rule version, and time roles; they do not own the semantics. Create an explicit governance task appointing the canonical IR semantic owner for `polisyos.ir.analytics.uncertainty` and its registry/contracts boundary. That appointed owner—not G, A, E, C, or F by implication—ratifies the semantic rules and schema ID. G remains research/verification only and receives no product or scientific authority.
