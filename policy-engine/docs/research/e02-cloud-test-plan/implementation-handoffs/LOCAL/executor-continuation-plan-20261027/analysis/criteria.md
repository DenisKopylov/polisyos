## Evidence and decision levels

1. **Source claim:** original criterion spans and cards describe required behavior and historical observations; card existence or status establishes no current closure. A Q0 next check is a source-backed proposal.
2. **Runtime evidence:** a result is restricted to its recorded source, fixtures, backend, input, consumer and selected cases. Rebind unchanged evidence against the final affected readset and execute a new discriminator only when the property or consumer requires it. Imported test names, interrupted runs and timeout-with-empty-output do not establish a passing case.
3. **Output availability:** the handover indexes 32 selected runs. For that index only, the 32 stdout, 32 stderr and 26 JUnit files marked available were read for existence and SHA-256 and matched; six interrupted indexed runs lack JUnit. This is not a census of all historical evidence or proof that the selected outputs establish each criterion. When deciding bytes cannot be obtained, mark the claim `not_established` and specify the exact rerun.
4. **G acceptance:** `g-review-followup-20261027/dispositions.json` records human-forwarded G feedback; it expressly notes no newly published G commit was supplied. Its accepted witnesses are bounded to their described source and property. Author-complete, a review of a packet, or composition on the author's lineage is not G integration acceptance.
5. **Formal closure:** the complete original ledger has 260 partial, 12 held, 1 open and 9 closed ID records. Preserve the nine historical closed statuses, particularly **B198**; the source-only B198 next-check entry is not an instruction to reopen it. The current handover and G27 packet record no new `closure_ids`; G adjudication is separate from source merge and from a later regression check.

In particular, its I1 `author_complete` applies only to the bounded eighteen-path exit, not the whole LA-041 matrix. Its S3 `verification_remaining` predates the later bounded two-test author packet and separate receipt; that packet still has no G S3/B31 disposition. R3's last ten selected expressions expanded to fourteen failures on the new fixtures; earlier unrelated or overlapping pass counts cannot be added. These are source-qualified status changes, not newly resolved findings.

G27-02/03/04 list Q0 as a supplemental reconciliation route, but original Q0 does **not** assign B13, LA-041, LA-028, LA-032, LA-033, LA-040 or LA-046. ## Pattern pass and admission rule

Relevant patterns: P01/P02/P12 require the real producer–artifact–bridge–consumer chain; P04/P05/P09/P15 prevent authority, status and LLM/projection claims from outrunning evidence; P07/P08 preserve rule, time and replay; P29/P31–P33 require behavioral, content-bound, class-wide negatives; P35/P36 require complete denominators and original finding-specific warrant; P37/P38 require the predicate actually established and a case where any proposed proxy diverges; P40 prevents instance-by-instance repair ladders; P41 forbids calling a red inherited without the exact slice-base replay and zero complete-input intersection.

The actual anti-pattern to avoid here is promoting a source map, an accepted bounded witness, a report hash, a card, a named test, or a declared basis into whole-criterion success. An occurrence may be `verification_missing`, `bridge_missing`, `producer_missing`, `surface_missing`, or another precise capability state only when its own evidence supports that label; historical labels in coverage or source-only analyses are time-qualified, not blindly current. For each authority-bearing gate, classify and freeze its decisive predicate at admission as recomputed, independently reconciled, consumer asserted, institutionally supplied, or not established; fail closed when the decisive predicate is in the last three categories. Record unknown source rights, currentness, population, law and calibration as typed limitations; do not fabricate them. Acceptance requires the specific producer and consumer path, valid positive, falsifying negative, exact source/input/profile and available deciding output, followed by separate G review and any formal adjudication.
