## Callback-cap composition finding

The independent review at `LOCAL/reviews/r4-composition.md` found F1: the aggregate-cap property diverged in the synchronous helper because `_SharedExecutor.is_current_worker()` used the logical worker/callback marker. Two outside-pool threads registering late callbacks on one completed Future were both treated as physical workers and dispatched inline at cap 1. This is the **same aggregate-admission class one mechanism level deeper** (P38/P40), not a new workload class. The old marker therefore measured callback context, while the property requires physical dispatches to stay within the configured pool cap.
