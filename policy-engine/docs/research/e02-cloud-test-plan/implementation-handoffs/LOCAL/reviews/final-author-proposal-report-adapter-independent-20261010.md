**Command status.** The property is whether a captured pytest command has the reported outcome; the former implementation treated a testcase-free JUnit aggregate or an early passing-testcase result as sufficient. A passing testcase paired with `timed_out=true` or a nonzero exit was a concrete divergent case. In the current `pytest_status` / `classify_metadata` path at lines 258–367, aggregate-only JUnit has no outcome; required context, stream digests, timeout, and launch-error fields are checked; explicit timeout yields `ERROR` before JUnit; and `PASS` requires actual testcase behavior, zero exit, matching wrapper/pytest statuses, a passed module-origin assertion, and a complete output inventory. `FAIL`, `ERROR`, and `SKIP` require matching testcase evidence and process/wrapper status. Missing or conflicting evidence remains unestablished. At lines 475–533, normalized outcomes are additionally qualified against the advertised status and manifest/source-manifest, plan, commit, and tree identities.

The author-packet join at lines 771–877 now emits only `UNADMITTED_AUTHOR_COMMAND_ATTEMPT_ONLY`, joins exact argv/cwd, assigned task route, occurrence binding, and stdout/stderr digests, and retains the run-manifest, completion, metadata, output, and packet refs in its de-duplication key. ## P40 disposition and residual

**P40 bucket: SAME_CLASS_DEEPER.** The earlier status and receipt findings were both the P38 class of command-evidence admission through a proxy. The current source widens the common status normalization mechanism and prevents structurally matched but unvalidated author packets from entering admitted status fields. No new same-class divergence was found by this static delta review. The falsifier to retain for the eventual frozen wave is a passing testcase with timeout/nonzero exit, aggregate-only JUnit, conflicting wrapper/process status, malformed/missing context, and identical manifest bytes with distinct completion/metadata outcomes; the present source paths conservatively refuse or distinguish those cases by inspection.

The bounded limitation remains explicit: this adapter does not establish the authenticity or ancestry of the root-supplied source census, does not run the registered v2 admission validator, and does not produce a registered v4 occurrence evaluation. Therefore even a status-bearing capture or exact author-packet match is an unadmitted observation; it cannot close an occurrence. The final report and root’s frozen-wave receipts still need their own execution/readback review before any claim about produced report contents or current candidate outcomes.
