## Independent final review by G root

### IR-Q1

I rechecked the writer/read asymmetry, generic typed-consumer escape, trusted-owner selection after ref serialization, and default/None/empty distinctions. The selected mechanism belongs at existing IR contracts/registry/I/O and actual typed loaders, not in 93 local conditionals. An artifact cannot select its own executable policy. Both supported nondefault writes and unknown-version refusals are specified, so a defaults-only implementation cannot pass. New resources are not new contracts. The remaining runtime work is concrete: actual producer/CAS/fresh reader, byte and value siblings, public/installed routes, and property removal.

Residual: unidentified historical owner meaning cannot be reconstructed from a regex/default. Preserve opaque bytes and independent current-family progress; do not invent broad compatibility.

### IR-Q2

I rechecked ADR-0107 against actual EvidenceBundle negotiation use and the registry's equality/overwrite shortcuts. Preserving the public advisory API while adding strict owned admission is the smallest compatible change. It avoids both breaking unrelated planning clients and allowing them to authorize a typed artifact. Source and target validators, immutable tuples/lineage, readers-first enablement and rollback are explicit. The catalog remains a derived view, not an admission oracle. Compatibility primitives avoid an ever-growing central hand-written pair table.

Residual: exact historical tuples and deployed external reader capabilities require evidence. They limit the associated compatibility claim; they do not require global production enumeration before repairing known current owners. Future version collisions must allocate a fresh meaning, not overwrite an identity.

### PAG and authority

I independently inspected the exact current scalar gate, the legacy orientation function, the local PAG validator/orientation scope and the always-refusing legal helper. Unknown law and unknown wire/graph meaning are different limitations. The new candidate route is allowed only over interpretable inputs whose actual operation is permitted; it creates no legal-positive receipt. Fixed arrowheads, latent confounding and sample-selection assumptions survive the computation. A new model is a conditional hypothesis, not a sovereign fact.

I selected a concrete bounded no-selection MAG membership path using existing graph types and optional causal-learn integration, with independent oracle conformance before enabling it. This retains useful exploration without promising all-class identification. CPDAG, selection-encoded and background-refined graph profiles remain separately named method limitations, not silently coerced inputs. DAG/ADMG candidate delivery can finish independently. In the final solo check I rejected a proposed two-node fixture expectation that treated a pure bidirected graph as an identification failure: with no directed X→Y path and only an X-mechanism shift, the Y marginal remains identified. The final fixture instead tests distinct formulas despite equal identified statuses. This prevents the future test oracle from simply copying an engine result or an incorrect research suggestion.

Residual: generic production currentness, operative legal applicability and real causal adequacy are external/scientific inputs. Library correctness/installed availability and resource enforcement are implementation verification, not facts established by a source link.
