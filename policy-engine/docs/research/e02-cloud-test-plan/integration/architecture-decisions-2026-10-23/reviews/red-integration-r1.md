## Findings

### R1-01 — Candidate bridge and trigger remain underspecified at the legal-gate seam

**Severity: high for the R4 positive path; confidence: high. Pattern bucket: P02/P05, same candidate-versus-authority class one level deeper (P40).**

The desired property is useful graph-conditioned candidate math while the protected legal result remains refused. At the source pin, the actual shared route cannot produce that positive: require_legal_source_relation_verification() always raises verification_missing (data_forge/domains/legal/source_relation.py@24b434966df130cafeaab3a196a64db2f3f18cb0); LegalConstraintBridge.get_constraints_for_policy() calls it at entry (lex/legal_evaluation/transport_constraints.py@1dc2894b20d0d3a373191db3d1fbf86f9ebe53ff); and TransportabilityResolutionLoop.resolve() catches it and returns the refused result before reaching _run_transport_solver() (scientist/nodes/builtins/causal/resolve_transport.py@05f4b55b4c89fdff94f5bceee9714b83158e9573, f12fb9895...). Thus “construct outer refusal, then invoke the solver” in R4 is a required branch change, not a call to the existing loop as-is. The named legal helper is an authority refusal, not a positive source-relation producer/consumer chain; the draft’s later producer_missing / artifact_missing / verification_missing labels are the accurate description of positive legal evidence.

The candidate branch also needs an explicit operation trigger. At the same source pin, absent pag_identification_policy resolves to probabilistic for a PAG and absent sample budget resolves to 100 (transport_resolution_inputs.py@ac4fc6e5fc2babd1fba6643db32390adeeb11cf6); the solver then takes its probabilistic PAG path (transport_engine.py@e070dc158f0f95ed9e3b5684d5d75e503624a7c8). R4 says candidate work is optional and “requested,” but does not say whether this existing default is the request. Without that decision, a newly reachable branch may run by default for every PAG transport operation, while an explicit-only interpretation may never run for omitted input.

**Concrete divergent case:** a PAG with a manually supplied, content-bound graph/query and inputs whose processing is permitted, but no operative policy-level legal relation. The current pinned route returns UNSUPPORTED before solver execution. A naive “no legal S-nodes” reconstruction could also turn missing legal evidence into an assumption that no legal mechanism changes the graph.

**Proposed correction:** make the split explicit in R4. Resolve and retain the current typed legal refusal first; independently freeze graph/query/context/profile and the existing compute/access admission; then run the candidate solver on that selected graph as a conditional graph-only calculation, carrying a typed verification_missing limitation that says no verified legal relation was applied. Do not treat absent legal mappings as evidence that no mappings exist, and do not invoke the solver when access to the actual inputs is refused. Specify whether the already-resolved PAG default is intentional default-on behavior (then pin its effective policy, budget, and seed as AD03 requires) or whether candidate diagnostics need explicit caller intent distinct from an injected default. Reuse the existing result/report/CAS and compute owner; do not add a legal service or a second transport engine.

**Spec-only discriminator:** on the permitted-input case above, the persisted nested diagnostic contains a substantive solver output while outer UNSUPPORTED/NONE/false, refusal code, and review requirement remain exact. With a real access denial, no protected input is materialized. Removing the candidate solver/property while keeping DTO fields fails the positive. The diagnostic says the legal relation was unverified and excluded, never that constraints were absent. These are acceptance requirements, not executed tests.

### R1-02 — New candidate field still calls a sampled fraction “confidence”

**Severity: medium; confidence: high. Pattern bucket: P10/P05.**

R4 correctly selects “sampled orientation sensitivity,” rejects posterior/calibrated/all-PAG claims, and removes new top-level scalar mirrors. However, its acceptance text still requires candidate_diagnostics.id_confidence_under_pag. At the pinned source, that ratio is transportable_count / total over sampled candidates, then used with a threshold to choose IDENTIFIED versus UNSUPPORTED and to scale final_confidence (foundry/methods/catalog/causal/transport_check.py@f26094e30d58b00600dcd9e0451c0b82dd8f4c85:385–441). Moving the same name into a candidate-only object weakens the authority boundary at every generic API, export, or consumer that reads labels without the surrounding prose.

**Proposed correction:** give the new nested DTO a semantics-first name such as sampled_transportable_fraction or sampled_orientation_sensitivity, with its actual numerator, unique-valid denominator, excluded/failed/duplicate counts, generator, and stopping reason. Keep legacy id_confidence_under_pag only when reading the historical representation; do not emit it in the new nested schema. Surface copy should say “sampled orientation sensitivity under [generator]” and never “confidence.” The outer refusal and all protected status/ranking remain driven by the existing authority result.

## Compatibility and maintainability pass

- **IR-Q1/Q2:** exact registered schema identities plus owner-controlled extensions fit Rule 12. Schema/version grammar is a governed vocabulary; it is not a finite menu of policy domains or methods. The drafts reject defaults-as-allowlists, preserve omitted/None/empty/malformed distinctions, and permit a nondefault extension without central dispatch edits. That is extensibility without arbitrary reader authority.
- **Migration:** reader-first rollout, preserved historical bytes, explicit directional tuples, no unknown-field dropping, and writer enablement after supported consumers are upgraded align with AD01/AD03/AD05. The 33-path R4 adoption seed and 93-callsite IR census are work-disposition inputs, not permanent closure caps; the docs correctly require implementation-time reconciliation and do not demand an exhaustive production archive census. Old strict readers refusing new versions is a compatibility limitation, not a reason to restamp old data.
- **Candidate/authority:** Q3’s distinction between input-access permission, candidate computation, and policy-level legal authority is consistent with ratified S0-K06 and the identity boundary. Unknown policy law may remain a typed limitation for candidate work; actual denied or unestablished access to the selected source still stops that operation. R4 correctly keeps a legal positive source relation outside this architecture choice.
- **Existing-owner reuse:** adding a nested diagnostic to the existing result/report, retaining selected profile and lineage, and using current graph/solver/Lex owners is proportionate. Keep accounting and attempt identity attached to existing attempt/resource/InputRef owners rather than creating a second ledger. No status lattice or CAS/hash rule change is warranted.
