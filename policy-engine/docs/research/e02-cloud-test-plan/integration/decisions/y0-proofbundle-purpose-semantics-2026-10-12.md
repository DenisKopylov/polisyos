# Open G decision: transport proof purpose and producer verification

**Status:** open; no institutional choice accepted.

**Question:** What may a paired `ProofBundle` call and protected transport consumer infer from a `TransportabilityResult`? A real Y0 direct-ID operation can return a mathematical expression under a declared graph/query, while its source-data existence, currentness, and authority remain unestablished. A native/simplified `identification_engine` label is also only a result field: the current result/report intake has no source-resolved, content-bound, verifier-provenance basis that would let the strict pass trust it. Neither path establishes empirical grounding, legal validity, publication eligibility, or institutional authority.

**Source evidence.** The canonical `ProofBundle` uses separate `proof_status`, `proof_stratum`, and `completeness_regime` fields (`src/polisyos/ir/analytics/causal.py@034f1b79b7a4684f8f7cb4ace0ccee675e7f7afc5178d358f22c002afeff326b:178-211`). The current R3 sibling projector maps a Y0 result to `A2_oracle_backed/sound_incomplete` (`symbolic_identify.py@228cedab5045da3884d6d1d4dfa9df3e2d74d59953944be8335421cf7b8262c2:162-192`), while the registered facade's positive branch emits `A1_extended/complete` with only status and mode (`transport_check.py@3d482f3e8860d73fc755ea3e73245683f736205478b69bb83feacc0e7b3b69af:210-224`). The direct `SymbolicIdentify` path also calls `proof_bundle_from_identification_result()`; the canonical family classifier does not name Y0 among its A2 families (`causal.py@034f1b79b7a4684f8f7cb4ace0ccee675e7f7afc5178d358f22c002afeff326b:1818-1846`). Independent finding R3-01 records this as P40 SAME_CLASS_ONE_LEVEL_DEEPER (`LOCAL/reviews/y0-whole-producer-protected-independent-r3-20261012.md@cefcb49e3c03eb01bd7b9cfb997b0c005f28ff481ab657a55d8900e56405e23c`).

**Alternatives for the G owner.**

1. **A2 / sound-incomplete with math status `identified`** (bounded R4 prototype). Preserve the real Y0 theorem result as identified, but keep A2/sound-incomplete and a typed review grade because the bundle crosses into a system whose consumers may not receive the paired transport result. This matches the existing Y0-aware sibling projection. It avoids an A1 law and keeps unresolved data authority explicit. Its cost is that bundle completeness may be read as incompleteness of the mathematical derivation, not incompleteness of evidence authority.
2. **A1 / complete for declared conditional mathematics only.** Preserve the mathematical result as identified/complete and add a separate typed purpose that says the bundle proves only a graph-conditional formula. This requires an accepted purpose vocabulary, public schema/surface work, and consumer changes so no bundle-only route can read A1 as empirical or authority-grade evidence. A metadata label alone is insufficient.
3. **`oracle_needed` or non-identification.** This avoids positive overreading, but hides an actual valid mathematical result and conflates source-data authority with mathematical identification. Do not adopt this without a distinct future case where the operation itself did not establish the formula.

4. **Keep native conditional mathematics visible while treating its protected origin as unknown.** Preserve an available native theorem family, AST, and `identified` status as a candidate, but mark the bundle `sound_incomplete` and the result grade `unknown` until a source-bound proof basis is resolved, content-bound, and verifier-provenance checked. The strict pass blocks the result meanwhile. This preserves the mathematical claim without trusting a backend string. It can block existing native/simplified result consumers that relied on the old no-additional-review path; that compatibility cost and any approved proof-basis verifier must be decided explicitly.

**Bounded candidate for engineering:** For the exact scoped direct Y0 operation, R4 retains `proof_status="identified"`, assigns A2/sound-incomplete, and keeps the grade `review_required` because source-data predicates are not established. For every other identified result, it preserves `identified` and any available theorem/AST as a candidate, assigns A1/sound-incomplete, and leaves the grade `unknown` until an actual proof-origin verifier exists. In particular, `native_id` and `simplified_legacy` strings cannot produce `NO_ADDITIONAL_REVIEW`. The protected pass blocks `review_required` and `unknown`; the packet export carries the computed grade and candidate scope. This is a fail-closed engineering proposal, not an adopted native proof-origin contract or an A1/A2 institutional choice.

**Compatibility and migration:** Candidate R4 leaves `ProofBundle` schema `1.0` and `TransportabilityResult` schema `2.0` unchanged, uses existing metadata, and adds decision-summary fields. The public-surface/generated inventory review and regeneration must be completed from the frozen source by the root owner. The current native/simplified protected-pass success path will now block until a content-bound, source-resolved proof basis with verifier provenance is available. The owner must identify affected native consumers, choose whether to reuse an existing proof verifier or specify a versioned typed-purpose/verification contract, and define migration/default behavior. Do not infer purpose from `identification_engine` or Boolean review markers. The existing TRSO population-profile/AST decision remains separate and untouched.

**Predicates and authority:** the direct Y0 operation, returned expression, graph/query scope, and family support are scoped by the local producer; direct Y0 source-data existence/currentness/authority remain `not_established`, so its protected grade remains `review_required`. Other result origins remain `unknown` until their proof basis is resolved, content-bound, and verifier-provenance checked. Caller review bits and backend labels cannot reduce the grade. No external-source fact or institutional act is inferred.

**Pattern pass:** `P29`, `P32`, `P37`, `P38`, `P40`. Existing failures: paired result/proof semantics disagree; a Boolean can proxy for operation scope; and backend labels can act as an unverified origin token. Target: shared projector plus a protected consumer that recomputes grades from verified scoped evidence and fails closed on unverified producer origins. Missing before R4: `verification_missing`, `semantic_test_missing`. Acceptance: real CheckTransportability, SymbolicIdentify, and SymbolicIdentifyV2 result/proof pairs preserve the actual math status; a facade→CAS/report→strict pass→decision-export run remains blocked after grade/Boolean removal; native/simplified/Y0 strings without proof-origin evidence remain unknown and blocked.

**Closure:** `closure_ids=[]`. No publication, legal, data, institutional, or formal closure is claimed.
