I found no profile-identity rule that requires copying the outer envelope tags into the profile: the two layers describe different facts. In `ir/analytics/uncertainty.py@64d7444`, `DistributionFamily` is the assumed family behind an interval, `UncertaintySource` is the interval's origin, `PropagationMethod` says how uncertainty was propagated before the IR, and `UncertaintyEnvelope.sample_size` is an optional generic count. By contrast, `PosteriorSummaryProfile` in `ir/analytics/posterior_summary.py@64d7444` names the finite-corpus functionals (`mean_functional="weighted_arithmetic_mean"`), ordered `draw_ids`, `sample_axis`, probabilities, and carrier/joint digests. Its denominator is the bound carrier rows/draw IDs, not necessarily an estimator's upstream observations or a generic envelope count.

The concrete E consumers show why those distinctions matter. `PropagationDispatcher._requires_monte_carlo_sampling` routes from a `PosteriorSamplesCarrier`; Welfare's `_admit_welfare_sampling_laws` likewise reads and validates that carrier, and `_draw_finite_empirical_law` samples its normalized rows. For this admitted carrier path, changing only `distribution_family` does not alter the finite empirical law or the recomputed posterior mean/quantiles; a family-only fallback is used only when no empirical law is admitted. `source` does not define the posterior law: `_origin_id_for_envelope` uses it as an origin label, so changing it can change lineage identity and must be supported by real provenance, but it does not alter the finite-corpus calculation or create authority. The outer `propagation_method` is also not the profile's summary algorithm: the latter is a literal profile functional. However `_infer_exactness`/`_infer_certificate_kind` and `_infer_certificate_radius` use the outer method, and the last can derive a DKW radius from `sample_size`; a consumer relying on those separate composition certificates must establish those method/count assumptions. A producer that asserts a count inconsistent with its intended DKW denominator could therefore misstate that certificate, but the profile independently binds and recomputes its actual empirical rows. The current adapter sets BAYESIAN/CALIBRATION/MONTE_CARLO and `sample_size=len(draws)`, a coherent choice for its own producer; a fit-observation count, posterior-draw count, source, or propagation history may legitimately differ at another boundary. A validly repersisted change to an outer tag alone proves neither changed profile math nor new authority. Enforce a binding only where a consumer has an expected identity (the current consumer APIs check parameter mapping, and `posterior_nominal_mean(..., binding=...)` checks an explicitly supplied estimand/unit binding); do not invent family/source/method/count equality. No strict equality check or new adversarial blocker is required for this bounded source slice.
