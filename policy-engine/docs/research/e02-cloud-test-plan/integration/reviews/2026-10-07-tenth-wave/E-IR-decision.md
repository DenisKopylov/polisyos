- **Do not promote equal-length marginal arrays into a joint law.** The profile permits `row_identity_basis="producer_input_order"`; the producer currently accepts a mapping of coordinate arrays, checks equal lengths, and can generate `draw:i` IDs when none were supplied. That is a valid joint identity only if the real producer contract guarantees the arrays came from one shared, ordered draw table. Length equality and a digest of the resulting arrays do not establish that premise. Require source-bound aligned-row evidence or explicit shared IDs; otherwise preserve the joint relation as unestablished and refuse law-dependent multivariate composition. - **Make the finite probability convention identical for mean and CDF.** In the proposed implementation, `posterior_summary_functionals` computes mean from the normalized stored `p_i`, while `empirical_cdf` accumulates float64 probabilities and forces the last positive CDF boundary to exactly `1.0`, rejecting only category collapse. When the represented probabilities do not sum exactly to one, the inverse-CDF's final atom is effectively the residual `1 - cumulative_before_last`, which may differ from stored `p_last`. Either canonicalize that residual once and use the same effective probabilities for both mean and quantiles, or state and enforce a numerical error bound/refusal rule. This is a narrow finite-float contract issue, not a reason to change the chosen mean/median/interval semantics.
