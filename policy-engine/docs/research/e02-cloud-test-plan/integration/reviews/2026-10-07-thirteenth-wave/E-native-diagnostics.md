The raw-CAS probes wrote one Profile2 envelope with a raw JSON boolean sample and a separate one with a numeric-string sample. For both artifacts, `load_posterior_summary_envelope` raised `ValueError: posterior summary raw carrier requires finite real non-bool values`. The same bytes loaded through legacy `load_uncertainty_envelope`, whose coercion yielded callback inputs `[0.0, 1.0]`; public `push_forward_envelope` mapped them to `[1.0, 2.0]`, persisted the result, and the strict fresh reader accepted the derived Profile2 envelope. Thus a transformation can erase the raw-type defect before a later strict read. This is a concrete raw-ingress bypass diagnostic; the raw inputs and resulting artifacts are not production evidence.

The join probe started with separate valid Profile2 inputs: one sample `[2]` with raw ratio weight sum `0.5`, and samples `[10, 14]` with raw ratio weight sum `1.0`. `join_envelopes` returned the generic carrier samples `[2, 10, 14]` and weights `[0.5, 0.5, 0.5]`; the persisted generic reader returned it, while the joined object and strict fresh read had no Profile2 profile. The joined envelope's outer-hull point/interval are recorded in the raw result. I do not assert a mixture interpretation or expected joined mean; the finding is that Profile2 declaration/context/functionals are absent after this public join path.

The positive control strict-loaded a supported Profile2 law `[1, 3]`, called the public mapper on both rows, produced `[2, 6]`, and successfully strict-read the fresh CAS result. Its stored Profile2 mean and an independent exact `Fraction` calculation both equal `4`.
