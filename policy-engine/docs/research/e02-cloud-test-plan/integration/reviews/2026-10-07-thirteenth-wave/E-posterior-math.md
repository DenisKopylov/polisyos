## Mathematical result

For admitted weights, the source uses p_i = Fraction(binary64(w_i)) / sum_j Fraction(binary64(w_j)). A common power-of-two scale is retained only if the exact ratio tuple and positive support are unchanged. The Profile2 mean is one final float rounding of sum_i p_i*x_i. Sorted quantile boundaries come from the same exact cumulative ratios, rounded downward before left-search; draw cuts round upward before right-search. Since the query values are finite binary64 values, no representable query lies between an exact rational cut and its directed adjacent float. This matches the owner’s exact finite-q/U contract.

The level conversion remains binary64: alpha = (1 - credible_mass) / 2, then the resulting finite q is classified exactly. The previously reviewed min-subnormal credible_mass boundary therefore remains an explicit interpretation limit, not a new delta: the decision promises exact classification of finite q, not exact rational algebra on the input level.

Independent analytic discriminator, not a call to the production reducer: take canonical ratio weights (0.5, 1.0, 0.5), samples (-2, 1, 2), and f(x)=x^2. The transformed finite law is values (4,1,4) with masses (1/4,1/2,1/4): its mean is 5/2, median is 1, and its 80% equal-tail interval is [1,4]. Applying the map to the input mean instead would yield (1/2)^2=1/4, so transform-then-reduce is necessary. The source maps each admitted row, retains its ratio weight/row identity, and recomputes the functionals from those transformed rows. This is the exact transformed finite empirical law; it does not claim exact expected output under the finite RNG mesh. For example, the existing declared [1e-20, 0.5, 0.5] law can be classified at finite U=0 while a 53-bit sampler mesh cannot represent its continuous mass exactly.

Population variance is accumulated as exact Fraction arithmetic before a scaled square root. The moment compression emits a normal approximation and marks provenance as approximate/non-gating; it is not represented as the original finite law or as posterior coverage. Nonlinear push-forward does not use f(mean) or infer a transformed law from mean/variance.

## Admission, composition, and limitations

- The transformed value path rejects bool/non-real, non-finite, float64 overflow, and nonzero-to-zero underflow. Finite zero and negative/cross-zero outputs are valid empirical atoms. If a callback itself raises (for example, a singular map at a sampled zero), no result is emitted; exceptions are not normalized into a dedicated map-error type.
- Scalar push-forward validates a declared Profile2 carrier and digest before invoking the map; it refuses multi-coordinate profiles before callbacks. It reissues carrier and joint digests after mapping, retains draw IDs/axis/weights, clears the source estimand binding, labels inherited lineage non-authoritative, and keeps gate_eligible=false.
- Interval/moment compression removes posterior-profile and joint-law declarations and records the loss. Particle compression preserves a profile only when the carrier is content-identical. Pull-back is emitted as constraint-only and drops profile authority. These match the stated semantics; arbitrary maps still do not establish units, estimand identity, source law, or fit authority.
- A raw-profile admission boundary remains: test_profile_raw_cas_types_refuse_before_legacy_coercion documents that generic load_uncertainty_envelope coerces raw bool/string samples into the same typed float carrier, while load_posterior_summary_envelope rejects them before coercion. The new composition helpers accept an in-memory UncertaintyEnvelope and cannot reconstruct whether it came through the strict reader. Thus a caller that takes the legacy-decoded model and passes it to push_forward_envelope can bypass the raw-type guard. The canonical Scientist nodes use the strict reader, and I found no production callsite for push/compress/pull; this is a public-helper ingress limitation, not a counterexample to the finite-ratio arithmetic. Do not claim generic loading as Profile2 admission. If that route is meant to be supported, bind it to the strict reader or carry an admitted-profile marker to composition.
- The committed V1 fixture index contains seven literal historical Profile1 CAS payloads; it is compatibility evidence, not a V2 law oracle. The V2 composition tests construct fresh finite profiles dynamically.
