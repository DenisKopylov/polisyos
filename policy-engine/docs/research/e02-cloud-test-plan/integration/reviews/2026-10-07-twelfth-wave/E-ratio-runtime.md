## Remaining consumer divergence

One real adjacent runtime gap prevents claiming Profile2 law preservation through every public IR composition helper. In unchanged `ir/analytics/uncertainty.py`, `_samples_from_payload` hands V2's unnormalized ratio weights directly to `_weighted_mean` and `_weighted_quantile`; `push_forward_envelope` and `pull_back_envelope` use those reducers without dispatching through the Profile2 exact functionals. `_weighted_mean` computes `np.sum(values * weights) / np.sum(weights)`, which can overflow despite a finite exact weighted mean: four valid samples all `1.6e308` with canonical equal ratio weights `(0.5, 0.5, 0.5, 0.5)` have exact mean `1.6e308`, while the numerator sums to `3.2e308` and overflows. The Foundry MC admission's float32 range check rejects this example on its path; these public IR helpers do not have that bound. Separately, `_weighted_quantile` linearly interpolates cumulative weights. For samples `(0, 1)`, ratio weights from `(1, 2)` and `q=0.34`, it returns an interpolated value instead of the inverse empirical-CDF atom `1` required by Profile2.

Those helpers also copy `envelope.metadata` while changing samples/point/bounds. That retains the old `posterior_summary_profile` and content hashes, so a later `read_posterior_summary_profile` rejects the transformed carrier/functionals. This is not a failure in the reviewed MC/Welfare selector and does not justify an outer family/source/method/sample-size equality gate. It does mean the current source must not be advertised as general Profile2 support through `compress_envelope`, `push_forward_envelope`, or `pull_back_envelope`. If those routes are in scope, the canonical IR owner should reuse the exact ratio reducers and recompute the resulting profile, or explicitly clear/limit the profile declaration before emitting transformed artifacts; add a real fresh-readback and inverse-CDF counterexample test. If they are out of scope for E, record that boundary rather than claiming every IR composition preserves Profile2.
