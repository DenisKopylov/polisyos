## Source-grounded basis

- The original B31 criterion at `policy-engine/docs/plans/active/agent-packages/PolicyOS_E02_Combined_Agent_Package/source/B_r19_original.md@198076863e143dea9f89f02734b13d50dae3eed5` (lines 593–606; packet records byte binding `9311ba1b…`) is specifically about retaining statistical/model uncertainty as a separate verified channel, not fabricating widths, and coordinating by estimand/unit while keeping method as provenance. Its point example permits a zero-width identification set so long as it is not presented as total precision.
- On A pin `8bfea70b2e2090ad5c541b103b7521efc01b30eb`, `runtime/quality/generation_cycle.py` has `ValueGateReceipt` with one `ValueOuterSet` and transport/calibration/WMR receipt data; it has no native-envelope or subject-relation field. `ValuePortObservation` refuses `value_ready` without the owner `value_receipt` and `method_selection_receipt`. `ValueOuterSet` separately stores bounds, identification status and epoch, and its persisted loader checks the width checksum; those checks do not establish equality to a statistical interval or to another artifact's subject.
- Existing reusable carriers are real but narrower: `ir/analytics/estimand.py` persists/loads normalized `EstimandAST` via `EstimandASTRef`; the AST contains the symbolic query/root, treatment, outcome, variables, method and side conditions, but not a complete typed population/contrast/unit/currentness identity. `core/artifacts/manifest.py` has CAS bytes/manifests and `InputRef(artifact_id, role, optional manifest_profile_sha256)`. `InputRef` records an edge; a free-text role is not itself proof that two outputs resolve to the same subject. `ir/kernel/units.py` has `UnitSpecType` (including rate ratio/percent), but that type alone does not prove every proposed conversion is semantically valid.
- On E pin `8d8e7b319e7eb6b57bc4ab3c4db5ca3070393f8c`, `NativeValueEstimandBinding` is explicitly caller-constructible, `contract_only_nonproduction`, and `production_value_eligible: Literal[False]`. It is unsuitable as a production join or verifier receipt. The versioned `UncertaintyEnvelope` carries interval semantics and general metadata but no typed resolved subject relation. The relevant A/E APIs are also present at G source pin `f00dd7661a8d3329fb1fa1b049decb0d1d2f277b`; no existing `ValueSubjectRelation` was found in the pinned IR/runtime paths.

Therefore, reuse of AST refs, existing CAS/manifest lineage, and unit types is correct; those pieces alone do not bind two actual output artifacts to one resolved subject. A small persisted join is a defensible bridge when the actual supplier emits independent identification and native-uncertainty refs. First inspect those exact producer artifacts: if both already share one canonical subject input ref and the consumer can verify its content-bound manifest, wire that existing identity and avoid a redundant standalone relation artifact.

## Conditions on the proposed contract

1. Resolve the subject from persisted canonical refs/manifests and the producer's declared inputs; never accept a caller's copied subject fields or `NativeValueEstimandBinding` as proof. Compare the actual output identities, not just matching labels or an asserted shared `role`.
2. Keep the relation a content-consistency result. Its hashes, manifest IDs, equality checks, or `production_value_eligible=false` do not confer admission/promotion authority. A positive must be grounded in the current existing Runtime/verifier provenance and then recomputed by the A/C10 consumer; missing, stale, foreign, or fabricated provenance remains limited/blocked.
3. Do not require fields the exact producer does not emit. Use the AST/ref and resolved producer basis for estimand identity; include population, contrast, unit/scale, source/model refs, or time/epoch only where the admitted producer actually supplies them and the consumer needs them to distinguish identity or currentness. Do not duplicate AST semantics as independently supplied strings. For inputs, seal the exact complete declared roster (including relevant source/model inputs), rather than a caller-selected subset.
4. Keep the unit profile narrow as proposed: equivalent typed units and a specifically supported exact ratio/percent scaling only. Do not infer FX, calendar, welfare, nonlinear or general conversion support from the registry type.
5. Preserve identification `4` and native interval `[1,10]` as separate channels through the actual persisted path. A relationship must not rewrite the interval, and a currentness/ref mismatch must refuse rather than silently fall back to the point.
