## What the bounded supplier demonstrates

The test fixture compiles `mechanism.runtime.income_tax@1.0.0`, then calls real `execute()` for the center and perturbation points. `IncomeTax.emit_patches` applies `reported_income * rate` to active agents and adds the sum to `government.balance`. With two active reported incomes of 2 and initial balance −2, the exact fixture law is

`y(r) = -2 + (2 + 2)r = 4r − 2`.

At rates 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, real persisted balances are −1, 0, 1 USD. The candidate uses a central difference with `h=.01` at `r=.5`: plus/minus executions yield about +.04/−.04 USD, so `(plus−minus)/(2h)` is `4 USD / ratio` (floating simulation tolerance applies). The response values are read from persisted StateSnapshot state through the CAS reader, not from the callback. `response_slots` explicitly rejects `summary_evaluator`; the separate `y=x−2`, `J=1` callback is only a mathematical control and is not evidence for native B190.

Independent exact arithmetic for the stipulated equally weighted two-point input law `{1/4,3/4}` gives `Var(r)=1/16 ratio²`; hence `Var(y)=J² Var(r)=1 USD²` and `SD(y)=1 USD`. This is a synthetic law oracle, not an estimate inferred by the producer. The second native slot `global.tax_rate=.125` is constant under this parameter and has `J=0`; all-zero reported incomes likewise preserve a true zero response. Scaling reported income from 2 to 2000 scales the numeric slope from 4 to 4000 while its declared output unit remains USD; that is not a currency conversion. Axis units are content-bound (`USD` and registered `rate`), so the Jacobian's quotient unit is inferable as USD/ratio; no separate derived-unit token or standard-deviation output is persisted. Because ridge is an untyped absolute scalar added to output covariance, Fisher magnitude changes with output numeric scale; a Fisher-based deviation needs an explicitly justified scale convention.

The local response profile persists a Jacobian, weighting matrix, Fisher matrix, and source/config/axis/replay basis; it does **not** accept an input law or compute output SD. Weighting is inverse sample covariance of center execution replicates plus ridge. On this deterministic one-output fixture, identical center replicas imply covariance 0; with default ridge `1e-8`, the numeric formula gives `W=1e8` and `JᵀWJ=1.6e9`. This is a regularized local sensitivity score, not empirical measurement noise, a likelihood-derived information claim, or an uncertainty estimate; do not invert it to create a standard deviation. `IDENTIFIED`/rank cannot supply scientific authority.
