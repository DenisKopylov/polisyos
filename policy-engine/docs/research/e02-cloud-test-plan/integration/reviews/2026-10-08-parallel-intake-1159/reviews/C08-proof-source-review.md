It distinguishes exhaustive-family common functionals from completion-conditional results; it states that source and graph assumptions are not authority. The engine audit in `foundry/methods/catalog/causal/causal_engine/artifacts.py` persists the supplied original graph through the existing typed graph artifact helper and refuses a missing graph or a contradictory supplied `graph_ref`.

The IR writer/reader in `ir/analytics/causal.py` then binds that reference to content. Before persistence, it resolves a typed `ir.causal_graph_model` manifest, loads the graph through `causal_graph.py`, and requires both the actual CAS-byte SHA256 and re-canonicalized typed-payload SHA256 to match the basis. It persists exactly one matching `causal_graph` `InputRef`, adding it when absent and refusing a wrong or duplicate explicit entry. A fresh `load_proof_bundle` repeats resolution, hash checks, and exact manifest-lineage validation. This is substantive content binding, not ID-spelling or marker checking.

The exact-source tests exercise both common and conditional cases, writer refusal before proof publication for unresolved/changed graphs, and fresh-reader refusal for absent/wrong refs, stale hashes, wrong/missing/duplicate lineage, and damaged graph blobs; they also preserve unrelated legacy proof compatibility. The consumer test uses the actual engine audit, fresh CAS, and a child-process readback, and checks refusal before proof publication when the original graph is absent or changed. These are meaningful producer-to-persisted-artifact-to-reader tests, beyond constructor tests.

The native-query fix is deliberately narrower: top-level full-observed static DAG queries with disjoint nonempty treatment/outcome and no bidirected edges use exact truncated factorization before ancestral shortcuts can discard an isolated treatment or leave outcome parents conditioned. Factors for non-intervened variables retain observed parents; summation removes variables outside treatment and outcome. IDC composes numerator/denominator ID results. The characterization update now expects the denominator to marginalize over outcome. This does not establish latent/PAG/temporal or universal identification.
