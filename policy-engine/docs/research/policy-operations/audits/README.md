# Policy-operations audits

Maintenance route: team-architecture.

Audit directories hold independent findings against a pinned research package. Cite the finding ID and the source revision it examines; later amendments do not silently change what an earlier audit established. Route responses through amendment and verification.

Owning entrypoint: `docs/reference/policy-operations-research-pipeline.md`.

This local document explains the directory role. Its presence does not certify the contents or close a product capability.
