# INT-R10 — Recommended Revisions Before Consolidation

## 1. Revision standing

The audited result is `NO_GO`. The fixed-family weighted-union theorem is correct, and the report
correctly identifies the missing canonical family projection. The blocking defect is narrower but
load-bearing: the report calls `3 * delta` sharp for the pinned owner after discarding exact
schedule and obligation-weight structure that owner enforces before execution.

The revision must not be an editorial substitution of `3/100` with another number. It must reopen
the source-level composition question and show, from exact source predicates, what the canonical
scope schedule implies before deciding whether a new effective-cap mechanism is needed at all.

## 2. Blocking revisions

### R1 — Recompute the pinned-source family envelope from the actual schedule owner

**Findings:** `INT-R10-B-001`, `INT-R10-E-002`, `INT-R10-I-001`.

Withdraw or suspend every sentence that says:

- the strongest pinned-source composition is `min(1, 3 * delta)`;
- live `delta = 1/100` therefore means `3/100`;
- the bound is sharp for the canonical owner;
- three ordinary scopes can each attain local event probability `delta`; or
- equal `1/300` caps are required to recover one `1/100` family bound.

The revised research must account for all of these verified source facts together:

1. one root-level `budget_delta` per canonical scope;
2. one global executed-check ordinal per scope;
3. the exact `_schedule_alpha()` formula;
4. schedule mass bound and the profile actually bound to each root;
5. the complete expanded obligation-class weight map;
6. the local good-event theorem over executed probabilistic checks;
7. adaptive class/check selection along a path;
8. deterministic zero-spend proofs and refused/ineligible profiles;
9. registry/source version binding; and
10. the relationship from check-level false claims to member-level false promotion under the named
    maintained assumptions.

The audit's `(9/20) * delta` calculation is a falsifier of the report's sharpness claim, not a
replacement canonical theorem. The amended research must independently derive and state the exact
claim it is willing to defend.

#### Acceptance evidence

A verifier or line-by-line proof can reproduce:

```text
all permitted per-check reservations
-> all-path local false-claim envelope
-> member-level false-promotion envelope
-> exact family union/composition
```

from pinned source, registry, schedule profile, class weights, and maintained assumptions.

#### Falsifier

The revision fails if its sharpness witness assigns probability `delta` to one member while the
same witness cannot satisfy the canonical schedule reservations and local theorem premises.

### R2 — Correct the mandatory negative-control oracle

**Findings:** `INT-R10-G-001`, `INT-R10-E-005`.

The current fixture treats

```text

```

as if it proved

```text
family false-promotion probability = 3/100.
```

Separate the fixture into independent properties:

- three distinct canonical scope/root identities;
- exact check-level reservations under each root;
- all-path local schedule envelope;
- cross-scope aggregate arithmetic;
- family membership/chronology/current-head completeness; and
- presence or absence of a canonical family projection.

The negative control must still go red for an unsupported public single-delta claim, but its failure
reason must match the exact missing property. A missing family projection is real even if the
underlying schedule arithmetic happens to fit inside a proposed family delta.

#### Falsifier

Changing only a root's displayed `budget_delta` field or counting three roots must not be enough to
establish a probability of `3/100` without evaluating the schedule law.
