# INT-R2 — Formal Argument Audit

## 1. Argument Map

The package makes six load-bearing moves:

1. `binding_gap` is not a shape discriminator.
2. A proposed acquisition can close a blocker only if its evidence channel can change the blocked
   predicate.
3. The eight commissioned missing objects require eight union branches rather than one generic
   `authorized` or `evidence_received` record.
4. A candidate artifact is not usable until producer standing, content binding, currentness and a
   purpose-scoped authority ceiling are admitted.
5. Re-entry permits the demanding owner to recompute; it does not auto-close the original gate.
6. `deeper_terminal` is a stronger negative result, not effort, delay or near-success.

Moves 1, 2, 4 and 5 are sound as boundary rules. Move 3 is substantially, but not perfectly,
discriminated. Move 6 is sound where the negative boundary is constructible; it is under-specified
where the claimed terminal is an open-world absence such as “no competent provider exists.”

The package's central non-closure invariant is also sound in its declared scope:

```text
same-stream row growth
+ unchanged required relation / estimand / mandate object
=> no closure of that non-data case
```

It does not imply that data can never contribute to those cases. A new experimental regime, a new
measurement, or facts satisfying a legal condition can matter. The package correctly requires the
change in evidence regime or authority object to be named instead of treating `n increased` as the
closure operator.

## 3. Union Discrimination

### 3.1 Pairwise audit

The test is not whether the prose differs. A pair is genuinely discriminated only if at least one
producer, sufficiency predicate, admission proof, authority ceiling, re-entry trigger or terminal
meaning is non-substitutable.

mandate; `NA` normative authorization; `IC` implementation capacity; `HD` competent human decision;
`IA` independent audit.

The package therefore passes the named “table rather than union” hazard. It also explicitly identifies
shared semantics instead of pretending every field is unique: relation and estimand share same-stream
invariance; legal, normative and write cases may share a scoped-grant base; decision and audit share
reconstructability.

### 3.2 Residual union defects

Two implementation-significant refinements remain.

**`owner_writability` is conjunctive.** Its own branch requires both substantive change authority and a
current technical execution grant. A single case can therefore hide two independently failing
predicates. The artifact must either carry two mandatory sub-obligations or split into an ordered
subcase sequence. An API credential must never close the substantive arm, and a substantive order must
never prove executable least privilege.

**`independent_audit` is a constrained assurance subtype over a competent work record.** This does not
justify merging it with `competent_human_decision`, but it does require an explicit no-substitution
rule. A valid `HD` artifact cannot satisfy `IA` merely because its author is external; an `IA` report
cannot satisfy the underlying management/professional decision merely because its conclusion is
favourable.

These are `minor` findings because the main report already states the non-substitution rules. The
amendment needs to make them explicit in the contract sketch.

## 4. Gap-Shape Assessment

### 4.1 What the pre-union assessment successfully does

`GapShapeAssessment` prevents three unsafe defaults:

- `binding_gap` cannot default to `data_gap`;
- “same-stream data cannot change this predicate” cannot by itself select one of eight branches;
- a compound gap can produce `split_required` instead of a ninth hybrid discriminator.

This is valuable. It preserves the fourteen later residuals as `not_established` rather than inventing
a row-level classification.

### 4.2 Where it defers the hard problem

The proposed fields are primarily a record of a classification result:

- `minimal_missing_object` already contains the answer the classifier must discover;
- `candidate_case_types` and `ruled_out_case_types` do not define how a candidate is admitted or ruled
  out;
- `same_stream_data_effect` distinguishes a data remedy from some non-data remedy, but cannot choose
  among relation, estimand, write, mandate, normative, capacity, decision or audit;
- positive P37 provenance says who supplied a predicate, not whether the predicate measures the right
  property.

Applying the package's actual machinery to the fourteen `shape:not_established` residuals therefore
classifies **none** of them. That is not a dishonest result: the row-level demanding predicates and the
later executing slice are unavailable at the pin. But the union is not yet usable as a classifier
merely because the record shape is specified.

A closing revision must define, for each branch, a minimal positive predicate and at least one sibling
falsifier. For example:

```text
estimand_binding candidate
  requires: blocked predicate concerns target meaning or target-to-analysis alignment
  falsifier: exact target is fixed and blocker is only observation availability

legal_mandate candidate
  requires: blocked predicate concerns actor/action competence under an authority source
  falsifier: competence is established and only execution permission is absent
```

The fourteen may remain unclassified after that revision, but each must name which missing evidence
prevents applying those predicates.

## 5. Authority-Ceiling Checkability

The package lists a rich envelope and states that complete end-to-end checkability is absent today.
That honesty does not cure a second overclaim: it repeatedly calls the sketch “subset-testable” without
defining the containment relation for most dimensions.

| Ceiling dimension | Representation in sketch | Gate-checkable today from the sketch? | Missing construction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| claim kind / action | registered refs | conditionally | canonical vocabulary, exact requested-use binding and conflict rule |
| subject / object | exact refs | equality only | object-version and descendant/part containment semantics |
| population | opaque scope ref | no | governed set expression and subset algorithm |
| jurisdiction | opaque scope ref | no | registered territorial/competence hierarchy and overlap rule |
| purpose / audience | lists of refs | membership only | purpose subsumption, audience derivation and incompatible-use precedence |
| source / target context | opaque refs | no | compatibility/transport predicate and evidence owner |
| valid / review time | timestamps | yes, locally | authoritative clock, effective-time role and stale-event precedence still needed end to end |
| evidence class | refs | no | registered evidence-class vocabulary and permitted-claim mapping |
| maintained assumptions | refs | no | owner-computed currentness predicate; “maintained” cannot be self-declared |
| maximum claim strength / commitment stage | refs | no | registered partial order and branch-specific ceiling composition |
| permitted operations / prohibited uses | refs | exact membership only | operation ontology, wildcard/scope rules and prohibition-wins composition |
| downstream gates / rule versions / epochs | refs | resolvability possible | bridge, current-head rule and replay/current-use distinction |

Only exact identity, timestamps, and literal membership are intrinsically checkable from the proposed
shape. Population, jurisdiction, context, claim strength and commitment stage remain narrative until
their algebras and owners exist. The package should have reported this field by field, not only as an
aggregate `absent/unallocated` statement.

## 6. Deeper-Terminal Semantics

### 6.1 Common test

The five-part common definition is strong:

1. an eligible procedure produced new admitted evidence;
2. that evidence excludes or narrows a plausible closure route;
3. the refusal becomes more specific, scoped, replayable and checkable;
4. no authority or near-success credit follows from effort;
5. re-entry requires a named regime/target/rule/owner/relationship/horizon change.

It correctly excludes timeout, silence, queue exhaustion, cost and near-threshold results.

### 6.2 Branch-pair audit

| Branch | Provisional member | Purported deeper member | Separability result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GR | no study found / incomplete dossier | scoped negative relation or formal non-identifiability | separable when the model, query and evidence regime are bound |
| EB | missing target attribute | ill-defined target or defined-but-non-identifiable target | separable |
| OW | grant/credential pending | operation absent from authoritative ontology or no competent change authority | separable only with bounded ontology/authority coverage |
| LM | delegation absent but a competent superior route exists | higher-order prohibition or no competent grantor | separable only with a bounded authority-order coverage proof |
| NA | review/consent pending | competent non-waivable disapproval or regime-established no route | separable for formal regimes; informal social licence correctly remains `not_established` |
| IC | current capacity absent but build/staging remains plausible | no credible build, narrower valuable stage or alternative channel within horizon | conceptually separable; search/coverage and horizon predicates are unconstructed |
| HD | no response or known referral pending | no competent authorised decision-maker and no governed alternative | open-world absence is not constructible from the proposed record alone |
| IA | provider pending or unresolved threat | no competent independent provider or unremediable threats | threat terminal may be constructible; provider-universe absence is not |

The weak cases share one class: **absence of every possible source or route**. A registry search that
returns no result is not proof that no competent actor exists. These terminals need a bounded coverage
artifact — declared universe, searched sources, exclusions, freshness, challenger route and unknown
remainder — or must be narrowed to “exhausted the declared registry/route at this epoch.”

Without that repair, the sixteen provisional/deeper fixture pairs can be labelled correctly by their
author but cannot independently prove that the deeper member earned its label.
