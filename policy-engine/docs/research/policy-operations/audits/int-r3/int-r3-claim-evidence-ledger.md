## Evidence classes

The audit uses the `W4-K02` labels for gate-bearing predicates:

- `recomputed`: the auditor directly re-read the pinned source or enumerated the complete committed
  register;
- `independently_reconciled`: two independent sources or derivations agree;
- `consumer_asserted`: a package or plan states the fact without an independent construction here;
- `institutionally_supplied`: the architect supplied a measurement the auditor did not execute;
- `not_established`: the available evidence cannot settle the predicate.

The last three do not support a positive authority-grade conclusion.

## Unsupported or overstated claims

### `implementable`

The protocol is detailed enough to implement technically. The package has not established that the
programme can recruit and support the declared population or obtain the adjudication and precision it
requires. Use `programme_execution_feasibility: not_established` until a feasibility receipt exists.

### `canonical benchmark owner missing`

The claim conflicts with the active Atlas master plan, which assigns the instrument to DS6. The audit
does not silently accept DS6 as an executable owner because DS6’s lifecycle may make that allocation
stale. It records the conflict and requires adjudication.

### `gate_standing: NO_GO` as a comprehension-use gate

The restriction is substantively sound but uses the `W4-K05` first-public gate field for a different
predicate. Keep the global DS12 gate and the local comprehension-claim-use restriction separate.

### `confirmed` repository-wide absence

The package read selected named paths. That does not settle a repository-wide zero. The result must be
either recomputed over a complete pinned tree or downgraded.
