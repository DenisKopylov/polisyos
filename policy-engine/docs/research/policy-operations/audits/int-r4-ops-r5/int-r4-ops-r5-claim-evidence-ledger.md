## Ledger Method

This ledger tests whether each load-bearing package claim is:

- **direct repository fact** — observed in source/contracts/fixtures at the pinned head;
- **external established result** — supported by the supplied survey’s primary/official evidence;
- **external bounded pattern** — recurring prior art, not a universal standard;
- **package synthesis** — a defensible construction assembled from several domains;
- **package recommendation** — a proposed design requiring ratification/validation;
- **bounded absence claim** — absence of an admitted chain in named canonical owners, not a global token zero;
- **unsupported transfer** — evidence establishes an adjacent property but the package claims more.

`Transfer` records what the cited evidence is entitled to establish. `Audit disposition` is independent
of the package’s own standing.

protection/learning asymmetry, bounded self-confirmation refusal, non-compensable metric roles and the
absence of an admitted end-to-end chain. Its weakest claims are not unsupported domain facts; they are
transfers from useful structure to stronger design authority: precedence, orthogonality, fixture
completion, capability labels and the O1 amendment.
