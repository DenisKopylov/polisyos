## Unsupported Or Overclaimed Statements

- **Capability:** “can/show/run/return” and “available immediately” lack type, producer, bridge, consumer, verification and surface.
- **Proof:** zero counterexamples in a finite suite does not establish all contexts.
- **Ownership:** discipline/lane strings are not holders.
- **Succession:** the 223-line measured baseline became a 105-line unresolved ledger.
- **Standing:** appendix correction does not erase the main non-member block.
