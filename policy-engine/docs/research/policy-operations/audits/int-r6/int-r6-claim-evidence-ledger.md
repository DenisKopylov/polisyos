## Unsupported Or Overclaimed Statements

### Current capability language

The main deliverable says the system can represent sources, show renditions, run checks and return vacancy refusals. The fixture appendix says those functions are “Available immediately”. No INT-R6 type, producer, bridge, consumer, verification or surface exists. W4-K06 makes Markdown an input, not a capability chain.

### Positive proof strength

The protocol’s finite suite can expose a single material divergence. It cannot establish that no unenumerated context exists. A passing certificate must report the exact denominator and residual uncertainty rather than silently moving from “no counterexample found” to “equivalent”.

### Routing

`architect/specification`, `runtime/specification`, `vocabulary review`, `future jurisdiction admission`, `empirical corpus/benchmark` and similar strings are destinations, not owners. An unappointed lane cannot close a finding.

### Repair succession

`repo-baseline-study.md` recorded current paths, denominators, crossings and status/Lex coordinates. `01-repository-baseline.md` states many of those same facts are unresolved. Calling the latter the successor does not preserve the former’s evidence.

## Confirmed Bounded Claims

The strongest part of the package is its negative discipline:

- UI locale is not legal authority.
- Translation does not create authenticity.
- Co-authentic texts do not acquire a synthetic English source.
- Labels do not replace IDs.
- Plain-language adaptation does not merge with translation.
- A missing holder does not authorise a pass.
- Structural parity does not establish semantic equivalence.
- A target-only permission or lost prohibition is a decisive failure.

These claims are consistently supported across the five external surveys: regulated regimes allocate authority/status/procedure rather than prove equivalence; direct en→uk evidence is thin; concept identifiers record a governance assertion rather than prove its truth; status systems are heterogeneous; and plain language/readability is independent from semantic preservation.
