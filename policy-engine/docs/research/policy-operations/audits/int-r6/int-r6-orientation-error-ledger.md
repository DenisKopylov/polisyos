## Corrections And Preventive Rules

- Replacement by name does not preserve evidence.
- Duplicate claims are a population; fixing one member does not close the set.
- Search is navigation, not census.
- Finite adversarial suites refute asymmetrically.
- “Works in model” and “works in repository” require different evidence.
- Routes require accountable identities.
- Every Stage 3 fix must alter each package artifact carrying the defect, not only a ledger.
- Audit residuals stay unresolved; supplied values do not fill them.
