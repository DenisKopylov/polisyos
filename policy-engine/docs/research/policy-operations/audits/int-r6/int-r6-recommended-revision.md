Stage 3 should amend the package, not this audit. Every correction must appear in every package artifact that carries the defective claim—not only in a revision ledger, not only in the appendix, and not only in the artifact a verifier opens while the reader-facing deliverable remains unchanged.

## Per-Finding Closure Criteria

### IR6-A01 — standing

Closed only when a complete eight-file walk finds no non-member standing field/token and no contradictory tuple. A statement in a revision ledger that the main block is superseded is insufficient unless the main artifact itself carries the supersession or corrected values.

### IR6-A02 — evidence custody

Closed only when every concrete row deleted from `repo-baseline-study.md` is either:

1. reproduced at a named current SHA with method and denominator;
2. retained as historical with its original executor and date; or
3. explicitly retracted with the reason it was invalid.

A successor must carry the same evidentiary substance or a typed disposition for each lost claim.

### IR6-A03 and IR6-A06 — architecture versus capability

Closed when all present-tense repository-capability verbs have full coordinates. Conceptual examples may remain, but must say `the target model can represent` or `the protocol would return`. The package’s W4 capability axis remains unchanged unless another authorised stage constructs and verifies the chain.

### IR6-A04 — locale census

Closed by an executable recursive leaf walker over exactly three JSON files. It must flatten primitive string leaves by full path, compare target values only on shared paths, report target-only/source-only key sets, and cross-check the count through an independent parser or test. Every number names the executing party.

### IR6-A05 — routing

Closed when each of the 30 finding rows names an existing owner or explicitly records that no owner is allocated. A team name is insufficient if the repository’s owner vocabulary uses another identity; a role definition is insufficient without appointment when a human decision is required.

### IR6-A07 — proof strength

Closed when the certificate’s positive claim is no stronger than its evidence. At minimum it binds:

- exact source/rendition digests;
- proposition and purpose;
- semantic frame version;
- complete declared fixture/context denominator;
- glossary/vocabulary versions;
- reviewer/decision basis where required;
- residual uncertainty and excluded contexts;
- invalidation on a new counterexample.

A stronger claim requires an independently defended completeness oracle, not another finite sample.

### IR6-A08 — partition language

Closed when selection dimensions and dependent transformations are explicitly distinguished. `PresentationVariant.parent_*` and certificate dependency must remain visible.

### IR6-A09 — citations

Closed when the wrong SCC link is repaired and the source register uses durable article/paragraph/page locators for VCLT, CILFIT, Skoma-Lux, Swiss authenticity, relevant Ukrainian provisions and ISO scopes.

### IR6-A10 — package entrypoint

Closed when the 21-line scaffold is declared historical/channel-proof and points to the substantive ten-section deliverable, or when another existing artifact gives an unambiguous inventory/disposition. The package remains eight files unless Stage 3 separately authorises a new index.

## Properties A Revision Must Preserve

1. D4-A1 is not amended: `en` authored UI, `uk` translation, `ru` frozen, source rendering separate, RTL UI unsupported.
2. UI locale never selects legal authority.
3. A legal source remains a versioned jurisdictional text/member/set, not a translation label.
4. Co-authentic members remain peers unless the jurisdiction supplies a precedence rule.
5. English may aid UI/indexing but is not a universal legal pivot.
6. Existing namespaced status/refusal owners are reused; no second lattice is introduced.
7. `stale`, `superseded` and `withdrawn` remain distinct in human and machine projections.
8. Translation and plain-language adaptation remain separate transformations and decisions.
9. All three binding falsifiers stay red-first against structural parity.
10. Zero holder cardinality remains representable and blocks only the governed purpose.
11. Historical source/rendition/certificate records remain append-only and invalidation does not rewrite history.
12. External practice stays evidence, not PolicyOS authority or repository capability.

## Verdict-Lift Conditions

The aggregate verdict may lift from `GO_WITH_REVISIONS` to `GO` only when all of the following hold:

- IR6-A01 through IR6-A10 are closed with the evidence above;
- every revision appears in **every package artifact carrying the defective claim**, not only a revision ledger and not only one of two locations;
- the current locale census is independently executed and attributable;
- the package delta remains Markdown-only and does not implement Stage 3/4 capability prematurely;
- severity re-audit finds zero blocking and zero material rows;
- the final package publishes one conforming W4-K05 tuple while the audit verdict remains separate;
- the architecture commendations C01–C04 remain intact;
- a fresh anchor pass resolves all principal links and precise source spans;
- a complete package inventory read shows no undeclared artifact or root-path regression.

A partial correction does not lift the verdict. In particular, fixing the appendix while leaving the main deliverable unchanged repeats the defect rather than closing it.

## Conditions That Force NO_GO

Any of the following forces a research-quality `NO_GO` on re-audit:

- a non-member or audit-verdict token remains in any standing field;
- deleted measurements are cited as current or recomputed without an executor;
- current catalogue figures are copied from the commission/architect instead of independently walked;
- MAEP certificate passage is represented as universal semantic proof without a complete denominator;
- an informative translation is promoted into an authority set by quality score, certificate name or UI locale;
- a co-authentic regime is forced through English fallback;
- Stage 3 creates a parallel status/refusal lattice rather than mapping/registering concepts;
- a vacant high-stakes role silently passes, self-appoints, or globally disables unrelated demonstrable functions;
- a generic lane is presented as an accountable owner;
- any package defect is “answered” only in a new revision ledger while the artifact carrying it stays unchanged;
- revision touches source, workflow, `AGENTS.md`, pattern register, binary or staging material without separate authority;
- final delivery cannot prove the amendment descends from the package head or cannot enumerate its exact delta.

## Residual Band

These recommendations do not write the amendment, choose production schemas, appoint owners, validate Ukrainian wording or open a public gate. They specify the minimum evidence needed for Stage 3 to cure this research package without destroying the architecture that survived audit.
