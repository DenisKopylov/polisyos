# INT-R8 recommended revision register

## 1. Use of this register

This is an executable register of research corrections, not a rewrite and not an implementation
plan. “Executed” means that a later INT-R8 amendment changes the exact cited proposition and
supplies the stated read-back evidence. A prose assertion that a problem was considered is not
execution.

Items R1-R10 are **required for standing** because they close the blocking finding or a
load-bearing material gap. Items R11-R13 are **improvements** that correct minor orientation,
source-custody and anchor precision defects without changing the central result.

## 2. Required for standing

### R1 — recompute the capability reality map from prerequisite evidence

**Findings:** `INT-R8-IX-001` (blocking); informed by parallel finding `INT-R7-X-001`.

**Exact defect.** The handoff assigns `producer_missing`, `bridge_missing` and
`verification_missing` to receipt, gate, transcript, frontend and accountant capabilities even
though their required consumers, endpoints or wired chains do not exist. The numeric accountant
is represented as pending production work even though INT-R8's accepted result says it is not
currently authorized or required.

**Required change.** Replace every new-capability row with a prerequisite-valid reality
statement derived from the pinned chain:

- retain `implemented` for the real four-audience projection substrate;
- retain the existing `build_public_export_bundle` producer as present, without claiming an HTTP
  bridge whose consumer endpoint does not exist;
- represent the receipt, loss gate, transcript contract, GY-PA3 and proof binding no later than
  their evidenced research/contract maturity;
- keep `semantic_test_missing` only for actual screenshot/print/export surfaces; and
- remove the numerical accountant from the capability maturity path unless a later architecture
  decision establishes a real contract and consumer.

Do not replace the bad labels with a new owner appointment.

**Execution evidence.** A revised complete handoff table names, for each label, the producer,
artifact, bridge, consumer and verifier evidence that satisfies its prerequisites or states the
earlier honest maturity. A hostile review can start at each row and resolve every claimed
prerequisite at an exact ref. No numerical-accountant row remains as owed engineering merely
because a number was refused.

### R3 — narrow the numerical-refusal theorem and add deterministic quantitative-flow models

**Finding:** `INT-R8-III-002` (material).

**Exact defect.** The research treats randomization as a necessary premise for numerical
disclosure composition in general. It is necessary for the DP theorem surveyed, not for all
quantitative information-flow or maximal-leakage models; deterministic channels can carry
numeric leakage under declared secret/channel/gain assumptions.

**Required change.** Replace the universal premise with the narrower conclusion:

> No canonical numerical disclosure-composition claim is justified for the current PolicyOS
> release path under any model established in the repository.

Add deterministic quantitative-information-flow/maximal-alpha or equivalent primary models to
the comparison and state the actual eliminating property: no declared secret/channel/gain or
support model, no locally valid quantity, no prospective allocation and no adaptive accountant.
Do not create a number.

**Execution evidence.** The primary decision, formal analysis and external transfer ledger all
use the narrower conclusion. The comparison contains at least one deterministic numerical model
and explains why its premises are absent rather than calling determinism itself disqualifying.

### R4 — bound executability to exact or proved-conservative decidable models

**Findings:** `INT-R8-III-004`, `INT-R8-IV-002` (material).

**Exact defect.** The work moves from a mathematically defined consistency set to a generally
“executable” verifier without closing finiteness, decidability, empty-set behavior, solver
termination, abstraction soundness or heuristic substitution.

**Required change.** State that the no-number formal result applies only when:

- the declared record/observation model is finite or lies in a decidable symbolic fragment;
- exact non-uniqueness/reconstruction is decided, or a conservative abstraction has a proved
  no-false-safe direction;
- `C(T) = empty` is a model/observation inconsistency, never a safe pass;
- timeout/resource exhaustion/unknown theory returns a typed blocked outcome; and
- sampled, classifier-based, calibrated or thresholded approximations do not inherit the theorem
  without separate validity research.

Do not select a solver or wire format.

**Execution evidence.** The formal artifact includes explicit premises and a truth table for
`two-or-more-values`, `singleton`, `empty`, `timeout`, `unsupported theory` and
`proved-conservative abstraction`. The falsifier suite has atomic cases for empty consistency
set, timeout and an unsafe heuristic substitution.

### R5 — scope prefix discipline to a declared controlled release universe

**Finding:** `INT-R8-III-006` (material).

**Exact defect.** “Every actual prefix” and “complete actual transcript” quantify over releases
that no PolicyOS owner can reproduce, including uncontrolled recipient copies and unknown
external disclosures.

**Required change.** Define the claim over a versioned **declared release family** under custody.
Separate:

- controlled registered releases/channels whose membership and bytes/observations must be
  reproducible;
- observed external copies admitted into the transcript; and
- uncontrolled or unknown channels with an explicit completeness disposition.

Missing controlled history blocks. Unknown uncontrolled history limits the claim and cannot be
silently treated as complete.

**Execution evidence.** Every prefix-discipline statement carries the declared release-family
boundary and a transcript-completeness disposition. A falsifier removes one controlled release
and turns red; a separate fixture shows that an unknown external copy narrows the claim rather
than rewriting controlled history.

### R6 — make the channel family open and add the missing channel attacks

**Findings:** `INT-R8-IV-003`, `INT-R8-VI-003` (material).

**Exact defect.** The threat list and F01-F25 omit at least five material release-channel
families while presenting broad coverage.

**Required change.** Extend the declared channel registry and add atomic red cases for:

1. locale/translation/fallback divergence;
2. email, push, webhook, RSS/Atom, social-card or chat syndication;
3. content-encoding/TLS-record/packet-length or range-request oracles;
4. sitemap/search/autocomplete/snippet/result-count discovery channels; and
5. cryptographic proof metadata such as key ID, certificate path, log position, witness set or
   proof size.

State that the channel family is versioned and open. An unclassified channel is out of model and
cannot inherit a broad safety claim.

**Execution evidence.** Five new atomic fixture rows identify exact input artifacts, one
mutation each, exact issue code and exact expected loss outcome. The formal threat model lists
their observation semantics and an `unknown_channel`/out-of-model disposition.

### R7 — operationalize materiality and constitutive procedural steps

**Finding:** `INT-R8-V-002` (material).

**Exact defect.** “Material,” “faithful condensation,” “constitutive step” and “load-bearing”
determine the verdict but are not connected to a versioned, falsifiable source relation.

**Required change.** Require each mandatory source item to bind to one or more governed semantic
effects—truth, scope, authority/status, permitted use, contestability, history/currentness or
privacy—and require every no-number custody claim to bind its constitutive event classes and
allowed condensations. State the competent-basis/evidence requirement and the mutation standard:
removing exactly one decisive item turns the relevant predicate red while removing duplicate
prose does not.

The research may leave institutional competence open; it may not call the resulting judgment a
mechanically closed algorithm before the relation is supplied.

**Execution evidence.** The semantic artifact contains a testable relation contract and separate
fixtures for: one decisive limitation removed, one decisive custody event removed, duplicate
wording removed, and two events faithfully condensed. Each has exact expected predicates and
issue codes.

**Execution evidence.** A revised reuse table shows one source of identifiers or an explicit
approved extension relation, no duplicate semantic label with conflicting meaning, and atomic
falsifiers for missing, noncanonical, mismatched and self-disclosing reasons with one exact code
each.

**Finding:** `INT-R8-VIII-002` (material); crosschecked with `INT-R7-VIII-003`.

**Exact defect.** The seam binds source, audience, retained set, reasons, verdict, rule and
transcript head but not the model identities that determine the verdict. It also does not
explicitly prevent a projection-loss failure from erasing issuer-side source authenticity.

**Required change.** Require the bindable proof input to identify or carry a typed disposition
for:

- declared uses;
- governed decision/materiality predicates;
- semantic inventory version and completeness;
- record/consistency model;
- protected-predicate family;
- channel and controlled release-family version;
- coalition/delegation model;
- auxiliary/background-information assumptions;
- exact rendered/exported public objects;
- empty/timeout/abstraction verifier status; and
- transcript-completeness disposition.

State the non-substitution rule: a failed/missing INT-R8 relation blocks public projection
faithfulness/current positive verification but does not imply the issuer never authentically
issued the source record. INT-R7 remains free to choose construction and serialization.

**Execution evidence.** The seam section lists the complete semantic input set, cites
`INT-R7-VIII-003`, and includes a truth table separating source issuance authenticity,
projection faithfulness and current/public authority. No algorithm or key policy is selected.

## 3. Improvements

### R11 — enumerate whole-tree public-export call sites accurately

**Finding:** `INT-R8-I-004` (minor).

**Exact defect.** The ledger calls the definition and re-export “two callers” and mentions one
operations runner while omitting the validation-tool caller.

**Required change.** Distinguish symbol-containing files from call sites. State the complete
whole-tree Python invocation-file set: definition plus two tools plus two tests, with four caller
files outside the definition. Preserve the no-HTTP conclusion.

**Execution evidence.** Orientation text and reproduction recipe produce the same five-file
invocation set and no longer call `runtime/quality/__init__.py` a caller.

### R12 — pin the ONS SRS and NSW dissent source versions

**Findings:** `INT-R8-II-002`, `INT-R8-II-003` (minor).

**Exact defect.** The ONS item names a 12 June 2023 edition while the living official download
has advanced; the NSW practice direction lacks a dated/versioned full-text anchor sufficient for
independent exact-text verification.

**Required change.** Bind an official edition identifier, publication date and stable locator or
content digest for each, or cite a living official page with an explicit as-of/version statement
and narrow the proposition to what was independently read.

**Execution evidence.** A fresh source verifier can retrieve the exact edition and reproduce the
quoted proposition without relying on a search-engine excerpt or mutable current download.

### R13 — name actual limitation carriers rather than a universal field

**Finding:** `INT-R8-V-004` (minor).

**Exact defect.** Reuse prose implies a universal existing `limitations` collection, while the
base source carries closeout limitation codes, projection gaps, deficit rows and
surface-specific limitation fields.

**Required change.** Replace generic field language with the concrete carriers and state that a
future receipt normalizes their semantic effect without asserting an already unified storage
shape.

**Execution evidence.** Every reuse assertion resolves to an exact existing field/helper; no
base-projection citation is used to support a top-level field that is not universally emitted.

## 4. Standing after revision

R1 is the only blocking repair. R2-R10 are load-bearing material corrections. Executing all ten
would preserve the work's strongest result while making its scope honest:

- no current canonical scalar is justified;
- exact bounded reconstruction remains a genuinely number-free predicate;
- prefix discipline remains adaptive and falsifiable;
- public-administration semantics remain load-bearing; and
- existing projection/public-export owners remain the substrate.

R11-R13 improve reproducibility and citation precision but do not decide the central standing.
None of these items authorizes implementation, publication, owner appointment or a numeric
budget.
