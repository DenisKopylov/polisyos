# INT-R9 — Recommended Revisions

## 1. Revision standing

The audited result is `NO_GO`, not because prospective governance is impossible, but because the
present protocol's central family-wise accounting statement is false or at least unproved under the
canonical source. Consolidation must not “fix” that with editorial language while retaining the
same three-slot numeric claim.

The ordered actions below state properties and evidence that a revised research result must
establish. They deliberately do not choose a package, schema, algorithm, service, owner, or
cryptographic primitive.

## 2. Blocking revisions — re-research required

### R1 — Prove or withdraw the single-`delta` claim for three distinct slots

**Findings:** `INT-R9-C-001`, `INT-R9-D-001`.

The revised research must begin from the actual source facts:

- N9's only admissible risk scope is derived for one problem binding;
- scope identity includes `design-problem:<design_problem_id>`;
- ordinals and prior spend are computed from current-scope events; and
- each selected fresh case is naturally a distinct design problem.

Before the protocol can claim one cumulative `delta`, the revised work must provide independently
checkable evidence for all of the following:

1. the exact family event being controlled is stated—specifically the event that any slot in the
   precommitted stop-on-first-positive sequence falsely promotes;
2. the relationship between the family and the three canonical N9 problem scopes is explicit;
3. three distinct design-problem IDs cannot each obtain a fresh unaccounted top-level budget;
4. refused, void, disputed, and completed earlier slots have a defined effect that cannot be
   bypassed by opening the next canonical scope;
5. the aggregate allocation or composition satisfies the declared bound under stated maintained
   assumptions;
6. the proof remains valid under adaptive continuation and the permitted between-slot revision
   policy, or the public claim is narrowed to exclude such a theorem;
7. the mechanism reuses or extends a canonical owner rather than creating a second confidence
   ledger; and
8. a verifier can reproduce the composition from live source/artifacts rather than from an
   author-written INT-R9 record.

#### Mandatory falsifier

A revised result fails if this fully compliant trace can still pass:

```text
slot 1 -> design-problem A -> scope A -> ordinal 0 -> delta
slot 2 -> design-problem B -> scope B -> ordinal 0 -> delta
slot 3 -> design-problem C -> scope C -> ordinal 0 -> delta
stop on first positive
```

A sentence saying “same cumulative scope” is not closure evidence if canonical N9 still produces
three scope IDs.

#### Permissible research outcomes

The re-research may establish a valid bounded composition, may narrow the numeric claim, or may
conclude that the three-slot design is incompatible with the current owner. A negative result is
acceptable. What is not acceptable is retaining `P(false first promotion) <= delta` by implication.

### R2 — Correct the result standing before any consolidation

**Finding:** `INT-R9-I-003`.

Until R1 is independently resolved, the artifact standing must be `blocked` or an equivalent
research-blocked disposition. `accepted_narrow_scope` incorrectly tells downstream work that the
load-bearing protocol is ready for adoption.

This is not merely frontmatter cleanup. Every executive, YAML, handoff, and final-answer sentence
that says cumulative accounting exists must be brought into the same blocked standing.

## 3. Material revisions — governance closure required after R1

### R3 — Make the adaptive-revision claim explicit

**Findings:** `INT-R9-C-002`, `INT-R9-D-002`.

The revised result must choose and state one honest claim boundary:

- either the decisive sequence evaluates one fixed implementation revision; or
- it is an adaptive development sequence in which later revisions may learn from earlier public
  failures.

If adaptive revisions remain permitted, the work must close the currently open distinction between
“general” and “case-specific” repair before a favorable later outcome can affect that distinction.
At minimum, the research must name the decision time, evidence, conflict rule, and consequence of
an unresolved classification. It must not pretend a later positive is a repeated look at the same
implementation.

#### Falsifier

A repair written without literal case identifiers but designed directly from a revealed failed
case must not automatically qualify as general merely because its code applies to a wider class.

### R4 — Close the materiality decision right prospectively

**Finding:** `INT-R9-D-003`.

Materiality controls whether dissent, source flips, obligation removals, deviations, and challenges
block a positive. The revised research must identify how a materiality decision becomes
checkable before its direction is known, how conflicts are handled, and what happens if the
question cannot be resolved.

This does not authorize appointment of a new owner. It requires consolidation to map the decision
to an existing competent owner or record the capability as unresolved.

#### Falsifier

An adverse dissent seen after voting cannot be made non-material by a friendly assessor under a
new rationale that was unavailable before sealing.

### R5 — Preserve, but narrow, the new-case independence claim

**Finding:** `INT-R9-E-002`.

The revised report must distinguish:

- answer secrecy;
- implementation-side non-access;
- author/implementation role separation;
- random selection within a committed pool; and
- independence of the pool's substantive construction from known system strengths.

The present design establishes the first four more strongly than the fifth. If the same unit
creates cases and answer packages, the public claim must retain the residual pool-author selection
boundary. A random draw from a purposive pool is not a random draw from policy space.

#### Falsifier

A case unit that intentionally authors all six pairs around mechanisms already known to work, while
otherwise complying fully, must not yield the statement “case selection was free of upstream
tractability judgment.”

### R6 — Convert independence dimensions from declarations into evidence requirements

**Finding:** `INT-R9-F-002`.

Preserve named humans, signatures, alternates, raw votes, no synthetic substitution, and the hard
readiness block. Then specify which independence dimensions require corroborating evidence rather
than self-declaration and how disclosed same-network, same-funder, reporting, governance, or
reputational ties affect standing.

The revised research need not claim that covert collusion can be disproved. It must prevent
“independent” from being computed solely from five friendly signatures saying no disqualifying
conflict exists.

#### Falsifier

Three friendly people with no direct code/case/criteria contribution, no direct line management,
and no contingent pay—but a shared organizational stake in success—must receive an explicit,
auditable disposition rather than automatic qualification.

### R7 — Narrow the anti-abstention claim

**Finding:** `INT-R9-G-002`.

Retain the public positive control, known-groundable seeds, exact refusal reasons, acquisition
records, and equal publication. Recast them as detectors of mechanical or unsupported refusal,
not proof that abstention is no longer dominant.

The report already admits it does not solve grounding economics. Its headings, YAML, executive
summary, and consolidation kernel must not imply otherwise.

#### Falsifier

A system that passes all public controls and gives precise owner-backed reasons while refusing all
three unseen cases remains compliant. The revised claim must survive that fact.

### R8 — Reconcile the useful-design denominator without redefining it

**Finding:** `INT-R9-G-003`.

The prose says a slot enters the denominator once inspected. The YAML says every precommitted slot
is included. Consolidation must use the existing canonical metric definition and state how
uninspected, retired-before-inspection, void, refused, disputed, and successful slots map to it.

INT-R9 is not authorized to create a new denominator. If the existing metric cannot express the
protocol chronology, record the interface gap instead of fixing it in YAML.

### R9 — Bind the actual INT-R1 result

**Finding:** `INT-R9-H-002`.

Replace the placeholder `ObligationSetDeclaration` interface with the delivered
`ObligationCoverageEnvelope` or an explicitly consolidated successor. Preserve INT-R1's exact
semantics:

- `bounded_complete` is relative to a named basis/language/cutoff and never the world;
- `known_incomplete` blocks the affected protected action;
- material `open_world_unresolved` blocks the affected protected action;
- candidate work may continue only with the ratified limitation; and
- a narrower protected action is a new prospective scope, not a post-result edit.

#### Falsifier

A slot that reveals a material coverage gap must not pass by narrowing the same scored claim after
inspection.

### R10 — Constrain the S0-GAP-02 “equivalent” language

**Finding:** `INT-R9-H-003`.

Preserve the strong reuse/defer table. Clarify that any replacement for S0-GAP-02 must be an
explicitly governed canonical supersession, not an INT-R9-local equivalent service or schema.

## 4. Material scope correction — YAML

### R11 — Demote the YAML from de facto executable contract

**Finding:** `INT-R9-I-001`.

The current file fixes enough identifiers, fields, literals, counts, blockers, state transitions,
and vote rules to function as a loadable protocol. That is unsafe while R1, repair classification,
materiality, INT-R1 mapping, denominator semantics, and institutional evidence remain open.

Before consolidation, the structured artifact must either:

- be clearly reduced to non-executable research examples with unresolved alternatives and no
  conformance standing; or
- be withheld until the unresolved research questions have accepted owners and an independently
  audited consolidated contract.

This recommendation does not choose a final serialization. It prevents an unresolved research
shape from becoming implementation law by convenience.

#### Falsifier

An implementer must not be able to load the current YAML, satisfy its fixed literals, and claim
first-promotion protocol conformance while D-001 remains open.

Consolidation should not trade away the following strengths while repairing the defects:

1. ua-msme remains ineligible as decisive primary or adjacent evidence;
2. all current cases remain public regression/calibration, never retroactive holdout;
3. no positive result is promised;
4. negative, void, disputed, exhausted, and no-attempt terminals remain publishable;
5. named natural-person adjudication is mandatory;
6. synthetic/model/role-only reviewers never satisfy decisive independence;
7. source-flip and obligation-removal remain semantic properties, not fixture IDs;
8. no-case-specific-code review includes historical binding provenance and departed authors;
9. public controls never enter real-promotion numerator;
10. passage remains bounded to named revision/environment/cases/assumptions/evaluator/protocol;
11. S0-GAP-02 retains generic oracle/evaluator custody ownership;
12. existing N9, confidence, CTM, and status owners remain canonical; and

## 8. Acceptance and kill rules

### Accept for consolidation only if

- R1 is independently validated or the numeric family claim is honestly withdrawn/narrowed;
- the result standing matches that outcome;
- all material decision rights are prospective and checkable;
- INT-R1 and S0-GAP-02 seams map to actual delivered owners/artifacts;
- no parallel ledger, status lattice, or oracle framework appears;
- exact anchors reproduce the facts; and
- a rule-following positive-seeking adversary cannot replay D-001.

### Keep blocked if

- three distinct case scopes can each start with a fresh `delta`;
- “cumulative” remains an author-written field rather than a recomputed owner property;
- a failed slot's risk disappears merely because the next case has a new scope;
- post-result scope narrowing can rescue a failed obligation basis;
- an unresolved materiality classification can be made after its direction is known; or
- the YAML remains executable while the research questions remain open.

### Kill the current three-slot form if

A rigorous re-research result shows that no composition compatible with the canonical owner and
the required bounded claim exists without creating a second ledger or weakening ratified honesty.
That negative result would be a successful research outcome, not project failure.

## 9. Final revision recommendation

Do not revise the audited branch by quietly editing prose and treating this audit as accepted. Land
a distinct research revision, preserve the full chronology, and subject the blocking question to
fresh independent audit before consolidation.

The correct order is:

1. re-research the family-risk claim;
2. set honest result standing;
3. close material governance seams;
4. correct structured-artifact standing;
5. repair factual anchors and orientation; and
6. re-audit.

Until step 1 succeeds or the claim is withdrawn, all later work remains subordinate to `NO_GO`.
