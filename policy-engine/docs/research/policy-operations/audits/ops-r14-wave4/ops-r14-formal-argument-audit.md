# OPS-R14 formal argument audit

## 1. Pass III — the seven-item refusal

The primary report's refusal is at
`ops-r14-custody-resilience-and-expiring-authority.md:31-56`. Each asserted absence was tested against
the pin rather than accepted from the report.

| Refusal premise | Genuine absence or absent-from-view? | Audit judgment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First-class watched dependency with role, lead time, evidence, grace authority, consequence, affected query and public effect | **Genuine at the pin.** Exact source searches found no admitted runtime contract or owner chain; the only Python `renewal` literal is worker-lease documentation. | The negative premise holds. |
| Per-class durable acknowledgement, RPO/RTO and restored predicate | **Genuine.** Runbooks and generic retention material do not define the seven classes, class acknowledgement or `Restored(c, cutoff)`. | The negative premise holds. |
| Complete independently failing cross-store recovery chain | **Genuine.** Useful components and procedures exist, but there is no admitted chain joining control prefix, CAS closure, public log, trust/status, source capture, dependencies and holds. | The negative premise holds. |
| General legal-hold lifecycle | **Genuine.** The snapshot tag/classification/GC path is narrow and tested; issuance, aggregation, release, third-party propagation and cross-store barrier are absent. | The negative premise holds while preserving the narrow implementation. |
| Executed measured disconnected drill | **Genuine for the inspected evidence chain.** Acceptance artifacts show a clean-machine rehearsal, release canary and tabletop, not the frozen corpus/failure injection/restored predicate required here. | The negative premise holds for custody-grade drill evidence. |
| Implemented GY-N12 currentness or PAO-R36 public-change runtime endpoint | **Genuine at the pin.** GY-N12 is a build-new plan contract; PAO-R36 was a parallel research branch, not a pin runtime endpoint. | The negative premise holds. |
| Institutional roles and independent custody commitments | **Genuine and inherently non-repository-only.** No research artifact can appoint or fund the competent institution. | The negative premise holds and must remain an institutional dependency. |

### Is the refusal over-broad?

The operative sentence is narrow: PolicyOS cannot **presently claim** custody-grade resilience
(primary `:31-33`). The final decision also says the recommendation is technically coherent and
checkable but unclaimable at the pin (`:467-479`). That conforms to the Stage-0 authority-band lens:
candidate work and implementation under separate authorization remain possible.

The metadata and terminal line, however, say only `standing: NO_GO`, without an independent field for
“operational capability/gate.” A downstream ledger may read that as rejection of the research result
itself. The work has, in fact, delivered a complete narrow architecture, comparison, semantic
contract, fixtures and source transfer ledger. That distinction is load-bearing.

### OPS-R14-III-001 — blocking — result standing and operational gate are collapsed

**Defect.** Unqualified `NO_GO` metadata conflates two propositions:

1. the research result is acceptable as a bounded architecture; and
2. the repository cannot currently claim/operate that architecture or open the first-public gate.

**Risk.** The first proposition can be lost during consolidation, or the second can be weakened by
relabeling the whole document positive.

**Required correction.** Preserve a positive bounded research standing such as
`accepted_narrow_scope`/`GO_WITH_REVISIONS`, and state a separate exact authority-band result:
`custody_grade_capability_claim = NO_GO` and `first_public_signature_gate = closed`. Equivalent
wording is acceptable; one unqualified status is not.

### OPS-R14-III-002 — commendation — all seven operational absences are real

None of the refusal premises was merely a missed file or a maturity-label shortcut. The report
preserves implemented fragments while refusing the aggregate capability.

### OPS-R14-III-003 — commendation — the body does not prohibit building

The selected architecture, proposed objectives and consolidation sequence are explicit. Hard
prohibitions prevent authorization, not candidate construction or separately authorized engineering.
This is the correct authority-band application.

### OPS-R14-III-004 — commendation — RPO/RTO are genuine systems propositions

`custody-class-objectives-and-recovery-closure.md:31-222` defines:

- acknowledged-event loss rather than backup frequency;
- independent failure domains and exclusions;
- class-specific loss consequences;
- explicit evidence for each objective; and
- `RC-01`–`RC-09` as a clause-by-clause restoration predicate.

The work states exactly what breaks when content and control diverge and how the divergence is
detected. This is the strongest part of the delivery.

## 2. Pass IV — acceptance evidence beyond the commission

At the pin:

- `docs/archive/reports/platform-acceptance.md:15` passes **runbook presence**;
- `:23` passes **retention and restore posture** because policy and runbooks cover it; and
- `:30` passes an **incident/runbook tabletop**.

`platform-acceptance-manual.md:85-95` records the tabletop as reading the alert-to-runbook path and
validating compose syntax. It does not restore an artifact family or measure RPO/RTO.

### OPS-R14-IV-001 — material — “DR closeout evidence” is too broad a characterization

The repository finding is real, but the wording needs precision. The baseline does **not** say that a
paper runbook proves custody-grade DR or `DurablyVerifiableAt`. It says documentation/posture/tabletop
checks pass. The defect is that the acceptance taxonomy lacks an explicit distinction between:

- document/procedure present;
- tabletop completed;
- restore path exercised; and
- custody-grade drill predicates passed.

Calling the existing row “DR closeout evidence” overstates what the baseline itself claims.

**Closure signal.** Register a baseline finding with a stable ID. Close it only when the acceptance
artifact either (a) relabels those rows documentation/tabletop-only and carries a separate non-green
exercise row, or (b) links a retained DE-01–DE-10 drill package and reports its bounded result.

### OPS-R14-IV-002 — commendation — a real evidence-quality gap was found

The work correctly identifies that green documentation/posture rows are insufficient inputs to
PV-K01 durable-verifiability claims. The finding should survive after its wording is narrowed.

### OPS-R14-V-001 — material — the first falsifier is only half closed

WD-12 guarantees that a failed timer does not extend authority. It does **not** establish that expiry
was surfaced prospectively as a governed dependency event. Under a watcher outage, the first visible
symptom can still be the protected request failing at runtime.

Add a checkable proposition over every watched right: before the lead-time/expiry boundary, a durable
due event or explicit missed-event incident must be present in the independent event history; periodic
reconciliation compares all rights approaching/at expiry against emitted events. A late-use refusal
is a safety backstop, not evidence that the scheduling objective was met.

### 3.2 Six structural families

The partition is exhaustive over the eleven classes and disjoint at the stated abstraction:

- 4 external/bilateral instruments;
- 2 technical credentials;
- 2 personal/role competence rights;
- consent;
- budget authority; and
- jurisdiction-pack review.

The families are structurally different because the entity competent to establish the next interval
changes: counterparty/instrument, credential issuer, delegator/certifier, subject, fiscal authority,
or internal currentness owner. These are not mere lead-time parameters.

### OPS-R14-V-002 — minor — “local intent cannot complete renewal” is too categorical

Use “local intent alone cannot establish renewal.” A contract can grant a unilateral option whose
exercise is performed locally by a competent role. The resulting renewal still derives from the
pre-existing instrument and timely competent exercise, not from desire or continued service.

### OPS-R14-V-003 — commendation — six-family reframe is sound

The reframe is exhaustive, materially differentiated and more useful than eleven flat variants. It
should survive consolidation.

### OPS-R14-V-004 — commendation — legal hold is correctly orthogonal

The report distinguishes preservation from authority, access, publication and currentness; aggregates
multiple holds; and requires a fresh disposal decision after final release. It neither invents legal
sufficiency nor lets correction/history be severed.

## 5. Auditor-constructed failures

These are audit fixtures, not implementation designs or new statuses.

### AF-01 — lawful partial split with disputed overlap

**Given** predecessor `O`; successor `A` lawfully inherits subject scope `X`; successor `B` lawfully
inherits `Y`; one signed predecessor record covers `X union Y`; evidence is competent for the
disjoint portions but conflicts over `X intersection Y`.

**When** queries request a record in `X-only`, `Y-only`, and the overlap.

**Expected** original issuer remains `O`; `A` is current custodian only for `X-only`; `B` only for
`Y-only`; overlap current custody is not established; neither successor may re-sign the predecessor
as original issuer. The result is query/scope-specific, not globally pass or fail.

**Detection** compare succession instruments, subject qualification, query scope and currentness
cutoff; assert zero issuer substitution.

### AF-02 — apparent independence with one compromised root

**Given** content, journal, checkpoint and trust/status copies in four services, all administered by
one control account and all checkpointed under one compromised root key.

**When** the operator deletes an event and re-signs internally consistent older heads across all four.

**Expected** recovery must not claim independent custody or latest head. The drill reports common-mode
independence not established, even though every local digest/signature passes.

**Detection** inspect governance/admin/root-key lineage and independently retained observations; a
count of services is not an independence proof.

### AF-03 — authenticated-time rollback across expiry

**Given** a delegation expires at `t_exp`; the scheduler and protected-use gate share a rolled-back or
frozen clock; the event journal still accepts events.

**When** an action is requested after real `t_exp` but the shared clock reports `t_exp - 1`.

**Expected** no authority-positive action. Authority-time evidence is not established until an
independently authenticated/monotonic time source resolves the coordinate; the incident and affected
interval append.

**Detection** compare trusted-time/checkpoint sequence, local wall clock, event effective time and
action count.

### AF-04 — canonicalization/parser differential after preservation migration

**Given** identical original bytes, two retained verifier implementations and one migrated
representation; parser A and parser B derive materially different signed statements while both report
syntactic success.

**When** disconnected verification runs under both preserved environments.

**Expected** historical semantic verification is non-positive/not established; original bytes remain
intact; no current parser silently wins; migration evidence and differential result append.

**Detection** frozen semantic test vectors, parser/version digests, canonical signing-input bytes and
protected-query comparison.

## 6. Pass III–VI conclusion

The argument is not unfalsifiable. Its central predicates and fixtures are observable. The operational
refusal is justified, while the research standing needs separation. The watched-dependency model and
drill contract are strong enough to consolidate after the prospective-event and coverage revisions
above.
