# OPS-R14 hostile independent audit

## 0. Executive verdict

**`GO_WITH_REVISIONS`.**

OPS-R14 is a strong, bounded architecture and a justified negative operational result. It names real
failure domains, defines acknowledged loss rather than backup slogans, supplies an evidence predicate
for restoration, differentiates six institutional renewal propositions, preserves PV-K02 under every
major replay/hold failure, and gives unusually executable disaster/drill specifications. Its public-
administration grounding is materially stronger than typical IT-DR research.

The delivery does **not** conform as written because its unqualified artifact standing `NO_GO`
collapses two different propositions:

1. the research architecture is a valid narrow result; and
2. the pinned repository cannot presently claim or operate custody-grade resilience, report a
   positive `DurablyVerifiableAt`, or open the first-public-signature gate.

The second proposition is correct. The first is not a refusal. This is a blocking consolidation defect
under the Stage-0 authority-band lens. The exact repair is R1 in the revision register.

No audit finding supports opening a gate or claiming runtime capability. After the required revisions,
the appropriate bounded research standing is positive while the operational capability/gate remains
`NO_GO`.

## 2. Competence-by-competence assessment

### Engineering — strong with two material gaps

Mechanisms are real and named: content-addressed store, immutable control journal, independent
checkpoint observations, signing-time status, verifier closure, hold barriers, authority-time gates,
affected-set queries, duplicate suppression and clean disconnected restore. Almost every rule states
its checker, input and outcome.

Material gaps:

- prospective expiry-event delivery/reconciliation is not separately measured from fail-closed use
  checking (`OPS-R14-V-001`); and
- one capability row uses a noncanonical label (`OPS-R14-IX-001`).

The research remains prose-level and does not accidentally freeze a wire/schema.

### Mathematical / systems — very strong

`custody-class-objectives-and-recovery-closure.md:31-222` supplies:

- a loss model with independently failing domains and explicit exclusions;
- durable acknowledgement as an independent-boundary proposition;
- class-specific RPO/RTO based on consequence, not one blanket number;
- `Restored(c, cutoff)` as `RC-01`–`RC-09`; and
- exact invariants/detectors when control, content, indexes, logs, trust and heads diverge.

This competence passes at the highest level. Remaining fixture gaps attack premises the model currently
assumes—independence, trusted time and canonical interpretation—not missing basic mathematics.

### Public administration — strong with one material transfer correction

The work correctly treats:

- bilateral agreement versus credential;
- delegation/certification versus subject consent;
- fiscal authority versus internal review currentness;
- a hold as preservation rather than authority;
- contract/audit/exit evidence as institutional dependencies; and
- archival/FOI/continuity/procurement rules as scoped regimes rather than generic TTLs.

The source ledger uses official U.S. and UK primary sources and honest transfer limits. The one
material problem is that procurement audit-right/exit/survival propositions are stated too generally;
they are instrument-specific inferences, not universal effects of FAR 4.805 or Procurement Act s.98
(`OPS-R14-II-001`).
