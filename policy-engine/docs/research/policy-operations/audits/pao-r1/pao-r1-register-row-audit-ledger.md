commit-pinned evidence and verdict applies in both columns. A letter `I` is not treated

## Ledger-wide runtime verification limit

Static review cannot establish the real institution, legal competence, finality,
partner-system authentication, production routing, external act performance or public
legal effect for any external row. A local DTO/test proves only its stated local
property. Every external row remains `external_verification_required`; every named
generic operator is provisional until a jurisdiction and pilot system are supplied.
