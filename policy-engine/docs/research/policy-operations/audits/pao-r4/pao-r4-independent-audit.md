# PAO-R4 hostile independent audit

## 1. Executive verdict

**Independent verdict: `NO_GO` for adoption of the current research contract.**

This is not a verdict that the research is empty. It contains three results that should survive
consolidation:

1. the explicit distinction among export-context, downstream-use and unobservable violations;
2. the proof that voluntary reporting cannot establish a complete non-use firewall because silence
   is observationally identical under compliant and violating worlds; and
3. refusal of individually actionable classes when no complete observation boundary can exist.

The current `GO_WITH_REVISIONS` standing is nevertheless too high because three defects strike the
commission's core rather than later implementation:

- the formal claim class conflates empirical population summaries with normative general rules, so
  its non-entailment is false over admitted objects;
- completeness of `B`/`L` and “material contribution” are assumed or self-reported, so the claimed
  semantic violation is not yet decidable; and
- F-01 declares the prohibited eligibility purpose in advance and therefore does not test the
  commission's silent allowed-purpose-to-individual-use drift.

R1–R10 in `pao-r4-recommended-revision.md` provide a bounded path to reconsideration. The architect
retains adoption authority.

## 4. What consolidation should preserve

### 4.1 Preserve without weakening

- denied-use monotonicity inherited from `PV-K04`;
- the material-reliance insight from C-634/21, without turning it into a compliance claim;
- the distinction between population probability and individual fact for empirical claims;
- the detection-location partition, refined rather than discarded;
- mandatory, reconciled returning evidence for use-time claims;
- `not_established` on absence or incomplete history;
- refusal of pointwise/resolvable classes when use cannot be observed;
- prerequisite-safe missing-state vocabulary;
- the narrow correction interface obligation.

### 4.2 Do not preserve as written

- the universal formal non-entailment over normative rules;
- the claim that `B`, `L`, auxiliary completeness and material contribution are already decidable;
- the unqualified “not weaker” comparison;
- the blanket executable-rule refusal;
- F-01 as the commission's silent-use test;
- canonical placement of the case-handoff gate in `public_export.py` without an owner decision.

## 5. Standing effect

The three blocking findings are independent: repairing F-01 does not repair the formal object class;
repairing the formal class does not make the counterfactual observable; and improving returning
evidence does not fix the request-time-only falsifier. Current adoption is therefore `NO_GO`, not
`GO_WITH_REVISIONS`. The recommended revision register identifies executable closure evidence rather
than asking for a rewrite by assertion.
