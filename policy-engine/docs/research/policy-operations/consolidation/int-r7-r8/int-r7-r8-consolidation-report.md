## 3. What the wave establishes

### 3.1 Public verification is a vector, not a signature bit

A mathematically valid detached signature is necessary for issuer-issuance authenticity and insufficient for an unqualified public `Verified` state. The controlling INT-R7 decomposition reports separately:

- `IssuerIssuanceAuthentic`;
- `ProjectionFaithful`;
- `PublicHistoryEstablished`;
- `DurablyVerifiableAt(t_v)`;
- `CurrentAuthorityAsOf(t_q)`;
- status-snapshot selection; and
- public evidence obtainability.

The point-of-use navigation and split are controlling at `int-r7/threat-model-and-verification-predicates.md:793-1024`; the profile composition is summarized at `int-r7-public-verification-lifecycle.md:35-69` and controlled in §21.

A current positive result is conjunctive and bounded to the identified evidence and query time. Historical authenticity is not erased by later withdrawal, and historical authenticity does not imply current authority. Negative and superseded states remain completed public results rather than disappearing records.

### 3.2 INT-R7 establishes a construction-neutral composite profile

The minimum research profile combines, without selecting vendors or formats:

1. domain-separated signing of the complete semantic statement;
2. institutional authority, audience, jurisdiction, claim-class and authority-boundary evidence;
3. trusted issuance time and retained signing-time status;
4. append-only transparency history;
5. independent common-view corroboration against split view;
6. GY-N12 as the sole epoch/currentness owner;
7. INT-R8 as the retained-claim/projection-relation owner;
8. long-term validation/preservation with timely re-anchoring;
9. independently authenticated offline verification closure; and
10. separate reporting of historical authenticity and current authority.

The profile is an authority-band contract. It does not fix algorithms, witness counts, credential syntax, certificate policy, log operator, preservation service, wire representation, or institutional delegation.

### 3.3 INT-R8 establishes use-relative conservative semantic parity

Semantic parity is not byte equality and is not a claim that every audience sees identical text. It is protected-query parity under a declared use package and a non-amplifying authority relation. A shorter public object is acceptable only when its surfaced claims remain traceable, governed decisions are equal or more conservative, denied uses do not shrink, material negative terminals and contestability remain visible, omissions are typed with governed effects, and unresolved model/materiality/history/verifier inputs block. The controlling statement is in `int-r8-compression-loss-and-disclosure.md:147-199` and its semantic contract in `int-r8/semantic-contract-and-loss-boundary.md:429-459`.

`CompressionLossReceipt` extends existing projection, omission, redaction, contest, recourse, denied-use and audit owners. It does not create a parallel confidence ledger, fifth audience, or global status lattice.

### 3.4 INT-R8 establishes categorical material-loss anchors

Three omissions are always material in the governed cases:

- a `delta` without its declared obligation set, maintained assumptions and relative basis;
- a hidden negative terminal or equivalent completed refusal/void/dispute/exhaustion result; and
- a no-number custody claim missing a unique constitutive event or required order relation.

These anchors are applications of already-ratified `INT-K02`, `INT-K08`, and `INT-K06`. They are not a new outcome vocabulary.

### 3.5 Reconstruction is bounded and typed

For a declared finite/enumerable, declared-decidable, or proved-conservative model, INT-R8 uses the consistency set `C(T)` to test whether a protected predicate is uniquely reconstructed across declared audiences, coalitions or release history. The executable contract at `int-r8-compression-loss-and-disclosure.md:201-247` requires typed dispositions:

- exact non-reconstruction;
- reconstruction;
- model/observation inconsistency;
- timeout;
- unsupported theory;
- conservative risk found;
- proved-conservative discharge; or
- unowned/unproved approximation.

No timeout, empty set, unsupported theory, heuristic, sampling result, or unproved approximation may inherit a safe verdict. The frozen suite establishes 30 red families, 71 atomic red rows, five green families, seven green controls, 78 total rows, and 0/78 routes by which an unproved approximation can inherit safety.

### 3.6 A numerical disclosure-composition budget is refused

The defensible result is:

> No canonical numerical disclosure-composition claim is justified for the current PolicyOS release path under any model established in the pinned repository.

Determinism is not the obstruction. Quantitative-information-flow and leakage families can value deterministic channels when a secret/channel/support or prior/gain model, local validity conditions, composition theorem and accountable owner exist. Those premises do not exist in the pinned repository. Therefore the wave does not authorize an epsilon, percentage, leakage score, remaining budget, or cumulative safety number.

The accepted alternative is prefix discipline: a Boolean `INT-K06` custody claim over a versioned declared release family, evaluated prospectively on each controlled candidate prefix with reproducible membership, chronology, model versions, inputs and dispositions. It proves enforcement of the declared checks, not universal privacy, attack completeness, legal compliance, or competence.

### 3.7 The cross-seam proof-metadata channel is real

INT-R8 demonstrates that proof machinery can leak record content through joins over key identifiers, certificate paths, transparency-log positions, witness sets, commitment identifiers, proof-object sizes and linkage patterns. INT-R7's controlling profile responds at requirement level by requiring privacy-safe addressing and by forbidding proof metadata from becoming a protected-value oracle (`int-r7/public-verification-profile.md:649-670`; `int-r8/reconstruction-composition-and-threat-model.md:425-436`).

The research establishes the attack and the obligation. It does not establish a particular mitigation or prove that a future construction passes.

## 5. Repository defects and capability gaps established by the wave

Research conclusions and repository defects are kept separate. The detailed closure signals are in `int-r7-r8-repository-findings-register.md`.

### 5.1 Live signature-lifecycle defect

At the pinned baseline, `core/artifacts/signing.py` signs artifact ID, blob/manifest digests and key ID while `signed_at` and `signer_identity` remain outside the signed statement; revocation is timeless key-ID membership (`signing.py:53-94,291-302,389-411,469-517,539-683`). The verifier cannot distinguish a genuine pre-compromise record from a post-compromise forgery backdated with attacker-controlled sidecar metadata.

This is an engineering defect with a future closure signal. It is not authorization for a patch or wire-format choice.

### 5.2 Unsigned/forgeable public presentation path

`runtime/quality/public_export.py` produces the real public bundle and has no public proof issuance. The dashboard packet uses a source-visible salt and 32-bit FNV-1a token, and the viewer presents browser recomputation as `Verified` (`int-r7-public-verification-lifecycle.md:105-123`). The producer exists; the proof producer, temporal/authority verifier and safe production bridge do not.

The correct missing-state classification is `bridge_missing` for the known producer-to-intended-route connection, not `producer_missing` for the export bundle itself.

### 5.3 Compression-loss capability is contract-only

The existing `projection_semantics.py` already owns omission/redaction/projection gaps, limitations, contested records, recourse, audience visibility and authority-laundering checks. `public_export.py` contains omission and projection-faithfulness vocabulary but no controlling lossy/material-loss receipt. There is no disclosure/composition/privacy/compression budget owner. GY-PA3 remains planned and `producer_missing`; no production caller or HTTP route wires the current public-export producer into the intended public path.
