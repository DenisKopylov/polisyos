## 3. What the wave establishes

### 3.1 Public verification is a vector, not a signature bit

A mathematically valid detached signature can establish only part of a governed public result. The controlling INT-R7 decomposition requires separately reportable propositions for:

- `IssuerIssuanceAuthentic`;
- `ProjectionFaithful`;
- `PublicHistoryEstablished`;
- `DurablyVerifiableAt(t_v)`;
- `CurrentAuthorityAsOf(t_q)`;
- status-snapshot selection; and
- public evidence obtainability.

The point-of-use navigation and split are controlling at `int-r7/threat-model-and-verification-predicates.md:793-1024`; the profile is summarized in `int-r7-public-verification-lifecycle.md:35-69` and controlled by its terminal §21.

Historical authenticity and current authority are non-erasing and distinct. Authenticated withdrawal, revocation, supersession, stale currentness, or loss of mandate can make current authority non-positive without rewriting an authentic historical record. Historical authenticity never establishes current authority.

### 3.2 INT-R7 establishes a construction-neutral composite profile

The minimum research profile combines:

1. a domain-separated signature over the complete semantic statement;
2. institutional authority, audience, jurisdiction, claim-class, and authority-boundary evidence;
3. trusted issuance time and retained signing-time status;
4. append-only transparency history;
5. independent common-view corroboration against split view;
6. GY-N12 as the sole epoch/currentness owner;
7. INT-R8 as the retained-claim/projection-relation owner;
8. long-term preservation with timely re-anchoring;
9. independently authenticated offline verification closure; and
10. separate historical-authenticity and current-authority results.

The profile binds the authority band. It does not select algorithms, certificate policy, witness count, log operator, timestamp service, archive, key lifecycle format, wire representation, or institutional delegation.

The controlling falsifier suite is `INT-R7-PV-FALSIFIERS-v2`: **23 immutable families and 29 mandatory exact subfixtures**. The source corpus denominator is **32**. The prior complete sweeps remain intact: 29/29 algebra, 31/31 value/status, 9/9 primary revision reachability, 12/12 conforming revisions, and 20/20 commendations.

### 3.3 INT-R7 establishes a live repository defect

At the pinned baseline, `core/artifacts/signing.py` keeps `signed_at` and `signer_identity` outside the signed statement and treats revocation as timeless key-ID membership (`:53-94,291-302,389-411,469-517,539-683`). The present verifier cannot distinguish a genuine pre-compromise signature from a post-compromise forgery accompanied by attacker-controlled backdated metadata. That defect belongs in the repository findings register, not in the ratified semantic kernel.

### 3.4 INT-R8 establishes use-relative conservative semantic parity

Semantic parity is not byte equality. It is protected-query parity under a declared use package and a non-amplifying authority relation. A shorter public object is acceptable only when:

- surfaced claims remain source-resolvable;
- type, basis, scope, assumptions, material conditions, and limitations are preserved;
- governed decisions are equal or more conservative;
- denied uses do not shrink;
- active negative terminals, dissent, contest, recourse, and currentness remain visible;
- dropped items carry governed reason/effect relations; and
- unresolved model, materiality, history, or verifier inputs block.

The controlling statement is in `int-r8-compression-loss-and-disclosure.md:147-199` and the eighteen-item proof interface is in `int-r8/semantic-contract-and-loss-boundary.md:429-459`.

`CompressionLossReceipt` extends existing projection, omission, redaction, contest, recourse, denied-use, and audit owners. It does not create a second confidence ledger, fifth audience, or global status lattice.

### 3.5 Three categorical omission anchors are established

The following omissions are always material in their governed claim classes:

1. a `delta` without its declared obligation set, maintained assumptions, and relative basis;
2. a hidden refusal, void, dispute, terminal no-attempt, exhaustion, or equivalent completed negative; and
3. a no-number custody claim missing a unique constitutive event or required order relation.

These are applications of `INT-K02`, `INT-K08`, and `INT-K06`, not a new outcome vocabulary.

### 3.6 Reconstruction is executable only under bounded model classes

INT-R8 formalizes cross-view reconstruction as a consistency set `C(T)` with strict-coalition reconstruction. A governed safe result is executable only for a declared finite/enumerable model, a declared-decidable model, or an abstraction with a proved no-false-safe direction for the exact obligation.

Exact dispositions include reconstruction, non-reconstruction, model/observation inconsistency, empty consistency set, timeout, unsupported theory, conservative risk found, proved-conservative discharge, and unowned/unproved approximation. Timeout, unsupported theory, incomplete history, heuristic sampling, empty set, or unproved approximation cannot inherit a safe verdict.

The controlling suite has **30 red families / 71 atomic red rows / 5 green families / 7 green controls / 78 total rows**, with **0/78** routes by which an unproved approximation inherits safety. Table structure, not prose merely naming fixture IDs, defines the denominator.

### 3.7 A numerical disclosure-composition budget is refused

The defensible result is:

> No canonical numerical disclosure-composition claim is justified for the current PolicyOS release path under any model established in the pinned repository.

Determinism is not the obstruction. Quantitative-information-flow families can value deterministic channels when a secret/channel/support or prior/gain model, locally valid measure, applicable composition theorem, prospectively enforced custody, selection-valid adaptive conditions, canonical owner, and named protected consumer exist. Those premises do not exist in the pinned repository.

The accepted alternative is **prefix discipline**: a Boolean `INT-K06` procedural claim over a versioned declared release family under custody. It handles adaptive next-release choice without assuming independence and carries no numerical privacy, leakage, performance, compliance, or universal-channel guarantee.

### 3.8 The proof-metadata channel is established

INT-R8 demonstrates that key identifiers, credential or certificate paths, commitment identifiers, transparency-log positions, witness sets, proof-object sizes, and linkage patterns can reconstruct protected record content. INT-R7 answers at requirement level by requiring privacy-safe addressing and preventing proof metadata from becoming a protected-value oracle (`int-r7/public-verification-profile.md:649-670`; `int-r8/reconstruction-composition-and-threat-model.md:425-436`).

The wave establishes the attack and candidate-acceptance obligation. It does not establish a mitigation or prove that an absent future proof construction passes.

## 4. Bidirectional seam result

INT-R7 requests from INT-R8:

1. stable retained-claim-set commitment;
2. deterministic/well-defined projection-redaction proof relation;
3. policy/version identity;
4. typed pass/failure outcome;
5. successor relation when retained content changes; and
6. evidence sufficient for offline proof.

INT-R8 supplies all six through its versioned semantic inventory, exact transformed-object and reason relation, model/rule identities, typed dispositions, predecessor/current/superseded relations, and reproducible evidence package. “Deterministic” controls the result relation for supplied inputs; it does not require one canonical deterministic projection generator.

Conversely, INT-R7 can bind all eighteen INT-R8 requirements: source revision; audience/surface; exact transformed object; retained set; omissions/reasons/effects; loss outcome/rule version; uses and denied uses; materiality package; inventory completeness; constitutive procedure; record/consistency model; channel/release-family version; coalition/delegation model; auxiliary assumptions; transcript head/completeness; exact/empty/timeout/unsupported/abstraction/approximation status; current/superseded state; and the unchanged projection-only authority boundary.

A dynamic solver status is not sufficient offline evidence by itself. The retained artifact must bind the exact inputs, model/rule versions, transcript, evaluator identity as applicable, completeness disposition, and historical result so that it can be reproduced or challenged later. A later exact result is a successor evaluation, not a rewrite of the earlier timeout or unsupported result.

INT-R7's provisional disconnected/offline semantic gap is therefore **superseded and closed**. The implementation remains absent and is separately classified as engineering/custody work.
