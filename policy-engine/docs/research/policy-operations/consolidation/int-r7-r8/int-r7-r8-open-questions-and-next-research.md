## 6. Additional research — no active commission

### NXR-01 — Candidate-specific proof-metadata mitigation

**Standing:** `dormant`.

**Research question**

> For a concrete proposed public-proof construction that otherwise satisfies INT-R7, what transformation, grouping or cryptographic design can prevent key identifiers, credential/certificate paths, commitment identifiers, log positions, witness sets, proof sizes and linkage patterns from reconstructing protected predicates under the declared coalition and auxiliary-information model, while preserving offline verification and the proof/content authority boundary?

**Why this is not editorial**

Changing labels or disclaimers cannot alter the information carried by proof topology, identifier correlation or object size. The obligation requires a construction-specific information/reconstruction argument and falsifiers.

**Why existing tasks do not own it now**

- INT-R8 established the attack class, channel obligation and exact/proved-conservative acceptance rule; it deliberately did not design mitigation.
- INT-R7 established proof requirements and the no-oracle boundary; it deliberately remained construction-neutral.
- DS12 owns implementation evaluation, not an abstract research commission before a candidate exists.
- GY-PA3 owns semantic loss receipts, not cryptographic metadata mitigation.

**Activation trigger**

Activate only when a concrete DS12 proof candidate fails RC-09 or cannot demonstrate a proved-safe metadata/channel treatment with the declared model.

**Required evidence/deliverables after activation**

- exact candidate construction and public metadata inventory;
- protected predicates, coalition/delegation model and auxiliary-information assumptions;
- exact or proved-conservative reconstruction analysis;
- offline-verifiability and currentness/preservation impact;
- comparative mitigations and their leakage/availability trade-offs;
- falsifier suite covering identifiers, paths, positions, witness sets, sizes and joins;
- independent audit and conformance verification.

**What it blocks after activation**

Admission of that concrete proof candidate as a governed public proof. It does not block other candidates or the current research-input closure.

**What it does not authorize**

Padding, batching, encryption, identifier replacement, log changes, witness changes, a proof format or implementation.

### NXR-02 — Model-specific numerical disclosure composition

**Standing:** `dormant`.

**Research question**

> For a named future product use that genuinely needs a numerical disclosure claim, what explicit secret/channel/support or prior/gain model, local measure, selection-valid composition rule, custody regime, canonical owner and protected consumer justify a bounded number without replacing prefix discipline or creating a second confidence owner?

**Why this is not editorial**

A numerical claim requires mathematical premises and prospectively valid local evidence. Wording, a dashboard counter or a budget field cannot establish those premises.

**Why existing tasks do not own it now**

- INT-R8 correctly refuses a current canonical number and does not select a model.
- GY-PA3 owns categorical semantic-loss production, not a future QIF/privacy theorem.
- INT-K04/INT-K07 constrain any composed number but do not supply the domain model.
- DS12 explicitly does not need a number under the current architect default.

**Activation trigger**

Activate only after the architect names a protected product decision that cannot be served by no-number prefix discipline and supplies the intended secret, channel, consumer and decision use.

**Required evidence/deliverables after activation**

- exact model and protected decision;
- local validity and measurement procedure;
- adaptive/selection-valid composition theorem where applicable;
- custody and one-owner integration;
- comparison against deterministic and randomized channels;
- refusal conditions and no-number baseline;
- executable falsifiers and independent audit.

**What it blocks after activation**

Only the requested numerical disclosure/composition claim. It does not block DS12, prefix discipline, categorical material-loss gating or nonnumeric custody.

**What it does not authorize**

An epsilon, score, budget, threshold, privacy promise, owner or implementation.
