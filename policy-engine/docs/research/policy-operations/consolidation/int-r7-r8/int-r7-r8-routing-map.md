# INT-R7 / INT-R8 routing map

## 1. Routing rule

Each surviving conclusion has one primary destination. A plan lane is named only where the pinned plans already identify it as a waiting consumer or dependency. Secondary effects are described after the table but do not create a second primary route.

No route authorizes an edit. The architect decides whether to ratify, amend, commission, sequence or leave a destination unchanged.

## 3. Consumer-specific consequences

### 3.1 Atlas DS12

DS12 may treat all four named research inputs as closeable inputs after architect disposition. It still must:

- consume the vector rather than a signature Boolean;
- ensure the FNV/checksum predecessor cannot emit a governed positive;
- reuse the real public-export producer without treating projection as authority;
- consume INT-R8 loss/denied-use dispositions and GY-N12 currentness when those producers exist;
- preserve typed negative and not-established outcomes; and
- carry no numerical disclosure claim.

None of those statements says DS12 may open its gate.

### 3.2 Atlas DS13

DS13 remains the primary later surface for public history, transparency/common-view, challenge, correction, withdrawal, supersession and successor explanation. INT-R7's first-public minimum does not move the full DS13 product lane into DS12.

### 3.3 Atlas DS14

DS14 remains the waiting consumer for cross-view and portable explanation. It must preserve source resolvability, denied uses, active negative terminals, material limitations, contest and recourse without claiming byte equality.

### 3.4 GY-PA3

GY-PA3 remains the single planned receipt-producer lane. It should extend existing projection semantics rather than create a parallel confidence, currentness or status owner. The route does not select receipt storage or API shape.

### 3.5 GY-N12

GY-N12 remains the sole epoch/currentness lane. R7 may bind its outputs; it may not duplicate their semantics.
