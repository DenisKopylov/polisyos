## 2. `INT-GAP-01` — Selection-valid family control for outcome-dependent repair

**Research question**

> Under what exact local theorem, filtration, selector, reach, and pathwise-allocation conditions can
> PolicyOS attach a family-wise false-promotion bound to a sequence in which earlier outcomes may
> change a later implementation, evaluator, evidence set, or procedure, while preserving canonical
> per-problem scopes and the confidence ledger as the single risk owner?

**Why current work is insufficient**

INT-R10 proves the fixed-family union theorem and formalizes a sufficient adaptive theorem, but the
live registry has no useful owner profile satisfying the selected-procedure premise. INT-R9 keeps
outcome-informed repair and therefore withdraws the number. The missing result is not “write down
predictable caps”; it is a theorem and verifier valid for the procedure actually selected from
history (policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r10-family-wise-risk-composition.md:420-515; policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/audits/int-r10/int-r10-revision-verification.md:160-450).

**Why this is not editorial**

Changing prose cannot establish history-conditional validity. A compliant repair selector can alter
the law of the later procedure while every allocation field and chronology marker remains present.

**Why this is not an existing task**

- GY-GAP2 is the engineering declaration/projection/reproduction gap for fixed source-derived
  composition.
- S0-GAP-02 owns evaluator/oracle custody, not statistical validity under outcome-selected repair.
- INT-R9 owns firstness and custody, not the confidence theorem.
- INT-R10 explicitly leaves the useful selected-procedure owner theorem absent.

**Required inputs**

- ratified INT-wave kernel, especially `INT-K04`, `INT-K05`, and `INT-K07`;
- revised INT-R10 theorem and verification;
- Option-B INT-R9 amendment and its eventual conformance verification;
- current confidence-ledger registry, schedule law, theorem-profile refusal machinery, and N9 scope
  derivation;
- GY-GAP2 closure criteria.

**Repository paths**

- `policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/confidence_ledger.py`;
- `policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/promotion_sequence.py`;
- `policy-engine/architecture/production_quality/confidence_ledger.toml`;
- `policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r9-first-promotion-evaluation-protocol.md`;
- `policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r10-family-wise-risk-composition.md`;
- `policy-engine/docs/plans/active/layer3-slices/GY-engine-subordination.md`.

**External domains**

Sequential selective inference; online family-wise control; alpha-spending/alpha-investing; e-process
and test-martingale validity under predictable selection; adaptive data analysis; reusable holdouts;
post-selection inference; filtration and optional-continuation theory; software/configuration lineage
under adaptive experimentation.

**Required comparative models**

1. fixed exact family with all member procedures precommitted;
2. predictable caps with history-selected but conditionally valid local procedures;
3. uniform-over-selector theorem families;
4. sample splitting or fresh-data reset with explicit information barriers;
5. e-value/e-process mergers under valid conditional e-values;
6. selective-inference correction tied to the actual selector;
7. Option B with no number as the mandatory baseline comparator.

**Required deliverables**

- exact controlled event and probability space;
- admissible selector/filtration model;
- sufficient theorem(s), proof, and non-transfer limits;
- mapping to existing confidence-ledger owner profiles or an explicit theorem-profile gap;
- owner-neutral semantic requirements for verification, without final schema or package names;
- adaptive-repair decision table;
- public-claim language and refusal language;
- executable falsifier specification and counterexamples;
- comparison with fixed-family INT-R10 and nonnumeric INT-R9 Option B;
- independent audit and conformance verification before consolidation.

**Falsifiers**

- a later procedure is chosen using prior outcomes but the local theorem was proved only for a
  fixed procedure;
- the selector, reach indicator, or cap is not measurable before the later outcome;
- a local anytime-valid certificate is valid only within a different filtration or null;
- pathwise reached allocation can exceed the family bound;
- the verifier checks markers but not the actual selected implementation/evaluator/evidence lineage;
- a repair is relabelled “general” and thereby escapes selection accounting;
- one local theorem depends on an obligation set or validator standing that INT-R1 marks unresolved.

**Dependencies**

INT-wave ratification; verified INT-R9 amendment; stable GY-GAP2 owner direction; S0-GAP-02 only where
evaluator custody is part of the theorem premises.

**Candidate owner**

`team-architecture` research commission with runtime-quality/confidence-ledger subject-matter input
and an independent mathematical auditor. This is not a canonical runtime owner appointment.

**Activation timing**

Activate only when the architect wants a numeric claim for outcome-dependent continuation. Do not
block Option-B nonnumeric custody, candidate exploration, or fixed-family GY-GAP2 engineering.

**What it blocks**

Any numeric family-wise statement for outcome-dependent repair, including a public first-promotion
risk number.

**What it does not authorize**

Implementation, a theorem profile, a family schema, a positive promotion, use of repaired procedures,
or relaxation of per-problem scope identity.

## 3. `INT-GAP-02` — Constructed independence for bounded obligation coverage

**Research question**

> What minimum technical, organizational, source/data, oracle, incentive, competence, and temporal
> separation must be constructed and evidenced before PolicyOS may rely on the conditional
> relative-inclusion theorem strongly enough to issue `bounded_complete`, and how must common-mode
> dependence, challenge, expiry, and revalidation be represented without creating a new authority
> owner or lattice?

**Why current work is insufficient**

INT-R1 proves only conditional relative inclusion and lists independence dimensions. It explicitly
finds that the repository has no complete independent producer/scorer/governance chain and that a
producer-filled record cannot establish independence. S0-GAP-02 is adjacent but limited to benchmark
oracle/evaluator custody; it is not a complete obligation-coverage independence model
(policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r1-obligation-coverage-and-open-world-completeness.md:35-101; policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/consolidation/stage0/stage0-additional-research-register.md:75-210).

**Why this is not editorial**

Role labels and attestations can be present while the compiler, expected-set author, validator,
reviewer, data source, and line management share the same failure mode. The question is whether the
independence evidence is sufficient and falsifiable, not how to phrase a disclaimer.

**Why this is not an existing task**

- S0-GAP-02 covers custody benchmark evaluator/oracle independence, not obligation-basis selection,
  compiler semantics, validator competence, and source-family coverage across protected actions.
- GY-GAP1 concerns obligation-instance representation.
- GY-GAP2 concerns family risk composition.
- Atlas DS17 is a consumer surface and cannot construct the producer chain it renders.

**Required inputs**

- INT-R1 amended research, audit, verification, and `OM-01`/coverage fixtures;
- Stage-0 K14/K16 and S0-GAP-02 outputs when available;
- identity/custody boundary and custody-time model;
- existing assurance-case, formal-invariant, evidence-spine, claim-registry, acquisition-planner,
  candidate-firewall, N9/N11/N12, and Atlas contracts;
- GY-GAP1 identity design once available.

**Repository paths**

- `policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r1-obligation-coverage-and-open-world-completeness.md`;
- `policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r1/artifact-and-state-machine-sketch.md`;
- `policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r1/benchmark-and-edge-case-fixtures.md`;
- `policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/assurance_case.py`;
- `policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/formal_invariants.py`;
- `policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/evidence_spine.py`;
- `policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/claim_registry.py`;
- `policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/acquisition_planner.py`;
- `policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/candidate_firewall.py`;
- GY and Atlas plans.

**External domains**

Audit independence and reperformance; conformity assessment; safety assurance and defeaters;
N-version/clean-room verification; organizational common-cause failure; separation of duties and
dual control; metrology and laboratory competence; benchmark/evaluator custody; source provenance;
public-sector accountability and challenge rights.

**Required comparative models**

1. same-team second implementation;
2. clean-room independent compiler/scorer;
3. independent expected-set/source-basis author plus separate validator;
4. external competent assessor with retained challenge and raw evidence;
5. dual-control approval with disclosed common-mode dependencies;
6. S0-GAP-02 evaluator custody reused only for its valid scope;
7. producer self-attestation as the mandatory negative comparator.

**Required deliverables**

- independence dimension model and evidence grades;
- competence, conflict, common-mode, access, incentive, and temporal-separation criteria;
- minimum sufficient profiles by protected-use consequence;
- source/basis, compiler, validator, scorer, governance, and challenge responsibility map using
  existing owners where possible;
- currentness, expiry, succession, and revalidation rules;
- public/internal projection requirements;
- noncanonical semantic handoff rather than a frozen schema;
- falsifier and mutation package;
- decision table mapping evidence to existing statuses and authority-band consequences;
- independent audit and conformance verification.

**Falsifiers**

- two components have different names but share the same code path, reducer, expected set, or
  author;
- an “independent” reviewer reports to or is rewarded by the evaluated implementer;
- the scorer receives answer-bearing material before commitment;
- shared source selection creates an unreported common-mode omission;
- competence is asserted but not evidenced for the obligation family and jurisdiction;
- challenge evidence cannot suspend current reliance;
- independence expires or ownership changes without revalidation;
- the result mints a new status or owner instead of feeding the existing lattice.

**Dependencies**

INT-wave ratification; S0-GAP-02 outputs where reusable; GY-GAP1 semantic identity direction;
identity/custody and custody-time decisions.

**Candidate owner**

`team-architecture` research commission with audit/governance, runtime-quality, and relevant external
competence input, followed by an independent auditor. No canonical producer is appointed here.

**Activation timing**

Activate before any plan or implementation proposes issuance of `bounded_complete`. It need not
block honest `open_world_unresolved`, conditional relative-inclusion research, candidate work, or
ordinary non-authority diagnostics.

**What it blocks**

Current or future `bounded_complete` issuance and any public claim that independence has discharged
the obligation-coverage assumptions.

**What it does not authorize**

A coverage schema, owner service, benchmark passage, legal-compliance claim, promotion, or assertion
that every obligation has been found.
