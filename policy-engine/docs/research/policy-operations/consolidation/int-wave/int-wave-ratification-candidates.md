# INT wave ratification candidates

## Ratification lens

A statement is proposed only when it binds the authority band and leaves candidate-band work available under explicit limitation (policy-engine/docs/system-design-decisions/stage0-custody-kernel-ratification.md:164-212). These are candidates for architect action; this file does not ratify them.

## INT-K01 — Relative obligation completeness

**Band bound:** `Authority band only`.

**Precise wording**

> No finite trace certifies global obligation completeness while an observationally invisible decisive extension remains admissible. Any positive completeness statement is relative to a declared, content-bound basis, obligation language, scope, purpose, audience, and cutoff, and every protected use carries an evidenced closure disposition.

**Supporting sources:** `policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r1-obligation-coverage-and-open-world-completeness.md:35-101; policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r1-obligation-coverage-and-open-world-completeness.md:300-432; policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/audits/int-r1/int-r1-independent-audit.md:31-73`.

**Known limitation:** It does not prove that every PolicyOS domain is open or that any competent basis is actually exhaustive.

**What it constrains:** Public and internal authority claims about completeness; fail-closed handling of the affected protected use.

**What it does not decide:** The schema, producer, owner, evidence source, or institutional competence for a closure basis.

**Supersession trigger:** A later ratified theorem or competent institutional model that soundly establishes stronger closure for a named domain.

## INT-K02 — Declared-set conditional for every delta claim

**Band bound:** `Authority band only`.

**Precise wording**

> A PolicyOS delta statement is incomplete and may not be projected as authority unless it identifies the declared obligation set and maintained assumptions and visibly carries the relative-basis rider. Delta is not a probability that no applicable obligation was omitted.

**Supporting sources:** `policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r1-obligation-coverage-and-open-world-completeness.md:35-101; policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/confidence_ledger.py:39-52; policy-engine/docs/plans/active/POLICYOS_ATLAS_SURFACE_IMPLEMENTATION_MASTER_PLAN.md:1206-1252; policy-engine/docs/plans/active/POLICYOS_ATLAS_SURFACE_IMPLEMENTATION_MASTER_PLAN.md:1402-1444`.

**Known limitation:** The declared set can still be wrong, stale, or incomplete; the rider exposes rather than repairs that fact.

**What it constrains:** Every public, reviewer, expert, and machine projection of promotion risk.

**What it does not decide:** The numerical value of delta, the obligation compiler, or whether a promotion is otherwise valid.

**Supersession trigger:** A superseding ratified risk semantics that explicitly includes and controls the omitted-obligation event.

## INT-K03 — Constructed independence for bounded_complete

**Band bound:** `Authority band only`.

**Precise wording**

> `bounded_complete` may not be issued from declared independence, role labels, a second function name, producer-filled metadata, or a self-authored benchmark. The required independence must be constructed and evidenced across the relevant organization, implementation, source/data, oracle, incentive, competence, and temporal dimensions.

**Supporting sources:** `policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r1-obligation-coverage-and-open-world-completeness.md:35-101; policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/audits/int-r1/int-r1-independent-audit.md:112-121; policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/audits/int-r1/int-r1-amendment-verification.md:31-70`.

**Known limitation:** The wave does not define the final evidence profile or appoint the independent producer.

**What it constrains:** Authority issuance of `bounded_complete` and any claim that the unknown remainder has been bounded adequately.

**What it does not decide:** Whether candidate exploration, ordinary relative inclusion checks, or non-authority diagnostics may run.

**Supersession trigger:** A later ratified independence/evidence model with implemented producers, falsifiers, and independent acceptance.

## INT-K04 — Family bounds require enforced caps and canonical custody

**Band bound:** `Authority band only`.

**Precise wording**

> For a fixed exact family, a family-wise false-promotion statement over several design problems is valid only when the exact reached-member event is declared, valid local bounds are fixed and enforced before member execution, their sum is within the declared family bound, and the canonical owner can reproduce family membership, chronology, current heads, assumptions, and the aggregate statement. Prose asserting a cumulative budget is not an invariant.

**Supporting sources:** `policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r10-family-wise-risk-composition.md:420-515; policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/audits/int-r10/int-r10-revision-verification.md:160-450; policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/promotion_sequence.py:356-374; policy-engine/docs/plans/active/layer3-slices/GY-engine-subordination.md:2440-2476`.

**Known limitation:** The theorem does not establish any local bound, any live family object, or selection validity for outcome-dependent repair.

**What it constrains:** Every numeric authority claim spanning more than one canonical confidence scope.

**What it does not decide:** The exact future record shape, allocation policy, family size, or public UI.

**Supersession trigger:** A superseding ratified composition theorem and owner contract that is at least as conservative and reproducible.

## INT-K05 — Per-problem confidence scopes remain non-resettable and distinct

**Band bound:** `Authority band only`.

**Precise wording**

> Canonical per-design-problem confidence scope identity is the correct primitive and must not be weakened, merged, or replaced merely to obtain composition. A future family relation is a reproducible declaration/projection over existing roots and receipts inside the same confidence owner, not a parent risk scope or second ledger.

**Supporting sources:** `policy-engine/src/polisyos/runtime/quality/promotion_sequence.py:356-374; policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r10-family-wise-risk-composition.md:36-103; policy-engine/docs/plans/active/layer3-slices/GY-engine-subordination.md:2440-2476`.

**Known limitation:** This does not choose a family identity format or prove that a family capability is implemented.

**What it constrains:** Architecture changes to the confidence ledger and N9/N11 relationship.

**What it does not decide:** How many families exist, which consumer requests one, or which allocation strategy is used.

**Supersession trigger:** A later ratified owner redesign that proves equivalent or stronger non-reset and audit properties without parallel authority.

## INT-K06 — Custody claim without risk claim

**Band bound:** `Authority band only`.

**Precise wording**

> A first governed promotion may carry a bounded custody and anti-selection statement even when it carries no sequence-level risk number. The statement may cover prospectivity, firstness, substitutions, chronology, adjudication, deviations, negative terminals, publication, and correction; it must not be projected as statistical family control, population performance, compliance, competence, or production readiness.

**Supporting sources:** `policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r9-first-promotion-evaluation-protocol.md:44-110; policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/audits/int-r9/int-r9-independent-audit.md:31-82`.

**Known limitation:** INT-R9 conformance is still verified-pending and the protocol is not executable at the pinned repository.

**What it constrains:** The meaning of any future first-promotion record and public wording.

**What it does not decide:** That a positive exists, that the cases or humans are available, or that canonical owner gates pass.

**Supersession trigger:** A later verified INT-R9 revision or ratified protocol that preserves the same claim boundary.

## INT-K07 — No numeric adaptive-repair claim without selection-valid local validity

**Band bound:** `Authority band only`.

**Precise wording**

> When earlier outcomes influence a later implementation, evaluator, evidence set, procedure, or allocation, the sequence is adaptive. No family-wise number may be attached unless the canonical owner verifies a local guarantee valid for the actually history-selected procedure and a pathwise aggregate bound; predictable accounting or an anytime-valid label alone is insufficient.

**Supporting sources:** `policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r9-first-promotion-evaluation-protocol.md:44-110; policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r10-family-wise-risk-composition.md:420-515; policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/audits/int-r10/int-r10-revision-verification.md:160-450`.

**Known limitation:** The wave does not prove adaptation impossible; it identifies the premises a valid theorem must meet and records their current absence.

**What it constrains:** Numeric public or internal authority statements for result-informed continuation.

**What it does not decide:** Whether transparent repair and candidate work may continue nonnumerically.

**Supersession trigger:** A later accepted selection-valid theorem, verifier, and live owner profile.

## INT-K08 — Negative completion is a valid governed result

**Band bound:** `Authority band only`.

**Precise wording**

> Refusal, void, dispute, terminal no-attempt, and exhaustion without promotion are valid completed outcomes of a first-promotion protocol. No success quota, deadline, substitution, or public compression may turn the absence of a positive into permission to weaken the gate or hide the chronology.

**Supporting sources:** `policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/int-r9-first-promotion-evaluation-protocol.md:44-110; policy-engine/docs/research/policy-operations/audits/int-r9/int-r9-independent-audit.md:31-82`.

**Known limitation:** This does not prove that every refusal is substantively correct or eliminate strategic abstention.

**What it constrains:** Stopping, publication, and historical custody of first-promotion attempts.

**What it does not decide:** Power, usefulness, representativeness, or a requirement that a positive eventually exist.

**Supersession trigger:** A later ratified protocol that supplies stronger anti-abstention guarantees without forcing authority.
