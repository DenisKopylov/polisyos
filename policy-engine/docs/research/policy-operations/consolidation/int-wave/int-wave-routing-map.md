## Consumer-specific consequences

- **Atlas DS17:** already treats `open_world_unresolved` as an honest steady state and requires basis-visible rendering; no new vocabulary is needed.
- **Atlas DS12:** should consume a verified nonnumeric first-promotion custody protocol and the declared-set rider; it must not imply a numeric INT-R9 family theorem.
- **Atlas DS12 status dependency:** remains on the existing status lattice; no `bounded_complete` shortcut or coverage-specific lattice is introduced.
- **GY-N9:** receives the obligation-instance semantic test through GY-GAP1 and continues to own substantive promotion gating.
- **GY-N11:** remains the only risk-accounting extension lane for family declaration/projection/reproduction.
- **GY-N12:** owns currentness, epoch, challenge, supersession, and reissue effects; old arithmetic may remain historically reproducible while current authority is withdrawn.
- **S0-GAP-02:** remains the benchmark oracle/evaluator custody commission; it is a dependency, not a family-risk owner or universal independence service.
