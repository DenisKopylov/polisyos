# Stage-0 Wave-2 Readiness and Sequencing

## Readiness conclusion

Most remaining Wave-2 **research** can proceed after the documentation
amendments and acceptance of local assumptions below. It need not wait for a
production `PolicyMatter`, a runtime boundary register, H2, or an executable
OPS-R15 benchmark.

H2 architecture must wait for S0-GAP-01 plus outputs from OPS-R1/2/3/4 and
INT-R5 sufficient to close identity, tenant/cell/authority, dependency, time,
and equivalent-protection questions. A scored OPS-R15 benchmark must also wait
for S0-GAP-02 and machine-readable benchmark engineering.

## Permitted local assumptions

Remaining research may assume:

1. an opaque fixture-local `subject_ref` can stand for an above-case subject,
   but it has no canonical package, wire, cardinality, or authority meaning;
2. existing IDs are not lifetime matter identity unless a future accepted
   mapping says so;
3. the five boundary planes are analytically distinct;
4. external acts stay external; evidence admission and PolicyOS claim reaction
   remain PolicyOS duties;
5. one authority grammar remains canonical; report-local states are not runtime
   states;
6. observations and wakes do not create authority;
7. source/effect, custody/transaction, and correction/replay roles must not be
   collapsed, while OPS-R4 owns names and placement;
8. missing decisive evidence is not a pass and the canonical consumer owns the
   reaction;
9. Atlas renders authority supplied by publication owners;
10. OPS-R15 contributes predicates and adversarial examples, not capability
    proof or required H2 internals.

Research may not assume a universal evidence envelope, mandatory
`policy_matter_ref`, 213 accepted boundary rows, fixed public states, thirteen
clocks, twenty gates, one WorldRelease schema, or a passed capstone.

## Decision horizons

### P0 — Before accepting Stage-0 research

- persist and hash the three original source reports;
- accept or amend S0-K01–S0-K16;
- replace authority-bearing “freeze/baseline/binding” language;
- remove canonical-owner, universal-envelope, parallel-lattice, and clock
  overclaims;
- correct repository facts, capability labels, failure-pattern IDs, and
  citations;
- mark OPS-R15 `blocked_pending_oracle_independence`;
- separate the five repository defects from research conclusions.

### P1 — Before relying on the anchors across Group B

- publish local subject and boundary assumptions;
- treat PAO-R1 as method/census, not adjudication database;
- route clock vocabulary to OPS-R4;
- route content/authority dependency semantics to OPS-R2;
- define which consensus predicates each task must falsify;
- commission S0-GAP-01 and S0-GAP-02 without blocking unrelated research.

### P2 — Before H2 architecture or implementation planning

- decide the minimum subject-reference ABI and semantic owner (S0-GAP-01);
- obtain OPS-R1/3 equivalent-protection and migration outcomes;
- obtain OPS-R2 authority-dependency and affected-set semantics;
- obtain OPS-R4 temporal/correction algebra;
- obtain INT-R5 action-specific competence/delegation model;
- define tenant/cell/generation/authority binding across checkpoint and control
  state;
- decide H2's orchestration boundary without acquiring external functions;
- approve benchmark oracle architecture if H2 conformance will cite it.

### P3 — Before a governed pilot or production claim

- verify competent institutional operators, jurisdiction, legal effect, and
  partner-system evidence;
- exercise public correction across every controlled surface;
- close jurisdiction fallback and tenant/cell defects;
- exercise long-term verification/key/archive and actual recovery drills;
- set deployment-specific RPO/RTO from topology and evidence;
- implement and run the independent capstone against sealed adjacent cases;
- retain immutable run/oracle/reviewer records and report the bounded claim.

## Recommended dispatch order

1. **Documentation/decision lane:** accept or amend the consensus kernel; amend
   PAO-R0/R1/OPS-R15; persist source artifacts.
2. **Immediate parallel research:** OPS-R4; INT-R1; INT-R9; INT-R5; INT-R8;
   PAO-R4; PAO-R36; INT-R7.
3. **Custody spine:** OPS-R1, OPS-R2, OPS-R8, with S0-GAP-01 running in
   parallel. These tasks may use opaque local subjects.
4. **Living world and evaluation:** OPS-R10, OPS-R11, OPS-R5, INT-R4, then
   OPS-R3, OPS-R9, OPS-R14.
5. **Benchmark lane:** amend OPS-R15 now; run S0-GAP-02; build the
   machine-readable kernel only after domain predicates stabilize.
6. **H2 architecture:** begin only at P2; it consumes accepted outputs and does
   not become their semantic owner.
7. **Deferred/pilot lane:** activate only on current typed triggers and
   re-adjudicate the exact planes at that time.
