# Wave 5 Ratification Candidates

## Candidate boundary

These are candidate propositions for the human principal. They bind only what PolicyOS may claim.
They do not govern what an institution must do, authorize implementation, appoint an owner or move a
gate. Evidence citations deliberately separate the audit line from the response line; audit line
numbers are not projected onto amended text.

## W5-KC01 — object-specific gap closure

**Proposition.** PolicyOS may claim that a non-data gap is resolved only when positive evidence
establishes the required object for that gap type and every applicable ceiling; additional rows in the
same data stream cannot by themselves establish a missing relation, estimand, mandate, capacity,
licence, authority or assurance object.

**Evidence across both lines.** Audit-line `INT-R2-AUD-F005`, `F007`, `F009`, `F010` and `F011` at
`dbdb1243a277f0864cae9af240ff1d13786d99df` establish the missing classifier, ceiling and
substitution conditions while preserving the eight-way discrimination (`C003`). Response-line
`0afc3779e2894f2793cc40150d6923589bd36ee6` adds eight positive predicates, sibling falsifiers,
two-part owner-writability and bounded open-world terminal rules. Verifier-line
`b48cdb131c2a8d4f9b30ce217dfa3efcd65119fa` closes F005/F009–F011 and leaves F007's vocabulary
ownership as a declared gap.

**Falsifier.** Produce a case in which the required non-data object is absent, only same-stream data
volume or precision improves, and the package's rule nevertheless permits an authority-grade
`resolved` claim.

**Non-effect.** Ratification would not establish the eight producers, vocabulary owners, common
ceiling algebra, 63-case benchmark, institutional signers or a live acquisition capability.

## W5-KC02 — operator comprehension is separately earned

**Proposition.** PolicyOS may claim that an operator population understands and can safely act on an
authority surface only from population-, modality- and task-bound behavioral evidence; conformance of
surface semantics, enforcement or instrument integrity cannot be projected as human comprehension.

**Evidence across both lines.** Audit-line `INT-R3-AUD-F006`–`F010` at
`8e9be1e5e737312f92579b57a7f011b9b14d3a46` separates absorption, feasibility, correct stale-state
behavior, pre-build predicates and the local/global gate predicate; `C001`–`C004` preserve the
no-human-result and accessible, set-valued study design. Response-line
`32cfebd02354b4d70fbf8beaca168aea6f2e72ee` partitions the twelve red predicates as 6 surface, 3
enforcement, 3 instrument-integrity and 0 behavioral and keeps `human_comprehension_established:
false`. Verifier-line `81635e8878ec99dd6d9e06fc7c53fb6f13ade434` confirms those repairs while retaining the
absence of a study and owner.

**Falsifier.** Keep every pre-build predicate green, supply no eligible-opportunity behavioral result
from the target population, and show a public/reviewer projection that claims human comprehension.

**Non-effect.** Ratification would not approve a study, set sample size or thresholds, appoint an
instrument owner/adjudicator, establish comprehension in any population or make INT-R3 a DS12 gate
input.

## W5-KC03 — causal diagnosis before learning authority

**Proposition.** PolicyOS may claim that post-deployment evidence validates or updates a causal effect
only after observation validity, intervention/version ancestry, context/interference and supported
mechanism contributors are admitted; unresolved diagnosis cannot be projected as causal
confirmation.

**Evidence across both lines.** Audit-line `AUD-F03`–`AUD-F05` at
`ea2eac5575e5b8fb4a5462c068a37bb913076952` establishes unresolved absorption risk, the distinction
between admission order and causal precedence, and the GY-O1 contradiction; `AUD-F18` preserves
protection/learning and source/destination separation. Response-line
`329edb60f77867f914581d380acfccf5882d607d` supplies the five-field admission shape, contributor
lanes, an interim no-posterior-mutation rule and holdout design. Verifier-line
`082ddc26c2f8db55104ccb95518b72d84d94a06b` closes F04, partially closes F03/F05 and proves the
unchanged report still conflicts on `expected_variation`.

**Falsifier.** Hold the diagnosis at `diagnosis_unresolved` or fail one admission relation while a
consumer still emits an effect-posterior, world-model or public causal-validity upgrade.

**Non-effect.** Ratification would not select the 0–6 implementation order, decide the
`expected_variation` update policy, set an abstention threshold, create a classifier/corpus/oracle or
authorize any posterior mutation.

## W5-KC04 — protective response is not causal evidence

**Proposition.** PolicyOS may report a verified protective response and an unresolved causal diagnosis
in the same record, but it may not project the protective response, detector trigger or safe outcome
as evidence that a causal explanation was confirmed.

**Evidence across both lines.** Audit-line `AUD-F04`, `AUD-F06` and `AUD-F18` at
`ea2eac5575e5b8fb4a5462c068a37bb913076952` preserves protection without learning while rejecting
unsupported causal ordering and unconstrained state combinations. Response-line
`329edb60f77867f914581d380acfccf5882d607d` separates diagnosis, response state and S13 destination
accountability and specifies forbidden tuples. Verifier-line
`082ddc26c2f8db55104ccb95518b72d84d94a06b` confirms the semantic separation but records that no state
engine or mutation suite exists.

**Falsifier.** Feed an unresolved case into the proposed response record, execute a correctly
preauthorized containment, and observe any claim or learning artifact treating execution or later
safety as causal confirmation.

**Non-effect.** Ratification would not authorize containment, define preauthorization, choose an
operation, set a threshold or waiting-harm rule, create H2, or constrain an external institution's
action. The action-governing half remains routed, not ratified.

## W5-KC05 — mutable-dependency authority is time-bound

**Proposition.** PolicyOS may not claim that a certificate computed from evidence through `t0` alone
determines authority at a later `t1` across all admissible future histories when a decisive dependency
can change; any current-validity claim must declare and evidence its profile-derived currentness
semantics.

**Evidence across both lines.** Audit-line `INT-R5-A-001`, `A-004`, `A-007` and `A-008` at
`247f89f016f71ee603ed76ef6dbb6403f7e651a0` corrects the false universal inequality while preserving
the information limit, requires independent decisive-field producers, adds the PAO-R4 conjunct and
requires explicit relation-back semantics. Response-line
`70f2db6d3a4330664c981721a9305f16bffe369b` makes the two-history non-inferability rule explicit and
specifies `snapshot_by_explicit_rule`, `issuer_authorized_lease`, `revalidate_before_commit` and
`continuous_checkpoint_revalidation` as profile-derived alternatives. Audit claim-ledger `CL-F03`
expressly treats them as a supported taxonomy, not proof that every regime fits one without profile
interpretation. Verifier-line
`d9223d12bf7cb4826c6f1f888d84275364c35fe7` closes those rows without claiming a live certificate
chain.

**Falsifier.** Construct two admissible histories identical through `t0` and divergent on a decisive
authority dependency after `t0`; the unchanged historical certificate is nevertheless claimed to
determine both `t1` states without additional profile rule or current evidence.

**Non-effect.** Ratification would not implement the graph/certificate producer, choose a profile,
make the four named modes exhaustive, select a regime's revocation effect, appoint a
signer/adjudicator, authorize an individual case or establish a PA2/DS9 acquisition bridge.

## W5-KC06 — multilingual authority cannot be inferred from presentation

**Proposition.** Translation presence, UI-locale support, structural parity or an English aid cannot by
itself establish source-language legal authority or semantic equivalence; any equivalence claim is
bounded to its declared proposition set, purposes, qualified holders and tested denominator.

**Evidence across both lines.** Audit-line `IR6-A03`, `A06`, `A07`, `A08` and commendations
`IR6-C01`–`C04` at `bae4f8c2b5e5ef340dda73f17bfe852c1d0d3cee` narrows capability language, separates current fact
from model, rejects finite-suite overclaim and preserves UI/source, co-authenticity and zero-holder
boundaries. Response-line `eb9b135089d4a54b648973db02f0312b276ea2ea` makes the MAEP certificate
denominator-bounded and withdraws the unsupported independent census positive. Delta-verifier line
`24b6813d11e87a30e849bf4a799293e682bd7fed` closes all audit defects while retaining one
independent-census gap and `absent/unallocated` standing.

**Falsifier.** Keep paths, keys and UI locale parity intact while changing legal modality, qualified
holder or status injectivity; the system still emits an unbounded equivalence or authority claim.

**Non-effect.** Ratification would not declare any translation authoritative, choose a mandatory
pivot language, certify a jurisdiction, appoint a holder, build the corpus or prove cryptographic
co-authenticity.

## Outcome-vocabulary forward note

No **third new element**—which would be the **fourth total outcome entry** under the Wave-4 count—is
established by this wave, so the INT-wave §8 trigger does not fire now.

The closest candidate is “protective response while diagnosis remains unresolved.” Its
assertion-only content is already composition of **declared unknown**, **custody without a number** and
the existing negative-completion rule: PolicyOS can report what was verified without claiming a cause.
Its genuinely new affirmative content would authorize or constrain protective action, which is outside
the authority-band ratification bar and is therefore routed rather than proposed as a constitutional
outcome. If the principal later rules that this is a distinct claim kind, it would be the triggering
third new element/fourth total entry and must be handled in one consolidated constitutional amendment
with the prior outcome vocabulary, never as a separate Wave-5 act.

## Propositions deliberately not presented for ratification

- the eight-condition `expected_variation` update request;
- response-operation charters, preauthorization, waiting-harm precedence and restart rules;
- numerical abstention/coverage thresholds;
- a mandatory English pivot, locale set or jurisdiction pack;
- any owner, signer, adjudicator, producer, gate or implementation schedule.

Each would constrain computation or action, or presuppose an institution/capability that does not
exist. They remain routing items.
