## CORR-RC01 — Shared envelope, split substantive predicates

**Proposition**

> PolicyOS may use one procedural correspondence-acceptance envelope, but causal intervention instantiation and legal governance correspondence are different substantive predicates and may not derive one another's positive authority.

**Falsifier:** a blinded predeclared test in which one domain-neutral predicate/profile reproduces specialist-valid decisions across both fixture families with no material specialist correction.

**Non-effect:** does not require two physical people, appoint anyone, define schema, or open a gate.

## CORR-RC02 — Constructibility is an exception to adjudication, not declaration

**Proposition**

> Human adjudication is unnecessary where a predeclared non-circular recognition rule derives correspondence from independently authoritative semantic identities. Field presence, names, units, hashes or registry declarations are insufficient to create that class.

**Falsifier:** a case admitted as constructible while a decisive semantic identity can change without changing registry/type/unit/hash checks.

**Non-effect:** does not ratify R4's exact counts or future subject spine.

## CORR-RC03 — Exact binomial local calibration

**Proposition**

> For frozen binary `confident_wrong` loss, fixed final sample size and prospectively defined stratum of effectively independent units, the one-sided exact binomial upper confidence bound is the preferred local finite-sample estimator.

**Falsifier:** dependency/selection/stopping violates fixed-binomial assumptions, or loss is non-binary.

**Non-effect:** **does not ratify `n=299` as proof of current system-level `delta_ground`.**

## CORR-RC04 — Pre-declaration is part of identification

**Proposition**

> Any correspondence calibration number is admissible only relative to a dated pre-outcome specification of evaluated system, estimand, unit, eligible frame, strata, sampling rule, stopping rule, gold provenance and expiry conditions.

**Falsifier:** outcomes can be inspected before redefining population while same governed number still issues.

**Non-effect:** does not forbid exploratory analysis in candidate lanes.

## CORR-RC05 — Constructed negatives and adjudicated positives are different evidence classes

**Proposition**

> Easily constructed mismatch cases may not dilute or replace independent positive-gold requirement unless their target-population mixture weight was fixed prospectively under a valid sampling theorem.

**Falsifier:** prospectively specified target distribution with valid predeclared weights for constructed component.

**Non-effect:** does not limit refusal/falsification suite size.

## CORR-RC06 — Minimum semantics of legal governance correspondence

**Proposition**

> A positive statute→lever claim must establish correspondence of legal subject, legal instrument/effect, scope/applicability and quantitative semantics; provision existence and compatible threshold are insufficient.

**Falsifier:** a formal legal methodology showing one listed dimension is logically redundant for PolicyOS lever contract.

**Non-effect:** does not determine substantive law or appoint legal authority.

## CORR-RC07 — Adopt prior art by layer, not by name

**Proposition**

> PolicyOS should reuse established methods only for the claims they actually establish: selective prediction for reject/risk geometry, matched finite-sample inference for its statistical object, and traceability/round-trip/legal-alignment for legal formalization. Method names do not establish guarantees when assumptions are absent.

**Falsifier:** an existing standard closes the complete PolicyOS conjunct under identical gold, population, authority and estimand assumptions.

**Non-effect:** does not claim a new PolicyOS scientific contribution.

## CORR-RC08 — Current constructibility does not remove blocking semantic seams

**Proposition**

> Under current typed surfaces, proof-by-construction removes adjudication only from internal proposal→owner-atom seam; it does not remove positive adjudication from independent causal evidence binding or statute→lever governance correspondence.

**Falsifier:** fresh independent census/transposition harness demonstrates a non-empty non-circular constructible class on either blocking seam.

**Non-effect:** does not ratify R4's exact denominator counts.
