## Result

The authoritative sources require **one classification per plane**. They support
`own` for PolicyOS's technical evidence interface, source-relative representation,
verification, custody and refusal, including its independently grounded causal
world-model producer; `integrate` for independently produced external
assertions whose output changes PolicyOS claim validity. They do **not**
support converting an unappointed semantic producer into a blanket prohibition
on B1/B2 mechanisms or vocabularies. B1's remaining scope question concerns the
meaning of its claimed authoritative legal membership, not the technical
identity or custody machinery. B2's independently grounded producer belongs to
PolicyOS's own world-model/design function; absent membership values, a currently
unappointed semantic owner or positive adjudicator do not reopen that boundary.

The debt row's present `open` status remains authoritative as status. Its later
sentence that legal-subject identity is “ruled `out_of_scope` ... under `CORR-B1`”
does not acquire a scope ruling from B1: the referenced B1 section states an
unallocated semantic authority, while its own package explicitly denies that
institutional absence prohibits building. This note records the warrant gap; it
does not override the register.

positive chain), P05/P15/P32 (do not turn source shape/signature/LLM interpretation
into authority), P35/P36 (read the cited finding and honor its denominator and

authority kept distinct.

existing consumer awaiting a semantic source is a `producer_missing` seam, and
the accepted-provenance verification requirement is named separately. These are
purpose-specific descriptions of the cited seams, not new register statuses.
