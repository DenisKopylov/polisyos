# INT-R2 semantics: R2 and R5

The inspected evidence supports reuse of existing comparison machinery and scoped semantic
owners. It supports neither a universal new normative/assurance vocabulary nor a categorical
refusal to provide the demanded internal correspondence. The estimand debt remains meaningful,
but its description as the **sole** owed deliverable is refuted by current R2 demand and its
unresolved comparisons. A ladder of names alone would not discharge its own target-identity
condition.

## Questions requiring architect disposition

These questions select the semantic correspondence to record, not permission to build the
already authorized internal chain. Routine team allocation is not a blocker. The present
generic mechanism already accepts candidate definitions; another generic registry or comparator
does not close the missing domain meaning.

1. **Normative correspondence:** The commissioned consent/waiver/ethics scope exceeds
   `value_schedule_for_ranking`. Which regime-specific operation definitions and evidence-to-use
   implications are to be admitted, and is the existing ranking owner a separately scoped member
   or an intake pattern only? The current source justifies neither widening its purpose nor
   deleting the other commissioned demands. The decision must preserve the actual regime's
   protocol/population/site/risk/version conditions; a routine owner assignment alone cannot
   decide those meanings.
2. **Assurance scope:** Which named assurance engagement profile must the already demanded
   `engagement.level` consumer interpret, and which core-audit/GY-AQ1 adapter and registrar carries
   subject/criteria/period/scope/independence and level definitions? May its recognized levels be
   incomparable across profiles? AUP remains outside assurance. The question appoints no provider.
3. **Estimand relation:** Is the owed object a registered implication relation over evidence for
   one content-bound target, with explicit equivalence/transport between targets, rather than a
   universal strength rank? Which existing question-binding artifact carries horizon and
   intercurrent-event/regime identity, and which Foundry owner demonstrates target-to-analysis
   alignment through the demanding gate? Compiler ownership alone does not answer that boundary.
4. **Operation scope:** Which actual canonical mutation and its current right/grant owners are the
   first required write vocabulary consumer? A CAS metadata DTO is insufficient allocation evidence.
   Keep external adjudication out of scope and the internal right/grant conjunction buildable.

These are semantic-scope/placement decisions, not waits for an institutional signer. Each can
end in a precise reuse, extension, scoped refusal or declared limitation. A refusal needs the
same source comparison and affected-demand disposition as a build decision.

## Pattern pass and research completion

Relevant patterns: P01/P02 (do not promote candidate ports to semantic capability), P04/P05
(separate meaning, support and authority axes), P13/P27 (reuse owners, avoid new universal
enums), P32 (shape/signature is not semantic adequacy), P35/P36 (bounded inputs and finding

found are normative owner absence inferred from density/case-sensitive search, assurance
no-demand inferred from one identifier, and estimand exclusivity inherited after its premise
was reopened. Their destinations are the finding table, not unsolicited production fixes.

The generic candidate chain is present in the inspected Fabric owner. For the broader
consent/waiver, assurance-level and complete estimand semantic correspondences this research
has not admitted an end-to-end owner-specific chain; `absent/unallocated` describes this
unadmitted semantic contract/appointment, **not** a complete-tree absence of internal
primitives. An already supplied adapter lacking only its connection would instead be
`bridge_missing`; that stronger maturity fact must be demonstrated by its implementing lane.
External publication/assurance authority remains separately typed-empty when no qualified
holder is supplied. Proposed integration tests remain `semantic_test_missing` in this
research artifact; no test execution or runtime capability upgrade is claimed.

Discriminating acceptance examples for the future decision are explicit: structured assumption
cannot become causal magnitude/transport; a horizon-only target change cannot compare equal;
ranking permission cannot approve a changed protocol; legal competence cannot satisfy consent
or write grant; a valid token cannot replace substantive right; a pilot cannot authorize a larger
load; an integrity PASS/AUP report cannot become reasonable assurance; a qualified external
decision cannot become an independent assurance engagement. These are proposed semantic
acceptance properties, not executed tests or a newly enumerated universal vocabulary.

The failure register was reopened before closeout. This draft is complete as bounded research
pending architect decisions above. It does not close either debt, invent an institution, or stop
