## 4. Cross-question resolution — distinctions, not one authority model

### 4.1 Constitutive correspondence versus evidential correspondence

The blanket “no amendment required” wording is removed. The opposite blanket rule—every external act requires a new amendment or second institutional re-performance—is also not adopted. The exact relation controls. [G02 §14.7; question audits; AX AUD-002]

| Question | Narrow proposition distinct from a constitutive relation | When the constitutive scope issue arises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CV-Q1 | A purpose-scoped assessment grounded in accepted legal and operational evidence. | A competent designation makes the specified relation authoritative for a named purpose. |
| CV-Q2 | A source records a particular consent/waiver/determination and its expressed scope. | The competent act creates or removes the named requirement for a use; the waiver-to-use implication requires its scoped treatment. |
| CV-Q3 | An admitted report contains a specified conclusion about an exact assertion and native scope. | A programme/institutional recognition act creates a status or entitlement to rely, rather than simply reporting professional judgment. |
| CV-Q4 | A definition denotes a mathematical target and an analysis/evidence relation is assessed separately. | An institutional designation or authorized-selection act constitutes the particular target/indicator correspondence being relied on. |
| CV-Q5 | Existing grounded right and active grant both cover an exact effect through their permitted native use. | A new admitted correspondence relies on the mandate/delegation act itself constituting the entitlement; component reuse does not waive §14.7. |

Where triggered, identify the act, issuer competence, subject, purpose, jurisdiction, scope/time, defeaters and exact amendment reach. The principal's competent scope act remains missing until supplied. A reusable scope disposition can avoid redoing the institution's act at every read, but its actual meaning and authority cannot be invented by this synthesis. [G01–G04]

### 4.2 P37: what the evidence actually establishes

| Question / deciding premise | Evidence capable of refuting the premise | Narrow result that can survive a missing stronger premise |

## 6. Suggested order of principal decisions and actual dependencies

**This is an order for explicit choices, not a build gate or a mandatory execution sequence.** Candidate construction can proceed in parallel; an absent external appointment must not become a scheduling prohibition. Nor is every internal write made dependent on all five domains.

First identify the exact proposition and intended consuming use for each actual case, including whether a constitutive relation is being admitted. This shared clarification prevents a profile, source or workflow choice from silently weakening the demand. It does not require one universal act or ontology.

Then address **CV-Q4's target-semantic unit** where a causal question is actually being carried through analysis. A specific target can make the relevant population, contrast, horizon and regime visible for adjacent evidence decisions. Physical representation should remain a bounded comparison, not a second truth source selected by label.

**CV-Q1 and CV-Q2 can be decided in parallel for the same actual use**, where those legal and participation/ethics requirements apply. Q1's source-relative correspondence does not replace Q2's acts or establish all legal requirements. Q2 may also need a data-processing basis outside participation consent; no requirement is invented merely to fill a common table.

**CV-Q5 should bind the actual protected effect and existing grounds**. It depends on Q1/Q2 results only where those specific grounds are genuinely material to that effect. A native internal custody authorization need not inherit a participant-consent or legal-court requirement that the effect does not have. The source/effect walkthrough and native reuse comparison may proceed independently of a live institutional appointment under proper candidate-test authority.

**CV-Q3's first named application follows the actual assurance demand**, not a generic desire for a high badge. A custody assertion, system-control assertion and policy-effect claim can require different reports and criteria. Native dossier tooling and candidate controls remain buildable before selecting a professional provider. Repeated demand, not an abstract neutrality label, justifies a reusable catalogue.

A principal may make any of these narrower decisions first when actual inputs are already available. The substantive dependency is the named premise of the named claim, not the numeric order Q1–Q5 or this suggested sequence.

## 8. What remains open and what this synthesis actually closes

The synthesis closes the **documentary disposition task**: seven identified audits are read, all their findings are retained and every recommendation is revised or narrowed visibly. It does not close the empirical, semantic-acceptance, enforcement or institutional gaps those audits identify.

The principal still lacks a demonstrated first legal-subject pair, actual pilot applicability dossier/first regime, first real report–demand pair and suitable assurance criteria, implemented target-core comparison, and a complete actual effect/right/grant witness. Some narrow native/source facts are established at M, and those are retained. They do not supply missing real-world premises. No error bound, price, workload saving, field trial, comprehension result or runtime acceptance is invented.

A1A, A3 and AX explicitly did not establish saturation. A1L, A2, A4 and A5 record bounded two-check stopping conditions but still retain access/execution limits. Complete CLM accounting therefore does not mean every source was fully checked. A4 and AX recovered some complete M debt rows that individual audits did not; this is additional attributed coverage, not retroactive repair of their read receipts or B membership.

Every package distinguishes stronger meaning principles from weaker representation/profile choices. The remaining dissent is not treated as a vote to be overruled: review can preserve shared error; requirement lists can omit obligations; a native professional report may not meet the demanded proposition; a perfect target can be wrong or inadequately specified; and substantial authorization code can still leave a missing effect or temporal premise.
