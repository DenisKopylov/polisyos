# CV-Q1 — Legal-subject correspondence
## Final research decision package — recommendation, not decision

This package incorporates the assigned research, received question audit(s), and applicable independent cross-question findings. It is self-contained for an architect without Library access. Findings and decisive evidence are summarized with portable citations; unavailable evidence is not reconstructed. The principal alone decides. Source and audit labels resolve in §§5–8.

## 1. Question and frame

### Exact commissioned question

> What does the positive assertion “this provision and this lever have the same authoritative legal subject” mean for a named recognition purpose: an external binding legal determination, or PolicyOS's own evidence-grounded semantic assertion? Name its wrong-making authority, source acceptance rule, jurisdiction/scope/time and issuer.

### Answer after audit

**Recommend an own, defeasible, purpose-scoped semantic assessment, with external binding determinations integrated as distinct evidence. Start with the narrowest source profile sufficient for the actual purpose; do not select a broad profile framework merely because it can contain a simpler one.** This preserves the useful part of the researcher's O3 while restoring a strong O2 as a genuine first implementation choice. O0 withholds an unsupported real positive; O1 governs a use that actually requires an external determination. [G01–G05; A1L §§2,5–8; A1A §§2,5–8]

The positive answers whether **this provision and this operational lever have the same purpose-relevant normative subject**, not whether their topic words, numeric thresholds or producer labels match. A broader overlap or a useful comparison is not silently renamed “same subject.” Other relations may be represented as other claims, but do not satisfy the original sameness demand. The meaning decision is distinct from permission to rely on it for a protected act. [G05 CV-Q1; research §§1,5,9]

The intended issuer is an accountable PolicyOS assessment capacity whose particular admission responsibility would have to be allocated through the existing process. This package appoints no person or body. For a binding external determination, the issuer remains the competent external actor; PolicyOS receives and preserves the act and its actual legal reach rather than becoming a court. Current source-relative recognition is not current legal authority. [G01; G05; G08]

**Wrong-making grounds are available now:** omitted exceptions, a mistaken legal reading, incorrect jurisdiction/applicability, a misunderstood operational lever, unsupported membership or a source profile inadequate for the named proposition. No later amendment or judgment is required to make a present interpretation wrong. Later legal/operational changes can instead alter current reuse while leaving a correctly qualified historical assessment intact. An external determination affects reliance within its actual reach, not every historical map. [Research §§5.3,9; A1A §2.3]

Source acceptance must identify jurisdiction, recognized source form and standing, provision version, operational version, relevant population/context, applicability interval, assessment time and allowed use. An official consolidation can be sufficient for an expressly source-relative purpose without itself having independent legal force. It is not universally sufficient; neither its official provenance nor its lack of independent force decides adequacy by itself. The first real jurisdiction, source hierarchy and provision–lever pair remain **unselected** in the supplied corpus. [Q1-E01; A1L RT-001; A1A RT-001]

Independence must concern the evidence: a non-producer needs access capable of disproving either membership, including evidence not merely selected to confirm the producer's answer. Separate files, organizations, signatures or reviewers are not that property. The requested substrate already contains synthetic subject annotations and a budget–tax transposition/removal control; auditors inspected those source definitions and synthetic annotations. That is useful mechanical evidence, not a fresh runtime result or real legal grounding. [Q1-C01; A1L RT-002; A1A §3.2]


The decomposition above clarifies this exact question; it does not replace it with permission to create a module, choose a generic framework, or stop candidate work. The first actual deployment facts identified as missing remain missing.

## 3. Recommendation and confidence

The final recommendation is **the scoped own-assessment meaning plus minimum sufficient implementation breadth**, not an unconditional O3 build. An adequate O2 may be selected for the first actual purpose without first constructing a differentiated framework. Broader O3 is warranted only by a material coverage or correctness benefit that justifies its additional maintenance/review burden. Until either real membership is grounded, retain O0 for that positive; choose O1 where the protected act requires it. No real jurisdiction/profile or positive chain is admitted here.

This is a substantive change from treating O2 solely as a conceptual subset of the preferred O3. The cheaper rival has a real winning condition. Both DR1 audits and the cross-question audit return **GO_WITH_REVISIONS** for this kind of narrowed research direction, not for production legal authority. Their overlapping findings are not independent votes.

**Confidence:** Strong confidence in the meaning/reliance and signature/substance distinctions within the inspected scope; moderate confidence in the minimum-sufficient-profile decision rule; low confidence in real reviewer performance, first-profile adequacy and comparative cost because those were not tested.

The received `GO_WITH_REVISIONS` verdict applies to the scoped research direction described by the auditor, not to a completed implementation or this final wording as a newly independently audited artifact. All criticisms are disposed of below; accepting a criticism does not fill its missing empirical premises.

### 4.5 Compatibility and constitutive scope

The common boundary is proposition-specific, not a new universal authorization engine. W5-K07 excludes statute-existence substitution for the legal-governance relation; W5-K08 excludes signature substitution for correspondence. The operative signature rule is §14, read with the earlier correction, not the superseded W4 attribution. [G02]

A nonconstitutive interpretation within existing admitted scope does not by itself require an amendment. If an authorized act **constitutes the correspondence being relied on**, the exact positive, issuer competence, subject, purpose, jurisdiction and temporal reach must be identified and the §14.7 scope amendment obtained through the existing competent authority. A professional opinion, participant choice, mathematical definition and institutional designation do not all become the same kind of act. This package supplies no exemption, amendment or replacement provenance class. [G01–G04]

P37 provenance stays `recomputed`, `independently_reconciled`, `consumer_asserted`, `institutionally_supplied` or `not_established`. The latter three cannot carry a positive authority result. Extraction of an assertion and evidence of its truth are different predicates. An explicitly narrower result may survive, but the original stronger demand remains unresolved unless it is actually satisfied; changing its wording after seeing the evidence is not success. [G04; received audits]

Candidate vocabulary, interpretation, comparison, simulation and truthful refusal remain buildable with declared unknowns. This does not permit an otherwise unauthorized production effect. Separately authorized custody of a refusal is not automatically prohibited because the requested effect was refused. Appointment governs the named act, not all vocabulary; evidence about an appointment is integrated when its validity matters and merely observed when it is background information. [G01 §9(5–6); G03 §4.3]


**This question's constitutive boundary:** For an ordinary evidence-grounded assessment, the relation is argued from accepted legal and operational sources. For a competent designation that makes a relation authoritative for a purpose, the actual act and competence are part of the basis; that version invokes W5 §14.7 scope treatment. An external signature alone establishes neither the designation's competence nor the lever's actual operation.

## 7. Residual dissent

The strongest unresolved objection is that O3, or a well-equipped O2, can relocate the missing semantic oracle into an expensive review process whose participants share assumptions or see curated evidence. The corpus does not show reliable real membership adjudication at acceptable cost. A constrained own assessment may also not supply the stronger institutional reliance a user actually needs. The remedy in this package is explicit narrower standing and a falsifiable rival, not a claim that the epistemic problem is solved.

The unresolved objections are preserved in their strongest form. No author majority, repeated label or syntactic completion settles them. A conditional recommendation can proceed as research advice while the actual positive demand remains unfulfilled.
