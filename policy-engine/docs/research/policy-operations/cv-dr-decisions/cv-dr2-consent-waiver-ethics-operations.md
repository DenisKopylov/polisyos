# CV-Q2 — Consent, waiver and ethics-determination purposes
## Final research decision package — recommendation, not decision

This package incorporates the assigned research, received question audit(s), and applicable independent cross-question findings. It is self-contained for an architect without Library access. Findings and decisive evidence are summarized with portable citations; unavailable evidence is not reconstructed. The principal alone decides. Source and audit labels resolve in §§5–8.

## 1. Question and frame

### Exact commissioned question

> For the already commissioned consent, waiver and ethics-determination purposes, which first regime-specific operation definitions and evidence-to-use implications must the adapter admit, preserving protocol/site/population/risk/version identity? Name its intake/registrar and whether the existing ranking owner is a separately scoped member or only a reusable intake pattern.

### Answer after audit

**Recommend requirement-specific implications for the smallest actual applicability scope, using existing intake and registration. Preserve consent, waiver and ethics determinations as different acts with different outcomes. Treat ranking as a reusable intake pattern in this adapter, not as consent evidence.** A genuinely sufficient single-regime O1 may be the first implementation; O2 composition is appropriate only where the actual pilot has concurrently applicable requirements that matter to the consuming claim. [G05 CV-Q2; G07 R2-SEM-02; A2 AUD-001/002; AX §7]

This does not select the pilot's country or imply that one country means one regime. No actual protocol, participant dossier or complete applicability assessment was supplied. Therefore the corpus supports a choice of **how the first positive must be specified**, but not the registration of a concrete US, EU, Ukrainian or other deployment profile. The researcher’s US illustration is not the user's pilot. [Research §§4.3–4.4,10; A2 §§8–9]

The first admitted use must state an exact consumer proposition: for example, evidence of a documented process, or satisfaction of a named participation requirement for a particular use. Those are not interchangeable with legally effective ongoing permission, comprehensive study clearance or participant comprehension. Where the demanding owner needs the stronger proposition, a documentary result can be retained as evidence but must not be called completion of the original demand. [A2 AUD-002/RT-001; G04 P37]

The semantic menu remains unregistered: participation/intervention consent; specified secondary-use consent; waiver or alteration of particular consent elements; waiver of documentation only; favorable ethics determination with its conditions; modification required, disapproval, suspension or withdrawal; and requirement-specific applicability/exemption determinations. A waiver is not evidence that someone consented. Waiving documentation does not waive the choice. An issued determination does not imply a favorable result. The actual profile selects only the meanings supported by its exact operative rules. [Q2-E01/E02; research §4.4; A2 citation inventory]

Preserve **jointly covered combinations** of protocol and content version, activity, site/valid coverage path, participant or population, risk context and relevant times. Separate approved objects must not be flattened into independent lists that authorize an unintended Cartesian combination. But differences in site strings or clerical versions are not automatically disqualifying: an actual reliance arrangement or justified nonmaterial-change assessment may preserve coverage. Permission to rank fails as consent for both a changed and an unchanged protocol, because purpose mismatch is independently decisive. [Q2-E03/E04; G07; research §5]

Use the existing **GY-AQ1/Fabric independent-owner and demanding-owner route**, retain **CV-OWN-01** candidate/refusal machinery and Runtime re-entry, and use **GY-VC1** for accepted versioned mappings and their losses. Registration records the meaning decision; it does not consent for a participant or grant an IRB waiver. The existing `NormativeValueScheduleOwner` retains `value_schedule_for_ranking` elsewhere. Pattern-only here does not forbid a future explicitly disjoint ranking member when a genuine shared-intake need is shown. [G07/G08; Q2-C01]

Applicability completeness is a separate claim. “Every requirement in the caller's list passes” is not “all applicable requirements are satisfied.” An independently scoped account must be able to expose an omitted obligation or an uncovered material amendment; competent disagreement must remain visible rather than decided by reviewer majority. A useful narrower requirement-level result does not close a stronger deployment requirement. [A2 RT-001; AX AUD-003]


The decomposition above clarifies this exact question; it does not replace it with permission to create a module, choose a generic framework, or stop candidate work. The first actual deployment facts identified as missing remain missing.

## 3. Recommendation and confidence

Adopt the **purpose-preserving, requirement-specific meaning** and the existing AQ1/VC1 route, while leaving the first real regime and exact positive unregistered until identified. Select strong O1 immediately when it is sufficient; select only the necessary O2 composition when a real concurrently applicable requirement makes it useful. Do not build O2 merely to discover that O1 suffices, and do not use O0 to prohibit candidate engineering.

Within this consent/waiver/ethics adapter the ranking owner is **pattern-only by default**, with its current ranking authority untouched. No empirical claim establishes that safe disjoint membership is impossible; it is simply not justified by a present shared-intake need. The received question and cross verdicts are **GO_WITH_REVISIONS** for this bounded direction. The original O2-first preference is narrowed, not promoted to an implemented or legally accepted pilot profile.

**Confidence:** Strong confidence in non-substitution and act/outcome separation; moderate confidence in the smallest-sufficient-scope strategy; low confidence in any claimed completeness, first regime, participant-process adequacy or comparative workload without an actual dossier.

The received `GO_WITH_REVISIONS` verdict applies to the scoped research direction described by the auditor, not to a completed implementation or this final wording as a newly independently audited artifact. All criticisms are disposed of below; accepting a criticism does not fill its missing empirical premises.

### 4.5 Compatibility and constitutive scope

The common boundary is proposition-specific, not a new universal authorization engine. W5-K07 excludes statute-existence substitution for the legal-governance relation; W5-K08 excludes signature substitution for correspondence. The operative signature rule is §14, read with the earlier correction, not the superseded W4 attribution. [G02]

A nonconstitutive interpretation within existing admitted scope does not by itself require an amendment. If an authorized act **constitutes the correspondence being relied on**, the exact positive, issuer competence, subject, purpose, jurisdiction and temporal reach must be identified and the §14.7 scope amendment obtained through the existing competent authority. A professional opinion, participant choice, mathematical definition and institutional designation do not all become the same kind of act. This package supplies no exemption, amendment or replacement provenance class. [G01–G04]

P37 provenance stays `recomputed`, `independently_reconciled`, `consumer_asserted`, `institutionally_supplied` or `not_established`. The latter three cannot carry a positive authority result. Extraction of an assertion and evidence of its truth are different predicates. An explicitly narrower result may survive, but the original stronger demand remains unresolved unless it is actually satisfied; changing its wording after seeing the evidence is not success. [G04; received audits]

Candidate vocabulary, interpretation, comparison, simulation and truthful refusal remain buildable with declared unknowns. This does not permit an otherwise unauthorized production effect. Separately authorized custody of a refusal is not automatically prohibited because the requested effect was refused. Appointment governs the named act, not all vocabulary; evidence about an appointment is integrated when its validity matters and merely observed when it is background information. [G01 §9(5–6); G03 §4.3]


**This question's constitutive boundary:** Distinguish “the source records this waiver with this expressed scope” from “this competent waiver removes this named requirement for this use.” If the latter constitutes the correspondence being admitted, obtain the narrow W5 §14.7 scope amendment. An audited non-trigger conclusion is possible only for the precise nonconstitutive meaning actually used, not as a silent exception. Do not require another committee to recreate the original act or assume the act's factual premises true from its signature.

## 7. Residual dissent

A finite, well-versioned profile can conceal the hardest judgment: what actually applies and whether a change matters. Independent reviewers can omit the same obligation; narrower requirement-level positives may not satisfy the operational clearance the actual pilot needs. The corpus supplies no actual dossier demonstrating completeness, comprehensible consent or acceptable review burden. The final recommendation deliberately does not manufacture those facts from a field list, committee name or source signature.

The unresolved objections are preserved in their strongest form. No author majority, repeated label or syntactic completion settles them. A conditional recommendation can proceed as research advice while the actual positive demand remains unfulfilled.
