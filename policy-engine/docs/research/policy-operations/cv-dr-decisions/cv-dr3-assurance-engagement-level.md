# CV-Q3 — Assurance engagement level
## Final research decision package — recommendation, not decision

This package incorporates the assigned research, received question audit(s), and applicable independent cross-question findings. It is self-contained for an architect without Library access. Findings and decisive evidence are summarized with portable citations; unavailable evidence is not reconstructed. The principal alone decides. Source and audit labels resolve in §§5–8.

## 1. Question and frame

### Exact commissioned question

> Which named engagement profile must `engagement.level` interpret, with what subject/criteria/period/scope, level definitions and cross-profile relation or declared incomparability?

### Answer after audit

**Recommend native, profile-qualified interpretation with no implicit default, beginning from an actual report–demand dossier rather than a predetermined professional engagement purchase.** The named ISAE 3000 (Revised)-based justification-custody attestation remains a candidate first application, not the selected meaning of an unqualified `engagement.level`. A reusable case-specific dossier, the strengthened O1-R, is the least-commitment starting posture while real demand and suitable criteria are absent. [G05 CV-Q3; A3 AUD-001/004; AX AUD-006]

The exact question's first-profile part is therefore **not substantively settled by this corpus**. The researcher specified a plausible candidate—attestation over a defined population of custody records/processes for a defined retrospective period—but no actual customer assertion, suitable criteria set, covered report or professional suitability assessment established its priority. This is a remaining selection, not absence of the commissioned assurance demand. Nor does an old README claim about a `src/` consumer establish a fresh consumer census: the audit distinguishes the synthetic corpus, historical census and architect-supplied current premise. [Research §§2.3,4.7,10; A3 §§1–4,9]

What is settled at the recommendation level is the information that must remain meaningful: standard and edition; local adoption/programme where relevant; attestation/direct or other native mode; actual subject and subject-matter information; suitable criteria; period; scope/exclusions and aspects; intended users/use; native level; exact asserted conclusion, qualifications, polarity and coverage quantifier. These are meaning obligations, not a mandated universal record format. A single report can assign different assurance levels to different assertions. [Q3-E01/E02; A3 §4]

For the inspected ISAE/ASAE context, reasonable and limited assurance concern different engagement-risk/work bases; neither is an invented numerical probability or absolute certainty. AUP is not the lowest assurance tier, and integrity PASS is not a professional engagement. A scope limitation, adverse conclusion and absent provider are different things. A report's higher native level does not repair the wrong subject, period, criteria or conclusion. [Q3-E01; G05/G07]

A precise source-bound documentary claim may state that this report expresses conclusion C about assertion S, under profile P and criteria K, for period T with limitations L. PolicyOS need not reperform the engagement to establish that document-content claim. But faithful extraction does not independently establish S's real-world truth or satisfy a stronger reliance demand. P37 applies to the actual deciding predicates of that stronger demand. A documentary fallback must remain a fallback, not retrospective weakening of the original question. [A3 AUD-002; AX AUD-003]

Two additional distinctions are decisive. **Material/aggregate coverage is not an every-record/no-error guarantee.** Also, an **unqualified opinion may concern an assertion that accurately discloses a defective state**; it does not thereby support the opposite “no defect” proposition. Native conclusion wording and a success enum cannot replace the assertion and its polarity. These are audit-supported constructed negatives, not discovered PolicyOS parser bugs. [Q3-E01 §§44,77(b); A3 RT-001/002]

No cross-profile alias, inversion, total rank or transitive closure is inferred from similar words. An unestablished relation is not proof of permanent incomparability. A valid native AA1000 report can be recognized for a suitable native documentary purpose without first being converted to ISAE; the audit's genuine report control demonstrates content, not independently reperformed work or complete AA1000 conformance. [Q3-E03; A3 §3.5,§6]


The decomposition above clarifies this exact question; it does not replace it with permission to create a module, choose a generic framework, or stop candidate work. The first actual deployment facts identified as missing remain missing.

## 3. Recommendation and confidence

Retain O3's **no-default, native-meaning principle**, but do not adopt its speculative first application or presume an extra profile catalogue. Use the strengthened O1-R as the initial least-commitment realization until a real report–demand pair identifies the necessary native profile. If repeated native demand justifies specialization, O2 or a minimal O3 can be selected; genuinely heterogeneous first demand may justify O4.

The **ISAE 3000 custody-attestation candidate remains explicit and unresolved**. Its subject, assertion, criteria, scope/exclusions, period and intended use must be completed from actual demand, not invented to make the preferred option useful. Choosing a professional provider is a separate act. A3 and AX return **GO_WITH_REVISIONS** for the conditional direction; this package does not upgrade that to an unconditional GO or a professional opinion.

**Confidence:** Strong confidence in native-profile/level/conclusion/scope separation and the identified non-inferences; moderate confidence in the least-commitment interpretation route; low confidence in the first named application, actual criteria suitability, stronger P37 reliance and comparative costs.

The received `GO_WITH_REVISIONS` verdict applies to the scoped research direction described by the auditor, not to a completed implementation or this final wording as a newly independently audited artifact. All criticisms are disposed of below; accepting a criticism does not fill its missing empirical premises.

### 4.5 Compatibility and constitutive scope

The common boundary is proposition-specific, not a new universal authorization engine. W5-K07 excludes statute-existence substitution for the legal-governance relation; W5-K08 excludes signature substitution for correspondence. The operative signature rule is §14, read with the earlier correction, not the superseded W4 attribution. [G02]

A nonconstitutive interpretation within existing admitted scope does not by itself require an amendment. If an authorized act **constitutes the correspondence being relied on**, the exact positive, issuer competence, subject, purpose, jurisdiction and temporal reach must be identified and the §14.7 scope amendment obtained through the existing competent authority. A professional opinion, participant choice, mathematical definition and institutional designation do not all become the same kind of act. This package supplies no exemption, amendment or replacement provenance class. [G01–G04]

P37 provenance stays `recomputed`, `independently_reconciled`, `consumer_asserted`, `institutionally_supplied` or `not_established`. The latter three cannot carry a positive authority result. Extraction of an assertion and evidence of its truth are different predicates. An explicitly narrower result may survive, but the original stronger demand remains unresolved unless it is actually satisfied; changing its wording after seeing the evidence is not success. [G04; received audits]

Candidate vocabulary, interpretation, comparison, simulation and truthful refusal remain buildable with declared unknowns. This does not permit an otherwise unauthorized production effect. Separately authorized custody of a refusal is not automatically prohibited because the requested effect was refused. Appointment governs the named act, not all vocabulary; evidence about an appointment is integrated when its validity matters and merely observed when it is background information. [G01 §9(5–6); G03 §4.3]


**This question's constitutive boundary:** A professional report normally asserts an evaluation; signing it does not make the evaluated world true. A programme/institution's act can instead constitute a recognition status or entitlement to rely. Identify the exact latter relation before applying W5 §14.7; do not label every external opinion constitutive or treat every programme label as already accepted.
