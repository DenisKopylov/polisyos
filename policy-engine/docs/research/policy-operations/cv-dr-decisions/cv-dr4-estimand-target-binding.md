# CV-Q4 — Estimand target binding
## Final research decision package — recommendation, not decision

This package incorporates the assigned research, received question audit(s), and applicable independent cross-question findings. It is self-contained for an architect without Library access. Findings and decisive evidence are summarized with portable citations; unavailable evidence is not reconstructed. The principal alone decides. Source and audit labels resolve in §§5–8.

## 1. Question and frame

### Exact commissioned question

> Which question-binding artifact is the source of truth for population/contrast/outcome/horizon/intercurrent-event/regime identity, and what evidence implications may be registered for that bound target or an explicitly justified equivalent/transported target?

### Answer after audit

**Recommend one versioned semantic definition of each intended target, independent of the chosen analysis, with explicit evidence-use relations. Do not make the whole current `DesignProblem` or an execution-dependent whole-AST hash the source of target meaning. Do not select a second independently editable truth merely to keep the target “problem-level.”** [G05 CV-Q4; Q4-C01–C03; A4 AUD-001/RT-001]

The proposed source-of-truth unit is **one intended target version with its quantity-defining references**, addressed from the problem and used by analysis/evidence associations. Foundry retains the semantic ownership already recorded. A factored target-oriented AST core with generated views can satisfy both “AST-first” and “problem-anchored”; it is a serious lower-commitment candidate, not proof that the unmodified current AST already supplies the required invariant. Physical type/storage placement is **not settled** by this corpus. [G08; A4 §§3.1,7.1,8.2]

Preserve population/eligible units, intervention and comparator strategies, outcome construct and functional/summary, time origin and horizon, intercurrent-event treatment, regime and relevant context. These may overlap semantically and should not become independently editable duplicate fields. Distributional, dynamic, interfering or operator-valued targets require an honest supported envelope rather than an unqualified clinical template. Unknown and explicitly inapplicable are not interchangeable; equal missing fields do not prove equal complete targets. [Q4-E01; research §§5–7; A4 §7]

The current `EstimandAST.query_str` is display prose excluded from canonical identity. Nested formal expressions may represent time/regime distinctions, so a missing top-level spelling does not prove an AST incapable of doing so. Conversely, the current AST contains analysis-related metadata; hashing all of it does not establish a future target-only identity invariant. `DesignProblem` contains broader request context and can encompass more than one target. The compiler selects/lower strategies; a compiled graph is not an executed estimate or proof that the original question was preserved. [Q4-C01–C03; A4 §3.1]

Keep **identity, justified equivalence, directional derivation and transport** distinct. A known distribution may justify a particular functional without making their questions identical. A formal transport result holds under its model and data assumptions; recording or signing those premises does not establish their real-world applicability. `BOUND`, `IDENTIFIED`, `ESTIMABLE` and `ESTIMATED` are distinct properties, not a universal strength ladder. [G05/G07; Q4-E02; research §7.2]

Outer immutability is insufficient. A target that points to a mutable outcome definition can change meaning while its own bytes stay fixed. Bind the interpretations/versions of **quantity-defining referents** or retain the affected comparison as unresolved. Separately, the target may remain identical while measurement or transport support loses validity. Revise the affected evidence implication and consuming claim, not the historical target itself. The package adds no unbounded ontology or global event engine. [A4 RT-002; Q4-E03]

The source report's aggregate **`bridge_missing` label is withdrawn as unestablished**, not replaced by `absent/unallocated` by vote. Existing request/AST/compiler primitives and candidate/refusal delivery are evidenced at their stated scope; the exact positive semantic producer, persisted relation and consuming verifier have not been demonstrated. A missing semantic premise is not automatically a wiring problem. Appointment evidence is integrated when validity depends on it; only background monitoring is observe. Candidate work remains available. [G04; A4 AUD-001/003; AX AUD-004/005]


The decomposition above clarifies this exact question; it does not replace it with permission to create a module, choose a generic framework, or stop candidate work. The first actual deployment facts identified as missing remain missing.

## 3. Recommendation and confidence

Adopt the **single-defined target and explicit evidence-relation property**, not the original O2 storage preference. For candidate evaluation, place the strongest single-core/generated-view realization of O1 alongside a genuinely distinct O2 only where the latter adds a necessary property. A concrete O1 should win if it preserves the same supported target distinctions and downstream validity with less continuing burden; no measured superiority of either exists yet.

Thus this package answers the semantic source-of-truth unit but deliberately leaves the physical existing/new artifact decision unresolved. “One core” is a recommendation against duplicated edit authority, not a new production type or guarantee of universal expressiveness. A4 and AX return **GO_WITH_REVISIONS** for the narrowed direction. No positive semantic capability or benchmark passage is certified.

**Confidence:** Strong confidence in target/analysis distinction, typed relation boundaries and withdrawal of the unsupported maturity label; moderate confidence in define-once/reference as the least-commitment design principle; low confidence in physical placement, complete target-envelope expressiveness, empirical transport and comparative maintenance costs.

The received `GO_WITH_REVISIONS` verdict applies to the scoped research direction described by the auditor, not to a completed implementation or this final wording as a newly independently audited artifact. All criticisms are disposed of below; accepting a criticism does not fill its missing empirical premises.
