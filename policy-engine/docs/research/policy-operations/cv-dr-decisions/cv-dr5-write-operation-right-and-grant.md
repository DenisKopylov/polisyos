# CV-Q5 — Write operations — substantive right and technical grant
## Final research decision package — recommendation, not decision

This package incorporates the assigned research, received question audit(s), and applicable independent cross-question findings. It is self-contained for an architect without Library access. Findings and decisive evidence are summarized with portable citations; unavailable evidence is not reconstructed. The principal alone decides. Source and audit labels resolve in §§5–8.

## 1. Question and frame

### Exact commissioned question

> Which actual canonical mutation and resource are demanded, and which existing substantive-right and technical-grant owners must jointly authorize that operation?

### Answer after audit

**The first concrete operation is the existing served acquisition action: `OperationClass.ACQUIRE`, with action kind `runtime.evidence.acquisition.execute`. Its resource is the actual governed resource and effect bound by the invocation—not merely a CAS directory. Use the existing native authorization chain first; add a semantic or enforcement function only after demonstrating the precise function the native consumer cannot supply.** This strengthens the researcher's reuse-first qualification and moves a documented O0 ahead of an assumed additional O1 adapter. It does not certify native sufficiency. [Q5-C01–C05; A5 §§2,7,8; AX §7]

No concrete deployment resource instance, principal, mandate or grant is supplied by this research series. The decision can name the canonical operation family/action and the required resource-binding relation; it cannot invent an actual authorized invocation. The resource context must preserve the acting and represented principal, relevant tenant/cell/run/route, source and native admission context, purpose and actual effect. A path or tenant label alone is not this identity. [Research §§1–2; A5 §7]

The inspected workflow has distinguishable phases: request and route preparation; authority decision and reservation; durable queueing; worker reconstruction of the operation, invocation and intent; supplier/owner execution; quarantine or native commitment; later admission; and recovery/re-entry. Preparation occurs before gateway reservation, but neither its name nor this limited inspection proves that it is effect-free. The audit did **not** establish an unauthorized preparation mutation. The outstanding obligation is to account for actual protected effects throughout the selected path. [Q5-C02; A5 RT-001]

The expanded audit of `execute_bound_effect` found checks of persisted authority, exact operation/invocation/intent and binding identity, current delegation/mandate evidence and a live action envelope before the handler is called. This is substantial positive evidence against assuming that the native chain is just a signature check. It remains static, scoped source evidence; it does not independently establish every underlying institutional premise, transitive effect or phase-specific channel grant. [Q5-C04; A5 §7.1]

For each protected effect, **both an applicable substantive right and an applicable technical grant must concern that same effect, resource, principal/representation, purpose and relevant time**. This conjunction is necessary, not sufficient to bypass other native prerequisites such as cost admission, source qualification, reservation integrity or world/epoch admission. Substantive right can arise from the appropriate internal ownership rule, delegation, scoped approval or external act; it is not automatically a new legal opinion or fresh consent for every internal write. Two independently falsifiable grounds do not prescribe two people, two signatures or two credentials. [Research §1.2; A5 §§2,7,10; Q5-E01]

Existing responsibilities remain distinct. PDC supplies operation identity; Runtime's acquisition owner supplies the served demand and lifecycle; mandate/delegation and, where applicable, `HumanDecisionService` supply scoped decision evidence and custody; action-bound authorization and guarded resources supply the technical permission route. HDS is neither an arbitrary signature wrapper nor a universal substantive-right issuer. Its accepted decision must cover this act. These are source-evidenced functional destinations, not appointments of new authorities. [Q5-C01–C04; G08]

The native action decision explicitly denies uses including `claim_evidence`, `publication_authority`, `promotion_authority`, `legal_authority`, `data_authority` and `permission_vocabulary`. A permitted reuse keeps it in its native dispatch purpose; any separate consumer must rely on underlying evidence through an actually permitted interface. Wrapping an `allowed` value or deleting its restrictions cannot create a new CV-Q5 authority-bearing interpretation. [Q5-C04; A5 §7.2]

Currentness is phase-specific. A revocable-before-effect grant must not survive revocation merely because an old request was persisted. An explicitly valid durable delegation to a worker is not necessarily revoked by disappearance of the caller's session. Later admission is not automatically authorized by initial acquisition; recovery of an already committed result is not a new license to repeat the external effect. Conversely, authorized custody/readback repair should not require an irrelevant fresh acquisition approval. These meanings must come from the actual existing owners; no universal refresh interval or two-approval ritual is selected here. [Q5-C02/C04/C05; A5 RT-002]


The decomposition above clarifies this exact question; it does not replace it with permission to create a module, choose a generic framework, or stop candidate work. The first actual deployment facts identified as missing remain missing.

## 3. Recommendation and confidence

Make **O0—native reuse with explicit evidence and consumer binding—the implementation presumption** for the existing served acquisition action. Retain the O1 semantic task only where a concrete missing proposition, evidence link or enforcement behavior is demonstrated; extend the existing responsible owner rather than creating another authorizer. Treat O3 as a possible realization inside either route. No present multi-operation case establishes O2's advantage.

This changes the initial O1-first emphasis without claiming the existing code has already passed the complete right/grant tests. A5 and AX give **GO_WITH_REVISIONS** to a bounded, reuse-first research direction, not production permission. The decisive remaining evidence is the exact useful positive plus both asymmetric negatives and effect/phase-drift controls through the actual served path.

**Confidence:** Strong confidence in the same-effect conjunction, lifecycle distinctions and existence of substantial native machinery at the inspected reference; moderate confidence in native reuse as the least-commitment presumption; low confidence in complete effect coverage, actual institutional grounds, phase-specific grant semantics and measured marginal cost.

The received `GO_WITH_REVISIONS` verdict applies to the scoped research direction described by the auditor, not to a completed implementation or this final wording as a newly independently audited artifact. All criticisms are disposed of below; accepting a criticism does not fill its missing empirical premises.

## 7. Residual dissent

The native chain may already establish the needed property, making a new adapter a duplicate or an impermissible repurposing of a restricted dispatch decision. Conversely, substantial native checks do not prove the complete transitive effect set, every real substantive basis or phase-specific technical grant. A documentation-only answer will not repair a genuine enforcement gap. The decision therefore favors native reuse without declaring it sufficient, and retains an exact falsifier for when a local extension is warranted. No observed exploit, production safety claim or measured cost winner follows.

The unresolved objections are preserved in their strongest form. No author majority, repeated label or syntactic completion settles them. A conditional recommendation can proceed as research advice while the actual positive demand remains unfulfilled.
