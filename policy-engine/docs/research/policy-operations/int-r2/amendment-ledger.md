## 3. Correction Criterion

A response is made **in place** when leaving the original sentence, cell or coordinate standing would
leave a factual error, an overbroad repository-zero, an unregistered standing value, or a source
identity that a repository-only reader cannot replay. In-place corrections therefore cover:

- the false F01 coordinate;

- any sentence that calls the twelve-field ceiling wholly checkable or the 63-case battery executed.

A response is made by an **explicit additive amendment** only where the earlier statement remains
true but lacks a construction, invariant or closure condition. The additive text names the affected
audit finding, states what it supersedes, and is part of this package directory rather than an
unlinked afterthought. This applies to classifier predicates, field-level ceiling algebras,
open-world terminal coverage, the two T4 refinements and the uninstantiated benchmark requirements.

A response is **preserved** when the audit commendation or refuted threat found the package correct.
Preservation creates no positive credit against a defect and changes no standing.

No finding is declined in this amendment. The four unwalked absence findings are not defended by an
unpublished census; their local positives are retained and their repository-wide absence components
