# INT-R2 — Integration Handoff And Finding Register

## 1. Later integration handoff

### 1.1 Sequence

A later implementation should proceed only after research audit, amendment/verification as needed,
consolidation and ratification. The safe dependency order is:

1. ratify the eight case meanings, pre-union classification rule and authority-ceiling doctrine;
2. appoint the canonical residual-shape/non-data acquisition owner and register every adopted
   vocabulary used by a gate;
3. extend the canonical acquisition planner boundary without creating a second planner;
4. bind each case type to its existing domain owner or an explicitly external institutional producer;
5. persist content-bound admitted artifacts and terminal/re-entry receipts;
6. wire each demanding canonical gate to re-resolve and enforce the exact ceiling;
7. instantiate and run the public regression and sealed-holdout semantic batteries;
8. project owner-produced states through the one Atlas lattice.

The draft consumer row named `gap_acquisition_case_union` is
`institutionally_supplied` demand evidence only. The package has no immutable branch/ref/path from
which this holder can reconstruct the row. Until that evidence is supplied, the slice is merged, and a
ratified producer/artifact exists, the row is not authority, owner allocation or consumer readiness.

### 1.3 Capstone linkage

The three capstone routes enter the handoff only through `GapShapeAssessment`:

  after the amended positive predicate is constructed;

## 2. Promotion and kill rules

### 2.1 `research_only` — current state

Required and currently true:

- result is Markdown evidence only;
- no canonical owner, producer registry, institutional signer or runtime contract is appointed;
- no public/governed/production gate may consume it directly;
- all eight case capabilities and the aggregate evaluator remain `absent/unallocated`.

### 2.2 `prototype_allowed`

A prototype is allowed only when all conditions hold:

- runs under an explicit `contract_testing`/research-only authority scope;
- uses synthetic producer identities and cannot emit production-admissible artifacts;
- implements the discriminator, P37 classification, the four directly checkable ceiling relations,
  fail-closed treatment of the eight deferred relations, and no-auto-close re-entry;
- instantiates all 63 public cases with stable IDs, exact inputs, an independent oracle and named
  mutants, and then passes with zero unsafe closes/escapes;
- exposes no default-enabled consumer or public-signature effect;
- labels external vocabularies as candidate mappings rather than registered truth.

The present package has not met the fixture-instantiation condition; its fixture capability remains
`semantic_test_missing`.

### 2.3 `governed_allowed`

Governed use requires, at minimum:

- ratified union semantics, pre-union classifier, deeper-terminal test and ceiling doctrine;
- a named canonical contract owner and per-type producer/owner maps;
- registered vocabularies for every positive-eligible claim strength, commitment stage, operation,
  assurance level and trigger used by a gate;
- persisted, resolvable, content-bound artifacts with non-producer verifier provenance;
- producer standing and currentness re-resolved at use time;
- demanding-gate bridges that recompute closure and enforce every defined ceiling relation;
- one-lattice status composition and four-audience projections;
- public regression plus sealed holdout, corruption, sibling-consumer and historical-replay tests;
- explicit fail-closed handling when an institutional producer or signer is absent.

Research ratification alone is not governed capability.

### 2.4 `production_candidate`

In addition to governed conditions:

- real institutional producers/providers are appointed and operating;
- retention, revocation, succession, expiry, contestability and incident/reissue paths are exercised;
- source-specific legal/normative/professional/assurance requirements are maintained;
- capacity and expert/adjudication thresholds are calibrated or honestly capped where calibration is
  unavailable;
- runtime orchestration, audit/API/dashboard surfaces and external verification are complete;
- zero protected false-close/ceiling-escape results survive production-shaped semantic fixtures.

### 2.5 Kill rules

Any one of the following is a `blocked` result for the affected implementation:

1. an unclassified residual defaults to `data_gap` or any union branch;
2. row-count growth closes `grounding_relation`, `estimand_binding` or `legal_mandate` while the
   required object is absent;
3. presence, shape, keyword, signature, `external=true` or self-declared verifier identity is accepted
   without resolve/content-bind/non-producer provenance;
4. the requested use is broader than a defined ceiling relation, or an unknown/deferred ceiling field
   is treated as permission;
5. producer scarcity converts an internal reviewer into an independent assurer, an available employee
   into a competent decision-maker, or PolicyOS into the external institution;
6. a route or artifact self-closes without demanding-gate recomputation;
7. `deeper_terminal` is emitted from timeout, no result, near-threshold score, extra effort or an
   unbounded registry search without new admitted boundary evidence;
8. a surface composes or mints readiness/authority not supplied by canonical owners;
9. external survey terminology is used as a gate vocabulary without registered mapping;
10. compound required objects are collapsed so that one branch silently closes another;
11. substantive mutation authority is inferred from a technical grant, or vice versa;
12. a competent human decision is consumed as independent assurance, or favourable assurance is
    consumed as the underlying management/authority decision.

### 2.6 `out_of_scope`

The institutional acts themselves remain out of scope: legislation/delegation, ethics adjudication,
consent, authoritative-register adjudication, professional licensing, independent-assurance service
and delivery-system operation. PolicyOS integrates evidence and owns its reaction, not those functions.

## 3. Open questions for consolidation

1. **Canonical placement.** Should the narrow common envelope live in `pdc`, with per-type adapters in
   `runtime/quality`, or should the entire candidate remain outside the waist until one producer path is
   proven? Stage 1 does not decide.
2. **Owner appointment.** Which existing team/canonical module owns residual shape and the generic
   non-data admission/re-entry plane? CG5 cannot own it because CG5 is explicitly a router.
3. **Vocabulary registration.** Which existing vocabularies can represent relation claim strength,
   estimand binding strength, legal/normative/write operations, capacity stages and assurance levels?
   External terms are not registered merely by citation.
4. **Causal adjudication.** Which domain-specific procedures, if any, can authoritatively classify a
   relation, and what maximum claim language does each permit? A universal threshold remains open.
5. **Social licence.** Is it represented only as a continuously evidenced legitimacy condition, routed
   to another task, or excluded from `normative_authorization` unless a formal regime supplies an issuer?
6. **Capacity owner/calibration.** Who owns direct prerequisite evidence, assessor independence,
   commitment-stage rules and longitudinal calibration without turning a checklist into authority?
7. **Compound gaps.** What ordering/dependency graph governs relation + writability, mandate +
   normative authorization, or capacity + decision + audit combinations?
8. **The 14 residuals.** The later complete row-level measurement and identity of the one data-shaped
   member must be imported and independently reconciled before any per-row classification claim.
9. **Human-decision overlap.** Consolidate with INT-R5/GY-PA2/Atlas DS9 semantics; do not create a
   second competence certificate.
10. **Audit overlap.** Extend `core/audit` packaging and runtime assurance owners while keeping the
    independent provider external.
11. **Consumer readiness.** The exact `gap_acquisition_case_union` row remains
    `institutionally_supplied` until an immutable branch/ref/path is supplied; it is not a binding
    consumer or authority source.
12. **Status composition.** Acquisition process states must map to the existing Atlas lattice without
    creating new readiness/publication authority.
13. **Institutional layer.** The package appoints no signers/providers. A technically complete
    integration can remain blocked indefinitely if that layer is not established.
14. **Ceiling algebras.** Eight field relations remain unregistered/unimplemented and fail closed.
15. **Benchmark instantiation.** Stable cases, independent oracle ownership and red-proven mutants
    remain a later implementation prerequisite.

Recommended consolidation owner remains a later architect decision involving GY architecture, Lex,
human-decision, core-audit, Atlas and the future knowledge/grounding acquisition owner. This sentence
does not appoint that owner.
