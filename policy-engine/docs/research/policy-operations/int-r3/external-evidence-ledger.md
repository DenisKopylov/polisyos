# INT-R3 external evidence ledger

## Use rule

The five commissioned deep-research surveys are **surveyed external practice**. They are not
repository capability, registered PolicyOS vocabulary, institutional authority or evidence about the
current surfaces. A rate measured in another domain is never copied into a PolicyOS estimate. Each
transferable result below states the transfer argument and its limit.

## Survey inputs

1. Comprehension of `unknown`, missing, intervals, incomparability and remaining risk budget.
2. Decision under time pressure and the choice to act, acquire, escalate or defer.
3. Blockers, refusals, overrides and weakest-link behavior.
4. Behavioral comprehension benchmark design and defensible ground truth.
5. Accessibility, numeracy and the populations where conditional meaning can fail.

## Preserved theoretical disagreement

The package does not collapse naturalistic decision making into heuristics-and-biases. NDM supplies
the empirical description that experienced professionals often recognize a situation and evaluate
one plausible action rather than compare a menu. The heuristics-and-biases tradition supplies the
boundary condition that subjective expertise is trustworthy only where the environment is
sufficiently valid and feedback supports learning. The benchmark therefore permits a fast path
**inside** a constrained envelope and tests explicit exits when the envelope is violated. It does not
require deliberation on every case and does not trust recognition without boundary cues.

## Thin intersections that remain open

The following are too thin for a design recommendation to be promoted to a behavioral fact:

- explicit epistemic `unknown` versus zero, missing, unavailable and not applicable;
- set-valued uncertainty without a probability distribution;
- strict `incomparable` versus tie, insufficient evidence and system failure;
- remaining policy-risk or δ budget as an operator display;
- quarantine compliance as a distinct human task;
- screen-reader or keyboard-only reasoning over weakest-link, epoch and conditional δ composition;
- the interaction of low numeracy with assistive technology and time pressure.

## Numbers and transfer discipline

Every reported external number remains attached in the source survey to its population, instrument,
task, alert or exposure type and denominator. No external override rate, accessibility accuracy rate,
numeracy prevalence or effect size is used as a PolicyOS prior. Stage 1 introduces no acceptance
threshold from those values.

Study power and safety thresholds must later be set from:

- the target operator population;
- participant and scenario variance;
- the number of **eligible** unsafe-action, false-pass and override opportunities;
- the smallest operationally meaningful difference or acceptable upper bound;
- an appointed governance loss/acceptance rule.
