# External primary-source and transfer ledger

## 1. Method and boundary

This ledger uses official legislation, official publication systems, official statistical policy,
archives guidance, preservation standards, and the named scholarly-governance source. Stable legal,
publication, product, and DOI identifiers are the anchors. Each row separates the proposition the
source establishes from the limited systems property transferred to PAO-R36 and from everything that
does not transfer.

Nothing here decides applicable law, legal sufficiency, institutional competence, deadlines,
publication venue, retention, remedy, or a real notification duty.

## 3. Transfer synthesis

The transferable pattern is reasons-bearing additive correction, preservation of the original,
durable publication linkage, explicit treatment of adverse effects and old-version significance,
accessible dissemination of independently required content, governed language-set enumeration,
transparent vintages, and bounded correction analysis.

The non-transferable elements are jurisdiction-specific competence, deadlines, hearing/notice/receipt
sufficiency, legal effect, publication venue, retention, disposal, remedy, language selection, and
translation-parity mechanics. Language-invariant correction identity belongs to the PAO-R36
interface requested from INT-R6, not to Council Regulation No 1. Accessibility attaches to content
required on another basis; it does not create that substantive basis.

No cited source authorizes implementation, publication, signing, or a capability claim. Before any
legal-sufficiency conclusion, the responsible institution and counsel must identify the issuer,
record class, jurisdiction, affected parties, publication-of-record rule, review route, and effect on
prior decisions.
