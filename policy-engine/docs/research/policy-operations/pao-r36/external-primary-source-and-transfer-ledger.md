# External primary-source and transfer ledger

## 1. Method and boundary

This ledger uses official legislation, official publication systems, official statistical policy,
archives guidance, and preservation standards. Stable legal or publication identifiers are the
citation anchors. Each row states both the limited systems property that transfers to a record
published and signed by PolicyOS and the proposition that does not transfer. Nothing here decides
applicable law or legal sufficiency.

## 2. Ledger

## 3. Transfer synthesis

Across the EU, US, and UK, the transferable pattern is reasons-bearing additive correction,
preservation of the original, durable publication linkage, explicit treatment of adverse effects and
old-version significance, accessible/language-aware dissemination, and transparent vintages. The
non-transferable elements are jurisdiction-specific competence, deadlines, legal effect, hearing or
notification sufficiency, publication venue, retention, and remedy.

No cited source authorizes PolicyOS implementation or publication. Before any legal-sufficiency
claim, the responsible institution and counsel must identify the issuer, record class, jurisdiction,
affected parties, publication-of-record rule, review route, and consequences for decisions already
taken.
