# PAO-R1 — Operational Boundary Method and Evidence-Interface Census

## Executive Finding

**Result: `accepted_narrower_scope`.**

The ratified four-way test is a strong boundary-review method only when it
classifies one declared plane at a time:

```text
external institutional act
→ evidence emission
→ PolicyOS receipt / verification / admission
→ scoped PolicyOS claim reaction
→ governed public projection
```

An external act remains externally owned and PolicyOS execution is prohibited.
If its result can change a PolicyOS claim, the evidence relationship is
**INTEGRATE**. Purpose-specific admission and the reaction of PolicyOS-owned
claims are **OWN**. The publication owner produces the governed projection;
Atlas renders it without minting authority. Missing external evidence is not
proof of non-occurrence, and the canonical consumer owns the claim-specific
fail-closed reaction.

The 213-row Appendix-C register and EC-01..21 catalogue in the original report
are research hypotheses, not a frozen adjudication baseline. The register is
retained as a non-authoritative census/questionnaire; the evidence catalogue is
reduced to family questions. PAO-R1 does not create a universal institutional
envelope, common evidence-status lattice, owner-state lattice, fixed clock
bundle, challenge workflow, quarterly review rule, or backlog
reclassification.

The exact supplied source is preserved at
[sources/pao-r1-original.md](sources/pao-r1-original.md). This compressed
revision supersedes its accepted conclusions while retaining the full research
record.

# 1. Task And Project Fit

## 1.1 Narrow research question

How should PolicyOS apply the ratified OWN / INTEGRATE / OBSERVE /
OUT_OF_SCOPE test without:

- acquiring an administrative anti-role;
- disclaiming responsibility for claims it signs;
- treating evidence transport as institutional performance;
- creating a universal schema or status system beside canonical owners;
- converting a research census into an authority decision?

## 1.2 Unit of analysis

The original formula remains useful as a review prompt:

> narrow function × PolicyOS claim at risk × real operator × evidence
> relationship × downstream PolicyOS reaction.

It is not one required database row. A valid review represents linked
analytical objects and assigns a verdict to one plane or relationship at a
time. If a proposed row contains more than one verdict, owner, lifecycle, or
authority transition, it must be decomposed.

## 1.3 Boundary verdict for PAO-R1

PolicyOS **OWNS** truthful classification of its own responsibility and the
effect of external evidence on claims it signs. It does not thereby own the
external functions catalogued. PAO-R1 itself is research guidance, not a
production boundary registry or authority grant.

## 1.4 False claims prevented

The method is designed to reject claims such as:

- “PolicyOS adjudicated the appeal” when it admitted an external outcome;
- “PolicyOS served notice” when it received a send-status event;
- “PolicyOS paid compensation” when it saw authorization or settlement data;
- “PolicyOS delivered the service” when it displayed an operator report;
- “the audit passed” when PolicyOS produced a verifiable package;
- “no incident occurred” when an external source was silent;
- “Atlas approved the record” when it rendered an upstream projection.

# 3. External Research Baseline

External sources support role separation but do not allocate universal public
authority:

- [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) separates entities, activities,
  agents, roles, and derivation; provenance does not establish competence;
- the [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)
  separates organizational governance, lifecycle responsibilities, and
  third-party dependencies; it is not a legal delegation;
- the [EU AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) distinguishes
  providers, deployers, oversight persons, and competent authorities within its
  scope;
- [GOV.UK Service Manual roles](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/the-team/what-each-role-does-in-service-team)
  illustrate end-to-end service ownership; a metric renderer is not the
  delivery operator;
- [eIDAS](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/oj) illustrates why
  dispatch, delivery evidence, identity, time, and legal effect must not be

- [ISSAI 100](https://www.issai.org/pronouncements/issai-100-fundamental-principles-of-public-sector-auditing/)
  separates auditor, responsible party, and intended users;

  assigns internal-control responsibilities in its US federal scope;
- the OECD report
  [*Governing with Artificial Intelligence*](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/governing-with-artificial-intelligence_795de142-en/full-report.html)
  compares distinct governance, data, infrastructure, procurement, and skills
  functions.

These sources are jurisdiction- and mandate-bounded examples. They do not prove
who operates a real partner system, whether an event is binding, or that
PolicyOS may perform the act.

# 4. Result

## 4.1 Plane-by-plane method

| Plane | Question | Typical PolicyOS relation |
|---|---|---|
| External act | Who legally or operationally performs the function? | External ownership or prohibited PolicyOS execution |
| Evidence emission | Who produces a record about the act, under what competence? | INTEGRATE relationship candidate |
| Receipt/verification/admission | For which purpose may PolicyOS rely on the evidence? | OWN, using canonical family/admission owners |
| Claim reaction | Which PolicyOS claims become limited, blocked, revalidated, corrected, or unchanged? | OWN, consumer-specific |
| Public projection | Which governed facts can be shown, with attribution and currentness? | OWN publication discipline; Atlas renders |

The verdict is attached to the declared plane or relationship, not to an
ambiguous noun such as “appeals,” “payments,” “records,” or “monitoring.”

## 4.2 Four-way test

For a narrow PolicyOS responsibility:

- **OWN** when PolicyOS must perform the custody/epistemic function to keep a
  claim it signs truthful;
- **INTEGRATE** when an external result can change a PolicyOS claim and
  PolicyOS therefore needs a typed, purpose-bound, fail-closed evidence path;
- **OBSERVE** when a signal informs context or acquisition but has no current
  authority effect;
- **OUT_OF_SCOPE** when PolicyOS has no legitimate custody relation or
  performing the act would violate an anti-role.

An OUT_OF_SCOPE act can have a separately classified INTEGRATE evidence
relationship. For example, payment execution is not a PolicyOS act, while
settlement evidence may be relevant to a narrow PolicyOS claim.

## 4.3 Boundary-review questionnaire

A candidate review should answer, without implying one storage schema:

1. What exact plane and activity are being classified?
2. Which PolicyOS claim would otherwise become false, stale, or misleading?
3. Who performs the real-world act?
4. Who produces the evidence, and are those actors the same?
5. What demonstrates competence, mandate, subject, jurisdiction, scope, and
   applicable time?
6. Which family-native payload and transport/provenance record crosses the
   boundary?
7. Who verifies and admits it for the declared purpose?
8. Which canonical consumer owns materiality and reaction?
9. Which publication owner supplies a projection, and what may Atlas render?
10. What happens when the evidence is missing, late, stale, contradictory,
    corrected, revoked, unavailable, or outside scope?
11. Which assumptions are jurisdiction-, pilot-, or partner-dependent?
12. Which anti-role, duplicate-owner, or authority-laundering falsifier applies?

The questionnaire records uncertainty; it does not adjudicate an institution's
legal powers.

## 4.4 Analytical roles

Use only roles demonstrated for the reviewed case:

| Role | Meaning |
|---|---|
| External operator | Performs or decides the real-world act |
| Competent authority | Supplies the mandate or legal competence |
| External evidence producer | Emits the family-native fact or record |
| Transport/provenance owner | Custodies receipt and lineage |
| Verifier/admission owner | Accepts or rejects use for a declared purpose |
| Claim consumer | Owns materiality and downstream PolicyOS reaction |
| Publication owner | Produces the governed record/projection |
| Renderer | Displays upstream state without minting authority |
| Research reviewer | Proposes or challenges the boundary analysis |

These are analytical roles, not mandatory fields and not an `owner_state`
enum. Capability completeness is recorded separately.

## 4.5 Evidence-interface composition

Do not create a universal `InstitutionalEvidenceEnvelope`. Compose:

```text
family-native external fact
+ transport/provenance reference
+ purpose-scoped authority and verification references
+ separately owned admission receipt
+ consumer-owned reaction
+ publication-owned projection
```

The source producer must not prescribe which PolicyOS claims change. The
admission layer must not pretend to have performed the external act. The
renderer must not convert the record into authority.

## 4.6 Absence and correction semantics

| Evidence condition | Source/admission responsibility | Consumer responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Missing/unavailable | Record unknown; never infer non-occurrence | Decide whether the dependent claim is limited, blocked, or unchanged |
| Late | Preserve source occurrence and custody history | Evaluate retroactive materiality without rewriting the old view |
| Stale/expired | Record failed freshness for the purpose | Revalidate affected claims |
| Malformed/unsigned where required | Quarantine or reject | No authority effect |
| Contradictory | Preserve competing evidence and dispute | Apply weakest safe boundary; route competent/human review if required |
| Unauthorized producer/scope mismatch | Reject for the declared use | Do not broaden claim scope |
| Corrected/revoked | Append successor/revocation relation | Perform scoped impact and correction/reissue/withdrawal as owned |

A generic evidence layer may report the condition and admission disposition.
Only the canonical claim consumer can choose the least-expansive safe reaction.

## 4.7 OBSERVE firewall

An observed signal cannot be “promoted” in place. It may trigger acquisition or
review, but authority requires a new purpose-bound artifact that is resolved,
content-bound, provenance-bearing, and admitted through its canonical path.
The observation remains historically visible as an observation.

# 6. Benchmark Or Fixture Proposal

All fixtures remain **proposed and unexecuted** until included in an independent
benchmark architecture.

## 6.1 Core metamorphic properties

1. Changing a UI label cannot change a boundary verdict.
2. Receiving evidence cannot imply PolicyOS performed the act.
3. A verified external result may change PolicyOS claims without transferring
   ownership.
4. Missing decisive evidence cannot become a pass.
5. OBSERVE cannot gain authority without a new admission.
6. OUT execution may have a separately classified INTEGRATE evidence edge.
7. Signature integrity cannot close subject, jurisdiction, purpose, or
   competence.
8. Corrected evidence appends a delta and does not rewrite historical replay.
9. Replacing the external provider requires fresh competence and trust
   evidence.
10. A projection cannot mint authority.
11. A generic wrapper cannot choose a family consumer's reaction.
12. A broad family must decompose when planes have different verdicts.

## 6.2 Critical predicates

Over a closed, independently labelled fixture population, future evaluation
should treat these as critical:

- external execution represented as PolicyOS action;
- INTEGRATE relationship without a deterministic absence route;
- observation-to-authority upgrade;
- duplicate canonical owner;
- competence/scope mismatch admitted;
- silent historical rewrite;
- public projection that omits operator, evidence posture, as-of/currentness,
  or authority limitation where material.

No executable corpus, runner, or numerical Stage-0 threshold is claimed here.

# 9. Promotion And Kill Rules

## 9.1 Research-only

Current standing. Operator mappings, competence, legal effect, partner systems,
canonical owners, and family contracts remain unresolved in many rows.

## 9.2 Prototype allowed

A synthetic questionnaire, adapter mock, or projection mock is allowed if:

- external execution is never performed;
- fixture facts are labelled scenario axioms;
- observations remain non-authoritative;
- absence, contradiction, correction, and revocation are represented;
- outputs cite canonical family owners or mark them unresolved;
- public wording identifies operator, evidence posture, and PolicyOS reaction.

## 9.3 Governed work

A governed integration requires a named real operator, competent evidence
producer, family-native contract, purpose-scoped admission path, provenance,
tenant/jurisdiction closure, consumer-owned reaction, correction/revocation
semantics, semantic negative tests, and governed projection.

## 9.4 Blocked

Block a proposal when:

1. it assigns PolicyOS an anti-role;
2. an external act is labelled as PolicyOS execution because evidence arrived;
3. INTEGRATE lacks fail-closed absence behavior;
4. OBSERVE affects authority without a new admission;
5. a dashboard, benchmark, signature, or transport status mints authority;
6. a universal envelope, status system, clock bundle, or owner-state lattice
   bypasses canonical owners;
7. external producers prescribe claim reactions;
8. operator, competence, subject, purpose, jurisdiction, or applicable time is
   assumed from a role name;
9. a future task or research team is presented as a runtime owner;
10. a task disposition or governance cadence is treated as ratified through
    this report.
