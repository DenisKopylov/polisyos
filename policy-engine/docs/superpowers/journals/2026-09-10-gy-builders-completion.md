change. Stage 2 implements the four tasks with their ratified negatives first. The
completion status is decided by executable behaviour, never institutional appointment.

The task-local decisions contain the detailed plans. Root owns integration, independent
locale parser B and its comparison gate, shared README/facade/registry changes, the
canonical GY plan entries and this handback. The A worker owns the Fabric mechanism and
new `runtime/quality/non_data_acquisition.py` composition bridge. The B worker owns the durable mechanism and its
minimal extension of `runtime/http/services/control_plane_store.py`. C owns the separate
operator and Lex mechanism files. `runtime/quality/README.md` and
`runtime/quality/__init__.py` are serialized through root: CR1 and CB1 never write them
concurrently. Git commits and governed-artifact mutations are also serialized by root.

Every deciding gate and removal probe retains its complete output. Oversized evidence
belongs in the ignored `gy-builders/**/raw/` directory; tracked sources are cited by
`path@sha`, not embedded. Every set-level count requires complete enumeration and a
second independent comparison. Unreadable members are ambiguous.

The default architecture gate is **not_completed**: its compiler chain invokes the
expressly prohibited debt-ledger checker. Neither `DEBT-REGISTER.md` nor `LEDGER.md` may
change. No directory-wide test run, backend suite or CI-parity run is authorized here.
