## PC-R01 — deciding rule, before the ruling

A reduction of whole obligations holds only when the authority slot with its
purpose/selection scope, the operative refusal mechanism, AND the missing condition
coincide. Shared wording or an identical refusal code establishes none of these.
An identical register acceptance criterion and an identical single deciding
predicate are distinct hypotheses and are adjudicated separately below.
