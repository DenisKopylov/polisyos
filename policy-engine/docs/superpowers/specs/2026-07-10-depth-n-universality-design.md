# GY-N10 Depth-N Universality Design

## Status

Approved architecture: extend canonical owners and add one thin recursive
router. This revision incorporates the five approval corrections: a mandatory
real non-panel positive, a bounded N4 owner-surface repair, one-directional N6
validation, an N7 consumer census before fencing bootstrap authority, and four
commit-gated implementation stages. The resumed revision additionally pins the
historical N10a receipt, extends the bounded N4 import reconciliation through
recorded formalizer evidence, cross-binds candidate levers to their complete
cycle context, routes disposition-only N4 output, and rejects wrong-checkout
proof execution structurally.

## Goal

Prove that the existing PolicyOS generation cycle is universal over arbitrary
plain-language `DesignProblem`s, recursion depth, genuinely distinct domains,
and Foundry method families. Universality means that every stage makes a real
owner attempt and emits an honest typed terminal. It does not mean every run
promotes.

The proof must include:

- a first-vertical regression run through the generalized path;
- an education run that materially advances beyond the committed N10a
  `a_spec_gap` grounding-entry baseline while preserving writability `0` and
  frozen CG2 production binds;
- an unseen no-pack domain that degrades to an acquisition, abstention, or
  spec-gap terminal without a crash or a first-/second-domain-shaped mismatch;
- a real non-panel `value_ready` receipt whose width comes from the selected
  method's native uncertainty contract;
- an honest education non-panel refusal when the estimand or unbound lever
  cannot support value authority;
- observed depth greater than two and real N5 coupling/joint-simulation
  evidence; and
- a live GY-G strangle receipt with zero production fixture callers.

## Non-goals

- Do not make education promote. Its zero writability and frozen CG2 binds are
  expected to block promotion.
- Do not create a domain enum, a name-keyed domain resolver, a second substrate
  registry, a second generation cycle, a second coupling algebra, or a second
  value authority surface.
- Do not complete N7 owner-registration derivation or raw journal-evidence
  persistence. They remain typed infrastructure residuals unless a required
  owner path already closes them without scope expansion.
- Do not refactor `intervention_substrate.py` beyond the bounded public-wrapper,
  hash-binding, and typed candidate-resolution surface required by the four
  bridges.
- Do not turn N10a's transport component into authority. It is content-bound
  candidate evidence and a query/input hint until real owners verify it.

## Governing invariants

1. **Candidate is never authority.** Pack levers, transport profiles, generated
   designs, and LLM-derived decompositions remain candidates until their real
   owners resolve, content-bind, and verify them.
2. **One intake and one emission.** Domain substrate evidence enters through
   the canonical S0/WMR intake. Value leaves through `ValueOuterSet`. Coupling
   leaves through the existing coupling/composition contracts.
3. **Data owns vocabulary.** Covariates, levers, contexts, regimes, outcomes,
   and method families derive from pack/registry/catalog data. Engine code may
   not branch on domain names or enumerate the Foundry family denominator.
4. **Fail closed on substance.** A path/ref prefix, present contract-shaped
   mapping, empty coupling graph, or self-attestation is not evidence.
5. **Honest terminals are success.** A real refusal is a successful execution
   outcome but cannot, by itself, prove that a positive projector path works.
6. **No authority inflation.** Simulation receipts remain K_sim evidence and
   cannot shrink K_world or authorize promotion. Candidate transport data
   cannot mint transport authority.
7. **Content address all replay inputs.** Pack, registry, WMR, method signature,
   native report/envelope, selection diagram, coupling graph, and composition
   inputs are hash-bound. Runtime timestamps and wall time are never included
   in content hashes.
8. **One mutating process.** Tests, validators, source flips, writes, and cold
   proof runs execute serially. Long owner lanes receive their full budget.

## 1. Content-bound pack intake and canonical substrate context

The N10a pack is consumed, not rebuilt. The generated-proof producer validates
the committed manifest content hash and projects its data into canonical
runtime contracts. No `src/polisyos/**` module opens
`layer3_gy_second_domain_pack.json` by filename.

N10a's `zero_engine_code` receipt is historical evidence, not a moving-head
gate. It is pinned to the immutable N9 merge base
`26cc7cc03efc9da44362dc2914a5bde8ac8f7e73` through the N10a proof head
`d8a8cf076da6233c66b0a90010647c0d437e81c4`. The checker replays that exact
commit range, proves both commits are ancestors of the current checkout, and
continues to rederive pack provenance from live owners. Repointing the receipt
at `HEAD` fails `historical_receipt_rebased_to_moving_head`; GY-N10 scope is
owned only by the GY-N10 contract.

The projection has two authority layers:

- **Canonical owner layer:** rederived `SubstrateRegistration`s, a persisted
  `SubstrateRegistry`, selected entry hashes, and a concrete
  `WorldModelRecord` built from those entries.
- **Candidate evidence layer:** pack-derived lever vocabulary and transport
  profiles carrying their original `candidate_unbound` and
  `candidate_context_only_not_transport_authority` postures.

One cycle-entry context binds the selected domain, DesignProblem hash, stable
pack substrate-input hash when present, S0 registry/ref, concrete WMR/ref, selected entry hashes,
candidate levers, transport evidence, and any verified writable L6 bundle.
Every candidate lever carries its parent pack/context hash and its selected S0
entry hash. Validation proves membership in the context, presence in both the
registry and WMR resolution, and equality of WMR/context registry hashes before
the lever can reach a consumer. Every downstream consumer receives that same
context. A cache entry is keyed by these content hashes and exact requested
slots, never `repo_root` or a domain name.

The context binds a stable `substrate_input_content_hash` computed only from
the owner-derived pack inputs. The final pack manifest also names downstream
cycle-trace and gap-report hashes, so using its final manifest hash as an input
to that trace would be a cryptographic cycle. Trace/gap refs and runtime metrics
are excluded from the substrate-input hash; the final manifest still binds all
of them in the outward direction and records the immutable N10a source-pack
hash from the historical proof head.

The intake rejects stale pack hashes, stale registry hashes, unresolved S0
entries, a WMR/ref mismatch, shaped strings without a resolved object, and
cross-domain/context substitution. A missing unseen-domain pack emits a typed
`domain_substrate_unresolved`/acquisition outcome before any fixed-world record
is constructed.

The following sibling bypasses are fenced as part of the same structural
change:

- fixed-UA boundary WMR construction;
- non-pending shaped-string WMR acceptance;
- global-registry reconstruction that ignores the selected pack/registry;
- implicit fixed-Ukraine WMR construction in lever resolution; and
- synthetic `n6.bootstrap.*` S0 authority.

Before the bootstrap fence lands, all callers are censused. N7 acquisition
tests and the N8 no-hints production path must either receive the canonical
registry/WMR context or return a typed owner refusal. No caller may silently
lose its fallback.

`derive_lever_space_prompt_slice` and all sibling N4 lever consumers accept the

### Pack levers to intervention resolution

`resolve_intervention_lever` remains the existing owner. Successful evidence

The resolver consumes the complete `CycleSubstrateContext`, not a loose
candidate plus independently supplied hashes. Cross-pack candidate
substitution, selected-entry mismatch, stale WMR, or a WMR whose registry hash

grounding/acquisition terminal. It may not replace the N4 result with grammar

all-empty special case. Stage 1 is not complete until
education generation proposes over exact pack levers, grounding receives the
real disposition/content hashes, and the cycle terminal is independently
typed and differs from `a_spec_gap`. The terminal is derived by the existing
mapping (`novel_cg3` currently routes through a grounding gap toward
`search_ceiling_repair_required`); the proof may not preselect
`acquisition_required`. Grounding dispositions and cycle terminals remain
separate vocabularies.

The Stage-1 gap artifact closes `s0_to_n4_l6_bridge_missing`,
`s0_to_n5_wmr_bridge_missing`, and `s0_to_l6_world_slot_bridge_missing` with

## 3. A1 transport de-hardcode

The transport resolver consumes the candidate's actual world relationship and
the cycle substrate context. It resolves an ordered set of transport dimensions
with source/target context IDs, observed values or bounds, variable provenance,
valid-time roles, query/pack hashes, and an authority posture.

IR's existing selection-diagram owner is extended to accept arbitrary measured
S-nodes. `ContextProfile` remains the common strict context surface; domain
variables such as `education_spending` and `school_quality` stay explicit
S-node dimensions.

No fixed covariate tuple, numeric source/target default, `post_conflict` default,
or jurisdiction-token inference remains. The first vertical derives equivalent
dimensions from its resolved substrate relationship. Education derives its
dimension names and candidate profiles from the pack by content. A third
pack-shaped vocabulary requires zero engine changes.

Candidate-only profiles may construct a real diagram and trigger a real
transport/calibration attempt, but the verifier may limit or refuse them. A
missing value, swapped context, stale hash, or unresolved role becomes a typed
acquisition/refusal rather than a fabricated number or role.

## 4. A2 Foundry-owned generic value projection

The cycle stops interpreting panel/DID report fields. A Foundry component next
to the existing consensus/output-contract owners resolves:

- the selected catalog signature and family from the live catalog snapshot;
- the exact typed output slot and required contract ID;
- treatment, outcome, population, context, and time through an explicit
  `EstimandSpec` binding;
- the report's native `UncertaintyEnvelope`, credible interval, prediction
  interval, distributional/transport bound, or rigorous validated bound;
- diagnostic gates, coverage semantics, truthfulness receipt, calibration
  evidence, and limitations.

It returns a typed value-evidence projection with `value_ready`, limited, or
refused status and an exact reason. A shaped mapping without the resolved
contract ID fails. A correct report for the wrong estimand fails. Diagnostic-
only methods are typed unsupported. Forecast, microsimulation, and predictive
contracts cannot masquerade as causal intervention effects.

`FoundryValuePort` remains the orchestration bridge. The real calibration
verifier remains authoritative: an interval alone does not auto-pass, while a
convergent report is not blocked by leftover panel counts. `ValueOuterSet`
consumes the projected envelope; its width begins with the native interval and
may only stay equal or widen under grounded transport/applicability limits.

### Mandatory two-part A2 evidence

The frozen artifact must contain both:

1. **Real non-panel positive.** Select a non-panel catalog method whose
   treatment and estimand genuinely bind. The preferred early feasibility lane
   is a Bayesian/posterior method over owner-resolved first-vertical
   `avg_income` observations and the real credit treatment binding. The solve
   must produce `value_ready`, and changing the native posterior interval must
   change `ValueOuterSet.width`. The Bayesian FQN is a planning expectation,
   never a runtime default: the existing registry/advisor must select it from
   the candidate, problem, and owner-data modality, with a receipt recording
   the ranked alternatives and why panel methods lost. The regression treatment
   feature must be joined from the candidate atom's real treated units and
   periods; an arbitrary or synthetic indicator is not admissible evidence. If
   that candidate is not semantically valid, another real bound treatment must
   be chosen before the Stage 2 exit gate can pass.
2. **Education honesty negative.** Run a real registry-selected non-panel method
   over education observations. If a `candidate_unbound` teaching-method lever
   cannot bind to the method's estimand, or calibration/transport evidence is
   insufficient, record the exact owner refusal. Do not force readiness.

The positive proves that the generic projector can mint value from a native
non-panel interval. The education refusal proves that it does not auto-pass.
Neither receipt substitutes for the other.

The validator derives the full Foundry family denominator from the live catalog
and requires a total supported/limited/refused classification. It does not
duplicate the 17-family list in engine code.

## 5. Thin depth-N router and observed coupling

The plain-language front door calls the existing NL `DesignProblem` compiler.
Proof recordings are content-addressed by raw request, compiler/tool schema,
model/provider identity, and raw response; they are not committed
`DesignProblem` fixtures. Re-derivation runs the real compiler over cached owner
responses, with one cold two-domain closeout when the owner is available.

The router accepts a validated `DesignProblem`, a recursion/budget policy, the
caller-resolved substrate context, and existing owner ports. It only:

- visits the existing recursive graph;
- invokes `GenerationCycleController` at executable leaves;
- projects leaf exits into existing `SubDesignContract`s;
- supplies the same child intervention set and coupling evidence to real N5;
- delegates classification, decomposition, composition, and law checks; and
- folds child outcomes into a typed root terminal.

Candidate module/decomposition output remains candidate-only. Depth is measured
from an observed execution graph, not inferred from `max_depth`. Lane 0 must
execute beyond depth two on synthetic mini-worlds.

Coupling evidence is explicit. Missing evidence uses `evidence_state="absent"`
and defaults toward entanglement. Empty edges do not prove independence. A
coupled parent invokes real N5; unsupported coupling emits a typed simulation
block. N5 receipts remain K_sim-only. Composition obligations are discharged
only through existing resolve/content-bind/verifier paths.
