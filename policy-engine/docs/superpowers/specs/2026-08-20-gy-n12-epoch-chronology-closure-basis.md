# GY-N12 Declared Closure Basis

## Status

Final declared basis: 128 headings and 128 unique IDs. The substantive combined
packet returned clean. Freeze takes effect only when the final status/record
delta over these exact bytes returns clean and the containing commit is read
back; any later modification reopens the affected review. Once frozen, future
implementation review uses the P40 bucket rule:
every escape is classified as a new class or the same class one level deeper,
and against design, record, or research method.

The decisive property is that the single policy-free chronology-proof protocol
reproduces and verifies the complete **admitted** history for a declared native
scope, while each family authority independently reconciles its complete source
denominator, semantic membership and native heads. Together they detect every
in-basis mutation or narrowing that could manufacture a pass, and family
currentness changes without erasing historical authenticity.

## A. Time and identity boundary

- **CB-A01 — native scope:** every member retains its existing family/root
  identity; no parent `DesignProblem` or merged scope is minted.
- **CB-A02 — native time:** each family carries only its applicable Custody Time
  Model roles and an explicit query coordinate; no required global timestamp.
- **CB-A03 — no universal envelope:** family payloads and persistence remain
  native; the shared owner cannot require irrelevant event fields.
- **CB-A04 — no authority by chronology:** recording, ordering, transport and
  projection cannot upgrade member authority or terminal disposition.
- **CB-A05 — historical authenticity:** stale, invalid, superseded, withdrawn,
  refused, void, disputed and exhausted members remain historically
  membership-verifiable.
- **CB-A06 — canonical currentness owners:** Decision Validity alone owns
  packet currentness and lineage heads; Claim Ledger alone owns claim-history
  transitions. Chronology supplies verified dependency evidence and cannot
  create parallel status/head ledgers.
- **CB-A07 — delivery is not authority:** GY-N12 may be the accountable lane for
  the protocol and adapters without absorbing source admission, legal effect,
  currentness, claim lifecycle, terminality or anchor acceptance.

## B. Common chronology proof

- **CB-B01 — complete declared basis:** the authority owner enumerates its full
  source denominator and content-binds the basis at admission.
- **CB-B02 — membership:** every admitted native member has a content-bound
  membership proof; valid-shaped substitution fails.
- **CB-B03 — two orders:** proof append/predecessor relations are content-bound
  and detect deletion, insertion, reordering and fork substitution offline.
  That integrity order is never a proxy for native semantic applicability,
  succession or currentness.
- **CB-B04 — consistency:** a later accepted head proves append-only extension
  from the pinned earlier head; post-hoc prefix/narrowing cannot pass.
- **CB-B05 — scoped heads:** heads are derived under the native family's scope
  rules, not from timestamps or list position; legitimate multi-head state is
  explicit rather than silently collapsed.
- **CB-B06 — unknown exterior:** an unobservable external copy yields a typed
  limitation; the owner makes no universal-completeness claim.
- **CB-B07 — offline replay:** the verifier needs the frozen native records,
  declared basis and proof material, not network state or wall-clock truth.
- **CB-B08 — recorder non-interference:** failure to record or project a
  completed governed result reports a custody gap but cannot rewrite the
  underlying result into permission or success.
- **CB-B09 — retained accepted anchor:** offline consistency is checked against
  an earlier independently accepted head. The anchor freezes accepting owner,
  proof-domain/log identity, signature, prior anchor, admission cutoff,
  witness/consumer-receipt basis, verifier provenance and provenance class;
  at least one holder is outside the writer's mutation authority. Without it,
  whole-history authenticity is `not_established`, not silently re-originated.
- **CB-B09A — query-bound anti-rollback:** the consumer binds the requested
  native query coordinate/cutoff and expected accepted-anchor lineage. A
  genuinely accepted older anchor remains valid for its own historical query
  but fails or becomes `not_established` when replayed as current at a later
  coordinate. Authenticity of the old prefix cannot manufacture a narrow pass.
- **CB-B10 — admission is not completeness:** membership/inclusion proof covers
  admitted members only. Each family owner independently reconciles them to
  its immutable complete source denominator; an omitted required member fails.
- **CB-B11 — two head predicates:** a commitment head proves prefix integrity;
  a native authority head expresses owner-derived current state. Neither may
  stand in for the other, and legitimate native multi-head state stays native.
- **CB-B12 — policy-free protocol:** the shared implementation owns
  canonicalization, commitments, consistency, proof dispositions and offline
  verification only. It cannot inspect family fields to decide eligibility,
  denominator completeness, semantic order, correction, terminality or a
  native authority head.
- **CB-B13 — adapters do not fork proof semantics:** family adapters map native
  records and supply owner proofs but cannot reimplement chain/consistency,
  anchor verification or mutation dispositions. A second family verifier that
  accepts a history the common verifier rejects fails.
- **CB-B14 — no N11 owner reuse:** reusable N11 hash-chain behavior and core
  canonical/CAS/audit primitives may be consolidated, but no chronology family
  is persisted under, keyed by or authorized through the confidence-ledger
  scope/root/head.
- **CB-B15 — algorithm-profiled property:** full-prefix hash-chain, Merkle or
  any other authenticated profile is admissible only when it passes the same
  membership, consistency, substitution, narrowing and accepted-anchor
  behaviors plus the measured complexity requirement. Algorithm name,
  root-field presence or exit status is not proof.
- **CB-B15A — proof-domain and profile isolation:** every commitment binds the
  native family, scope/root, proof-domain identity and canonicalization/hash/
  signature/schema profile versions. Unknown profiles reject without fallback;
  a valid proof replayed across a family or scope fails.
- **CB-B16 — head orthogonality:** an authority-only dependency transition can
  move a native currentness head without moving the commitment head; an
  annotation-only appended member can move the commitment head without moving
  authority. Both directions are tested.
- **CB-B17 — no mandatory native head:** a proof domain always has a
  commitment head; a family with no meaningful native authority head, including
  movement, is not forced to invent one.

## C. Epoch derivation and unresolved scope

- **CB-C00 — complete boundary-source denominator:** the epoch owner resolves
  every boundary source admitted by canonical source owners, including but not
  limited to L5 regimes, L3 amendments and N13b acquisitions. A valid novel
  data-only source registration is accepted without an engine edit; a malformed
  or unadmitted source fails closed.
- **CB-C01 — L5 full denominator:** boundaries derive from every applicable
  `schema_regime` entry resolved from the L5 authority, not a domain enum.
- **CB-C02 — L3 full denominator:** boundaries also derive from every applicable
  L3 amendment validity window, not sampled legal rows.
- **CB-C03 — fixed semantics:** one epoch binds the complete owner-admitted
  semantic-basis manifest. Its ratified minimum includes model class,
  obligation language and declared obligation set, calibration scope,
  measurement semantics, implementation semantics, equilibrium semantics,
  validator version, rule logic, schema/data regime and declared
  interpretation.
- **CB-C03A — facet-complete free-grow:** every registered semantic facet
  participates in the epoch identity. Adding a valid novel facet through the
  owner registry or changing any minimum facet while L3/L5/data stay fixed
  opens a new epoch and stales exact dependents without an engine conditional.
- **CB-C04 — explicit coordinate:** resolution consumes the scope-native owner
  coordinate; transaction/observation time cannot stand in for validity.
- **CB-C05 — unresolved honesty:** no sufficient regime/amendment data, or any
  missing, malformed, unknown or unadmitted mandatory semantic facet, returns
  `epoch_scope_unresolved`; no partial epoch, epoch zero, current head or
  nearest date is assumed.
- **CB-C06 — data-only free-grow:** adding a new domain regime/amendment or
  owner-admitted boundary-source registration through data alone changes
  resolution with zero engine code edit.
- **CB-C07 — Ukraine as case:** v1/prewar and v2/wartime resolve from data as the
  first test case and are absent from engine enums/conditionals.
- **CB-C08 — append-only revision:** a semantic revision appends a new epoch and
  may move a current head; it never mutates the previous epoch.
- **CB-C09 — annotation-only rename:** a renumbered rule with unchanged logic
  hash appends an annotation/correction relation without invalidating the
  epoch's semantics.
- **CB-C10 — epoch is not a clock:** `epoch_ref` is a content-bound semantic
  equivalence class resolved at explicit valid/effect and visibility
  coordinates. Maximum timestamp, receipt order or commitment position cannot
  select semantic applicability or the native epoch head.
- **CB-C10A — N13b/N12 one-time-semantics relation:** N13b preserves its
  overlay-local append/revision coordinate separately, while its passport and
  N12 resolve the identical semantic `epoch_ref`, complete basis and native
  valid/effect plus visibility coordinates. The overlay counter cannot select
  the epoch; a mismatch rejects or is `not_established`, and an admitted
  acquisition enters the same stale cascade as L3/L5 change.

## D. Certificate validity and perturbation

- **CB-D01 — issuance binding:** every certificate binds issuance epoch, the
  complete semantic-basis manifest from CB-C03, inputs, authority purpose,
  provenance, rule/schema/profile versions and applicable time coordinates.
- **CB-D02 — stale transition:** a relevant semantic perturbation makes the
  affected current certificate `stale`/`revalidation_required` through the
  canonical Decision Validity owner; an irrelevant perturbation does not.
- **CB-D03 — public fail closed:** stale or revalidation-required certificates
  cannot appear as current/publishable in public output.
- **CB-D04 — recipe is certificate:** every derived artifact binds its
  derivation recipe, input certificates and canonical producer. N12 emits the
  revalidation trigger; that producer executes the recipe and Decision Validity
  consumes the new certificate. An absent producer/bridge remains explicitly
  incomplete and chronology cannot recompute by projection.
- **CB-D05 — transitive invalidation:** invalidation propagates through exact
  lineage, stops at unaffected branches, and never uses filename/proximity as
  lineage.
- **CB-D06 — cascade vocabulary:** chronology never orders
  `annotation_only`, `invalidate`, `reissue`, `supersede` and `withdraw` or
  composes raw source events. It records an order-independent vector keyed by
  exact target, authority purpose and canonical owner, with one owner-
  adjudicated aggregate disposition per key.
- **CB-D06A — event is advisory:** arrival of an incident, appeal, correction,
  retraction, legal change or discovered-bias event cannot carry its declared
  `required_action` into authority. Before owner adjudication its ceiling is
  the applicable `review_required`, `contested` or annotation band.
- **CB-D06B — adjudicated reaction:** only a resolved, content-bound canonical-
  owner disposition can select `annotation_only`, `invalidate`, `reissue`,
  `supersede` or `withdraw`; changing the owner disposition while keeping the
  supplied event constant changes the reaction.
- **CB-D06C — claim lifecycle bridge:** affected public-claim transitions flow
  into the canonical Claim Ledger/lifecycle bridge; chronology cannot project
  those transitions directly.
- **CB-D06D — mixed-outcome oracle:** different target keys remain distinct;
  `annotation_only` may accompany but cannot cancel an authority transition;
  conflicting aggregates for the same key yield `contested`/
  `review_required` and freeze rather than a guessed maximum. Permuting input
  event order does not change the owner-adjudicated vector.
- **CB-D07 — historical query:** a certificate invalid now remains verifiable
  as authentic at its issuance coordinate with its later invalidation linked.
- **CB-D08 — OpenWorldRisk relation:** PolicyOS consumes typed scope evidence
  signed by the competent external/deployment owner rather than inferring scope
  by observation. Intake resolves and content-binds the evidence and verifier
  provenance; signature/presence alone is only `institutionally_supplied` and
  cannot carry the gate. The deployment lifecycle/query owner establishes the
  required scope role at the native coordinate—`authorized_intended` before
  deployment, `actual` after deployment begins—and the caller cannot select
  it. The N12 relation producer recomputes or independently reconciles that
  required scope against every declared model, obligation and calibration
  scope component. The content-bound component vector, not caller role or
  severity, is the predicate.
- **CB-D08A — OpenWorldRisk composition:** the result is `open_world_risk` if
  any component is independently proven outside, `within_scope` only if every
  required component is independently proven inside, and `not_established`
  otherwise. Missing/unknown never collapses to low/false, and no unconditional
  numerical risk claim is authorized.
- **CB-D09 — promotion freeze:** an admitted revision trigger,
  `open_world_risk` or `not_established` scope relation freezes promotion in
  exactly the affected scope; a supplied low/false value, deadline, quota,
  projection or LLM cannot weaken the freeze or limitation.
- **CB-D10 — current-valid decision front:** revalidation removes stale or
  revalidation-required certificates from decision-front eligibility; only
  owner-established `current_valid` certificates remain.
- **CB-D11 — freshness non-receipt:** an absent, unknown or expired current-
  status proof becomes `not_established`/`revalidation_required`; it cannot
  inherit the last observed green state. Historical issuance validity remains
  a separate query.
- **CB-D12 — complete executable recipe:** the derivation certificate binds the
  actual input, tool, code and admitted environment-profile closure. Removing
  an influential dependency while retaining a shaped recipe makes automatic
  reuse/revalidation fail.
- **CB-D13 — missed-obligation reissue:** discovering an obligation after
  issuance causes challenge -> current-authority invalidation -> appended new
  widened-basis epoch -> canonical-producer recompute -> appended reissued
  certificate. A same-epoch recompute or mutation of the old certificate fails.
- **CB-D13A — basis-delta projection honesty:** the old certificate and
  arithmetic remain historically reproducible under their closure epoch and
  declared set while current authority is withdrawn. A delta caused only by
  the widened basis cannot be projected as a better result.

## E. Controlled release family

- **CB-E01 — native release membership:** every controlled release binds its
  native release identity, model/rule versions, inputs and verifier
  dispositions/provenance.
- **CB-E02 — immutable family declaration:** family membership criteria and
  declared denominator are content-bound; later narrowing is an appended
  correction and cannot rewrite the original basis.
- **CB-E03 — deletion fails:** removing a controlled release or its verifier
  disposition breaks membership/consistency verification rather than making
  the remaining set pass.
- **CB-E04 — disposition authenticity:** a shaped/present/self-attested
  verifier field cannot satisfy admission; verifier provenance and content
  binding are resolved.
- **CB-E05 — withdrawn verifiable:** withdrawal changes current release status
  while retaining historical membership and proof.
- **CB-E06 — prefix issuance:** PV-K07 can issue only from a verified pinned
  prefix and append-only consistency proof; an unknown external copy limits
  the claim.
- **CB-E07 — required-submission reconciliation:** adding a required release to
  the immutable family denominator without admitting its leaf fails the
  transcript even though every presented leaf has a valid inclusion proof.
- **CB-E08 — transcript head meaning:** GY-GAP3's current transcript head is
  the latest consumer-accepted commitment head at the requested cutoff. It
  proves the accepted prefix only and cannot be projected as current release
  authority, preference or validity.
- **CB-E09 — release-head orthogonality:** annotation-only append may move the
  transcript head without changing release authority. A native release head
  exists only if a separately competent owner supplies a successor/withdrawal
  relation; no such head is inferred from append order or created by GY-GAP3.

## F. Production recursive-run enumeration

- **CB-F01 — production denominator:** membership covers the complete source-
  derived denominator of family-native production events at the authoritative
  emission boundary, including start, receipt, resolution and custody-gap roles
  when the native owner emits them; it never comes from a later filesystem,
  dashboard, terminal-only filter or fixture census.
- **CB-F02 — exact problem binding:** every run binds exact `DesignProblem`
  content and retains each per-root identity under INT-K05.
- **CB-F03 — exact cycle binding:** every member binds recursive graph and cycle
  content identities, not only run ID or timestamp.
- **CB-F03A — event/terminal role separation:** every family-native event binds
  exact `DesignProblem`, graph and cycle identities, but only an event resolved
  by the competent producer/lifecycle owner may bind a terminal. Start, receipt
  and custody-gap events remain chronology members without inferred terminality.
- **CB-F04 — terminal authenticity:** search and lifecycle terminals remain
  distinct owner values; refusal/void/dispute/exhaustion are completed members.
- **CB-F05 — per-root heads:** current heads are derived per existing root from
  an explicit owner-native retry/supersession/branch relation; no synthetic
  family parent. If that relation is absent, head status is unresolved rather
  than maximum append position or timestamp.
- **CB-F06 — resolved-terminal projection:** projections consume owner-validated
  terminals and exact membership; projection cannot manufacture terminality.
- **CB-F07 — run deletion/narrowing:** deletion, substitution, reordering or a
  post-hoc narrowed production denominator fails offline verification.
- **CB-F08 — recorder failure:** a recorder failure is an explicit custody gap
  and does not alter the run's governed terminal; complete chronology remains
  `not_established` until the receipt is reconciled.
- **CB-F09 — no unrecorded sibling producer:** a source-derived census of every
  live production recursive-run entry path proves all of them cross the single
  receipt-or-gap emission boundary. Adding a sibling producer that returns a
  run without either outcome fails the behavioral strangle test even though no
  recorded member was later deleted.
- **CB-F10 — never-receipted run:** suppress the receipt for a run that reaches
  a governed terminal. The producer-owned terminal remains unchanged under
  GY-GAP5's additive/non-blocking rule; INT-K08 separately retains every
  negative terminal as a completed member and forbids missing custody from
  becoming green. Independent source-denominator reconciliation detects the
  unreconciled production member and the owner cannot claim a complete run
  chronology. A proof over all successfully recorded leaves is insufficient.

## G. Per-row movement composition

- **CB-G00 — complete eligible-row denominator:** at a bound scope/cutoff, the
  denominator contains every row present in the N13b admission store and bound
  by the canonical acquisition receipt to the exact `DesignProblem`/cycle for
  consumption. N13b admission plus the producer-owned receipt recompute
  eligibility outside the movement adapter; caller filters cannot narrow it.
- **CB-G01 — exact row identity:** movement starts from an admitted N13b row
  bound to observation, passport, raw-evidence hash/artifact, L5 tier and
  resolved semantic epoch.
- **CB-G02 — exact problem/run identity:** that row is linked to the exact
  `DesignProblem`, recursive run and generation-cycle content identities.
- **CB-G03 — same-cycle re-entry:** the movement receipt binds N7 source and
  re-entry indices and they are equal under the same problem.
- **CB-G04 — deeper terminal:** before/after terminals are recomputed owner
  values and the “after” is causally downstream of the admitted row/re-entry.
- **CB-G05 — no adjacency proxy:** count changes, timestamps, later runs or
  neighboring rows cannot mint movement.
- **CB-G06 — absent is absent:** a missing link produces no movement fact; it is
  distinct from a producer-established “no movement.”
- **CB-G06A — per-row reconciliation:** every eligible denominator member maps
  to exactly one proved movement, endpoint-owner-proved `no_movement`, or typed
  `movement_not_established` gap. Silence or adapter omission is never
  owner-proved no movement.
- **CB-G07 — deletion/substitution:** removing or swapping any row/passport/raw
  evidence/run/re-entry/terminal link fails verification.
- **CB-G08 — no movement head invention:** movement has a proof commitment head
  but no independent native authority head. Its projection consumes exact
  endpoint-owner currentness and terminal facts.
- **CB-G09 — whole-row omission:** omit one eligible N13b row before composition
  while keeping every remaining relation and proof valid. Denominator
  reconciliation and the common consumer fail closed; a valid suffix of
  movements cannot claim complete per-row history.

## H. Gate provenance and behavioral verification

- **CB-H01 — frozen predicate provenance:** every decisive admission predicate
  is labeled at admission as `recomputed`, `independently_reconciled`,
  `consumer_asserted`, `institutionally_supplied`, or `not_established`.
- **CB-H02 — authority classes:** only recomputed or independently reconciled
  predicates can carry authority-grade admission; the other three fail closed.
- **CB-H03 — supplied epoch falsifier:** keep a positive, unique supplied
  `epoch_id` but falsify its regime coordinate; admission must fail.
- **CB-H04 — form falsifier:** retain marker strings and field names while
  deleting decisive content/consistency validation; the real-path gate fails.
- **CB-H05 — novel-member falsifier:** a new valid-shaped family member with an
  unknown relation/provenance fails without updating an enumerated code list.
- **CB-H06 — sibling consumer:** a second consumer cannot bypass the single
  intake/emission owner or reinterpret a limitation as pass.
- **CB-H07 — adjudication falsifier:** hold the supplied event and its declared
  action constant while changing canonical-owner admission/adjudication; the
  authority reaction follows the owner decision, not the event marker.
- **CB-H08 — whole-history substitution:** retain valid-shaped members and a
  self-consistent replacement head, and replace every anchor writable by the
  transcript actor. Verification must still fail against an independently
  held accepted anchor. If no independent anchor exists, the verifier returns
  the declared `not_established` limitation; it cannot bless a new origin.
- **CB-H09 — omitted-recipe-dependency falsifier:** hold recipe fields and the
  declared dependency list constant while changing an actually influential
  omitted tool/environment input. Reuse or revalidation must fail rather than
  return a cache-like green result.
- **CB-H10 — policy-leak falsifier:** hold the proof bundle and commitment head
  fixed while changing a canonical family-owner admission/currentness
  disposition. Proof integrity remains unchanged and the authority result
  follows the family owner; the shared protocol cannot adjudicate it.
- **CB-H11 — algorithm mutation:** keep profile names, root fields and marker
  strings while deleting actual predecessor/consistency verification. The
  whole-history substitution and deletion negatives fail the real path.
- **CB-H12 — writer-self-anchor:** give the writer a valid signature and a
  self-consistent rewritten history but no independently accepted/retained
  anchor. Whole-history authenticity remains `not_established`.
- **CB-H13 — terminal/receipt divergence:** suppress the run chronology receipt
  after a producer-owned negative or positive terminal. Terminality is
  byte-identical while chronology completeness fails against the independent
  production denominator.
- **CB-H13A — start/recorder-failure divergence:** emit a correctly bound start
  event, then suppress durable recording before any owner-resolved terminal.
  The start remains enumerable against the independent source denominator, no
  terminal is inferred, and the complete-chronology claim fails until the
  receipt/gap is reconciled.
- **CB-H14 — valid-prefix denominator omission:** keep every presented member,
  commitment and consistency proof valid while omitting one required member
  from the family source-of-truth denominator at the bound cutoff. Independent
  reconciliation and the authority consumer fail closed.
- **CB-H15 — authentic-anchor rollback:** present a genuinely accepted old
  anchor, its original denominator and a valid consistency proof for a later/
  current requested coordinate. Query-bound anchor-lineage reconciliation
  fails or returns `not_established`; the old prefix remains valid only for its
  historical query.
- **CB-H16 — unknown proof profile:** retain valid-shaped profile/version fields
  but supply an unknown canonicalization/hash/schema profile. Verification
  rejects without defaulting to a known profile.
- **CB-H17 — cross-domain replay:** replay a valid member, proof and anchor
  under another family, scope or root while retaining all hashes. The bound
  proof-domain identity makes verification fail.
- **CB-H18 — N13b/N12 disagreement:** keep the positive unique overlay-local
  counter and passport shape valid while making the passport's semantic stamp/
  basis disagree with N12 resolution at identical native coordinates.
  Admission fails; once agreement is restored, the admitted acquisition causes
  the same dependent stale transition as an L3/L5 boundary.
- **CB-H19 — semantic-facet mutation:** with L3/L5/data fixed, mutate each
  ratified fixed-semantics facet in turn and add one novel registered facet.
  Each relevant change opens a new epoch and stales exact dependents; an
  unregistered/unknown facet fails closed rather than being ignored.
- **CB-H20 — OpenWorldRisk premise falsifier:** hold a supplied `low`/`false`
  value and green intended-scope evidence constant, then advance the owner-
  established lifecycle to actual deployment and move actual scope outside one
  declared component; the result is `open_world_risk`. Remove required actual-
  scope evidence or a required component and the result is `not_established`,
  never intended-scope `within_scope`; both results freeze promotion.
- **CB-H21 — missed-obligation cascade:** add one obligation after issuance.
  Mutation of the old certificate or same-epoch recompute fails; only the
  append-only widened-basis epoch/reissue chain passes, and its delta cannot be
  presented as an improvement.
- **CB-H22 — cascade mixed outcomes:** while holding owner-native semantic
  predecessor/successor relations fixed, permute transport/arrival order,
  combine disjoint target dispositions and present conflicting aggregates for
  one owner/target key. The target vector is transport-order-independent,
  disjoint outcomes stay separate, annotation cannot cancel authority change
  and the conflict freezes as `contested`/`review_required`.
