## CB1 build and falsification design

A canonical strict, frozen behavioral contract binds corpus/version, source and build identities,
construct, modality, safety cell, target role, authority level, tenure band, environment and sealed
admissible action set. Training and sealed partitions must be disjoint by item identity AND content
identity; counterfactual families cannot leak across partitions. Corpus inputs include unknown,
outer-set, incomparable, delta-budget, currentness, quarantine and missing-owner conditions,
with keyboard/screen-reader/low-numeracy/time-pressure dimensions. These are synthetic test items,
never formative co-design or real operator evidence.

The runner consumes frozen scenario packets and raw trial events: primary blocker selection,
pre-feedback confidence, action attempt, interlock outcome, terminal commit/defer and timeout.
Duplicates reconcile by identity; conflicting events or out-of-order confidence invalidate rather
than silently impute. The after-hours scenario has unavailable escalation and an explicit safe
abstention route. Attempted unsafe action and committed unsafe action are separate measures.
Each eligible-opportunity measure retains its numerator and denominator. No eligible result produces
`not_established`; absent coverage cannot become a zero score. An internal exact-binomial upper-bound calculation consumes failures, eligible opportunities and
explicit candidate alpha; numerical endpoints are tested against analytic synthetic controls. Zero
opportunities yields no estimate. Public cell upper bounds and all threshold values remain typed
empty under WP-09, with measure/population/horizon/assumptions/authority provenance slots
preserved for a later governed implementation.

The demonstrability rule is sealed before execution: unavailable route/event integrity, all-invalid
stratum, or zero eligible opportunities yields the corresponding fixed non-result with run inputs
bound. A later caller cannot relabel failure as passage. Synthetic harness success yields only an
instrument-integrity diagnostic. Real study execution, recruitment/ethics and external adjudication
remain with W5-R3-Q06 and W5-R3-Q14 institutional work; no appointed owner is manufactured.

Red first: conformance-only inputs cannot establish comprehension; no eligible opportunities cannot
emit score. Additional negatives cover partition leakage (ID and content), retrospective blocker
credit, attempted/committed conflation, changed sealed key, after-hours phantom responder,
conflicting duplicate and missing safety cell. Persisted record readback recomputes the outcome and
refuses a corrupt decisive field. A removal probe disables actual scoring/refusal behavior while
keeping contract markers: the same negatives must fail.

## ML1 build and falsification design

A content-addressed EN–UA high-stakes synthetic corpus carries source and target bytes, independently
addressed candidate semantic frames and complete context/action ground truth. It includes the three
ratified promotions: limited→confirmed-with-caveat, prohibition→advice, and the collapse of
stale/superseded/withdrawn. Whole-proposition action profiles compare allowed/required/forbidden
sets over every declared context, including qualifiers, actor, modality, numeric and temporal scope.
Matching catalogue keys or matching asserted frames cannot establish natural-language meaning.
A changed source/rendition digest invalidates an earlier comparison; unknown scope remains refused.

The candidate certificate form binds exact proposition IDs, purposes, qualified holder IDs (empty),
source/rendition identities, versioned complete tested contexts, exclusions and validity interval.
Every consumer requires a requested member of all declared dimensions, so a finite result cannot
be read as general equivalence. The signer, trust roots and key custody remain typed empty; no key
material is generated and MAEP exposes no signing API. Existing `core.artifacts.store.FileSystemCAS`
`verify_signature` and `core.artifacts.signing.Ed25519Verifier` already own detached cryptographic
verification, trust/revocation and signer-identity binding. MAEP reuses that strict verifier with
empty trusted keys rather than creating a second cryptographic owner. Content integrity is actually checked from resolved source/rendition/corpus bytes. Read-time
validity and an append-only content-bound revocation/invalidation chain reject stale, changed or
revoked objects; historical replay retains the old record without reactivating current use. Trust-root
rotation and key-custody input contracts remain empty, and neither placeholder keys nor self-signed
certificates can satisfy their admission. The nonsigning integrity/revocation machinery is built
now; institutional roots and signed issuance remain refused. Named RTL source-content fixture pack `IL-Hebr`
is candidate-only and explicitly distinct from UI-locale admission; its ten WP-12 evidence slots
remain empty. WP-11's pivot split is carried as withheld, never encoded as admission policy.
W5-R6-Q05 co-authentic reconciliation and Q07 security/cryptographic appointment remain external.

The independent current catalogue census covers EVERY direct locale-directory member and all
primitive string leaves under `apps/runtime-dashboard/src/shared/i18n/locales/`. Invalid JSON,
duplicate keys, arrays/nonstring leaves, non-JSON members or unreadable cases fail as ambiguous.
Parser A is allocated to the C agent at `lex/knowledge/locale_census.py`; parser B is allocated to
the root agent at `tools/quality/validation/locale_census_independent.py`. Both expose
`read_locale_catalogues(directory: Path) -> dict[str, dict[tuple[str, ...], str]]`, without shared
parsing helpers or cross-imports. The root owns `check_multilingual_locale_census.py`, which compares
exact maps and independently read byte identities, then emits concise counts and missing/extra and
identity measures. Corrupting a deciding field in either parser's result alone must make independent
reconciliation fail. Derived leaf maps stay in memory; no duplicate source representation is stored.

Red first: W5-K06 scope escape and each FX-001–003 promotion are separate negatives, plus structural
parity with changed legal modality, matched fake frame over changed bytes, empty holder and corrupt
result readback. Adjacent corrected synthetic cases must permit diagnostic comparison while still
refusing equivalence. RTL pack cannot admit UI, source authority or jurisdiction. Removal probes
must remove the decisive comparison or scope check, retaining markers, and make negatives fail.
