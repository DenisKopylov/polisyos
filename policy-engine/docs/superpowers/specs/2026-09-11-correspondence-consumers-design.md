## Normative findings

Read the complete standing-rule row, including its same-day membership correction
and replacement of points 3–5. It binds exactly the two correspondence members
in this lane. The register supplies the rule, never evidence about today's tree.

1. **Point 1:** only confident bindings are adjudicated for wrongness, under
   `false correspondence > abstain`. Abstentions are unlabelled. The possible
   future number bounds `P(confident-wrong)` within the stated cell and epoch;
   it says nothing about recall or completeness.
2. **Point 2:** pre-declare and date the decisive-property stratum before the
   lane's execution. Historical known negatives are disclosed as such; rerunning
   them does not turn them into unseen calibration evidence. `INT-K07` still
   blocks a number obtained through outcome-informed selection without validity.
3. **Amended point 3:** construction proves positive correspondence only for a
   pre-declared class with independently authoritative semantic identities and
   a non-circular mechanical recognition rule. Otherwise known mismatches are
   constructed negative gold and positive correctness requires independent,
   purpose-qualified adjudication. Neither member has such positive evidence here.
4. **Amended point 4:** without that proof or appointment, carry `INT-K06` custody
   with no numerical correctness claim; every consumer requiring it refuses.
5. **Amended point 5:** `CAUSAL_INSTANTIATION` and
   `LEGAL_GOVERNANCE_CORRESPONDENCE` share a procedural envelope, not substantive
   predicates, qualifications, populations or counts. A person must be separately
   qualified and appointed for both purposes to exercise both.

`W5-K07`, ratified in wave-5 §12.2 on 2026-09-10, forbids substituting statute
existence for governance of a lever. Its §12.3 explicitly names **W4-K01** for
the signature half: custody is not correctness. No local rule or W5-K08 is created.
Publication grade is not correspondence gold; signed observations are not calibration.

CGF §E.4.1 (ruled 2026-09-08, written 2026-09-10) fixes the causal cell to
`operator_family × target_type × domain × difficulty_tier`; difficulty is derived
from inputs alone. `proposer_model`, `prompt_version`, `atom_birth_cohort` and
`reference_epoch` are epoch scope. A move stales affected certificates immediately,
transfers no bound, and requires a dated frame with the complete denominator.
Model/prompt changes forbid calibration reuse; reference-only reuse requires proof
that the repair touches no member; a new cohort is cold-start. No adjudication
budget is spent by this lane. The ratified design ceilings (`delta_ground=0.05`,
`delta_RT1_relation=0.04`, per-bind `0.01`, prospective `n=299,k=0,alpha=0.05`,
four authority-grade binds per run) are requirements, not achieved measurements.
The approximate thirty-six-cell discussion is not a measured tree census here.

discovery is the registered `policy-design-case-layer3-gy-intervention-substrate-contract`
family and `python -m tools.quality.validation.check_layer3_gy_intervention_substrate_contract`.

The lane runner is invoked explicitly from its documented path; it is an evidence
recorder, not a new product command, generated family or acceptance authority.

Execution begins with in-process removal of the subject comparison and causal
critical-veto guard, each applied to an unchanged deciding test. Expected failure
must be an assertion about the semantic property, not import/setup failure.
Unmodified exact nodes follow. Constructed-negative success never mints a bound.

by path and SHA-256. Gate argv, node IDs, timeout, duration and return code are retained.
Every gate is the only command in its invocation. No directory-targeted test or full suite.

Serialize git, shared files, legal production WMR/CAS creation and the causal live
frame check. Other tests use isolated pytest stores and can overlap. Root owns all
shared writes; at most three workstreams. Read data without changing the held source;
copy mutable CAS into lane scratch. Use AST for definition/call inventories.

Python offline sync was attempted and lacked cached jaxlib. The isolated Python
3.14.0 interpreter uses already-installed third-party packages through a read-only
site-packages path; local `src` wins. This is disclosed dependency reuse, not a
successful frozen install. `corepack pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` completed before
any TypeScript scanner is trusted. No dependency manifest or lock is changed.
